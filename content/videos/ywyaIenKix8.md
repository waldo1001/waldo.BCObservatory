# Subscription Billing

> Subscription billing in Business Central: a feature set for managing subscription contracts, running recurring billing, recognizing subscription revenue, and running usage-based billing via CSV or custom API import. The video states it is part of the essential Business Central license.

## Overview

A short introduction to the subscription billing feature set in Business Central. It covers managing subscriptions with properly documented contracts, including terms, services, billing frequency, renewals and termination conditions.

It also describes running the recurring billing process to fit your billing rhythm, recognizing subscription revenue in line with your finance reporting cadence, and running usage-based billing by importing quantities and prices. The video says subscription billing is part of the essential Business Central license.

## Key points

- Subscription billing is a feature set for managing subscriptions with properly documented contracts.
- Contracts can be managed with the correct term, services, billing frequency, renewals and termination conditions.
- The recurring billing process can be adapted to your billing rhythm so subscription bills go out on time.
- Subscription revenue can be recognized following your reporting cadence in finance.
- Usage-based billing works by importing quantities and prices for the recurring billing process via CSV file or custom API.
- The video states subscription billing is part of the essential Business Central license.
