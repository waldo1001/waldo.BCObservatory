# What's New: Payables Agent Update (2025 release wave 2)

> Payables agent in Business Central (2025 release wave 2): AI-powered vendor invoice processing, in preview in the US, UK, Australia and New Zealand. The video demos email-based PDF ingestion, draft review with reasoning, and vendor creation with blocking, then covers the roadmap.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xc3ISQ7NAaQ) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2025-10-01 · 14:44 · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Overview

The video presents the payables agent, which handles vendor invoice processing from a monitored email address. It reads PDF invoices, proposes a draft with line items and deferrals, and shows its reasoning. A human must review the AI-generated content and confirm before a purchase invoice is created.

It also covers setup, which needs little configuration and no mapping of items or accounts. The roadmap includes better vendor matching, purchase order matching, e-invoices, fraud detection, approvals, optional autonomous posting and general availability with possible language and geographic restrictions.

## Key points

- The payables agent is in preview in four countries: United States, United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand.
- The agent monitors a configured email address for PDF invoice attachments. You can expose the address to vendors or use it only for internal forwarding.
- All agent output is AI-generated and needs human review. A human must confirm before the final purchase invoice is created.
- Vendors the agent creates are set with Blocked = all. The user must unblock them before processing continues, which respects due diligence and approval processes.
- Setup is minimal, with no mapping of items or accounts. Optional parameters can adjust the processing workflow and matching algorithms.
- Planned but not yet implemented: better vendor matching, two-way and three-way purchase order matching, e-invoices, fraud detection, approvals from anywhere, and optional autonomous posting.
- General availability is intended to be worldwide, but language and geographic restrictions may remain and timing is to be announced.
