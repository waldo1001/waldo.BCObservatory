## Overview

The video covers a small addition to Business Central 2025 wave 1 that addresses confusion caused by the full list of product languages. Administrators can open an allowed languages setting and choose which languages users may select.

The demo shows the administrator selecting languages, then the user's My Settings page, where only the allowed languages appear. The presenter says a community member built the capability and that it ships with 2025 wave 1 through a pull request.
