# What's New: Co-Development Initiative (2024 release wave 2)

> Business Central co-development initiative in 2024 release wave 2: 156 community contributions shipped. Five demos cover Manual Setup page cleanup, a duplicate external document notification, default GL account quantities, Copilot-assisted number series setup, and a ship-to phone number field.

## Overview

Co-development is the Business Central team's ambition to develop the BC business application together with the partner community. The video says 156 community contributions shipped in 2024 release wave 2, and that a small group of four community members added many of them.

Five contributions are demoed: a reorganized Manual Setup page, a notification for duplicate external document numbers on sales invoices, default quantities for GL accounts in Sales Setup, Copilot generating number series for a module, and a phone number field on custom ship-to addresses in sales orders. The video also covers a Contribution Hero award and ends with a call to action to engage.

## Key points

- Co-development is the BC team's ambition to jointly develop the BC application with the partner community.
- 156 community contributions shipped in 2024 release wave 2; a few of them came from just four community members.
- The Manual Setup page is reorganized and grouped to reduce clutter and make setup options easier to find.
- A notification warns when a sales invoice is entered with an external document number that was already used.
- Sales Setup has a new setting for default quantities on GL accounts; the quantity fills in when a GL account is selected on a sales invoice.
- Copilot can generate and configure all required number series for a module such as Purchase and Payables; users can review and change them before applying.
- The phone number field on custom ship-to addresses in sales orders required modifying 783 objects.
