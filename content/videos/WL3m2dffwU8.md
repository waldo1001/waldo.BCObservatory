# Introducing: New Microsoft Copilot Chat in Business Central (2026 release wave 2)

> New unified Microsoft Copilot Chat in Business Central (2026 release wave 2): a Copilot button opens the unified Microsoft Copilot experience. It answers general questions and questions about Business Central data, using the user's permissions, context and company, and supports side-by-side data viewing and follow-up questions. With an additional Microsoft Copilot license it also uses Work IQ data such as calendar, email, Teams and OneDrive/SharePoint files. Short demo (about 3.5 minutes).

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WL3m2dffwU8) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2026-10-01 · 3:34 · tier official · reviewed

## Overview

The video introduces a new unified Microsoft Copilot experience that now appears in Business Central through a new Copilot button. The same Copilot works across Microsoft products, and in Business Central it has access to the user's data while respecting permissions and context.

The demo shows Copilot answering general questions and questions about Business Central data, opening data side by side with the answer, and handling a multi-turn conversation about sales orders. With an additional Microsoft Copilot license, Copilot can also reach Work IQ data such as calendar, emails, Teams conversations and files from OneDrive or SharePoint.

## Key points

- A new Copilot button in the Business Central interface opens the updated, unified Microsoft Copilot experience.
- Copilot can answer general questions and questions about Business Central data, using the user's context, permissions and company.
- Data can be opened side by side with the Copilot answer, so the response and the underlying records can be read together.
- Multi-turn conversation is demoed: follow-up questions can be asked about the same Business Central data.
- Work IQ access (calendar, emails, Teams conversations, OneDrive or SharePoint files) requires an additional Microsoft Copilot license.
- The presenter says the feature is available in the newest Business Central and invites users to try it.
