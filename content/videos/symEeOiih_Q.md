## Overview

In this two-minute BCTalent story, iFacto explains its business focus and how it used the ReSkill program to recruit new Business Central consultants. The program found candidates the partner says it could not have found on its own, because it looked in other areas than the partner normally would.

The video covers which skills new consultants need, including BC product knowledge, AI, business intelligence, Power Apps and consulting skills. iFacto says the people hired through the program are enthusiastic, eager to learn and were rapidly integrated into the team, and it recommends the program to other partners.
