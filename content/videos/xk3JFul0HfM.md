## Overview

Mike Morton talks about the Business Central talent gap and presents ReSkill, a program built by Microsoft together with its development partners. It recruits people who have industry knowledge or transferable skills and gives them product and professional training so they can become proficient Business Central professionals.

The video is about 2 minutes long. It gives two figures: new hires can contribute to billable customer activities within 8 to 12 weeks after onboarding, and the retention rate of new hires over the last 12 months is 99.2%. It closes with Microsoft's stated commitment to investing in Business Central talent and an invitation to join.

## Key points

- ReSkill is a collaboration between Microsoft and development partners to cultivate new Business Central talent.
- It recruits individuals with industry knowledge or transferable skills.
- It includes product and professional training, described as affordable and low risk for partners.
- Training and coaching let new hires contribute to billable customer activities within 8 to 12 weeks after onboarding.
- The retention rate of new hires from ReSkill over the last 12 months is stated as 99.2%.
- Microsoft says it is committed to investing in Business Central talent.
