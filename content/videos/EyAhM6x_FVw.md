# Episode 518: You Have 18 Months: The AI Marketing Reckoning Nobody's Ready For

> Dynamics Corner episode 518 with Lisa Cole, chief marketing and AI officer at 2X, on how AI answer engines and agents change B2B marketing and buying. It covers AI visibility, building in public, brand gravity, employee voices, and a nine to 18 month window to adapt.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EyAhM6x_FVw) · Dynamics Corner podcast · 2026-05-20 · 59:44 · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Overview

Lisa Cole, chief marketing and AI officer at 2X, discusses how AI is changing marketing on two fronts: buyers use answer engines and AI agents for research, and marketers can use AI to produce and distribute content. She cites research that buyers do most of their research anonymously and form a finalist list before talking to sales, so visibility in that phase matters.

The conversation covers building in public, creating enough digital mass for brand gravity, and favoring individual employee and leader voices over corporate pages. It also covers websites designed for both humans and agents, and practical starting points such as lead enrichment and outsourcing to AI-forward partners. Cole says organizations have roughly nine to 18 months to get a handle on this. The episode is a marketing discussion, not a Business Central product walkthrough.

## Key points

- Buyers do most research anonymously and, per the cited studies, form a finalist list with 85% accuracy before speaking to sales, so visibility during research is critical.
- Content must be findable and understandable by both humans and AI agents, and agents may still need to choose your product.
- AI repurposing of core human content across channels builds digital mass, but it needs an original point of view; generic AI content reads as slop.
- People trust individual leaders and subject matter experts more than corporate pages, so invest in amplifying employee voices and support those employees.
- Websites are moving toward simple AI chat and agent-friendly access rather than heavily branded navigation.
- Suggested AI starting points: lead enrichment before sales hand-off, and outsourcing to AI-forward partners; one example cut campaign time-to-market from 92 days to 2 weeks.
- Cole says leaders have about nine to 18 months to get a handle on AI and avoid random, uncoordinated AI use.
