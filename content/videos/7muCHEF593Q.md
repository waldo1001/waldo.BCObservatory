# How to Access All Features in Business Central (2025)

> Business Central role-based homepages (business manager, accountant, sales and relationships, project manager) share one layout of activity tiles and charts. The video also covers ways to reach all features: the menu header (including explore more roles), the search bar and Alt-key shortcuts. A short 2-minute demo.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7muCHEF593Q) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2025-05-22 · 2:28 · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

The video shows the role-based homepages in Business Central and what each one surfaces. The business manager homepage shows sales and purchase documents, payments needing attention and balances. The accountant homepage covers payments, approvals, finance charts, receivables and payables. The sales and relationships homepage covers opportunities, quotes, campaigns and pipeline. The project manager homepage covers work in process, budget, jobs, tasks, resources and purchasing.

All homepages share one layout. Activity tiles lead to daily tasks, and charts can be adjusted. Users can change their role and configure the homepage in settings. To reach features not on the homepage, the video shows the menu header, the explore more roles section, the search bar and Alt-key shortcuts.

## Key points

- Homepages are available for business manager, accountant, sales and relationships, and project manager roles.
- All homepages share the same layout; tiles in the activity section lead to daily tasks such as processing orders or invoices.
- Charts on homepages can have their period customized, or be switched to another chart through section headings.
- Role and homepage configuration are changed in the user's settings.
- Clicking the menu header opens all features; expanding the explore more roles section shows additional roles.
- The search bar finds specific pages.
- Pressing Alt triggers shortcut keys; for example, J and S open sales, then S again opens sales quotes.
