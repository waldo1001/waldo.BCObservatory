# Business Central Under the Hood episode 1: Developing AI in Business Central

> Developing AI features in Business Central: how Microsoft's team designs LLM features, covering prompt techniques (few-shot, Chain of Thought, function calling, meta-prompting), the Copilot chat service architecture, hallucination and jailbreak prevention, and the AI toolkit for partners.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jdevJbGgB-k) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2024-02-13 · 26:05 · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Overview

Episode 1 of the Under the Hood series, published 2024-02-13, explains how the Business Central team builds AI features. It mentions marketing text generation and bank reconciliation matching as shipped features, and describes a chat Copilot that focuses on guidance and navigation and is still improving in quality.

The talk then covers practical prompt engineering: few-shot learning, Chain of Thought, function calling, feeding the LLM current data to limit hallucinations, and meta-prompting against jailbreaks. It also describes the Copilot service architecture with a chat pane, orchestrator and plugin service, the difficulty of testing fuzzy inputs and outputs, and Azure OpenAI Studio as a tool for building prompts.

## Key points

- Marketing text generation (generally available) creates item marketing text from keywords; bank reconciliation already uses LLMs to match bank records.
- Few-shot learning shows the LLM examples of input and expected output, and makes it easy to specify formats such as JSON or XML.
- Chain of Thought tells the LLM to reason step by step; it outputs intermediate steps and takes longer to respond.
- Function calling gives the LLM function definitions and parameters; the LLM does not execute code, it only says what to call and with which parameters.
- To limit hallucinations, always feed the LLM current data and instruct it in the prompt to answer only from the provided data.
- Chat architecture: a chat pane in the client talks to a cloud Copilot service with an orchestrator for multi-turn history and context and a plugin service for skills.
- Meta-prompting and Azure OpenAI content filters reduce jailbreaking, but new jailbreak techniques keep appearing; the user must stay in the loop.
