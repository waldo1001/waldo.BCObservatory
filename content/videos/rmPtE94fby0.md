## Overview

This 3-minute BCTalent Stories video from March 2024 features trainees and a company perspective on the Rescale program at Ciellos. Trainees describe starting to learn Business Central through the program and finding tasks easier than expected.

The company side says the program walks students through cases and processes in a methodical way. It expects less development in the future and a need for people who understand business processes, with training that includes Copilot and AI. They say the program is challenging but the value is paying off compared with the cost.

## Key points

- The Rescale program teaches soft skills and business consulting skills, working case by case and process by process.
- The program is described as challenging, with a lot to learn.
- The program emphasizes Copilot and AI skills in Business Central.
- The company expects less development work in the future and more need for people who understand business processes.
- A trainee said changing fields, expected to be long and hard, turned out to be quite easy.
- The company says the value from what it pays for the program is paying off, and that it would be much more expensive otherwise.
- A clear path of what to do makes the career transition easier, according to a trainee.
