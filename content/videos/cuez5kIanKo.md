# What's New in Shopify Connector: Activate Sales Channels (2025 release wave 1)

> Shopify connector in Business Central (2025 release wave 1): activate specific sales channels to control which products are sold through which channel. The demo activates channels on the Shopify sales channels page, exports products to Shopify, then checks each created product's channels in Shopify. If the Shopify sales channels page is not configured, products default to the online store.

## Overview

This short demo covers activating specific sales channels in the Shopify connector. Multiple sales channels are set up in the Shopify admin on the Shopify sales channels page, where any available channel can be activated as needed.

The video then exports products from the products list as Shopify products. Afterward it opens the created products in Shopify and checks their sales channels against the settings on the Shopify sales channels page. It also notes that products default to the online store if that page is not configured.

## Key points

- Specific sales channels can be activated in the Shopify connector to control which products are sold through which channel.
- Multiple sales channels are set up in the Shopify admin on the Shopify sales channels page, where any available channel can be activated.
- Products are exported by navigating to products and exporting them as Shopify products.
- If the Shopify sales channels page is not configured, products default to the online store.
- After export, open the created products in Shopify and check their sales channels to confirm they match the sales channels page settings.
