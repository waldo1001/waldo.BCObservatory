## Overview

A short testimonial from Sandlapper, a partner that launched recently and found the BC Talent program soon after. The speaker compares Great Plains with Business Central, saying that even after 20 years with GP, Business Central is easier because it does automatically things that used to need button presses.

The speaker credits the cross skills program and BC Talent with their certification and with the confidence to take part in GP to BC migration conversations. They also say they now know how they will train future team members through the program.

## Key points

- Sandlapper found the BC Talent program not long after launching its business and calls it a game changer.
- The speaker says Business Central does automatically things that GP users had to press buttons to do.
- The speaker says they would not have achieved certification or gained confidence without cross skills and BC Talent.
- The cross skills program involves dedicating time on purpose to become proficient with Business Central.
- The biggest value the speaker names is confidence going into GP to BC migration conversations.
- Sandlapper plans to use the program to train future team members when they join.
