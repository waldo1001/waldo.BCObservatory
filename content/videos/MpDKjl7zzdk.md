# What's New: Suggest Number Series with Copilot  (2024 release wave 2)

> Suggest Number Series with Copilot in Business Central (2024 release wave 2) is demoed for generating number series for a record, a module, or a whole new company, and for preparing existing series for the next year. ## Overview

The video, published 2024-10-08, shows how Copilot in Business Central can generate new number series, modify existing ones, or prepare them for the next year. This reduces the manual work needed during a new implementation, recurring maintenance, or rollout of a new module or company.

The demo starts with a service management module rollout and generates a series for the contract credit memo. It then prepares existing series for the next year and generates a complete set of series for a new company with the user experience set to premium. The presenter warns that AI generated content might be incorrect and that wrong suggestions may need to be deleted by hand.

## Key points

- Copilot can generate number series for a specific record, a module, or an entire company, using a customizable prompt.
- The number series dialog includes prompt guides for common tasks: new company, preparing for next year, and modifying existing series.
- Year numbers, such as 24 for fiscal year 2024, appear in a series only if the prompt asks for them explicitly.
- When a series is suggested, Copilot updates the related setup page automatically, for example service management setup.
- In the year preparation demo, Copilot set a starting date of 1 January 2025. A prompt that is not specific enough can also produce suggestions for unrelated series.
- For a new company, the scope of generated series follows the user experience setting. A premium company gets series for all modules.
- AI generated content might be incorrect. In the demo a sales credit memo series was suggested that was not requested, and it had to be deleted manually.
