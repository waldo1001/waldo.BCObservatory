## Overview

The video covers why larger companies that must report under CSRD or similar regulations want an approval step on sustainability journals, to improve compliance and control. It describes a new workflow template for sustainability journal batch approval that reuses the existing Business Central approval workflow functionality, with no custom development.

It then goes through the setup and shows a live demonstration. Users send or cancel an approval request from the sustainability journal. The approval runs like any other Business Central approval workflow.

## Key points

- Approval is available for a whole sustainability journal batch only, not per individual line.
- A new workflow template for sustainability journal batch approval is provided and uses the standard Business Central approval workflow functionality.
- Setup needs minimal additional configuration; the pages mentioned are Workflows and Approval user setup.
- From the Sustainability journal page, users can send or cancel an approval request for a batch.
- Requests are handled on the Approvals page, the same as other approval workflows in Business Central.
- The motivation given is compliance and control for companies that report under CSRD or similar regulations.
