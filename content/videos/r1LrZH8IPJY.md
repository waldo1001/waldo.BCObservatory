## Overview

JourneyTEAM, a Business Central partner, describes growth of roughly 30 percent or more over the last few years and the difficulty of finding people with the right skill sets. Few people have both BC experience and consultant skills, so the company looked for a new source of talent.

Its answer is ReSkill. The program picks people with industry experience who understand business processes, teaches them BC technology, and builds their consulting skills. It also includes professional skills training on mindset and soft skills, because technologists tend to focus only on technical skills. The speaker reports gains in technical knowledge and in clarity of expectations for how consultants work.
