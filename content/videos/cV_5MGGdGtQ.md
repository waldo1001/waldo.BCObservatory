# Make your KPIs look sharp and dazzling in Business Central (Advanced Spreadsheets and Reporting)

> KPI cards in the Advanced Spreadsheets and Reporting app for Business Central: a demo of a basic card, auto card type, compare value, target bar or donut, trend display and status with warning and critical levels. Useful for building KPI dashboards in spreadsheets.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cV_5MGGdGtQ) · Erik Hougaard · 2026-09-21 · 6:26 · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

Erik Hougaard shows how to format KPIs as cards using the Advanced Spreadsheets and Reporting app for Business Central. He starts with a basic KPI card and the auto card type, which formats the card based on the settings you have defined.

He then adds a compare value to show whether the main value is above or below another value, a target visualization (for example a target of 1,200 with 83% reached), a trend display, and a status indicator with warning and critical levels. The video ends by suggesting the cards as building blocks for a dashboard of KPIs.

## Key points

- A KPI card can show a value, a comparison, a trend, a target and a status.
- Card type auto formats the card depending on the settings you have defined.
- A compare value shows whether the main value is above or below the comparison value.
- The target visualization shows progress as a percentage, in the demo a target of 1,200 with 83% reached, and can be a bar or a donut chart.
- The trend display shows movement over time without a full chart with numbers.
- Status uses warning and critical levels; the two can be combined and the direction can be changed.
- Without specified levels, the status in the demo showed as on track.
