# #BCTalent Stories: Abakion

> Abakion, a Business Central partner, describes how the BC Talent program and its Upskill program helped it recruit and onboard graduates. It reports 14 hires directly from education and a pool of 150 qualified candidates.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P1cS4g-Lj2M) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2026-06-17 · 1:58 · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

A 2-minute #BCTalent Stories video in which Abakion explains why it joined BC Talent. Before joining, the partner had an inconsistent flow of new talent and carried a heavy internal training burden.

Abakion says the Upskill program let it move a large part of onboarding out of its own system. Graduates come out with technical Business Central knowledge and consultant soft skills, and the Microsoft brand makes the roles more attractive to young applicants. The partner also mentions community support across geographies, including the US and Asia. It reports 14 people onboarded directly from education and a choice between 150 qualified candidates.

## Key points

- Problem named by the partner: an inconsistent flow of new talent before joining BC Talent.
- The Upskill program lets a partner move a large part of its internal onboarding and training to the program.
- The program covers technical Business Central skills and the soft skills needed to be a consultant.
- The partner says the Upskill program together with the Microsoft brand makes the roles more attractive to young applicants.
- The BC Talent community supports participants and partners across geographies, including the US and Asia.
- Reported result: 14 people onboarded directly from education, with 150 qualified candidates to choose from.
