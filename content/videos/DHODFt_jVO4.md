# What's New in Manufacturing: Costing (2025 release wave 1)

> Manufacturing costing changes in Business Central 2025 release wave 1: production BOM and routing on stock keeping units, standard cost calculated per SKU, and non-inventory costs included in production cost calculation. Both are shown in a short demo.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DHODFt_jVO4) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2025-04-01 · 3:48 · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Overview

The video covers two costing areas in manufacturing. First, a production bill of material or routing can be defined on a stock keeping unit, which helps when a SKU represents a product variant or a location-specific way of producing an item. Standard cost for items produced for that variant or location is then taken from the SKU instead of the item card, using a new function that calculates production standard cost at SKU level.

Second, non-inventory costs can be included in the production cost calculation. The demo shows new columns for rolled up material non-inventory cost and single level material non-inventory cost on the cost shares page and in production order statistics. Both features need to be activated in manufacturing setup.

## Key points

- A production bill of material or routing can be defined on the stock keeping unit, for variants or location-specific production. It requires activation in manufacturing setup.
- Standard cost for items produced for a specific variant or location is copied from the SKU instead of the item card.
- SKU-based standard cost applies only when the SKU has a routing or bill of material defined.
- A new function calculates production standard cost at the SKU level.
- In the demo, two items share the same bill of material and have identical material cost, but for the SKUs subcontracting costs replace part of the cost, so total cost differs.
- Non-inventory costs can be included in the production cost calculation after enabling this in manufacturing setup.
- The cost shares page and production order statistics show new columns: rolled up material non-inventory cost and single level material non-inventory cost.
