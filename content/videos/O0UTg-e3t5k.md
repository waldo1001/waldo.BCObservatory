## Overview

This short video shows how to jump from a referenced field to its master entity with a keyboard shortcut. In the demo, a sales order is open and an item is selected on a line. Holding Control and Alt and pressing the Down arrow opens the item card for that product.

The video says the shortcut works for any referenced field, so the same approach applies to customers, items and other master entities.
