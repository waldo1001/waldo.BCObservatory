> The BC Talent program in EMEA, started three years ago, has onboarded more than 700 new Business Central professionals with a 91% retention rate, billable in 10 weeks. Senior staff can save up to 80% of their time by not having to train juniors. ## Overview

The video is a two-minute pitch from the Business Central team on the BC Talent program. The program started three years ago to respond to talent challenges and to build the next generation of Business Central professionals. It reports more than 700 new professionals onboarded, a 91% retention rate, and billable readiness in 10 weeks. It also says senior staff can save up to 80% of their time when they do not have to train juniors.

The second part covers AI. It says AI will not replace people, and that building autonomous AI agents needs people who understand industry and business processes and also have AI fluency. It points to upskilling existing teams and recruiting Gen Z talent, and ends with a call to hire people the program has turned into Business Central experts.

- The message: AI won't replace people, but people with AI skills will.
