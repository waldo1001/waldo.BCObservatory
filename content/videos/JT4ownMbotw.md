# What's New: Manufacturing Analytics (2025 release wave 2)

> Manufacturing analytics in Business Central 2025 release wave 2 (version 27.0): the PowerBI app for manufacturing now shows all capacity measures in one unit of measure chosen in manufacturing setup. Manufacturing reports get Excel and Word layouts plus in-client help and teaching tips linked to Microsoft Learn. Six updated and two new reports were postponed from 26.2 and now ship in 27.0 along with three more, and the landing page at aka.ms/bc-manufacturing-analytics collects related resources.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JT4ownMbotw) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2025-10-01 · 9:35 · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

The video covers Microsoft's investment in manufacturing analytics for Business Central. It explains the modernization themes, the changes to the PowerBI app, and how built-in manufacturing reports are being modernized with Excel and Word layouts.

The PowerBI app now shows every capacity-related measure in a universal unit of measure selected in manufacturing setup. Six updated and two new reports ship in version 27.0, after a delay from 26.2. The video also covers teaching tips, the in-client help pane with Microsoft Learn links, and the landing page at aka.ms/bc-manufacturing-analytics.

## Key points

- The PowerBI app for manufacturing shows all capacity measures in a universal unit of measure chosen in Business Central manufacturing setup.
- Install or update the PowerBI app separately from the built-in reports.
- Manufacturing and analytics reports offer Excel layouts alongside legacy layouts, so end users can filter, sort, and change layouts without customization. Word layouts are also available.
- Six updated and two new manufacturing reports ship in version 27.0, including Where Used (Top Level), Quantity Explosion of Bill of Material, and Production Order List. They were originally planned for 26.2.
- Three more reports beyond the originally planned eight are mentioned.
- Report request pages show teaching tips, and an in-client help pane links to Microsoft Learn articles for each report.
- The landing page aka.ms/bc-manufacturing-analytics covers personas, use cases, and resources.
