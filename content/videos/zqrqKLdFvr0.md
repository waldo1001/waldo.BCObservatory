# Introduction to Business Central (2025)

> Introduction to navigating Business Central (2025): role-based home pages, activity KPIs, quick access to lists, performance charts, the navigation bar, page and report search, and guided tours for new users. A 2-minute orientation video.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zqrqKLdFvr0) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2025-05-22 · 1:44 · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

This short video shows how to get around Business Central and where to find information. It starts with the role-based home page, which holds tasks and information for roles such as business manager, accountant, production planner and sales manager.

It then walks through the parts of the home page: an activities section with operational KPIs, quick access to lists such as customers, vendors and items, actions for creating documents and processing transactions, and charts of performance KPIs. It ends with the navigation bar for other departments' features, search for pages and reports by name, and guided tours for getting started.

## Key points

- Home pages are role-based, with similar pages for business managers, accountants, production planners and sales managers.
- The activities section gives an immediate overview of operational KPIs.
- Common lists such as customers, vendors and items are reachable quickly from the home page.
- Actions on the home page let users create new documents, process transactions and view detailed reports.
- A series of performance charts on the home page can be flipped through.
- The navigation bar gives access to features and lists that belong to other departments.
- If you know the page or report name, you can search for it instead of browsing; guided tours, videos and articles help new users get started.
