# What's New: APIs in E-Documents (2025 release wave 2)

> E-Documents APIs in Business Central (2025 release wave 2): five APIs and two business events for code-free integration, for example through Power Automate. Demos cover creating an e-document from SharePoint and a business event fired when the e-document service status changes to exported.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S5Xw-b8YF-c) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2025-10-01 · 11:33 · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Overview

The video explains why APIs were added to e-documents: to allow flexible, code-free integration. The delivery is five APIs and two business events. They can trigger processes from e-documents and create e-documents from external sources such as SharePoint, OneDrive or other XML sources.

Two demos follow. In the first, a flow creates an e-document in Business Central from a file in SharePoint. In the second, the business event "e document service status changed" starts a flow when the status becomes exported. The flow then retrieves the e-document through the APIs, including OData filtering on e-document and service data. The presenter says the APIs are not meant to be the first channel of communication, but a parallel or additional one.

## Key points

- Delivered: five e-document APIs and two business events, aimed at flexible, code-free integration such as Power Automate.
- The APIs are not intended as the first channel of communication. Use them as a parallel or additional channel.
- When creating an e-document through the API, the file contents must be base 64 encoded and the e-document service must already exist.
- The business event 'e document service status changed' was demoed. In the demo it fires only when the status is exported.
- The flows in Power Automate or similar tools must be set up manually to react to the event, for example to forward the e-document by email or to a custom service.
- OData filter queries can be used on e-document and e-document service data, for example by entry number and service code. This needs OData syntax knowledge.
- The video says the APIs also allow new custom agents built with Copilot Studio, which needs a Copilot Studio license. This was not demonstrated.
