# Reskill: Program explainer

> Reskill program explainer: a Microsoft program that recruits people with industry experience and retrains them as Business Central professionals, consultants or developers, to help partners with a talent gap. It uses three pillars: hiring, training and 6 months of coaching.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9c-j29nAFM) · Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (YouTube) · 2023-11-09 · 4:36 · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

The video describes a gap in experienced ERP consultants, especially in the Dynamics field, and presents the Reskilling Program as a way for partners to address it. Microsoft helps recruit people with industry experience, trains them, and then coaches them to follow their success.

Partners and participants share their views on hiring and career development. The video closes with five reasons to join and a call to action, including onboarding flexibility through a new hire community and a low-risk, cost-effective model.

## Key points

- The program targets the shortage of experienced ERP consultants, especially in the Dynamics field.
- It has three pillars: hire people with industry experience, train them, and coach them for 6 months.
- The program is aimed at partners experiencing talent gaps.
- The Microsoft Talent Program provides training and onboarding so new hires are up and running quickly.
- Training covers professional skills learning as well as product learning.
- A new hire community lets partners onboard people at any moment.
- The video describes the model as low risk and cost effective.
