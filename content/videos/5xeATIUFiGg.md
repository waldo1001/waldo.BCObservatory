# 20261012 - Unlocking Subscription Billing in Dynamics 365 Business Central

> Subscription Billing in Business Central: setup, packages, item types, customer and vendor contracts, recurring billing proposals, vendor deferrals, usage-based billing with data import, and reports with the Power BI app. A 56-minute Areopa webinar with demos of the full workflow.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5xeATIUFiGg) · Areopa webinars · 2026-10-19 · 55:57 · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

The webinar explains how Subscription Billing is used to create recurring invoices for customers or vendors on a monthly, quarterly or yearly basis. It starts with the history (nothing usable before October 2024, general availability of the first version in October) and then covers setup: contract setup, number series, posting groups, deferral policy, contract types, packages and package templates.

The demos follow a sales-to-billing flow: items assigned to packages, subscription lines created on shipment, contracts built from subscription lines, direct subscription creation without a sales order, renewal quotes, recurring billing proposals, and vendor deferrals. It ends with usage-based billing from imported data, Excel reports and the Power BI app, which was shown with sample data.

## Key points

- Subscription Billing is generally available and part of standard Business Central under the normal license, not an add-on. It needs manual setup and is not created automatically with demo data; Contoso demo data must be generated explicitly.
- In Subscription Contract Setup you choose whether a subscription starts on the shipment date or the posting date, and whether deferrals are always created, never created, or decided per contract.
- Items have four subscription types: no subscription, sales with subscription, subscription item, and invoicing item. Packages assigned to an item can be standard or selectable.
- Subscription lines are created when the sales order is shipped, not when the invoice is posted. Subscriptions can also be created directly without a sales order.
- Contracts group subscription lines and are not directly connected to projects; use dimensions if project tracking is needed. Recurring billing creates proposals that can be reviewed, and invoices for the same customer can be combined.
- Vendor deferrals release gives no confirmation or error message, so check the general ledger entries manually to confirm it worked.
- Usage-based billing uses data exchange definitions to import data. Separate definitions are needed per region or format, and vendor invoices cannot be posted automatically.
