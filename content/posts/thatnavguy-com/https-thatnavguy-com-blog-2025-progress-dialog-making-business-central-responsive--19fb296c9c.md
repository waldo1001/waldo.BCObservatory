## Key points

- Progress Dialog prevents user frustration and duplicate entries by showing that a process is running and indicating its progress
- Use Progress Dialog for tasks taking more than a few seconds, processing many records, or involving multiple steps
- Effective progress messages are specific, show counts or percentages, update at meaningful milestones, and include a success message at the end
- Poor Progress Dialog implementation with vague messages, too many updates, or overuse can become an obstacle rather than a help
