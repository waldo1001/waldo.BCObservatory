> Emojis can be displayed in Business Central to improve user experience by providing quick visual cues for statuses, warnings, and categories. They help users scan data faster and identify issues at a glance, but should be used sparingly to avoid negatively impacting the interface.

## Key points

- Emojis can represent statuses, warnings, or categories visually in Business Central
- They enable quick recognition without requiring users to read text
- Emojis make the UI more engaging and user-friendly
- Overuse of emojis can degrade user experience, so they should be applied strategically
