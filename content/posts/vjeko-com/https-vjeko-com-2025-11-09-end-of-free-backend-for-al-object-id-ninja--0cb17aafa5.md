# End of free backend for AL Object ID Ninja

[Read the post](https://vjeko.com/2025/11/09/end-of-free-backend-for-al-object-id-ninja/) · Vjeko.com (Vjekoslav Babić, MVP) · 2025-11-09 · 704 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> AL Object ID Ninja, a free tool for managing object IDs in Business Central development, transitions from a free community service to a premium subscription model on January 1, 2026, while offering self-hosting as a free alternative.

## Key points

- The free backend for AL Object ID Ninja will end on December 31, 2025, after four years of free service
- Developers can choose between subscribing to Premium Ninja (with new features, support, and management portal) or self-hosting on their own Azure subscription
- Premium pricing will be based on fair usage, likely per app, with a free tier for minimal consumption
- Self-hosted users will maintain full control but must handle updates, redeployment, and maintenance themselves
- The tool has prevented hundreds of thousands of conflicts across thousands of development teams
