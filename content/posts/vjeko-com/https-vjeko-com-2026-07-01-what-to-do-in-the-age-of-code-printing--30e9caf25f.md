> The post argues that organizations should build centralized code-printing machines powered by AI rather than letting each developer create their own. Developers become machine operators focused on design and experimentation, enabling companies to test many ideas at the historical cost of testing one.

## Key points

- Standardized AI scaffolding controlled by top engineers produces better outcomes than letting each developer build their own approach
- Design responsibility remains with humans while machine operators handle construction using tight scaffolding and constraints
- AI-driven development enables rapid experimentation at scale, allowing teams to explore many ideas instead of committing to a single path
- The approach combines constraint-based automation with human creativity for discovery and innovation
