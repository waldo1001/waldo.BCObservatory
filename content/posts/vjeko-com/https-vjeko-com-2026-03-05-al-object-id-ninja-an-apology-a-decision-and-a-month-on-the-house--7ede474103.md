> The author of AL Object ID Ninja, which already went commercial after four self-funded years, is ending private self-hosted backend support and closing the source code. He says this is because an established BC company bundled its own Ninja-compatible backend into its paid offering. Private backend users get a one-month grace period on the commercial platform, or they can turn off auto-update and keep their current extension version.

## Key points

- AL Object ID Ninja was free for four years but became unsustainable due to growing infrastructure costs
- A third-party company bundled Ninja-compatible backend access with their own paid offering, routing around the commercial service
- Private backend source code is no longer open and self-hosting is no longer officially supported
- Users with private backends get one month free access to the commercial platform to migrate, or can stay on their current extension version
- The decision prioritizes sustainable development funding while unfortunately affecting users with legitimate operational reasons for self-hosting
