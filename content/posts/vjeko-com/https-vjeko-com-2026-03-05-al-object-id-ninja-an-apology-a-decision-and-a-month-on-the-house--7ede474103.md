# AL Object ID Ninja: An Apology, a Decision, and a Month on the House

[Read the post](https://vjeko.com/2026/03/05/al-object-id-ninja-an-apology-a-decision-and-a-month-on-the-house/) · Vjeko.com (Vjekoslav Babić, MVP) · 2026-03-05 · 1290 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> AL Object ID Ninja, a free tool for managing object IDs in Business Central, transitions to a commercial model after four years of self-funded operation. The creator closes the self-hosting option due to a competitor bundling private backend access to undercut the commercial service, and offers a one-month grace period for affected users.

## Key points

- AL Object ID Ninja was free for four years but became unsustainable due to growing infrastructure costs
- A third-party company bundled Ninja-compatible backend access with their own paid offering, routing around the commercial service
- Private backend source code is no longer open and self-hosting is no longer officially supported
- Users with private backends get one month free access to the commercial platform to migrate, or can stay on their current extension version
- The decision prioritizes sustainable development funding while unfortunately affecting users with legitimate operational reasons for self-hosting
