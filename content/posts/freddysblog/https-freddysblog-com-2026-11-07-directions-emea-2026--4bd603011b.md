> Reflections on organizing Directions EMEA 2026, covering session selection from 800+ submissions, conference logistics, and the Developer Zone roundtables. The post shares insights on AI in development, open-source adoption, DevOps trends, and the growing Business Central community.

## Key points

- Session selection from 800+ submissions balanced audiences, speakers and diversity; the average session score rose to 4.51 from 4.49 the year before
- AI agents are already giving partners efficiency and quality gains, but developers stay responsible for AI-written AL and need ways to verify it
- AL-Go for GitHub is gaining popularity as a partner DevOps choice, and Microsoft is committed to supporting it
- Microsoft has open-sourced the Base Application, System Application and localizations but not the platform; open-source projects need maintenance, support and sponsorship
- A roundtable concluded that the most valuable future Business Central professional may be a solution architect with development skills and deep business process knowledge
