# Rec.Truncate in AL

[Read the post](https://duiliotacconi.com/2026/02/10/rec-truncate-in-al/) · Duilio Tacconi (Duilio Tacconi, MVP) · 2026-02-10 · 1186 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Rec.Truncate is a new AL statement that rapidly deletes all records from a table by unlinking database pages instead of removing rows one by one. It works best when deleting 50-60% or more of a table's content without filters, but falls back to slower row-by-row deletion if constraints are not met, such as when filters are applied, security filters exist, or the table has media fields or marked records.

## Key points

- Rec.Truncate is based on SQL Server TRUNCATE TABLE and can delete large tables in milliseconds when all conditions are met
- It does not work efficiently with filters; truncate without filters deletes 50K records far faster than DeleteAll, but with filters it becomes dramatically slower
- The statement has multiple constraints: it cannot be used on system/virtual tables, fails if security filters are active, subscribers to OnBeforeDelete/OnAfterDelete exist, or Media fields are present
- Truncate becomes advantageous when deleting at least 50-60% of table content; deleting less than that negates its benefits due to the overhead of saving and restoring records to keep
- Best use cases are staging tables and log tables that are either completely emptied for refilling or kept entirely, not partial deletions
