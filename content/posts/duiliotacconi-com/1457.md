# Dynamics 365 Business Central 2025 Wave 1 (26.x): real values to upgrade

[Read the post](https://duiliotacconi.com/2025/07/03/dynamics-365-business-central-2025-wave-1-26-x-real-values-to-upgrade/) · Duilio Tacconi (Duilio Tacconi, MVP) · 2025-07-03 · 1319 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Dynamics 365 Business Central 26.x introduces a less-locking inventory posting engine and optimizes FlowField calculations on pages. A production environment with 2,000+ background sessions per hour saw nearly all Item Ledger Entry deadlocks disappear after upgrading and enabling these features, though subsequent third-party application deadlocks required targeted optimization.

## Key points

- The new inventory posting engine using Number Sequence objects reduces locking on Item Ledger Entry by changing transaction order, though it initially revealed deadlocks in third-party applications
- Skipping FlowField calculations for hidden page columns significantly reduces query duration, sometimes by over 50 percent on list pages
- When upgrading, enable both features carefully: test concurrency thoroughly with Business Central Performance Toolkit and verify no visible columns depend on hidden FlowFields
- Optimization strategies include using appropriate isolation levels instead of locktable, applying SIFT Bucket No. distributed by session ID, and preferring Number Sequence over Autoincrement for bulk insert performance

## Quotes

- "Item Ledger Entry deadlocks and lock timeouts… puff. Gone with the wind." (Demonstrates the tangible impact of the new inventory posting engine on reducing contention in complex manufacturing environments)
- "The difference, sometimes, is HUGE. (overall when you have invisible FlowFields that does not have a supporting index)" (Shows why skipping FlowField calculations for hidden columns can dramatically improve page performance)
- "When you upgrade and enable the new inventory posting engine, you must test the concurrency A LOT." (Emphasizes that the feature requires comprehensive testing to surface new deadlock patterns that may emerge)
