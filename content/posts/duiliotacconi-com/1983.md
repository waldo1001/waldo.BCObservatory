- Index management moved from development environment to client UI in Table Information page
- Granular control available at company level or across all companies simultaneously
- Feature enables faster writes by removing unused indexes and faster reads by activating needed indexes
- Ranks third in performance features since 2023 Wave 2, considered ground-breaking for online versions
