# Business Central Case Study: How World Emblem Automated High-Volume Manufacturing Processes

[Read the post](https://www.olofsimren.com/business-central-case-study-how-world-emblem-automated-high-volume-manufacturing-processes/) · Olof Simren (Olof Simren) · 2026-10-09 · 1524 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> World Emblem, a high-volume embroidered garment manufacturer, used Business Central automation and configuration-driven customizations to scale operations without proportional headcount growth. The case study shows how moving tribal knowledge into system rules, migrating legacy logic from external systems, and building maintainable solutions enabled them to quadruple revenue since 2018.

## Key points

- Automated warehouse routing and order decisions that previously required manual tribal knowledge from one or two employees
- Built configuration tables for business rules instead of hard-coded customizations, enabling business users to adapt rules without developer involvement
- Migrated freight calculation logic from external systems into Business Central for better maintainability and traceability
- Used technology and automation as a growth strategy to handle increased volume without hiring proportionally
- Focused on partner relationships that prioritize client needs over consulting hours and transfer knowledge for internal capability

## Quotes

- "One thing I really like about Dynamics NAV and now Business Central is that it is highly adaptable." (Demonstrates how Business Central's flexibility enables manufacturing companies to evolve their processes as the business grows and changes)
- "Configuration tables beat hard-coded customizations every time, especially for business rules that change frequently." (Captures the key principle of building sustainable, maintainable solutions that empower business users rather than create dependency on developers)
