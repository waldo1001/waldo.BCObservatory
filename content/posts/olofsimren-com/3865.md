# Business Central Case Study: How World Emblem Automated High-Volume Manufacturing Processes

[Read the post](https://www.olofsimren.com/business-central-case-study-how-world-emblem-automated-high-volume-manufacturing-processes/) · Olof Simren (Olof Simren) · 2026-10-09 · 1524 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> World Emblem, a high-volume manufacturing company, demonstrates how strategic automation and configuration-based customizations in Business Central enabled them to quadruple revenue without proportionally increasing headcount. By converting tribal knowledge into system-based rules and migrating legacy logic into Business Central, they improved scalability, reduced bottlenecks, and created a more maintainable operation.

## Key points

- Converting manual decision-making into configuration tables creates business independence and reduces the need for custom development cycles
- Migrating business logic from external systems into Business Central improves maintainability and reduces support overhead
- Strategic automation of high-volume processes (4,000 - 5,000 invoices daily) allows employees to focus on value-added work rather than routine tasks
- Partnering with a vendor who understands industry-specific requirements and prioritizes client self-sufficiency over dependency yields better long-term results

- Features: automated warehouse routing, turnaround time configuration, freight calculation automation, process automation, legacy system integration
