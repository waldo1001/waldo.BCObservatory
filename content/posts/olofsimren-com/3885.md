# Mobile Solutions for Business Central: Why the Native App Is All You Need

[Read the post](https://www.olofsimren.com/mobile-solutions-for-business-central-why-the-native-app-is-all-you-need/) · Olof Simren (Olof Simren) · 2026-10-19 · 2858 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> The native Business Central mobile app since version 23 offers barcode scanning, tablet support, and full AL extensibility, making expensive third-party mobile solutions unnecessary for most companies. This post explains why the fourth-generation native app is superior to legacy ADCS systems and dedicated third-party apps, covering practical implementation methods and real-world examples.

## Key points

- The native BC mobile app includes barcode scanning (camera or dedicated scanners like Zebra), tablet optimization, and full AL extensibility since version 23
- Implementation can be as simple as setting ExtendedDatatype = Barcode on a field, or more complex using AL code for programmatic scanning and custom workflows
- The native app avoids costs and maintenance burdens of third-party solutions - no middleware, no separate licensing, no vendor dependency, and upgrades work seamlessly
- Mobile customizations use the same AL development process as desktop Business Central, deployed through Visual Studio Code with full IntelliSense and version control support

## Quotes
