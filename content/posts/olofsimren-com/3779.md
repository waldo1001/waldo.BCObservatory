# Approval Workflows in Planning Worksheet

[Read the post](https://www.olofsimren.com/approval-workflows-in-planning-worksheet/) · Olof Simren (Olof Simren) · 2026-07-13 · 1933 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central version 28 adds approval workflow support to planning, requisition, and subcontracting worksheets at the batch level, allowing managers to review and approve suggested actions before they become real purchase orders or production orders. This feature prevents costly mistakes when planners carry out planning suggestions and addresses compliance requirements for segregation of duties.

## Key points

- Once a requisition, planning or subcontracting worksheet batch is sent for approval, its lines cannot be inserted, changed or deleted, and the batch stays locked until the request completes or is canceled
- Approvers handle the whole batch at once and can approve, reject or delegate it; a rejected batch returns to the planner with comments for rework
- Approval is batch level only, so splitting MRP runs into several batches (by location, product type or planner) keeps approvals manageable
- Setup uses the Requisition Worksheet batch approval workflow template on the Workflows page, with status tracked on Approval Entries and Power Automate support available

## Quotes

- "Approval workflows have been extended to the requisition and planning worksheets." (This is the core capability addition that closes the gap for internal controls on planning decisions that were previously impossible to enforce.)
- "You can't insert, modify, or delete requisition or planning lines while an approval request is active." (This mechanism ensures the approver reviews exactly what was submitted and prevents scope creep during the approval process.)
