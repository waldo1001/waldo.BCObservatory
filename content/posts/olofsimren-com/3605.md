# Copilot in Planning Worksheet

[Read the post](https://www.olofsimren.com/copilot-in-planning-worksheet/) · Olof Simren (Olof Simren) · 2025-10-16 · 548 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> A Copilot extension for Planning Worksheet in Business Central helps planners understand material planning (MRP) suggestions by providing AI-generated explanations. The extension analyzes multiple suggestions for the same item, location, and variant together, presenting prompts that guide users to ask detailed questions about order recommendations, quantity changes, reschedules, and cancellations.

## Key points

- Copilot analyzes all MRP suggestions for the same item, location, and variant as a group rather than individually
- Pre-filled prompts guide users to ask questions about specific suggestion types like new orders, quantity changes, reschedules, and cancellations
- The extension reduces the need for planners to contact support for explanation of suggestions
- Also available for Requisition Worksheet and is a free extension in AppSource
