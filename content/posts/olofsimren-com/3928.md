# Business Central Case Study: Engineer-to-Order Manufacturing at Zook

[Read the post](https://www.olofsimren.com/business-central-case-study-engineer-to-order-manufacturing-at-zook/) · Olof Simren (Olof Simren) · 2026-10-26 · 1154 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> ZOOK, a manufacturer of rupture disks and pressure-safety devices, demonstrates how Business Central and Dynamics NAV support complex engineer-to-order manufacturing with custom extensions. The case study shows how partner expertise in manufacturing combined with deep customization achieved significant operational efficiencies.

## Key points

- Custom product configurator determines production methods, inspection rules, and documentation requirements beyond simple component selection for engineer-to-order products.
- Automation reduced certificate generation from 30 minutes to 2 minutes while enabling production of 30+ different document types sold across 170+ countries.
- Shipping integration for Canadian UPS operations was reduced from 20 minutes per shipment to 2 seconds through custom interface development; ZOOK handles 45-80 daily Canadian shipments.
- ZOOK's approximately 30% customized NAV environment is being evaluated for migration to Business Central with careful assessment of which customizations are still needed versus standard capabilities.

## Context

- Features: custom product configurator, certificate generation automation, document template system, data consolidation tools, shipping integration, UPS WorldShip interface
