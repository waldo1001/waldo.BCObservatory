# Business Central Case Study: Engineer-to-Order Manufacturing at Zook

[Read the post](https://www.olofsimren.com/business-central-case-study-engineer-to-order-manufacturing-at-zook/) · Olof Simren (Olof Simren) · 2026-10-26 · 1154 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Case study of ZOOK, a maker of rupture disks built to order. With partner Naviona, ZOOK heavily customized Dynamics NAV with a custom configurator, certificate automation, a tool for merging data across environments and a Canadian UPS WorldShip interface. ZOOK is now moving to Business Central and reviewing which customizations to keep or replace with standard features.

## Key points

- An off-the-shelf ISV configurator was too limited, so a custom one was built. It also drives production, inspection and the information each step needs.
- Certificate creation dropped from about 30 minutes to about 2 minutes. ZOOK can now build its own documents and has around 30 types for customers in more than 170 countries.
- A table copy and merge tool cut consolidation of three NAV environments from an estimated 70 to 95 hours to about two hours.
- A NAV to UPS WorldShip Canada interface cut shipment handling from 12 to 20 minutes to about two seconds, for 45 to 80 shipments a day.
- The NAV system is about 30 percent customized. In the migration, each customization is reviewed against what Business Central now offers as standard.

## Context

- Features: custom product configurator, certificate generation automation, document template system, data consolidation tools, shipping integration, UPS WorldShip interface
