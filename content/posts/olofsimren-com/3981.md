# Shop Floor and Warehouse Mobile for Business Central

[Read the post](https://www.olofsimren.com/shop-floor-and-warehouse-mobile-for-business-central/) · Olof Simren (Olof Simren) · 2026-11-05 · 1647 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Shop Floor and Warehouse Mobile is an AL extension for Business Central that enables real-time data capture directly on shop floors and in warehouses through mobile devices with barcode scanning, task-focused pages, and label printing integration. It replaces paper-based workflows and separate systems by consolidating operations, warehouse processing, and time tracking within Business Central itself.

## Key points

- Real-time barcode scanning and posting eliminate paper travelers, keyboard re-entry delays, and reconciliation overhead
- Task-focused mobile pages let operators start/stop operations, post output/consumption, capture downtime, and handle warehouse receipts, picks, counts, and transfers directly from tablets or Android scanners
- Label printing integrates with BarTender Cloud for rule-based design and printing to physical label printers from any device without separate infrastructure
- Smart barcode system on job cards and labels allows single-scan task execution, preventing errors and reducing manual data entry
- Solution is built entirely as an AL extension inside Business Central, extending existing production orders and warehouse documents rather than requiring external WMS or MES systems
