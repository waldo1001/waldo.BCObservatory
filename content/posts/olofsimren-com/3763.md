# ERP JumpStart: A Better Way to Get Started with Business Central

> ERP JumpStart is a guided self-service implementation product for smaller companies transitioning to Business Central. It combines pre-configuration, step-by-step tasks grouped in work packages, and integrated learning content to enable companies to set up and learn the system at their own pace without complex traditional ERP methodology.

[Read the post](https://www.olofsimren.com/erp-jumpstart-a-better-way-to-get-started-with-business-central/) · Olof Simren (Olof Simren) · 2026-06-26 · 900 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Designed for smaller companies that find traditional discovery and analysis phases impractical, allowing them to get started immediately
- Uses Digital Consultant to organize implementation tasks into work packages with dependencies, videos, documentation, and direct links to Business Central configuration pages
- Tasks can be assigned to multiple key users across departments with progress tracking and effort estimates
- Provides foundational configuration including countries, chart of accounts, posting groups, and sample master data to avoid starting from scratch
- Enables scaling and extending solutions as companies grow without limiting future capability expansion
