# Dynamics 365 Business Central: important changes in PTE management.

> The Business Central Admin Center is gaining new PTE management capabilities, allowing administrators to upload, schedule installation, and uninstall per-tenant extensions directly. Starting in version 30 (2027 wave 1), the Extension Management page will no longer support PTE installation and will be limited to viewing only.

[Read the post](https://demiliani.com/2026/08/19/dynamics-365-business-central-important-changes-in-pte-management/) · Stefano Demiliani (Stefano Demiliani, MVP) · 2026-08-19 · 576 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Administrators can now upload PTEs and schedule their installation via the Admin Center's Manage Apps page, with flexible deployment timing options
- PTE installations can be scheduled immediately, during the next update window, or during minor/major updates, with database sync mode options available
- Only users with Dynamics 365 Business Central Administrator or Dynamics 365 Administrator roles can access the Admin Center for PTE management
- Version 30 (2027 wave 1) will remove PTE installation from the Extension Management page, leaving it for viewing only
- A new Admin Center API version 2.29 enables programmatic PTE installation and scheduling
