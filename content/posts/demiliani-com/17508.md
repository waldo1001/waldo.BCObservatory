- AI agents (native AL, Copilot Studio, Azure Logic Apps, Foundry Agents) running in Business Central need monitoring and governance across different platforms
- Application Insights telemetry enables observability, cost tracking, and forecasting for agent workloads with dashboards for throughput, latency, and quality
