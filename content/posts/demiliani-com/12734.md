# Dynamics 365 Business Central: announcing Italy North availability.

[Read the post](https://demiliani.com/2025/11/05/dynamics-365-business-central-announcing-italy-north-availability/) · Stefano Demiliani (Stefano Demiliani, MVP) · 2025-11-05 · 279 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Starting with version 27.3, Dynamics 365 Business Central tenants for Italian customers will be deployed to the Italy North datacenter in Milan, with existing environments eligible for migration after unlinking from Power Platform. This reduces network latency from 42-55ms to 26ms for Italian users.

## Key points

- Italy North datacenter deployment available from Business Central version 27.3
- New IT customers automatically deployed to Italy North; existing customers can migrate after unlinking from Power Platform
- Network latency reduces to 26ms from Italy North versus 42ms (West Europe) or 55ms (North Europe)
- Improves performance for Italian customers and partners by moving data closer to end users
