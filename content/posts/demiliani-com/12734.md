# Dynamics 365 Business Central: announcing Italy North availability.

[Read the post](https://demiliani.com/2025/11/05/dynamics-365-business-central-announcing-italy-north-availability/) · Stefano Demiliani (Stefano Demiliani, MVP) · 2025-11-05 · 279 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> From version 27.3, Business Central tenants for Italian customers are deployed to the new Italy North datacenter in Milan, and existing Italian environments will also be moved there. Environments previously linked to a Power Platform environment must be unlinked first. Measured latency from Italy drops to about 26 ms, versus 42 ms for West Europe and 55 ms for North Europe.

## Key points

- Italy North (Milan) hosting for Italian Business Central tenants starts with version 27.3
- Existing Italian environments will be moved too, but any Power Platform link must be removed before the move
- Average latency from Italy is about 26 ms to Italy North, versus 42 ms to West Europe and 55 ms to North Europe
- Until now, tenants were hosted only in West Europe or North Europe

## Quotes

- "Starting from Dynamics 365 Business Central version 27.3, all IT customers will start be deployed on Italy North datacenter, located in Milan." (Clearly states when the new regional availability begins and its location)
- "Bringing your data closer to you is a key benefit of the cloud platform (and the power of Azure)." (Explains the strategic value of regional datacenters for cloud performance)
