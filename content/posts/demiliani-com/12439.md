# Dynamics 365 Business Central: controlling the Summary system part.

[Read the post](https://demiliani.com/2025/09/03/dynamics-365-business-central-controlling-the-summary-system-part/) · Stefano Demiliani (Stefano Demiliani, MVP) · 2025-09-03 · 267 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Business Central version 27 introduces a controllable Summary system part for Copilot that developers can show, hide, or configure on card and document pages. The Summary FactBox uses AI to generate role-tailored record summaries and identify urgent information across the current page and related data.

## Key points

- Version 26 added the Summary FactBox but it couldn't be controlled; version 27 makes it configurable
- The Summary part uses Copilot to generate role-tailored summaries and identify urgent and important information
- Developers can control the Summary part using the DefaultSummaryPart identifier on Card, Document, and ListPlus pages
- Only one Summary system part is allowed per page, and it cannot be added to List pages

## Quotes

- "You can only have one Summary system part per page." (Important constraint developers must know when designing pages with the Summary part)
