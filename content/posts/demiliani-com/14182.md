# New EU rules for software: what you need to know.

> The EU Product Liability Directive (2024/2853) takes effect December 9, 2026, legally treating software and AI systems as products under European law with strict liability for defects. Software developers and companies must demonstrate professional testing, security update management, vulnerability tracking, technical documentation, and decision records to comply.

[Read the post](https://demiliani.com/2026/08/26/new-eu-rules-for-software-what-you-need-to-know/) · Stefano Demiliani (Stefano Demiliani, MVP) · 2026-08-26 · 1850 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- All software types (installed, SaaS, cloud, embedded, AI) fall under strict liability starting December 9, 2026
- Defective software includes data loss, security breaches, missing patches, and unresolved known vulnerabilities
- Developers are responsible for damage regardless of intent if they cannot prove professional development and maintenance practices
- Acceptable damage claims cover physical harm, financial loss, data corruption, and documented psychological harm
- Free and open-source software developed by unpaid volunteers is exempt unless offered commercially or with paid support
