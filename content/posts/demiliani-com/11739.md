> The post explains how the new Sales Order Agent in Business Central 2025 Wave 1 automates email-based sales quote processing and details the billing models for agentic AI features. It covers Copilot Studio message-based pricing (pay-as-you-go at $0.01 per message or prepaid message packs at $200/month for 25,000 messages), environment linking requirements, and cost scenarios ranging from $77 to $803 monthly based on agent complexity and usage.

## Key points

- Sales Order Agent automatically processes customer email requests, clarifies details through multi-turn conversations, and generates formatted PDF quotes with pricing and availability information
- Business Central must be linked to a Power Platform environment to enable agent billing and inherit Copilot Studio settings
- Two billing models available: prepaid capacity (message packs at $200/month for 25,000 messages) or pay-as-you-go ($0.01 per message), both can run simultaneously with prepaid consumed first
- Agent message consumption varies by task complexity, with costs depending on generative answers, autonomous actions, tenant graph grounding, and agent flow actions
- ROI justification is essential, as agents can reduce manual workload and improve customer satisfaction but require cost-benefit analysis for sustainability
