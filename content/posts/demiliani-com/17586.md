# Dynamics 365 Business Central: service update to .NET 10 and upgrade window.

[Read the post](https://demiliani.com/2026/11/04/dynamics-365-business-central-service-update-to-net-10-and-upgrade-window/) · Stefano Demiliani (Stefano Demiliani, MVP) · 2026-11-04 · 239 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Microsoft is transitioning Business Central online and on-premises environments from .NET 8 to .NET 10 before November 10, 2026. The migration happens at cluster level and may cause brief service interruptions outside scheduled maintenance windows, though this is normal and improves security.

## Key points

- Microsoft is moving supported Business Central releases, online and on-premises, from .NET 8 to .NET 10 before November 10, 2026
- For online environments Microsoft handles the move itself, applying it as an urgent platform hotfix at the cluster (VM) level rather than per environment
- Because a cluster hosts several environments, the update can land outside an environment's scheduled update window and cause a short outage plus a Message Center notice
- A brief interruption during this process is expected and not a malfunction
- Microsoft recommends setting the maintenance window to a time that limits impact on the organization

## Quotes

- ".NET 10 update is considered an urgent platform hotfix and this is applied not at the environment level, but at the cluster level (VMs)." (Explains why updates can occur outside maintenance windows and affect multiple environments simultaneously)

## Heads up

- warning: "These required updates can occur outside your environment's regularly scheduled update window."
