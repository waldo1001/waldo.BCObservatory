# Dynamics 365 Business Central: service update to .NET 10 and upgrade window.

[Read the post](https://demiliani.com/2026/11/04/dynamics-365-business-central-service-update-to-net-10-and-upgrade-window/) · Stefano Demiliani (Stefano Demiliani, MVP) · 2026-11-04 · 239 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Microsoft is transitioning Business Central online and on-premises environments from .NET 8 to .NET 10 before November 10, 2026. The migration happens at cluster level and may cause brief service interruptions outside scheduled maintenance windows, though this is normal and improves security.

## Key points

- .NET 10 migration is happening across all Business Central releases during a specific timeframe
- For online environments, Microsoft manages the transition automatically at the cluster level, not per environment
- Updates may occur outside your configured maintenance window because they apply to VMs serving multiple environments
- Brief service interruptions can occur during this process, but indicate normal platform enhancement rather than malfunction
- Organizations should verify their maintenance window configuration is set to an optimal time

## Quotes

- ".NET 10 update is considered an urgent platform hotfix and this is applied not at the environment level, but at the cluster level (VMs)." (Explains why updates can occur outside maintenance windows and affect multiple environments simultaneously)
