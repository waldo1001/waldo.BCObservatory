# Dynamics 365 Business Central: automatic PTE unpublishing after update.

[Read the post](https://demiliani.com/2025/07/10/dynamics-365-business-central-automatic-pte-unpublishing-after-update/) · Stefano Demiliani (Stefano Demiliani, MVP) · 2025-07-10 · 130 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Starting with SaaS environments, Business Central now automatically unpublishes older versions of private extensions (PTEs) after a new version is installed, keeping only the immediately preceding version to simplify tenant management and prevent clutter.

## Key points

- Old PTE versions are now automatically unpublished in SaaS when a new version is installed
- The version immediately before the current installed version is retained for safety
- This prevents accumulation of obsolete extension versions in tenants
- Applies to SaaS environments, simplifying extension lifecycle management

## Quotes

- "Microsoft will automatically unpublish every uninstalled version of the PTE except the version immediately preceding the current installed version." (Clarifies the safety mechanism that preserves one previous version for potential rollback needs)
