- Business Central now includes built-in copilots and agents, with multiple ways to build custom AI solutions using different technologies.
- The book addresses choosing the right approach and making AI solutions secure, testable, and affordable for production.
- Chapters progress from LLM basics and Copilot Studio agents through AL copilots, testing strategies, built-in features, custom agent design, MCP integration, Azure Logic Apps workflows, and .NET development.
- Readers learn through a hands-on narrative following Cronus Events company, seeing how AI solves realistic business problems.
