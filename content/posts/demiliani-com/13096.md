# Dynamics 365 Business Central UI performance: what about the browser and the local machine?

[Read the post](https://demiliani.com/2025/12/29/dynamics-365-business-central-ui-performance-what-about-the-browser-and-the-local-machine/) · Stefano Demiliani (Stefano Demiliani, MVP) · 2025-12-29 · 1894 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central UI performance depends on network latency, browser capabilities, and local machine hardware, not just backend optimization. The post explains how to measure and monitor these client-side factors during go-live and production.

## Key points

- Business Central is designed for browser-to-datacenter latency of 250 to 300 ms or less, and latency is the first thing to check
- Use current stable Edge, Chrome or Safari; policies that block local storage or IndexedDB stop performance optimizations such as page caching
- Each new browser tab reloads resources, takes around 10 seconds to open Business Central, and uses memory
- CPU, RAM (16GB or more), GPU and SSD affect rendering speed; the author found recent Apple Silicon machines fastest
- Page View telemetry, the Speedometer 3.1 benchmark and a Puppeteer-based Node.js script can help measure client performance, ideally on real hardware

## Quotes

- "Business Central is designed for networks that have a latency of 250 - 300 milliseconds (ms) or less." (Establishes the key performance threshold for network latency that organizations must meet)
