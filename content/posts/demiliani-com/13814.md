- GPT-5.3-chat is now the default model for Agents and Managed AI service in version 28.1, with a new model switcher on the Agents page
- Available AI models can be selected via UI or programmatically through new Agent codeunit methods: SetModelId, SetModelIdToAuto, GetModelId, and GetModelName
- GPT-5.3-chat suits conversational and brainstorming tasks, while GPT-4.1-latest is better for complex data parsing and heavy workloads with large reference documentation
- GPT-5.5-chat is recommended as a future option due to 52.5% fewer hallucinations and faster response times compared to GPT-5.3-chat
