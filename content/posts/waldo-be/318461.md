# BC Telemetry Buddy  – When Your 12-Year-Old Accidentally Helps You Find a Problem

> BC Telemetry Buddy is an AI-powered tool that lets you ask natural language questions about Business Central telemetry data instead of writing complex KQL queries. It helped uncover a critical performance issue where a background job was wasting 67.7 hours daily through inefficient queries and missing retention policies, diagnosed in 15 minutes and fixed by adding indexes and enabling retention.

## Key points

- BC Telemetry Buddy uses AI and MCP to make telemetry exploration accessible through conversational questions rather than complex manual KQL analysis
- The tool successfully identified a customer experiencing massive CPU waste from a queue processor doing full table scans on an unindexed table with missing retention policies
- Performance improved dramatically after the fix: query count dropped from 19,946 to 18 over a week, and wasted time went from 67.7 hours to virtually zero
- The tool can document investigations and save conclusions automatically, allowing for follow-up validation of fixes over time
