## Key points

- BC28 avatars display who created or modified records, helping users distinguish AI-generated from human changes without opening cards
- Dynamic editability can be controlled via Boolean variables bound to field properties, evaluated per record in OnAfterGetRecord
- Copilot output token costs are reduced by narrow prompts, omitting explanations, and choosing cheaper modes like Ask over Agent
- Moving older AL apps to namespaces improves object clarity, reduces naming friction, and aligns with modern AL practices
