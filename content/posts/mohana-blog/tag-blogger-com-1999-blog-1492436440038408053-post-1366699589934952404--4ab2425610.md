# Direct transfers from warehouse-enabled locations in Business Central 2026 wave 2

[Read the post](http://mohana-dynamicsnav.blogspot.com/2026/09/direct-transfers-from-warehouse-enabled.html) · Mohana's Dynamics NAV & BC blog (Mohana Yadav) · 2026-09-09 · 994 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Business Central 2026 wave 2 enables direct transfers to work with warehouse-enabled source locations by supporting outbound warehouse handling and allowing posting method configuration per transfer route instead of company-wide only.

## Key points

- Direct transfers now support outbound warehouse handling (Require Pick, Require Shipment, Bin Mandatory, and Directed Put-away and Pick) at the source location
- Two posting methods exist: Direct Transfer (one Posted Direct Transfer document with 2 item ledger entries) and Shipment and Receipt (separate shipment and receipt with 4 item ledger entries)
- Posting method can be set at company-wide default level (Inventory Setup) or overridden per transfer route (Transfer Routes page)
- Completion workflow depends on source location's warehouse configuration; destinations with bins must have valid Transfer-To Bin Code
- Direct transfer posting does not create inbound receipts at destination and destination cannot use Directed Put-away and Pick

## Quotes

- "The transfer order uses the route setting when one exists, and falls back to the Inventory Setup default when it does not." (Explains the hierarchy of posting method configuration that allows flexibility between company-wide defaults and specific transfer routes.)
- "Direct transfer posting does not create inbound warehouse receipts or inventory put-aways." (Critical limitation that defines the scope of inbound warehouse control and shapes how destinations must be configured.)
