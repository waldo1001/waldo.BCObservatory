- Purchase quotes can be created with contacts not yet associated to vendors, removing upfront vendor setup requirements
- A vendor template is automatically selected based on territory, country/region, and contact type
- Quote details and lines are entered normally without a vendor number
- Vendor records are created automatically only when the quote is released or converted to an order
