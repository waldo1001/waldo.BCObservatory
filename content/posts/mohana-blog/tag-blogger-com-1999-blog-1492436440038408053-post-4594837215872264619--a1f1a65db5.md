# Composite Layouts in Business Central 29.0: Brand One Report End to End

> Business Central 29.0 enables composite layouts where branding is split into three separate parts (header/footer, theme, and body) that merge at render time instead of baking everything into one Word document. The post walks through a complete example of branding the Standard Sales Invoice report for Contoso, covering setup in both the UI and AL code.

## Key points

- Composite layouts separate letterhead/footer, fonts/colors (theme), and document structure (body) into three reusable parts
- Parts must be approved before use and Body layouts can only accept theme and header/footer parts, while Default layouts cannot
- Defaults cascade across four levels (global, company, report, layout) with most specific taking precedence, enabling multi-company branding at scale
- Themes use .dotx Word templates while header/footer and body layouts use .docx files; file extensions matter for compilation
