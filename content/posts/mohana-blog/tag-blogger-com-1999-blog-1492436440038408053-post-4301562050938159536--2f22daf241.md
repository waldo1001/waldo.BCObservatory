# Expense Agent just got half as expensive, and you can now try it for free

[Read the post](http://mohana-dynamicsnav.blogspot.com/2026/11/expense-agent-just-got-half-as.html) · Mohana's Dynamics NAV & BC blog (Mohana Yadav) · 2026-11-11 · 201 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Expense Agent pricing is cut in half to 25 Copilot Credits per receipt in version 29.1, and a free trial of 50 expenses per environment becomes available before the agent's December general availability release.

## Key points

- Cost per expense receipt drops from 50 to 25 Copilot Credits, reducing typical monthly spend from $125 to $62.50 for 250 receipts
- Free trial of 50 expenses allows full workflow evaluation including extraction, categorization, and expense report creation
- Pricing and trial changes ship with version 29.1 before December general availability
- Pay-as-you-go rate is $0.01 per credit, making the new cost approximately $0.25 per receipt

## Quotes

- "Expense Agent currently consumes 50 Copilot Credits per receipt processed, flat, no matter how many lines come out of it. That drops to 25." (Explains the exact pricing change that reduces operational costs for organizations using the Expense Agent)
- "50 expenses free per environment. Same shape as the Payables Agent trial Microsoft shipped recently with its 50 free invoice uploads." (Shows the trial offering structure and positions it within Microsoft's broader Copilot agent trial strategy)
