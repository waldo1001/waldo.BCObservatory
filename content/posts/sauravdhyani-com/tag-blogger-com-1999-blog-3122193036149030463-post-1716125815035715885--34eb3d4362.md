> BC28 introduces configurable currency symbols on numeric fields to help finance users quickly identify whether transaction amounts are in local or foreign currency. Administrators can choose symbol position and display type globally or per currency without requiring custom development.

## Key points

- Currency symbols can be displayed before or after numeric amounts on transactional pages
- Two display options exist: LCY/FCY symbols or LCY/FCY currency codes
- Configuration is available both globally in General Setup and individually on each Currency page
- The feature works in both SaaS and on-premises deployments without custom development
