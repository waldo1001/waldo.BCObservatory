> Business Central 27 enforces stricter Number Series validation during record insert, breaking master records that worked in BC 26 due to deferred validation. The fix involves auditing master entities, validating setup tables, and ensuring Number Series assignment before insert.

## Key points

- BC 27 validates Number Series earlier and more strictly than BC 26, rejecting implicit numbering paths that previously passed
- Master entities without explicit Number Series setup and custom tables relying on default behavior are most affected
- Solution requires creating new Number Series in setup with Manual Nos enabled and refactoring insert logic to enforce assignment before insert
- The error 'The No. Series does not exist' occurs in TestManualInternal validation during record insertion
