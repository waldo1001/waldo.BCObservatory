> Microsoft Copilot's new unified chat experience in Business Central combines internal business data, web search results, and general AI knowledge in a single conversational interface. Available as part of Business Central 2026 release wave 2 public preview, it requires administrator enablement on the Copilot & Agent Capabilities page and represents a shift toward AI-assisted application interaction.

## Key points

- The unified chat combines three sources: Business Central data, web search results, and general AI knowledge in one interface
- Available to all Business Central license holders, with additional benefits for users having Microsoft Copilot licenses
- Administrators must enable the feature on the Copilot & Agent Capabilities page once it rolls out to their environment
- This reflects Microsoft's broader strategy to align Business Central with the wider Copilot ecosystem and conversational interaction patterns
- Implications extend beyond the chat feature itself, affecting how developers design extensions, consultants approach training, and administrators manage governance
