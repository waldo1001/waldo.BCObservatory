# How User and AI Agent Avatars Improve Record Ownership in Business Central BC 28

> Business Central BC 28 adds visual avatars for record creators and modifiers on list pages, showing who created or last changed a record without opening the full card. This helps teams identify ownership, reduce duplicate work, and distinguish human-created from AI-generated records.

[Read the post](https://www.sauravdhyani.com/2026/06/how-user-and-ai-agent-avatars-improve.html) · Saurav Dhyani (Saurav Dhyani, MVP) · 2026-06-25 · 537 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Avatars display creator and modifier information directly on list pages with full names and timestamps on hover
- Feature supports named users, system users, and AI agents to reflect Microsoft's evolving identity model
- Eliminates need to open card pages or check change logs to identify record responsibility and recent activity
- Particularly valuable in multi-department environments where automation and AI processes work alongside human users
