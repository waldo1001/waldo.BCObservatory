# How Business Central 2026 Improves Item Variant Management with Pictures and Attributes.

> Business Central 2026 adds pictures and variant-level attributes to item variants, enabling faster product identification and more accurate product information management. This helps organizations with multiple product variations reduce errors and maintain better inventory data.

[Read the post](https://www.sauravdhyani.com/2026/07/how-business-central-2026-improves-item.html) · Saurav Dhyani (Saurav Dhyani, MVP) · 2026-07-24 · 564 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Item variants can now have their own pictures for visual identification, supporting import, take picture, export, and delete operations
- Attributes can be defined at the variant level so each variant maintains distinct values for characteristics like color, size, material, and capacity
- Variant-level attributes automatically inherit from parent item attributes, reducing setup effort and maintaining data consistency
- A new Update Variant Attributes action synchronizes attribute values from items to variants to keep information aligned across the product catalog
