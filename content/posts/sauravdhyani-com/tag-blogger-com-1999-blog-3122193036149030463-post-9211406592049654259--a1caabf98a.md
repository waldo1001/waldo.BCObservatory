# Business Central Version 29: Recently Searched in Tell Me and Recently Used Lookups

[Read the post](https://www.sauravdhyani.com/2026/09/business-central-version-29-recently.html) · Saurav Dhyani (Saurav Dhyani, MVP) · 2026-09-11 · 660 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central Version 29 introduces Recently Searched in Tell Me and Recently Used Lookups to help users navigate faster and enter data more efficiently. These personalized features reduce repetitive searching and clicks by surfacing frequently accessed pages and records based on user behavior patterns.

## Key points

- Recently Searched in Tell Me displays previous search history for quick access to pages, reports, and settings via Alt+Q
- Recently Used Lookups automatically surface frequently accessed customer, vendor, item, and other records when opening lookup dialogs
- Both features use server-side usage signals and platform intelligence to provide personalized recommendations tailored to each user's work patterns
- Full search and browse capabilities remain available alongside intelligent suggestions, maintaining user flexibility
