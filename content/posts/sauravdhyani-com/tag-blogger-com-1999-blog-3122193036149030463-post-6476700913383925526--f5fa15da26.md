# Automating Posting Periods in Business Central with Date Formulas

> Date formulas enable automatic posting period adjustments in Business Central based on today's date instead of fixed dates. This feature applies to General Ledger Setup, User Setup, and General Journal Templates, reducing manual updates and posting errors.

[Read the post](https://www.sauravdhyani.com/2026/06/automating-posting-periods-in-business.html) · Saurav Dhyani (Saurav Dhyani, MVP) · 2026-06-03 · 279 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Date formulas are dynamic expressions that calculate allowed posting ranges relative to today's date
- Date formulas can be applied to General Ledger Setup, User Setup, and General Journal Templates
- Posting restrictions are validated in priority order: User Setup first, then General Ledger Setup, then General Journal Template
- Formulas support relative date calculations like current month, rolling days, and multi-period ranges
- Fixed dates and date formulas can be mixed, allowing one boundary to be dynamic and the other static
