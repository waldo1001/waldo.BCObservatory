> Application of entries is the process of matching transactions like invoices and payments in Business Central to maintain accurate financial records. The post explains what open entries are, why matching is critical for accounting accuracy, and describes two main application methods: manual application and applying during payment posting.

## Key points

- Open entries are unmatched transactions (lonely socks) such as unpaid invoices or unapplied payments that remain in the system until paired
- Correct application ensures accurate outstanding balances, identifies overdue items, prevents double-counting, and keeps ledgers clean
- Two main application approaches: manual application for complex scenarios and automatic application during payment posting for efficiency
- Item ledger entries often apply automatically when items are sold, but customer and vendor entries typically require manual or configured auto-apply rules
