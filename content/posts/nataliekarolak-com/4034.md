> A BC blogger observes that by 2026 most technical blogs in the community seem AI-written, recognizable by formulaic headings such as Why it matters, and longer without adding substance, so readers spend more time for less. She contrasts this with the varied, handcrafted posts of 2022, admits she now skips such posts, and asks whether fighting AI with AI is the only answer.

## Key points

- AI-generated blog posts are now common in the BC community and identifiable by repeated section title patterns
- AI-crafted articles tend to be longer but contain less actual content than earlier handwritten blogs
- The shift to AI content increases reader effort while decreasing the unique personality and distinction between blogs
- Traditional blogs had varied formats and reflected their creators' individual styles and even their mistakes

- "AI is serving the author, but not the reader." (Captures the core concern that AI content optimization benefits creators at the expense of reader value and efficiency)
- "In 2022, blog posts were handcrafted. Their content and their style, but also their language mistakes and typos, reflected their creator." (Highlights how individual character and authenticity in blogs have been replaced by standardized AI output)
