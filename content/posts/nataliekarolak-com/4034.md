> A long-time BC blogger reflects on how AI-generated content has become prevalent in the community since 2022, noting that AI-crafted posts tend to be longer with less substantive content and formulaic section titles like 'Why it matters', making them harder to distinguish and less rewarding to read than handcrafted blogs.

## Key points

- AI-generated blog posts are now common in the BC community and identifiable by repeated section title patterns
- AI-crafted articles tend to be longer but contain less actual content than earlier handwritten blogs
- The shift to AI content increases reader effort while decreasing the unique personality and distinction between blogs
- Traditional blogs had varied formats and reflected their creators' individual styles and even their mistakes
