# Business Central 29: The New IsDirty() Flag — Finally a Smarter Way to Detect Record Changes

[Read the post](https://aardvarklabs.blog/2026/11/06/business-central-29-the-new-isdirty-flag-finally-a-smarter-way-to-detect-record-changes/) · Aardvark Labs (Marcel Chabot) · 2026-11-06 · 552 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 29 introduces the IsDirty() method, a built-in flag that detects whether a record has been modified since retrieval. This simplifies validation logic, sync operations, and audit trails by eliminating the need for manual field comparisons or temporary records.

## Key points

- IsDirty() returns true if a record has changed since being loaded or written, false if untouched
- Replaces error-prone patterns like temporary record copies or manual field tracking
- Works alongside IsEmpty() and IsTemporary() to provide three essential record state checks
- Reduces unnecessary Modify() calls in high-volume or integration scenarios
- Makes code cleaner and easier to maintain by eliminating boilerplate dirty-flag logic

## Quotes

- "The new IsDirty() method lets you check whether a record has been modified since it was retrieved or last written." (This is the core capability - it provides a built-in way to detect changes without manual workarounds or hacks.)
- "IsDirty() now joins two other small-but-essential record flags that AL developers rely on: IsEmpty() and IsTemporary()." (It completes a trio of record state checks that developers use constantly when working with records.)
- "It's the kind of quality-of-life upgrade that makes Business Central feel more modern, more predictable, and more fun to build on." (Highlights the practical developer experience improvement from having this built-in capability rather than custom solutions.)
