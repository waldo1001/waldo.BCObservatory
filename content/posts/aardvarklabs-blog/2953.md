## Key points

- JSON responses must be parsed using JsonObject to extract specific fields needed for your business logic
- Public APIs can be found through search engines or in service documentation, with resources like GitHub's public-apis list available
- Business Central shows a security prompt when extensions attempt to access external services to prevent unauthorized data exfiltration
