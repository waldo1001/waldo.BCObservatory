# Enhance Your Video Calls with OBS Studio

> This post explains how to use OBS Studio, an open-source video software, to enhance your appearance and add personalization to video calls in Microsoft Teams or Zoom. It covers installation, configuring video and audio sources, adding visual elements like images and text, using chroma key filtering with a green screen, and switching between multiple scene configurations.

## Key points

- OBS Studio is a free, open-source program originally designed for streamers that works well for improving video call appearance
- Set up video and audio capture devices as sources, then resize and position them in your scene
- Add visual elements like images and text to personalize your video feed and stand out in meetings
- Use chroma key filtering with a green screen for accurate background removal instead of built-in Teams or Zoom background tools
- Create multiple scenes to switch between different configurations, such as showing your camera or sharing a screen with your camera in the corner
