# Push Documents from Business Central to SFTP via Power Automate

[Read the post](https://aardvarklabs.blog/2026/07/10/push-documents-from-business-central-to-sftp-via-power-automate/) · Aardvark Labs (Marcel Chabot) · 2026-07-10 · 1425 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> This post demonstrates how to push a Sales Invoice PDF from Business Central to an SFTP site whenever an invoice is posted, using a staging table, a custom codeunit that subscribes to posting events, and a Power Automate workflow to handle the file transfer and status updates.

## Key points

- A staging table (ARD_PAFTPSend) stores invoice files as blobs and tracks whether they have been sent to SFTP
- The codeunit subscribes to the OnAfterPostSalesDoc event, generates the invoice as a PDF, and fires a business event when ready
- Report parameters are extracted as XML and parameterized to enable dynamic report generation with the invoice number
- Power Automate listens for the business event, retrieves the file and metadata from the staging table, uploads to SFTP, and updates the staging record
- The pattern supports retries, failed transfers can be retried by re-firing the business event, and alternative endpoints like SharePoint or network storage can replace SFTP

## Quotes

- "For this to work neatly and allow for retries and other advanced management tools we are going to start with a staging table." (Explains the architectural choice to use an intermediate table, providing resilience and visibility into the transfer process rather than direct posting.)
