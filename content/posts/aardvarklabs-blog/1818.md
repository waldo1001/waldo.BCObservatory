[Read the post](https://aardvarklabs.blog/2025/06/20/creating-low-code-ai-agents-with-copilot-studio-and-business-central/) · Aardvark Labs (Marcel Chabot) · 2025-06-20 · 1009 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Copilot Studio enables low-code creation of AI agents that connect to Business Central and other platforms without requiring extensions. The post walks through building an accounts receivable assistant by configuring the Business Central Find Records tool, adding PDF knowledge sources, and publishing to Teams.

## Key points

- Copilot Studio is a cross-platform tool that works with Business Central, Teams, D365 Sales and Marketing, and other platforms
- Use the Find Records (V3) Business Central tool to enable agents to query tables and access data
- Add knowledge sources like PDFs or Dataverse to supplement the agent's capabilities beyond Business Central data
- Agents must be published through channels; Teams and SharePoint are available options when using Microsoft Authentication
- Data access is limited to what each authenticated user has permission to see in Business Central
