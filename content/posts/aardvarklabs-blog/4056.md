# Smarter Keys, Better Performance: Exploring Business Central v29 Index Updates

[Read the post](https://aardvarklabs.blog/2026/10/09/smarter-keys-better-performance-exploring-business-central-v29-index-updates/) · Aardvark Labs (Marcel Chabot) · 2026-10-09 · 649 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central v29 allows administrators to manage indexes and SIFT keys directly through the Index Management page rather than only through AL code, enabled by a new unified table structure. This lets teams adjust or retire indexes as performance needs change without requiring development work.

## Key points

- The unified table structure in v29 removes previous restrictions on non-AL based index management that existed because native and extension fields were stored separately.
- Administrators can now activate, deactivate, or modify indexes and SIFT keys from the Index Management page for the entire system or individual companies.
- Keys can now span both native fields and extension fields in the same index, which was previously impossible and caused errors in v28 and earlier.
- Over time, indexes that were initially beneficial may become unnecessary and degrade performance, so this feature allows teams to manage them without waiting for development support.
