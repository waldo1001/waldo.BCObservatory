# Truncate vs. DeleteAll: Speed in Dynamics 365 Business Central AL

[Read the post](https://aardvarklabs.blog/2025/10/10/truncate-vs-deleteall-speed-in-dynamics-365-business-central-al/) · Aardvark Labs (Marcel Chabot) · 2025-10-10 · 426 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> The new Truncate AL method in Business Central 2025 Wave 2 deletes large data volumes 118 times faster than DeleteAll by bypassing triggers and event subscriptions, but only works on specific table types without certain field types or security restrictions.

## Key points

- Truncate deletes 500,000 records in 166 milliseconds versus 19.5 seconds for DeleteAll and 648 seconds for Record.Delete in a loop
- Truncate cannot be used on tables with OnDelete triggers, event subscriptions, media fields, system tables, or when security filters apply
- A fail-through delete pattern is recommended to use Truncate first and fall back to DeleteAll if it fails
- Truncate locks the entire table during operation, which is why it achieves such speed by bypassing row-by-row processing
