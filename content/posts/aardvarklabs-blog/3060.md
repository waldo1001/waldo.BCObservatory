# Getting Started with AI for Rapid Software Development for Business Central

[Read the post](https://aardvarklabs.blog/2026/08/14/getting-started-with-ai-for-rapid-software-development-for-business-central/) · Aardvark Labs (Marcel Chabot) · 2026-08-14 · 4545 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Shows a simple AI-assisted workflow for Business Central projects. Copilot Chat pulls functional requirements out of a sample meeting transcript for a fictional space travel agency that needs usage-based asset depreciation and launch costing. A second prompt then turns those requirements into a Business Central functional design document that identifies the extension development needed.

## Key points

- Functional requirements describe what the system must do, not how, and should be testable as yes or no statements; Copilot Chat is well suited to extracting them from transcripts or emails.
- The example system groups reusable assets into LaunchEvents, depreciates them by time and by usage, handles refurbishment capitalisation and allocates mission costs.
- Business Central serves as the GL and invoicing system, with batched journal posting, a two-way sync and full audit traceability back to telemetry and approvals.
- The functional design prompt adds product specifics, and the AL Development Collection extension supplies helpful boilerplate.
