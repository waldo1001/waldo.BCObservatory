## Key points

- Functional requirements documents should capture what a system must do (not how), making them ideal for Copilot Chat to extract from meetings, emails, and transcripts
- A space travel agency accounting system must track reusable assets with usage-based depreciation (launches, flight hours, engine burns) alongside time-based methods, grouped into LaunchEvents that consume assets
- The system requires bidirectional sync with Business Central for asset masters and GL posting, plus telemetry ingestion APIs, refurbishment workflows, cost allocation, and full audit traceability from GL back to source telemetry and approvals
- Functional design moves from requirements (what) to implementation specifics (how), requiring product knowledge and AL extension development for features Business Central does not provide natively
