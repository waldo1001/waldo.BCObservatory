# Simple Text Editor for Business Central

[Read the post](https://miljan.rs/2025/11/24/simple-text-editor-for-business-central/) · miljan.rs (Miljan Milosavljević, MVP) · 2025-11-24 · 845 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> A lightweight AL extension provides a simple text editor dialog for Business Central users to edit and store longer plain text notes in BLOB fields without complex formatting. It solves the limitation of cramped multi-line fields by opening a clean modal editor accessible via ribbon actions on customer lists and cards.

## Key points

- Addresses the gap for plain text editing in Business Central by providing a modal dialog for BLOB field text storage
- Minimal design with no formatting controls, intentionally designed for basic text entry and pasting
- Easily reusable across any table with a BLOB field by following three basic setup steps
- Positioned as a lightweight alternative to rich text editors and complex documentation systems
