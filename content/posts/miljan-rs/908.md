# Oops, I Did It Again: Reversing G/L Transactions in Business Central

[Read the post](https://miljan.rs/2025/12/15/oops-i-did-it-again-reversing-g-l-transactions-in-business-central/) · miljan.rs (Miljan Milosavljević, MVP) · 2025-12-15 · 619 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The post explains three methods to reverse General Ledger transactions in Business Central: batch reversal via G/L Register, correction entries with correction flags, and transaction-level reversal from G/L Entries. Each method maintains audit trails with balancing entries rather than deleting original entries.

## Key points

- Reverse Register on the G/L Registers page undoes a whole posted journal batch with balancing entries and a REVERSAL source code
- Register reversal only works for registers from journal posting, not from modules such as sales or purchasing
- The correction flag method copies selected lines from Posted General Journal into a new journal, flips signs and posts them, which requires the Copy to Posted Jnl. Lines option on the batch
- Reverse Transaction on General Ledger Entries targets a single transaction; reversals post on the original posting date
- All methods keep the audit trail by adding offsetting entries instead of deleting originals

## Quotes

- "reversing General Ledger (G/L) transactions in Business Central is like hitting Ctrl+Z for developers… only with extra paperwork." (Sets context that reversals in BC create tracked entries rather than true undo operations)
- "Business Central makes sure every reversal is properly tracked with balancing entries and correction flags." (Core principle that all reversals maintain audit trails through offsetting entries)
