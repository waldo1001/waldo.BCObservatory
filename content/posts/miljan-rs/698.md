# Conditional Page Breaks in Word Layouts for Business Central Reports

[Read the post](https://miljan.rs/2025/08/18/conditional-page-breaks-in-word-layouts-for-business-central-reports/) · miljan.rs (Miljan Milosavljević, MVP) · 2025-08-18 · 800 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Adding conditional page breaks in Word layouts for Business Central reports is technically possible but problematic. The approach uses Word's 'Page break before' paragraph option, though the setting often resets after editing, making it fragile for production use.

## Key points

- Word layouts don't offer the fine-grained control of RDLC for page breaks; they rely on Word's paragraph 'Page break before' option
- Conditional page breaks per line require polluting datasets with artificial data items and are not recommended
- Page breaks after sections like totals work but the setting frequently disappears when editing the layout, requiring reapplication
- Hard page breaks risk creating unwanted blank pages when sections contain no data
- Word layouts lack native conditional page break support, unlike RDLC report engines
