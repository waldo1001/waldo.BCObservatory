# Dynamics 365 Business Central – Why when I create new users do they get full access by default

[Read the post](https://thedynamicsexplorer.com/2026/02/09/dynamics-365-business-central-why-when-i-create-new-users-do-they-get-full-access-by-default/) · The Dynamics Explorer (Gavin Whittaker) · 2026-02-09 · 381 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> New users in Business Central automatically receive full access to their first company based on their assigned license. Administrators can prevent this by modifying permission sets in the License Configuration page.

## Key points

- New users get default permissions from their security groups and from the licence they are assigned
- By default a licence such as Business Central Essential grants full access to the first company a user logs into
- In the Licence Configuration page, edit the licence, turn on Customise Permissions and delete permission sets such as D365 BUS FULL ACCESS
- Partner access can be controlled the same way by editing the Delegated Admin agent - Partner licence

## Quotes

- "Now when I create a new user that's been assigned this licence they won't have full access." (Demonstrates the practical outcome of modifying license configuration to enforce principle of least privilege)
