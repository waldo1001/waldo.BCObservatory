# Dynamics 365 Business Central – How to proportionally split costs across Dimensions using Statistical Accounts and Allocation Accounts

> This post explains how to automatically split costs across cost center dimensions in Business Central using Statistical Accounts and Allocation Accounts. It demonstrates a scenario where heating expenses are distributed proportionally to departments based on square footage, streamlining accurate cost allocation without manual intervention.

[Read the post](https://thedynamicsexplorer.com/2026/04/13/dynamics-365-business-central-how-to-proportionally-split-costs-across-dimensions-using-statistical-accounts-and-allocation-accounts/) · The Dynamics Explorer (Gavin Whittaker) · 2026-04-13 · 839 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Statistical Accounts store non-financial metrics like square footage that drive cost allocation calculations
- Allocation Accounts use Statistical Account data to automatically distribute costs across selected dimensions
- When posting purchase invoices to an Allocation Account, the system proportionally splits amounts based on dimension values stored in the Statistical Account
- Updating the Statistical Account journal automatically applies new allocation proportions to future transactions without reconfiguring the Allocation Account
