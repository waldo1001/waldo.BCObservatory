# Dynamics 365 Business Central – Three ways to post Sales Ledger Cash Receipts in Business Central

[Read the post](https://thedynamicsexplorer.com/2025/09/29/dynamics-365-business-central-three-ways-to-post-sales-ledger-cash-receipts-in-business-central/) · The Dynamics Explorer (Gavin Whittaker) · 2025-09-29 · 1286 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> This post explains three methods for posting customer cash receipts in Business Central: using the Cash Receipt Journal, the Register Customer Payments feature, and posting cash directly through the Bank Reconciliation process. Each method suits different workflows and experience levels, with the bank reconciliation approach offering efficiency by combining cash posting with bank statement reconciliation.

## Key points

- Cash Receipt Journal is the most common traditional method, requiring manual entry of customer reference, amount, and application of cash to invoices
- Register Customer Payments offers a more intuitive interface for users familiar with invoice ticking workflows, but requires Payment Registration Setup
- Bank Reconciliation method streamlines operations by posting cash receipts and reconciling them simultaneously when importing bank statements
- Balancing to Bank Account rather than GL codes creates Bank Account Ledger Entries needed for Bank Reconciliation functionality
- Bank Reconciliation approach allows posting multiple transaction types (cash, direct debits, bank charges) in one process
