# Dynamics 365 Business Central – How to post a Sales Credit for an Item without affecting Inventory using an Item Charge

[Read the post](https://thedynamicsexplorer.com/2025/10/10/dynamics-365-business-central-how-to-post-a-sales-credit-for-an-item-without-affecting-inventory-using-an-item-charge/) · The Dynamics Explorer (Gavin Whittaker) · 2025-10-10 · 1016 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> When issuing a sales credit for an item that remains with the customer, using an Item Charge linked to the original sales shipment is preferable to posting directly to a GL account because it creates value entries that correctly update inventory reports and the Sales Amount (Actual) on item ledger entries.

## Key points

- Posting a sales credit memo with an inventory item automatically creates item ledger entries that return the item to inventory, which may not match the business scenario.
- Using a GL account for the credit avoids inventory adjustments but leaves the original item ledger entry's Sales Amount (Actual) unchanged, causing inventory-based reports to show incorrect values.
- Item charges can be assigned to the original sales shipment, creating value entries that properly adjust the Sales Amount (Actual) on the item ledger entry.
- The item charge approach maintains accurate inventory records and ensures reports based on inventory tables reflect correct sales amounts.

## Quotes

- "any reports based on the Inventory tables will reflect the accurate values." (Demonstrates the practical benefit of the item charge approach for downstream reporting accuracy.)
