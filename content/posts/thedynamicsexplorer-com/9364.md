# Dynamics 365 Business Central – A Closer look at the Free “Internal Administrator” and “Dynamics 365 Administrator” Licences

[Read the post](https://thedynamicsexplorer.com/2025/06/24/dynamics-365-business-central-a-closer-look-at-the-free-internal-administrator-and-dynamics-365-administrator-licences/) · The Dynamics Explorer (Gavin Whittaker) · 2025-06-24 · 637 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Business Central offers free administrative licenses to Microsoft 365 tenant administrators who perform setup and security tasks. The Internal Administrator and Dynamics 365 Administrator licenses are automatically assigned to Global Administrators and Dynamics 365 Administrators respectively, giving them limited administrative capabilities without purchasing paid licenses.

## Key points

- Internal Administrator license is automatically assigned to Global Administrators in Microsoft 365 when they access Business Central
- Dynamics 365 Administrator license is automatically assigned to Dynamics 365 Administrators in Microsoft 365 when they access Business Central
- Both free licenses enable users to manage other users, assign permissions, and create permission sets with read-only access to all Business Central areas
- These free administrative licenses reduce licensing costs and improve segregation of duties by separating admin tasks from day-to-day operations
