# Dynamics 365 Business Central – How to default a General Ledger account onto a Purchase Invoice using “Recurring Purchase Lines”

[Read the post](https://thedynamicsexplorer.com/2026/01/23/dynamics-365-business-central-how-to-default-a-general-ledger-account-onto-a-purchase-invoice-using-recurring-purchase-lines/) · The Dynamics Explorer (Gavin Whittaker) · 2026-01-23 · 446 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Recurring Purchase Lines automatically default General Ledger accounts and other details onto purchase invoices when a vendor is selected, streamlining repetitive invoice entry such as monthly rent payments.

## Key points

- Create a Recurring Purchase Line by entering a code, description, GL account, and dimensions
- Link the Recurring Purchase Line to a vendor with 'Automatic' setting to auto-populate invoice lines
- This feature works for both GL accounts and items, and can be assigned to multiple vendors
- Saves time and ensures accuracy by preventing manual GL code selection for recurring transactions
