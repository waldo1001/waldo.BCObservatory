# Dynamics 365 Business Central – Some Considerations when Changing Standard Cost on an Item Card

> Changing standard cost directly on an item card in Business Central has two critical side effects: stock keeping units are not automatically updated, and existing inventory is not revalued. Understanding these considerations is essential before making cost changes to avoid unexpected cost adjustments during sales transactions.

[Read the post](https://thedynamicsexplorer.com/2026/05/18/dynamics-365-business-central-some-considerations-when-changing-standard-cost-on-an-item-card/) · The Dynamics Explorer (Gavin Whittaker) · 2026-05-18 · 662 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Updating standard cost on the item card does not automatically update standard costs on related stock keeping units for specific locations
- Existing on-hand inventory retains its original cost and is not revalued when you change the standard cost
- When you sell items after a cost change, the system initially uses the new cost but cost adjustment reverts it to the original cost based on value entries
- The Standard Cost Worksheet is the proper tool to use if you need to revalue existing inventory
- Automatic cost adjustment must be set to 'Always' or you must manually run 'Adjust Cost - Item Entries' to see cost adjustments

- "The system doesn't update the standard cost of any Stock Keeping Units." (This is a critical gotcha when changing costs on the item card with location-specific SKUs, requiring manual updates to avoid posting at incorrect costs)
