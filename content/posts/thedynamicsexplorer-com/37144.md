# Dynamics 365 Business Central – Handling the message “Only the Posting No. Series can be filled in on recurring journals” in Recurring Journals

[Read the post](https://thedynamicsexplorer.com/2026/03/02/dynamics-365-business-central-handling-the-message-only-the-posting-no-series-can-be-filled-in-on-recurring-journals-in-recurring-journals/) · The Dynamics Explorer (Gavin Whittaker) · 2026-03-02 · 878 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Recurring General Journals in Business Central prevent using standard No. Series due to line persistence after posting. The post explains why this restriction exists and demonstrates two techniques: using Posting No. Series on the batch header, or employing placeholders in the Document No. field to automatically generate unique document numbers based on posting dates without manual updates.

## Key points

- Recurring journals retain lines after posting with updated posting dates, preventing direct No. Series usage to avoid number reuse
- Posting No. Series on the batch header generates unique document numbers for posted entries while keeping descriptive numbers on journal lines
- Placeholders like %1 (day), %3 (month), %4 (month name), and %6 (year) dynamically replace in document numbers using the posting date
- Placeholders eliminate manual document number updates for recurring journal postings while creating meaningful, date-based references

## Quotes

- "After posting a Recurring General Journal the journal lines remain and the posting date is updated based on the recurring frequency." (Explains the core design of recurring journals that creates the document numbering challenge)
- "When I post this for May, there's no need to update the document number. The system automatically replaces the placeholders using the posting date." (Demonstrates the key benefit of using placeholders for automating unique document number generation)
