# Credentials setup on Microsoft Fabric Mirroring in Business Central

> Setting up credentials for Microsoft Fabric Mirroring in Business Central 29.1 requires configuring either Microsoft's first-party app or a custom app registration with appropriate permissions and secrets.

## Key points

- Business Central 29.1 introduced Microsoft Fabric Mirroring in preview mode
- You can use Microsoft's first-party app or create your own app registration
- Custom app registration requires specific permissions and a client secret
- Principal ID is obtained from the managed application in local directory
- The app registration must be added to your Fabric workspace either manually or via Business Central action
