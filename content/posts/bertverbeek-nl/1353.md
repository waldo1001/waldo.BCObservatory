# Credentials setup on Microsoft Fabric Mirroring in Business Central

[Read the post](https://www.bertverbeek.nl/blog/2026/11/07/credentials-setup-on-microsoft-fabric-mirroring-in-business-central/) · Discover MS BC - Bert Verbeek (Bert Verbeek) · 2026-11-07 · 200 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Setting up credentials for Microsoft Fabric Mirroring in Business Central 29.1 requires configuring either Microsoft's first-party app or a custom app registration with appropriate permissions and secrets.

## Key points

- Fabric Mirroring is available in preview from Business Central 29.1
- You can use Microsoft's first-party app, or your own app registration if the first-party app has constraints or does not work
- An own app registration needs the required permissions, admin consent granted, and a client secret plus client ID
- The principal ID is the object ID found under Managed application in local directory
- Personalize Business Central to show and enable the custom app registration field, then add the app to the Fabric workspace manually or with the Add to workspace action
