# Introducing Nubimancy: Where Fantasy Storytelling Meets Real Business Central Development

[Read the post](https://jeremyvyska.com/2025/10/22/introducing-nubimancy-where-fantasy-storytelling-meets-real-business-central-development/) · Jeremy Vyska (Jeremy Vyska, MVP) · 2025-10-22 · 1380 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Nubimancy is a fantasy storytelling framework for teaching Business Central development that wraps real AL code, architecture patterns, and best practices in a narrative where five retired adventurers run businesses demonstrating different BC modules. The project combines technical rigor with engaging story-based learning to make complex concepts more memorable and accessible.

## Key points

- Five hero characters each run businesses showcasing specific BC domains: warehouse management, fixed assets/service, projects, CRM, and analytics
- Real, production-quality AL code and multi-app architecture are built to solve actual business problems within the fantasy narrative
- Story-based learning improves retention and accessibility compared to traditional technical documentation alone
- Four-phase roadmap from foundation through community learning, with Phase 2 focusing on BC extension ecosystem currently underway

## Context

- Features: warehouse management and directed put-away/pick, fixed assets and service management, project accounting and resource management, CRM and customer journey mapping, Power BI analytics integration, Azure AI integration, M365 Copilot integration, CI/CD automation
