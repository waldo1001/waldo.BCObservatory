# You Are the Art – Using Agents to Be a Better Speaker

> A speaker shares how he used AI agents and scripts to analyze his conference talk recording, measuring delivery metrics and psychological design elements to improve his craft. The post emphasizes that AI should help humans evaluate and refine their work rather than replace human judgment, and provides an open-source toolkit for other speakers to analyze their own talks.

## Key points

- AI agents can measure delivery mechanics like speaking pace, filler words, pauses, and phrase frequencies from talk recordings to surface actionable coaching insights.
- Effective AI use divides labor: the AI handles pattern-matching across large transcripts while the human interprets results and decides what to improve based on intent and understanding.
- A complementary analytical layer detects psychological design signals based on influence principles, thesis reinforcement, psychological safety, and call-to-action clarity beyond basic delivery metrics.
- Human growth comes from the interpretation and iterative refinement process, not from the AI's analysis itself - the speaker becomes better through applying measured feedback to future talks.

## Quotes

- "The AI didn't get better at evaluating speakers. I got better at evaluating myself." (Captures the core principle that AI is a tool for augmenting human expertise, not replacing it.)
- "The people getting real leverage from AI aren't the ones generating the most output. Those people are mostly just producing mediocre work faster." (Clarifies that AI's value lies in strategic enhancement of existing skill and judgment, not in raw productivity gains.)
