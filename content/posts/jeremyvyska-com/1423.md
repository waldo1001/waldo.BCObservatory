- Delegating work through business context and architectural constraints yields more thoughtful solutions than asking for isolated code pieces
- The author built a complete BC TechDays demonstration in 5 days using only prompts and no direct code writing
- Organizational transformation requires systematic infrastructure and frameworks beyond individual AI mastery
