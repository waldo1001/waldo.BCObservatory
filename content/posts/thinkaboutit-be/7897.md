# How Do I: Manage and Postpone BC Updates in the Admin Center

> Managing Business Central SaaS updates in the Admin Center allows you to schedule, delay, and respond to rollout pauses. This matters because environments upgraded to a problematic version cannot be rolled back, while postponement controls and notification setup help you avoid becoming the test cohort for production issues.

[Read the post](https://thinkaboutit.be/2026/05/how-do-i-manage-and-postpone-bc-updates-in-the-admin-center/) · think about IT - Steven Renders (Steven Renders) · 2026-05-08 · 1109 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Major updates occur twice yearly with a staged rollout, and Microsoft pauses when critical issues occur mid-rollout
- Environments already upgraded during a pause are not rolled back and remain stuck with the issue, while unupgraded ones are rescheduled seven days after the original date
- Set up notification recipients to receive proactive alerts about version availability, scheduled updates, failures, pauses, and extension compatibility warnings
- Schedule updates toward the end of the allowed window rather than immediately at GA to avoid early production issues
- You can control the update window timing and scheduled date but cannot skip major updates
