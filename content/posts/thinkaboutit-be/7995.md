# Quick Tip: Troubleshooting Business Central Power BI Reports: My 5-Minute Checklist

[Read the post](https://thinkaboutit.be/2026/06/quick-tip-troubleshooting-business-central-power-bi-reports-my-5-minute-checklist/) · think about IT - Steven Renders (Steven Renders) · 2026-06-30 · 665 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Most Business Central Power BI issues stem from configuration problems rather than Power BI itself. The post provides a systematic 5-step troubleshooting checklist covering environment connectivity, data source verification, access permissions, refresh timing, and only then investigating actual Power BI problems.

## Key points

- Installing a standard Power BI app does not connect it on its own. It must be pointed at the right tenant, environment and company, and a report that still shows CRONUS is the usual sign this was missed.
- Many reports of wrong data are correct data from the wrong environment or company, so check those first.
- Power BI and Business Central each have their own security model and both must be set up. SUPER in Business Central does not grant Power BI access.
- Before digging deeper, check the last successful refresh, the refresh schedule and any refresh failures. Data posted just before opening a report may not have been refreshed yet.
- Suspect Power BI itself only after checking configuration. Authentication, connector and service problems happen, but much less often than setup mistakes.

## Quotes

- "Most were configuration issues somewhere between Business Central and Power BI." (Identifies that Power BI problems are rarely about Power BI itself, setting the stage for a configuration-focused troubleshooting approach)
- "Having SUPER in Business Central doesn't automatically grant access to Power BI, and Power BI permissions don't override Business Central security." (Clarifies a critical misconception that both security models must be independently configured, preventing access issues)
- "Power BI is simply the last step in that chain. When one of the earlier steps is misconfigured, the dashboard becomes the messenger." (Reframes the troubleshooting mindset from blaming Power BI to examining the entire configuration chain)
