# Business Central Analytics: The Technology Is Not the Hard Part

> Business Central analytics tools like Power BI, Data Analysis mode, and APIs enable dashboards and insights, but adoption success depends more on helping users ask better business questions than on technology maturity.

[Read the post](https://thinkaboutit.be/2026/03/business-central-analytics-the-technology-is-not-the-hard-part/) · think about IT - Steven Renders (Steven Renders) · 2026-03-06 · 442 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Business Central supports analytics through standard Power BI apps, in-app Data Analysis mode, custom Power BI models via APIs, and embedded Power BI.
- The technology for analytics is mature and no longer the main challenge.
- Adoption is the real difficulty: users often return to Excel despite available dashboards.
- Effective analytics requires starting with business questions and insights rather than treating dashboards as final deliverables.
- Analytics succeed when users learn to explore their own data rather than just consume pre-built reports.

## Quotes

- "The real challenge is rarely the technology. It is adoption." (Identifies that successful analytics implementation depends on user engagement rather than technical capabilities.)
- "the real value appears when we help users ask better questions about their business." (Emphasizes that business value comes from facilitating user-driven discovery rather than providing static reports.)
