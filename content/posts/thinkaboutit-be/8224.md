- Version 29 is particularly significant for developers with improvements to AL tooling, testability, page scripting, ALGraph, and the agentic developer loop
- Reporting receives major enhancements including composite document layouts, improved Word capabilities, financial reporting features, and Business Central mirroring to Microsoft Fabric
- AI and agents are a major theme, with improvements to Copilot Chat, the Expense Agent, Sales Order Agent, and MCP Server, appearing on both user and developer sides
- Microsoft has published over 30 organized What's New sessions covering specific areas like financial management, supply chain, governance, and country-specific features
