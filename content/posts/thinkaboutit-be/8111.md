# Quick Tip: You Can Now Create a Business Central 29.0 (Preview) Sandbox

> Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (version 29.0) is now available as a preview sandbox from the admin center until early October 2026. Preview sandboxes are automatically deleted 30 days after general availability and cannot be migrated to other versions, so they serve primarily as testing windows before upgrade projects begin.
