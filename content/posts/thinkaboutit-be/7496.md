# Quick Tip: Business Central Update 27.3 – What’s New

[Read the post](https://thinkaboutit.be/2026/01/quick-tip-business-central-update-27-3-whats-new/) · think about IT - Steven Renders (Steven Renders) · 2026-01-19 · 389 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Update 27.3 for Dynamics 365 Business Central introduces enhancements to the Payables Agent for invoice processing, expands regional availability, and adds compliance features for Spain and US tax forms. This incremental update is part of the 2025 release wave 2.

## Key points

- Payables Agent now matches purchase invoices to orders intelligently and shows confidence scores to prioritize review work
- Payables Agent expands to more countries and regions for broader adoption
- New compliance support includes Spain's Verifactu requirements and bulk printing of US IRS 1099 forms
- Update includes various bug fixes and technical improvements across application and platform layers

## Quotes

- "By exposing a confidence score, users can focus their attention on invoices that need review, instead of manually checking every draft." (Demonstrates how Copilot provides decision support through confidence levels to prioritize work)

## Context

- Features: Match purchase invoices to orders with Payables Agent, Prioritize invoice draft changes using Payables Agent confidence level, Payables Agent regional expansion, Embedded Verifactu mode for Spain, Bulk printing of IRS 1099 forms
- Versions: 27.3, 2025 release wave 2
