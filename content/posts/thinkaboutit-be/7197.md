# Introducing Power BI Monitor: Unified Power BI Management in Business Central

[Read the post](https://thinkaboutit.be/2025/11/introducing-power-bi-monitor-unified-power-bi-management-in-business-central/) · think about IT - Steven Renders (Steven Renders) · 2025-11-16 · 809 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Power BI Monitor is a Business Central extension that centralizes Power BI management, allowing users to monitor workspaces, datasets, dataflows, reports, and dashboards without leaving BC. It enables refresh management, error tracking, and provides a dedicated role center for unified control of Power BI resources from within Business Central.

## Key points

- Unified view of Power BI resources including workspaces, datasets, dataflows, reports, and dashboards with real-time refresh status
- Flexible refresh management with options to refresh individual resources or bulk operations, complete history logging, and performance tracking
- Proactive error monitoring with a 30-day error dashboard showing statistics and detailed diagnostics for troubleshooting
- Uses OAuth 2.0 authentication and service-oriented architecture to securely connect with Power BI REST APIs
