# Quick Tip: Business Central Release Plans Are Going Away. Where Do You Look Now?

> Microsoft is retiring the Dynamics 365 Release Plans and moving Business Central roadmap information to the AI at Work roadmap, starting September 2026. Business Central's release cadence remains unchanged with two major releases per year, but future capabilities will be disclosed continuously rather than grouped into release waves.

[Read the post](https://thinkaboutit.be/2026/09/quick-tip-business-central-release-plans-are-going-away-where-do-you-look-now/) · think about IT - Steven Renders (Steven Renders) · 2026-09-29 · 828 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Release Plans are being retired; new roadmap information moves to AI at Work roadmap starting September 2026
- Business Central release cadence stays the same: two major releases per year plus monthly minor updates
- Roadmap items now flow through stages like In Development, Rolling Out, and Launched instead of wave-based disclosure
- Release Planner tool will be retired; transition completes by November 15, 2026
- Release wave terminology may eventually disappear as features are disclosed continuously rather than grouped
