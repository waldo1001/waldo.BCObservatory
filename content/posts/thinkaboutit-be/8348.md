# Directions EMEA 2026, Day 1: Copilot Moves from Answers to Actions

[Read the post](https://thinkaboutit.be/2026/10/directions-emea-2026-day-1-copilot-moves-from-answers-to-actions/) · think about IT - Steven Renders (Steven Renders) · 2026-10-28 · 1993 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> A remote recap of Directions EMEA 2026 Day 1 centered on Edit with Copilot, which lets users create, update and delete Business Central records and run actions via natural language, including on custom AL extension pages. Partner early access started October 27, 2026 on partner sandboxes running 30.0.56564.0 or later, with public preview planned for April 2027. It also covers Business Skills, the Expense Agent and what AI interaction means for AL page design.

## Key points

- Edit with Copilot enables users to modify business data using natural language, not just retrieve information
- Demonstrated capabilities include creating records, updating fields, deleting records, executing actions, and multistep tasks across pages
- Copilot can interact with custom AL extensions including pages, fields, and actions without separate AI integration
- Early-access requires Business Central 30.0.56564.0+, partner sandbox, US/UK/Germany, English only; public preview April 2027
- Business Skills guide AI processes through natural-language instructions while AL enforces mandatory business rules

## Quotes

- "Microsoft is moving from Copilot answering questions to Copilot performing actual work in Business Central." (Signals a fundamental shift from Copilot as information tool to Copilot as action executor)
- "AI can decide which steps to take. Business Central must still determine which operations are allowed." (Establishes the architectural principle that AI handles procedures while AL enforces rules and permissions)
