# Quick Tip: What’s in Business Central 2026 Release Wave 2, Update 29.0 Preview

> Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (update 29.0) introduces a unified Copilot chat experience, new data model for table extensions, composable report layouts, withholding tax for vendors, subcontracting improvements, Shopify B2B support, and sustainability tracking enhancements. This preview is live for evaluation before October 2026 general availability.

[Read the post](https://thinkaboutit.be/2026/09/quick-tip-whats-in-business-central-2026-release-wave-2-update-29-0-preview/) · think about IT - Steven Renders (Steven Renders) · 2026-09-07 · 897 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Unified Copilot chat reasons over business data, web results and general knowledge together instead of in separate task-specific interfaces
- New table extension data model stores all fields in the same underlying table for improved performance and cross-field key support
- Composable report layouts allow independent application of themes, headers and footers across document reports with global or report-level defaults
- Withholding tax now applies to both vendor and employee transactions in standard Business Central
- Shopify B2B support adds company tax identifiers, catalog management and price synchronization capabilities
