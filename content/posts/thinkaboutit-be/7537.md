> Analysis Mode on Business Central list pages can replace many traditional reports for data exploration and ad-hoc questions like customer balances and inventory movements, eliminating the need for custom report development while enabling users to answer follow-up questions independently.

## Key points

- Analysis Mode works best for answering questions rather than producing formatted documents for distribution
- Common reports like Top Customers by Balance and Vendor Balance to Date can be recreated using grouping, aggregation, and filtering on ledger entry pages
- Interactive exploration with pivoting and drill-down replaces static exports to Excel
- Classic reports remain necessary for statutory requirements, complex calculations, and external distribution needs
