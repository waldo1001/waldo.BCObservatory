> Update 28.4 for Business Central 2026 Release Wave 1 was released on August 3, 2026, bringing three new features to general availability or preview: Payables Agent known senders to skip redundant email classification, payments integration with the electronic document framework, and Peppol preview on purchase drafts. The update is mostly hotfixes across platform, application, and country-specific areas.

## Key points

- Three new features available: Payables Agent known senders (GA), payments with electronic document framework (GA), and Peppol preview on purchase draft page (public preview)
- Platform hotfixes address six issues including a silent client crash during CopyFilters and implicit app dependency binding problems
- Over 100 application hotfixes cover critical issues like aged receivables reports ignoring period settings, double counting in inventory valuation, and incorrect depreciation calculations
- Field Service integration now respects Dataverse Convert to Customer Asset setting; Copilot and agent data may process in different Azure geographies
- Azure OpenAI privacy notice approval required before using Expense Agent; Belgium has three localization-specific fixes
