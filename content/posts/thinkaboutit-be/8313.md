# Business Central 29: Manage Per-Tenant Extensions from the Admin Center

[Read the post](https://thinkaboutit.be/2026/10/business-central-29-manage-per-tenant-extensions-from-the-admin-center/) · think about IT - Steven Renders (Steven Renders) · 2026-10-27 · 1125 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 29 moves Per-Tenant Extension (PTE) management to the Admin Center, allowing administrators to upload, install, update, schedule, and monitor PTE deployments centrally. The existing Extension Management experience will be deprecated starting with Business Central 30 in April 2027.

## Key points

- PTEs can now be deployed through the Admin Center with scheduling options: immediate, next update window, next minor update, or next major update.
- PTE updates can be coordinated with Business Central environment updates, allowing multiple version schedules simultaneously for the same extension.
- Admin Center APIs expose PTE deployment operations, enabling automation; S2S applications no longer require in-environment registration for PTE uploads.
- Extension Management in Business Central and the Automation API will be deprecated with Business Central 30.0; simultaneous use of both experiences can hide pending deployments.

## Quotes

- "A scheduled PTE deployment is only visible in the experience where it was scheduled until the deployment completes." (Explains why simultaneous use of old and new deployment experiences can create incomplete visibility of pending operations.)
- "With Business Central 30.0 scheduled for April 2027, it's worth planning the transition now." (Provides a clear timeline for partners to migrate PTE deployment processes away from deprecated experiences.)

## Heads up

- breaking: "PTE management through the existing Extension Management pages and the Automation API will be deprecated starting with Business Central 30.0" (about version 30.0, version April 2027)
- gotcha: "A scheduled PTE deployment is only visible in the experience where it was scheduled until the deployment completes." (about version 29)
- warning: "Microsoft also recommends avoiding simultaneous use of the old and new deployment experiences within the same environment." (about version 29)
- warning: "Early installation bypasses Microsoft's normal staged deployment process." (about version 29)
