# New Application Ideas of Dynamics 365 Business Central (2025 APR)

[Read the post](https://gerardorenteria.blog/2025/05/08/new-application-ideas-of-dynamics-365-business-central-2025-apr/) · Gerardo Renteria (Gerardo Renteria) · 2025-05-08 · 1132 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> A list of community ideas for improving Dynamics 365 Business Central, collected for April 2025 and grouped by area: data migration (mostly GP to BC), development, financial management, general usability, localization, inventory, purchasing, sales and tenant administration. Each idea is credited to its author, and a few are marked as planned for 2025 wave 1 or wave 2.

## Key points

- Community members proposed improvements for data migration tools, particularly for GP to BC migrations with serial/lot number history and posting account transfers
- Development requests focus on API enhancements, debugger improvements, AppSource app management, and support for unbound actions as first-class citizens
- Financial and operational areas include requests for exchange rate improvements, aging reports, permission previews, default column sorting, and localization features
- Multiple requests aim to enhance user experience through navigation shortcuts, tab-based interfaces, quick access role centers, and intercompany transaction improvements
- Some requests target specific localizations like Spanish payment orders, Dutch apply entries, and Indian inventory accounting standards
