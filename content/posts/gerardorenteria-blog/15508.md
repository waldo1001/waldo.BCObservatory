## Key points

- Roughly 60 community ideas grouped by functional area, each with the submitter's name
- Development requests include dark mode, removing AutoIncrement from a field, and replacement points before IsHandled is restricted

- Inventory ideas include consignment inventory by bin, customer-owned inventory, and costing for service-type items
- Admin ideas cover Job Queue entry recovery, configurable API throughput, and wider recipient options for credit overage notifications
