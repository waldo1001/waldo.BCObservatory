# 🧹 Cleaning and anonymizing copies in Business Central: environments, companies, and traceability

> A reusable solution for automatically anonymizing sensitive data, marking copied companies, and adjusting settings when copying Business Central environments or companies. It provides table and field configuration, manual or event-triggered execution, and comprehensive logging for audit trails.

[Read the post](https://gerardorenteria.blog/2026/08/20/%f0%9f%a7%b9-cleaning-and-anonymizing-copies-in-business-central-environments-companies-and-traceability/) · Gerardo Renteria (Gerardo Renteria) · 2026-08-20 · 2789 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## Key points

- Configure which tables and fields to anonymize using setup tables, with options to anonymize selectively across copies
- Leverage standard events (OnClearDatabaseConfig, OnClearCompanyConfig, OnAfterCreatedNewCompanyByCopyCompany) to run cleanup automatically or manually
- Mark copied companies visually with [COPY] tag and adjust configuration settings to distinguish test copies from production
- Log all cleanup executions with header and detail records showing trigger type, company, start/end times, duration, and individual step results for traceability and audit
