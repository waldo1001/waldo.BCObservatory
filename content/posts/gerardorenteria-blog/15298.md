# 🚩 Evidence Flags: More trust signal, fewer words

[Read the post](https://gerardorenteria.blog/2026/08/31/%f0%9f%9a%a9-evidence-flags-more-trust-signal-fewer-words/) · Gerardo Renteria (Gerardo Renteria) · 2026-08-31 · 1163 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> Evidence Flags is a Copilot skill that adds brief labels to claims to distinguish what is backed by evidence, reported by users, or inferred by the model. It helps when explicitly requested via /ef commands to clarify trust levels in responses without cluttering simple questions.

## Key points

- Uses seven labeled tags across two families: provenance (Supported, Reported, Unchecked) and AI analysis (Inferred, Hypothesis, Forecast, Judgment), plus optional warnings for contested or time-sensitive claims
- Activated only when explicitly requested with /ef commands like /ef on, /ef check, /ef trail, keeping normal conversations unaffected
- Helps distinguish between what Copilot verified in files, what users stated, and what the model deduced, without subjective qualifiers like maybe or seems
- Does not verify truth, assign confidence scores, or replace technical review; Supported means claim is backed by evidence inspected in that conversation only

## Quotes

- "Instead of filling a response with qualifiers such as maybe, I think, or it seems, a brief label is added to relevant claims." (Explains the core design principle: using structured labels instead of hedging language makes trust levels explicit and scannable.)
- "Supported does not mean absolute truth: it means the claim is supported by evidence the model inspected during that conversation." (Critical boundary on what the skill does: it clarifies context-specific verification, not universal truth.)
