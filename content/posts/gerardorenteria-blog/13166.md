> An RSS Feed Reader module for Business Central enables users to monitor categorized feeds (news, regulatory alerts, market information) directly within standard pages and role centers. The solution provides centralized feed management, automatic refresh at configurable intervals, and contextual display through FactBoxes and ListParts integrated into relevant areas like the Chart of Accounts or Item List.

## Key points

- Modular architecture with centralized codeunit for fetching, parsing, and storing RSS 2.0 and Atom feeds
- Three tables manage configuration settings, RSS feed sources with URLs and categories, and downloaded entries
- Generic FactBox and ListPart pages filter feeds by category and embed into standard pages via PageExtensions
- Automatic refresh intervals (60 minutes default) and retention policies (30 days default) with manual cleanup options
- Extensible design pattern allows reusing components across multiple pages with category-based filtering
