> Navigation History is a feature that automatically tracks every page and record you visit in Business Central, letting you quickly reopen them, view your complete chronological history, and manage entries with cleanup actions. - Distinguishes between list page visits and individual card record opens with visual identification
- Supports reopening any historical entry with a single click and includes cleanup actions like delete entries older than 30 days
- Implemented using event subscribers on page events (OnOpenPageEvent for lists, OnAfterGetCurrRecordEvent for cards)
