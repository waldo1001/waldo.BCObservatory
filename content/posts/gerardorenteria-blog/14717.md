## Key points

- Community-submitted ideas for May 2026 are grouped by area, from data migrations and development to finance, purchasing, sales and warehousing
- Development and general ideas include a ShowAsTree property for list pages, more SharePoint Graph methods, index and key usage telemetry, and a timezone setting on the Microsoft Entra Applications page
- Finance ideas cover changing VAT product posting groups after prepayment, bank reconciliation improvements, Belgium VAT VIES corrections and stock received or shipped not invoiced reports
- Localization requests include a Norwegian chart of accounts for demo data and India TDS thresholds based on PAN number
- Purchasing and operations ideas include a GLN field on Order Address, a responsibility centre on vendor ledger entries, physical inventory approval workflows and double units of measure in manufacturing
