> A Birthday Message Wall extension adds human celebration features to Business Central's HR module, enabling automated company birthday greetings, peer-to-peer messages, birthday tracking, and dynamic role center headlines. ## Key points

- Implements automated daily job queue processing to identify birthdays and send personalized company messages to employee inboxes
- Enables employees to exchange birthday messages with colleagues through a two-click interface and 7-day visibility window
- Stores birthday data via extended employee table with automatic calculations for next birthday date and days remaining
- Integrates with Role Center headlines that dynamically display birthday context for each user
- Provides HR analytics dashboard with birthday metrics and full audit trail of all celebrations
