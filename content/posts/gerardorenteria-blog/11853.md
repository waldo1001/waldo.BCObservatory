- Analyzes orders over 90-day periods calculating estimated CO2 impact, weather-adjusted CO2, CO2 savings potential, and climate risk scores (0-100)
- Provides sustainability scoring (A+ to D grades) and dynamic filtering by risk level or optimization opportunities
- Demonstrates real business cases: optimal delivery days, natural cooling system viability, and concrete pouring weather suitability
