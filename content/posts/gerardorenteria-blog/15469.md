## Key points

- Financial management ideas include native IFRS 15 and IFRS 16 accounting, using actual bank conversion amounts in payment reconciliation, and several PEPPOL and e-invoice fixes.
- Localization ideas cover Italian translation corrections, Dutch XAF 4.0 and RGS support, and a French credit note type for global allowances.
- Development ideas include showing bitmaps on list pages via Media and Workload Identity Federation for AL HttpClient calls.
- Inventory and sales ideas include consignment, bin-aware application for lot-tracked items, over-shipping sales orders and an editable Shipping Advice on sales quotes.
- General ideas touch on security group profiles, analysis mode on tablets and mobile, and approval workflow restrictions.
