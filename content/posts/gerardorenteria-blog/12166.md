# 📅 Exporting ICS (Calendar) Files for Business Central 💡

[Read the post](https://gerardorenteria.blog/2026/03/29/%f0%9f%93%85-ics-calendar-export-extension-for-business-central-%f0%9f%92%a1/) · Gerardo Renteria (Gerardo Renteria) · 2026-03-29 · 1236 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> This extension enables users to export critical time and planning information from Business Central (customer meetings, order dates, project schedules) as ICS calendar files for seamless integration with Outlook, Teams, Google Calendar, and Apple Calendar. It provides two complementary approaches: record-level contextual actions and field-level assist edits that pre-populate calendar events with relevant data.

## Key points

- Bridges the disconnect between Business Central scheduling data and external calendar tools used daily by employees
- Implements RFC 5545 compliant ICS format supported universally across all modern calendar platforms
- Offers dual integration: record-level actions from main pages and field-level assists with visual indicators on date fields
- Automatically preconfigures calendar events with business context (titles, locations, organizer emails, dates) that users can customize before exporting
- Reduces duplicate work and missed deadlines by synchronizing planning information across systems without manual data entry
