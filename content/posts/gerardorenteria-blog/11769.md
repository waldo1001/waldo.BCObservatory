# 🏆🎯 Accelerate your BC Go-Live with Gamification

[Read the post](https://gerardorenteria.blog/2025/08/06/%f0%9f%8f%86%f0%9f%8e%af-accelerate-your-bc-go-live-with-gamification/) · Gerardo Renteria (Gerardo Renteria) · 2025-08-06 · 2023 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

> A gamification system for Dynamics 365 Business Central accelerates user adoption during go-live by letting teams compete in mastering key processes like sales and customer creation. The solution uses three tables and multiple pages to track achievements, points, and leaderboards across user roles.

## Key points

- Gamification addresses resistance to change and low user motivation common in ERP go-lives by fostering healthy competition among teams
- Solution uses three core tables (Achievement Definition, User Achievement Profile, User Achievement Log) with separate pages for users and administrators
- Achievements trigger automatically via events when users perform actions like creating customers, posting sales documents, or creating items
- Users progress through 11 levels from Novice to Mythical, earning points per achievement and viewing individual progress on a gaming dashboard
- Leaderboard page shows cross-user rankings by points and department, making competition transparent and motivating adoption
