> A monthly roundup of Business Central Ideas portal suggestions from August 2025, listing each idea with its author and grouped by area such as development, financial management, general, inventory, manufacturing, purchasing, reporting, sales, service, tenant administration and warehousing.

## Key points

- Development ideas include viewing all rows of temporary tables in the debugger and making SetSelectionFilter work with temporary tables
- Finance ideas include recurring billing in cash flow forecast, full SAF-T support in W1 and carrying bank statement lines to the next reconciliation
- General and reporting ideas include dark mode, language-based report layouts and multilingual email bodies for sales and purchase documents
- Manufacturing, inventory and service ideas include blocking expired batches, more shelf number fields and zero-amount service contract invoices
- Tenant administration ideas include custom role centre tiles and installing PTEs during the update window
