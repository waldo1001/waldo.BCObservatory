# ⚡ “Compacting conversation…” small message, big impact on your session 🔄

> VSCode automatically compacts agent conversation history when the context window approaches its limit to maintain session continuity. You can manually control compaction with the /compact command or start a fresh session, balancing between preserving context and maintaining response quality.

## Key points

- Context windows are finite and fill up faster with agentic tool usage than simple chat
- VSCode compacts conversation automatically when limits are reached, or you can trigger it manually with /compact
- Manual /compact with instructions lets you guide what information gets prioritized in the summary
- Quality may decline as context limit nears; alternatives include manual compaction or starting a new session
