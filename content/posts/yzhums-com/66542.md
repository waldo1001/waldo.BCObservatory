# Dynamics 365 Business Central: What does the category (Lists, Tasks, Administration, Archive……) mean when searching from Tell Me? (Usage Category)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/66542/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-08-15 · 543 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The UsageCategory property in AL determines how pages, reports, and queries appear when users search via Tell Me in Business Central. Setting this property correctly categorizes objects in search results and the role explorer, helping users navigate and improving productivity.

## Key points

- UsageCategory is an AL property that makes pages, reports and queries show up in Tell Me; if it is None or not set, the object does not appear in search
- The property has seven options: None, Lists, Tasks, ReportsAndAnalysis, Documents, History and Administration; History is shown as Archive in Tell Me
- Lists, Tasks and Administration fall under Pages and Tasks, while ReportsAndAnalysis, Documents and History fall under Reports and Analysis
- Role explorer uses UsageCategory too: its Reports and Analysis and Administration actions show objects set to those values
- Queries can be added to Tell Me starting with BC23.1

## Quotes

- "A consistent approach helps guiding the user and improves productivity." (This emphasizes that consistent categorization of objects is a best practice that directly impacts user experience and efficiency in finding what they need.)
