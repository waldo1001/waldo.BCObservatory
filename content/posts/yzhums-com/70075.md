# Cumulative Update Summary for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (January, 2026)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/70075/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-01-18 · 780 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Lists the January 2026 cumulative updates for Business Central: BC27 CU03 (27.3, separate cloud and on-premises platform builds), BC26 CU09, BC25 CU15 and BC24 CU18. It also gives lifecycle tables for on-premises releases under the Modern and Fixed policies, and marks BC13 and BC15 through BC24 as end of life.

## Key points

- BC27 update 27.3 uses platform 27.0.44534.0 in the cloud and 27.0.44301.0 on-premises; new customers start on 2025 wave 2 (27.3).
- BC25 lets admins manage environment updates more flexibly: the major update window grows from 30 (60) days to 5 months, and minor updates become optional.
- Under the Modern Policy, version 27.x is supported from Oct 1 2025 to Apr 6 2027, and 26.x until Oct 13 2026.
- BC24 and older modern releases are marked end of life; BC14 under the Fixed Policy had its extended end date on Oct 14 2025.

- "Dynamics 365 Business Central on-premises (Modern Policy) follows the Modern Lifecycle Policy." (Clarifies the support model for on-premises deployments with extended support windows.)
