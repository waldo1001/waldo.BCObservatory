# Cumulative Update Summary for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (January, 2026)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/70075/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-01-18 · 780 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> This post announces the January 2026 cumulative updates for Business Central across multiple versions (BC27 CU03, BC26 CU09, BC25 CU15, BC24 CU18) with version numbers, platform builds, and support lifecycle dates. It provides comprehensive information about release schedules, environment management features, and end-of-life dates for various Business Central releases from 2019 through 2025.

## Key points

- BC27 CU03 is released with platform 27.0.44534.0 for cloud and 27.0.44301.0 for on-premises, introducing production and sandbox environment management in Admin Center
- BC25 now offers flexible environment updates with major updates extended from 30-60 days to 5 months and optional minor updates
- Multiple versions are supported simultaneously: BC27 (wave 2, 2025), BC26 (wave 1, 2025), BC25 (wave 2, 2024), and BC24 (wave 1, 2024 at end of life)
- On-premises releases follow Modern Policy with version 27.x supporting from Oct 1 2025 to Apr 6 2027
- Older versions BC23 through BC13 are end-of-life, while BC14 (Spring 2019) remains in extended support until Oct 14 2025

- "Manage environment updates more flexibly - Major update: 30(60) days -> 5-month and Optional minor updates" (Highlights a significant change in BC25 update management, giving customers more control over update timing.)
- "Dynamics 365 Business Central on-premises (Modern Policy) follows the Modern Lifecycle Policy." (Clarifies the support model for on-premises deployments with extended support windows.)
