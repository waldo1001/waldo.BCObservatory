# Cumulative Update Summary for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (September, 2026)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/75794/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-09-04 · 699 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Monthly roundup of Business Central cumulative updates for September 2026: BC29 is in public preview, BC28 CU05 (28.5), BC27 CU11 (27.11) and BC26 CU17 (26.17) are released with their build numbers, and BC25 back to BC13 are end of life. It also lists lifecycle dates for on-premises Modern and Fixed policy versions.

## Key points

- BC28 CU05 is version 28.5 with platform 28.0.54016.0 and application 28.5.54151.54178 for cloud and on-premises
- BC27 CU11 is 27.11 and BC26 CU17 is 26.17, each with its application and platform builds
- BC29 (2026 release wave 2) is available in public preview
- BC25 and all older versions down to BC13 are marked end of life
- Under the Modern Lifecycle Policy, version 28.x is supported until Oct 13, 2027 and 26.x until Oct 13, 2026
