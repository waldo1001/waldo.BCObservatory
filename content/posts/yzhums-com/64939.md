# Cumulative Update Summary for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (June, 2025)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/64939/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-06-06 · 659 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> June 2025 cumulative updates released for Business Central across multiple versions (BC26 CU02, BC25 CU08, BC24 CU14). Includes platform and application build numbers for both cloud SaaS and on-premises deployments, plus lifecycle information showing support dates for versions from 2019 through 2025 release waves.

## Key points

- BC26 CU02 (26.2) cloud build is Platform 26.0.34852.0 with Application 26.2.34746.34995; on-premises uses Platform 26.0.34736.0 with Application 26.2.34746.34832
- BC25 CU08 (25.8) ships application build 25.8.34727 on platform 25.2.34690; BC24 CU14 (24.14) ships application 24.14.34725 on platform 24.0.34688
- BC23 back to BC15, plus BC13, are listed as end of life; BC14 is at end of mainstream support
- Under the Modern Lifecycle Policy, 26.x is supported until Oct 13, 2026, 25.x until Apr 4, 2026 and 24.x until Oct 7, 2025
- Under the Fixed Lifecycle Policy, version 14.x extended support ends Oct 14, 2025

## Quotes

- "Dynamics 365 Business Central on-premises (Modern Policy) follows the Modern Lifecycle Policy." (Clarifies the support model for modern versions, important for planning upgrade timelines)
