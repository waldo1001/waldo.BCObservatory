> Microsoft retired the old Dynamics 365 Partner Portal and consolidated partner resources into the microsoftpartners.microsoft.com domain. Partners can access Business Central materials through the AI Business Solutions section and Resource library, where they can filter by product or search by title for sales and reporting purposes.

## Key points

- Partner portal consolidated from dynamicspartners.transform.microsoft.com to microsoftpartners.microsoft.com
- Business Central resources found under Explore AI Business Solutions partner resources section
- Resource library accessible with filtering by product and search capabilities
- Materials useful for internal reporting and pre-sales activities
