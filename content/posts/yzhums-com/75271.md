# The Early Access Preview for Dynamics 365 Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29) is available

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/75271/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-08-14 · 355 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> The Early Access Preview environment for Dynamics 365 Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29) is now available for testing in select regions. Partners and users can deploy sandbox environments in the US, DE, or GB to explore upcoming features before official release and provide feedback to the engineering team.

## Key points

- Early Access Preview sandboxes are available for BC29 in US, DE, and GB regions only
- Sandboxes automatically receive latest updates throughout the development cycle
- Preview environments are for testing only and cannot be updated to production versions
- Feedback can be submitted directly to the Business Central team through the admin center

## Quotes

- "Your sandbox will automatically be kept up to date with the latest updates." (Important for understanding that preview environments don't require manual updates)
- "The environment cannot be updated to a production version. It must remain in early access preview until it is deleted." (Critical constraint showing preview sandboxes are isolated testing environments only)

- Features: Early Access Preview environments, Preview sandbox deployment, Automatic sandbox updates, Release feedback mechanism
