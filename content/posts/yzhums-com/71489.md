# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Reverse drop shipments when sales and purchase documents aren’t invoiced

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71489/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-18 · 591 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 (BC28) adds the ability to reverse posted drop shipments when neither the sales shipment nor related purchase receipt has been invoiced. This simplifies corrections that previously required credit memos or manual line recreation.

## Key points

- Drop shipments involve vendors shipping items directly to customers; they link sales and purchase orders with the customer specified in the Ship-to field
- Previously, posting a purchase receipt for a drop shipment automatically posted the linked sales shipment, preventing either from being undone
- BC28 allows undoing a posted sales shipment from a drop shipment via the Undo Shipment action, which automatically reverses the linked purchase receipt and creates correction entries
- The undo functionality does not work directly from the Posted Purchase Receipt line; you must start from the sales shipment side

## Quotes

- "You can reverse posted drop shipments when both the sales shipment and the related purchase receipt aren't yet invoiced." (Defines the exact scope and precondition for the new reversal capability)
- "Business Central reverts the linked purchase receipt, creates correction entries, and reapplies the purchase and sales entries to their reversal entries." (Explains the technical mechanism of how the reversal process works behind the scenes)
