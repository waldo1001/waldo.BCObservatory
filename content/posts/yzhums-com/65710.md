> This post documents the July 2025 cumulative updates for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central across multiple versions (BC26, BC25, BC24), including cloud and on-premises releases with their build numbers and support lifecycle information.

## Key points

- BC26 CU03 released with platform and application builds for both cloud SaaS and on-premises environments
- BC25 wave 2 introduces more flexible environment update management with extended major update windows and optional minor updates
- Multiple versions supported simultaneously with defined support dates: BC26 ends Oct 13 2026, BC25 ends Apr 4 2026, BC24 ends Oct 7 2025
- On-premises versions follow Modern Lifecycle Policy with continuing support status from October 2019 onwards
