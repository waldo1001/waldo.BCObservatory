> Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central released November 2025 cumulative updates across multiple versions (BC27, BC26, BC25, BC24) with version 27.1 for 2025 wave 2 introducing flexible environment management in the Admin Center. The post provides version numbers, release dates, and lifecycle support information for all active and end-of-life versions.

## Key points

- BC27.1 (2025 wave 2) is now available with flexible environment management capabilities in the Admin Center
- BC25 (2024 wave 2) now offers flexible major updates (extended from 30/60 days to 5 months) and optional minor updates
- Multiple versions released: BC27 CU01, BC26 CU07, BC25 CU13, and BC24 CU18 (end of life)
- BC27 support runs from October 1, 2025 to April 6, 2027 under the Modern Lifecycle Policy
- Comprehensive lifecycle information provided for versions BC14 through BC27 with specific start and retirement dates
