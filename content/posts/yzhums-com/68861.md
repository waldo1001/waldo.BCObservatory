# Cumulative Update Summary for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (November, 2025)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68861/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-11-09 · 786 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The November 2025 cumulative update roundup lists BC27.1 (2025 wave 2, CU01) with its SaaS and on-premises build numbers, plus BC26 CU07, BC25 CU13 and BC24 CU18 (end of life). It also gives lifecycle tables for on-premises versions under the modern and fixed policies, with support dates.

## Key points

- BC27.1 is generally available; new SaaS customers get it automatically, and existing customers are notified by email
- Updates released: 27.1, 26.7, 25.13 and 24.18, with application and platform build numbers
- BC27 on-premises support runs from October 1, 2025 to April 6, 2027 under the Modern Lifecycle Policy
- Lifecycle tables cover versions 15 to 27 under the modern policy and versions 13 and 14 under the fixed policy
- The post links to an earlier article on the BC25 change to more flexible environment updates

## Quotes

- "Managing Production and Sandbox Environments in the Admin Center" (This new capability in BC27.1 allows better control over SaaS environments, improving operational flexibility)
- "Major update: 30(60) days -> 5-month and Optional minor updates" (BC25 now provides extended update windows, giving customers more time to plan and test updates before mandatory deployment)
