# Cumulative Update Summary for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (August, 2025)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/66403/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-08-04 · 676 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> This post provides a comprehensive overview of cumulative updates released for Business Central in August 2025, covering BC26 (v26.4), BC25 (v25.10), BC24 (v24.16), and older versions, along with support lifecycle dates and environment management updates. It helps administrators stay informed about available patches and version support windows across all active and end-of-life release waves.

## Key points

- BC26 CU04 (version 26.4) is listed for cloud and on-premises with separate platform and application builds
- BC25 CU10 (25.10) and BC24 CU16 (24.16) are the other supported updates this month
- Since BC25, the window for scheduling major updates grew from 30 or 60 days to five months, and minor updates became optional
- BC15 through BC23 and BC13 are end of life, while BC14 has reached end of mainstream support
- Lifecycle tables give end dates such as October 13, 2026 for version 26.x and April 4, 2026 for version 25.x

## Quotes

- "Manage environment updates more flexibly - Major update: 30(60) days -> 5-month and Optional minor updates" (Shows significant change to update strategy allowing organizations more control over when updates are applied to their environments)
