# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Scheduling Financial Reports via Email (Send Regular Financial Reports to Your Inbox) – No Customization

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/72470/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-04-24 · 546 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 Wave 1 (BC28) introduces scheduled distribution of financial reports via email. Users can set up recurring financial report schedules with multiple recipients, custom filters, and automatic email delivery, eliminating the need for customization.

## Key points

- Financial reports can now be scheduled for recurring distribution to multiple recipients via email and report inbox
- Schedule configuration includes export format (PDF, Excel, Word), recipients, recurrence rules, and custom filters
- Scheduled reports are processed by job queue at specified times and automatically distributed to recipient inboxes
- User email addresses must be configured in User Setup; custom date filters support dynamic date ranges
- This capability was unavailable in BC27.5 and earlier versions

## Quotes

- "Historically, Financial Reports in BC has lacked support for scheduling when generating or printing reports. But that has finally changed." (Highlights the significance of this release as addressing a long-standing gap in the product.)
