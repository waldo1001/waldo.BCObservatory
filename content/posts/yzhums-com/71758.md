# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Get item insights with advanced KPIs and Summary (new Item Statistics page)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71758/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-26 · 509 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces a new Item Statistics page that provides financial and performance metrics for items, including inventory values, sales growth rates, gross margins, and return rates across multiple time periods. Copilot can summarize these insights automatically when enabled.

## Key points

- The Item Statistics page displays financial metrics like current inventory value, expired inventory value, and sales-related KPIs
- Four time periods are available for analysis: current fiscal period, current fiscal year, last fiscal year, and lifetime
- Sales metrics include growth rate, net sales, gross margin percentage, and return rate to track demand and profitability
- The page is accessible from the Item Card through Related > Statistics > Item Statistics or by searching Tell Me
- Enabling Summarize with Copilot automatically activates Copilot-powered item summaries with advanced KPIs
