# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28.4): Speed up processing with Known Senders in Payables Agent

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/75245/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-08-13 · 602 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 Wave 1 introduces Known Senders in the Payables Agent, allowing administrators to define trusted email senders so invoices from them can be processed automatically without manual approval. This feature reduces manual intervention while maintaining oversight of unverified sources.

## Key points

- Known Senders lets the Payables Agent process invoices from trusted senders without supervisor approval
- A new Email review setup field offers Always, Never or Only if untrusted (recommended), and no default is preselected
- Each known sender gets its own policy: Ask, Approve or Reject
- Under the per-sender policy, the agent checks in this order: uploaded invoices, monitored subfolder, Known Senders list, then internal senders
- When the per-sender policy is active, reviewed senders are added to the list automatically and each addition is logged in security telemetry

## Quotes

- "Only emails from senders who aren't on the list of known senders require review. This is the recommended setting for most organizations." (It identifies the recommended configuration that enables autonomous processing while maintaining security)
