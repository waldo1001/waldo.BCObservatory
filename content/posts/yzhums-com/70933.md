# The public preview for Dynamics 365 Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28) is available

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/70933/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-02 · 311 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Dynamics 365 Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28) public preview is available for testing in sandbox environments. Preview sandboxes cannot be upgraded to production and may be updated or deleted without notice.

## Key points

- Public preview for BC28 (W1 28.0) can be created in the Business Central admin center
- Preview sandboxes are for testing only and cannot be converted to production environments
- General availability is expected on March 18, 2026
- Major update timing changed in BC25: from 30-60 days to 5 months with optional minor updates

## Quotes

- "Sandboxes on a preview version are intended only for testing and review of features coming in the next release." (Clarifies that preview sandboxes serve a specific testing purpose and have limitations)
- "The environment cannot be updated to a production version. It must remain in preview until it is deleted." (Critical constraint for administrators to understand that preview environments cannot transition to production)
