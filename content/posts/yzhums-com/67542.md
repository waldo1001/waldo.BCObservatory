# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): Use enhanced chat with Copilot (Supports 20 more languages)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/67542/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-09-09 · 382 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27) enhances Copilot chat with support for 20 additional languages, improved understanding of multi-sentence prompts, and faster performance. Users can leverage AI-powered chat to find business data using natural language, get task guidance, and understand field purposes without leaving the application.

## Key points

- BC27 Copilot chat supports 20 more languages, understands prompts with several sentences or intents better, and runs faster and more reliably.
- Users can find and open records such as customers, vendors, sales orders and items by asking in natural language.
- Copilot can group records or do sums and averages, and shows the results on an analysis tab.
- Copilot gives step-by-step task help, and can explain a field when the user picks Ask Copilot in its tooltip. It links to the articles it used.
