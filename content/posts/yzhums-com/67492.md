## Key points

- BC27 (2025 release wave 2) is in public preview.
- BC26 CU05 is version 26.5. The post gives the cloud and on-premises platform and application build numbers separately.
- BC25 CU11 is update 25.11 and BC24 CU17 is update 24.17, each with application and platform builds.
- Under the Modern policy, version 26.x is supported until Oct 13, 2026 and 25.x until Apr 4, 2026. Version 14.x under the Fixed policy ends Oct 14, 2025.
- BC13 and BC15 through BC23 are marked end of life. BC14 is past mainstream support.
