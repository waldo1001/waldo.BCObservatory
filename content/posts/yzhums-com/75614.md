# The End of Release Plans: Dynamics 365 & Power Platform Roadmaps Move to AI at Work roadmap (Moving from release waves to always-on disclosure)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/75614/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-09-01 · 638 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Microsoft is retiring the traditional Release Wave model for Dynamics 365 and Power Platform, moving to a continuous disclosure approach through the AI at Work roadmap starting September 2026. Features will now be disclosed as they progress through development, preview, and launch stages rather than waiting for twice-yearly release waves.

## Key points

- Release Plans stop being published from September 2026, and there will be no 2026 release wave 2 plan
- New Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Dataverse capabilities go to the AI at Work roadmap and are disclosed continuously instead of twice a year
- Roadmap items move through the stages In Development, Rolling Out and Launched
- Release Planner retires on November 15, 2026; items with preview or GA dates from June 1, 2026 move over between September and November
- A Release Communications MCP Server gives programmatic access to roadmap data; the author notes Dynamics 365 was not yet in the new roadmap

## Quotes

- "Roadmap items will be continuously updated as they progress through key lifecycle stages: In Development → Rolling Out → Launched." (Explains how the new model provides real-time visibility into feature development status instead of waiting for scheduled releases)
- "Microsoft is introducing the Release Communications MCP Server, providing programmatic access to roadmap information." (Shows new capability for teams to integrate roadmap data into custom AI workflows and planning tools)
