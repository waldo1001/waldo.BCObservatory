# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Calculate withholding taxes for vendors (Withholding Tax)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71463/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-21 · 941 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces built-in withholding tax functionality for vendors, eliminating the need for third-party solutions or customizations. The feature automatically calculates and withholds taxes from vendor payments based on configurable rules and posting groups.

## Key points

- Withholding tax is calculated when posting or paying vendor invoices based on setup configuration
- Setup requires enabling withholding tax, configuring revenue types, and assigning business and product posting groups to vendors and items
- Calculation rules, minimum invoice amounts, and realization timing control when withholding tax is applied
- The feature includes withholding tax entries, settlement processing, and reporting capabilities

## Quotes

- "Business Central calculates withholding tax when you pay a vendor invoice, or when you post it, based on your setup." (Describes the core automatic calculation mechanism that applies withholding tax at the appropriate transaction point)
- "Withholding Tax feature is currently in preview." (Important warning that testing in sandbox before production use is strongly recommended)
