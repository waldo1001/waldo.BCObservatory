# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27.4): Envision and design AI agents in Business Central (Custom Agent)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/70502/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-02-09 · 1237 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> BC27.4 makes the agent designer a public preview, letting users build custom AI agents that turn natural language goals into actions within set permissions, with every action logged. A wizard creates an agent from scratch or from the Sales Validation template, then sets the profile, permissions and instructions. Instructions can be refined in an optional task playground before or after activation. Custom agents use Copilot credits.

## Key points

- Custom agents interpret natural language business goals and translate them into actionable steps while maintaining user control through transparent logging and enforced permissions
- The agent designer wizard allows creation from scratch or predefined templates like Sales Validation, with configuration for profiles, permissions, and agent instructions
- Agents operate within role-based profiles and permission boundaries, can be customized for specific business scenarios, and consume Copilot credits on a prepaid or pay-as-you-go basis
- Agent instructions guide behavior through detailed prompts, and the task playground allows iterative refinement before activating agents for production use

## Quotes

- "Agents can interpret high-level business goals expressed in natural language and translate them into actionable steps." (Describes the core capability of custom agents to understand natural language instructions and execute complex multi-step processes autonomously)
- "Every action is logged, permissions are rigorously enforced, and users remain in control at all times." (Highlights the governance and accountability mechanisms that make agents suitable for production use in regulated business environments)
- "Custom agents consume Copilot credits as they perform their steps. Credits can be provisioned through prepaid or pay-as-you-go models." (Explains the cost model for agent usage, which is critical for planning and managing operational expenses)
