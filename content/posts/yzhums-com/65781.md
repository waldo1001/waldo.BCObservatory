# Business Central 2025 wave 1 (BC26.3): Chat with Copilot to learn how to use installed add-on apps

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/65781/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-07-10 · 955 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2025 wave 1 update 26.3 enhances Copilot chat to explain and guide users through installed add-on apps, using online documentation from app publishers to answer questions about custom pages, fields, and business processes.

## Key points

- Copilot can now answer questions about ISV add-on apps installed in Business Central, transforming it from a data librarian into an expert on your unique configuration
- Administrators can enable Bing Search to enhance Copilot with web content, though data processing moves outside geographic and compliance boundaries
- The Ask Copilot icon appears in tooltips for custom fields from installed apps, and Copilot provides field explanations with links to referenced documentation
- Feature is available as a preview in sandbox environments only and currently supports English only
- Copilot's quality depends on app publishers' documentation and caption clarity; Microsoft recommends improving captions, tooltips, and online help for better AI output

## Quotes

- "Copilot explains and guides based on online documentation provided by the apps installed in your Business Central environment." (This clarifies that Copilot relies on app publishers' documentation quality, which is critical for understanding limitations and best practices for implementation)
- "These enhancements are currently available in sandbox environments only." (This is an important limitation - organizations cannot use this feature in production yet, so planning must account for this being preview functionality)
