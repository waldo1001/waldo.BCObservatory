# General Available: Dynamics 365 Business Central 2025 release wave 2 (BC27)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/67988/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-10-01 · 217 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2025 release wave 2 (BC27) is now generally available for cloud and on-premises deployments. The release includes version 27.0 with updated platform and application components, and existing customers will receive notifications about availability for their environments.

## Key points

- BC27 is now GA with automatic signup for new cloud customers
- Two deployment versions: W1 27.0 cloud (Platform 27.0.39966.0 + Application 27.0.38460.39974) and on-premises (Platform 27.0.40210.0 + Application 27.0.38460.40242)
- BC25 maintenance updates changed from 30/60 days to 5-month cycles with optional minor updates
- BC27 on-premises version 27.x enters support on October 1, 2025 with retirement on April 6, 2027
- Release resources available including full release plan, partner deck, and community feedback channels

## Quotes

- "Dynamics 365 Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27) is generally available." (Announces the official general availability date and version number for the new release)
- "New customers will automatically get the latest builds of Business Central 2025 rw2 (version 27)." (Clarifies that new cloud customers are automatically provisioned with BC27 without manual action required)
- "Business Central 2024 wave 2 (BC25): Manage environment updates more flexibly - Major update: 30(60) days -> 5-month and Optional minor updates" (Highlights the significant change in update frequency and flexibility for the previous release)
