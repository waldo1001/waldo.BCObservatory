# Cumulative Update Summary for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (May, 2025)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/63887/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-05-05 · 663 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> This post announces cumulative updates released in May 2025 for Business Central versions BC26, BC25, BC24, and BC23, including platform and application build versions for both cloud and on-premises deployments. It also documents support lifecycles and end-of-life dates for multiple release waves from 2019 to 2025.

## Key points

- BC26 CU01 (W1 26.1) is out for online and on-premises, and each has its own platform build.
- BC25 CU07 (25.7), BC24 CU13 (24.13) and BC23 CU18 (23.18) were also released, each with its application and platform build numbers.
- Since BC25, the window for a major update has grown from 30 or 60 days to five months, and minor updates are optional.
- Under the Modern Policy, version 26 is supported until October 13, 2026 and version 25 until April 4, 2026.
- BC13 and BC15 through BC23 are end of life. BC14 follows the Fixed Policy and is out of mainstream support, with extended support ending October 14, 2025.

## Quotes

- "Manage environment updates more flexibly - Major update: 30(60) days -> 5-month and Optional minor updates" (Shows BC25's key improvement in update flexibility allowing organizations longer update windows and optional minor updates)
- "Dynamics 365 Business Central on-premises (Modern Policy) follows the Modern Lifecycle Policy." (Clarifies that on-premises versions follow modern lifecycle policy with ongoing support, distinguishing from fixed policy versions)
