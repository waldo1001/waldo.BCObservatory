# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Analyze customer loyalty and other sales scenarios

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/73030/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-25 · 330 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces new Power BI reports on customer loyalty and analysis views for sales orders, along with subtotals in Excel-based sales reports and enhanced demo data. These features help sales and finance teams understand customer trends and manage pipelines more effectively.

## Key points

- Two new Power BI reports, Customer Retention Overview and Customer Retention History, cover customer loyalty and require the Power BI app to be installed
- Excel layouts of five built-in sales and inventory reports, such as Salesperson Commission and Customer/Item Sales, now include subtotals
- New analysis views on the Sales Orders list are planned for expected sales volume, customer sales by volume and aged accounts by month, though the author could not find them in BC28.1
- Demo data now includes more sales orders and back orders to support testing and demos
