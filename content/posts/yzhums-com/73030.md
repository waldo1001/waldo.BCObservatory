# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Analyze customer loyalty and other sales scenarios

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/73030/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-25 · 330 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces new Power BI reports on customer loyalty and analysis views for sales orders, along with subtotals in Excel-based sales reports and enhanced demo data. These features help sales and finance teams understand customer trends and manage pipelines more effectively.

## Key points

- New Power BI reports (Customer Retention Overview and Customer Retention History) track customer loyalty patterns
- Built-in sales reports now include subtotals in Excel layouts to reduce manual calculation work
- Analysis views on Sales Order List provide insights into expected sales volume, customer demand, and aged accounts
- Enhanced demo sales data enables more realistic testing and training scenarios
