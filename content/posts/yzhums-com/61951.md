# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to disable/hide Power Automate action group for all users

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/61951/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-04-28 · 346 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Disable the Power Automate action group for all Business Central users by marking 'Disagree for Everyone' on the Privacy Notices Status page, rather than managing permissions individually.

## Key points

- Power Automate Automate action group was introduced in BC20 (2022 wave 1) on all pages with data table context
- Users cannot hide or move the Automate menu using personalization, though flows can be moved
- The feature can be hidden per user via permission 9630 Allow Action Automate
- Disabling for all users is simpler: use Privacy Notices Status page and select Disagree for Everyone on Microsoft Power Automate row
- Sign out and back in after making the change for it to take effect

## Quotes

- "You can't hide or move the Automate menu or the Power Automate submenu and its actions." (Explains a key limitation of personalization for Power Automate, making permission-based or tenant-wide controls necessary)
