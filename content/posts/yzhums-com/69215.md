# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Manage credit limits (Warnings and Workflow)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/69215/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-08-27 · 762 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Managing credit limits in Business Central involves setting maximum credit amounts on customer cards, configuring warning options in Sales & Receivables Setup, and using standard workflows to approve credit limit changes. The post explains how warnings work when customers exceed limits or have overdue balances, plus a code example for blocking sales posting when credit limits are exceeded.

## Key points

- Credit Limit field on customer cards specifies maximum credit before warnings; value 0 means unlimited credit
- Sales & Receivables Setup controls warning types: Both Warnings, Credit Limit only, Overdue Balance only, or No Warnings
- Standard workflows can require approval before credit limit changes are applied, reverting values until approved
- Blocking posting when credit limit is exceeded requires customization using an event subscriber on Sales-Post codeunit

## Quotes

- "Credit Limit field on a customer card specifies the maximum amount you allow the customer to exceed the payment balance before warnings are issued." (Explains the core function of credit limits and how they trigger warnings in Business Central)
- "You can post even if the credit limit exceeds." (Clarifies that standard BC allows posting over credit limits, establishing the need for customization if stricter control is needed)
