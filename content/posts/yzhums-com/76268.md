# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29): Set up vendor-specific number series for self-billing invoices

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/76268/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-09-23 · 318 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29) adds vendor-specific number series for self-billed purchase invoices. Organizations can now define and assign dedicated numbering sequences per vendor to comply with supplier-specific invoicing requirements and reduce manual errors.

## Key points

- Define unique number series for individual vendors in self-billing arrangements
- Automatically apply vendor-specific numbering when creating self-billed invoices
- Maintain separate invoice sequences across different suppliers
- Improve compliance with vendor agreements and local numbering requirements

## Quotes

- "You can define and assign dedicated number series for self-billed purchase invoices on a per-vendor basis." (Shows the core capability to customize numbering by vendor, enabling compliance with supplier-specific requirements)
