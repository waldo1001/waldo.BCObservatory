# The Early Access Preview for Dynamics 365 Business Central 2027 release wave 1 (BC30) is available

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/76863/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-10-21 · 339 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Dynamics 365 Business Central 2027 release wave 1 (BC30) Early Access Preview is now available for testing in DE, GB, and US regions. Partners and users can deploy sandboxes to explore new features, provide feedback, and prepare for the official release.

## Key points

- Early Access Preview environments automatically stay updated with latest changes throughout the development cycle
- Sandboxes can be created in US, DE, or GB regions from the Business Central admin center
- Preview environments are for testing only and cannot be converted to production versions
- Feedback can be submitted directly to the Engineering team via the preview interface
- Preview terms apply and environments may be force updated or deleted without notice

## Quotes

- "Your sandbox will automatically be kept up to date with the latest updates." (Explains the automatic maintenance benefit of preview sandboxes during development)
- "The environment cannot be updated to a production version. It must remain in early access preview until it is deleted." (Critical constraint showing preview environments are strictly for testing, not upgrade path to production)
