# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): Deferral reporting enhancements (Replacing RDL layouts with Word and Excel formats and adding three new list pages)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68328/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-10-10 · 487 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2025 wave 2 modernizes deferral reporting by replacing RDL layouts with Word and Excel formats for three summary reports and adds three new list pages for ad hoc analysis of unposted deferral lines. This gives finance teams better flexibility, easier customization without developer support, and improved visibility into deferral data across all documents.

## Key points

- RDL layouts replaced with Word and Excel formats for G/L, Sales, and Purchasing Deferral Summary reports
- Three new list pages added for Sales, Purchasing, and G/L deferral lines with analysis mode capability
- Controllers can analyze current and upcoming deferrals and customize report layouts without developer support
- Improved discoverability and documentation help users understand report purposes
- Analysis mode enables review of unposted deferral lines across all documents for better forecasting
