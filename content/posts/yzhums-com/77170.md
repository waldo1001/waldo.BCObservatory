# Microsoft Announces New Business Central + Copilot Bundles Starting January 2027

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/77170/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-11-09 · 544 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Microsoft is launching Business Central and Microsoft 365 Copilot bundles starting January 1, 2027, offering cost savings of $6 per user per month. The announcement includes expanded Copilot capabilities such as direct integration into Business Central and a new Edit with Copilot feature that lets users perform supported actions using natural language.

## Key points

- Two permanent CSP bundles start January 1, 2027 for customers with up to 300 seats: Essentials plus Microsoft 365 Copilot Business at $95 and Premium plus Copilot at $125 per user per month, saving $6 compared with buying separately
- No qualifying Microsoft 365 base subscription is needed; the bundles appear in the CSP price list preview on December 1, 2026, and local currency pricing comes later
- From BC29 (2026 release wave 2), Copilot is available directly inside Business Central as a unified chat experience
- Partners can join the Edit with Copilot early access preview from October 27, 2026; it lets users make supported changes in natural language
- Partners can sell the bundles to existing and new customers and offer Copilot onboarding, training and AI agent setup services

## Quotes

- "Starting January 1, 2027, two new CSP bundles will be available for customers with up to 300 seats." (Establishes the launch date and eligibility scope for the new bundled offering)
- "Users can use natural language to perform supported actions and make changes in Business Central." (Explains the core capability of Edit with Copilot, moving beyond information retrieval to task execution)
- "Microsoft is clearly moving toward a closer integration of Business Central and Copilot." (Summarizes the strategic direction and its implications for the platform's evolution)
