# Microsoft Announces New Business Central + Copilot Bundles Starting January 2027

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/77170/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-11-09 · 544 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Microsoft is launching Business Central and Microsoft 365 Copilot bundles starting January 1, 2027, offering cost savings of $6 per user per month. The announcement includes expanded Copilot capabilities such as direct integration into Business Central and a new Edit with Copilot feature that lets users perform supported actions using natural language.

## Key points

- Two new CSP bundles available January 2027: Essentials at $95/month and Premium at $125/month per user, for customers up to 300 seats
- No qualifying Microsoft 365 base subscription required to purchase bundles
- Edit with Copilot Early Access Preview launched October 27, 2026, enabling natural language task execution
- Copilot integrated directly into Business Central starting with 2026 release wave 2 (BC29) for unified experience
- Partners can offer Copilot onboarding, training, and AI agent configuration services to customers
