# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Create purchase quotes for contacts (without a vendor)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71008/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-04 · 359 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Starting with BC28 (2026 release wave 1), you can create purchase quotes for contacts without requiring a vendor record first. When converting the quote to an order, Business Central automatically creates a vendor using a template based on contact details.

## Key points

- Purchase quotes can now be created for contacts not yet associated with vendors, eliminating the need for pre-setup
- Team Members license users can now create sales or purchase quotes
- Upon quote conversion or release, Business Central automatically generates a vendor record using a vendor template
- The appropriate template is selected based on territory code, country/region code, and contact type from the contact record

## Quotes

- "You can create purchase quotes for contacts that aren't yet associated with a vendor." (This is the core feature enabling preliminary purchasing discussion without vendor record setup)
- "When you convert the quote into an order or release the document, Business Central automatically creates the vendor using a vendor template." (Explains the workflow mechanics and how vendor records are generated from quote conversion)
- "Team Members license can also be used to create sales or purchase quotes." (Clarifies licensing eligibility for the new purchase quote capability)
