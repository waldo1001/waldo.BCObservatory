# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): Access open-sourced Power BI apps

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68178/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-10-06 · 380 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27) open-sources Power BI apps for sales, purchasing, and projects, joining previously open-sourced finance, inventory, and manufacturing apps. Organizations can now fully customize semantic models and reports to match their business processes and KPIs without starting from scratch.

## Key points

- All standard Business Central Power BI apps are now open-sourced, providing complete code access
- Open-sourced apps available: Finance, Inventory, Inventory Valuation, Manufacturing, Subscription Billing, and Sustainability in BC26; Sales, Purchasing, and Projects added in BC27
- Developers can access source code through PowerBIReports in Business Central or by adding dependencies in app.json, then edit and customize in Power BI
- Customization saves development time by building on ready-made models rather than starting from scratch

## Quotes

- "Instead of starting from scratch, you can build on high-quality, ready-made models and visualizations, saving significant development time and effort." (Highlights the practical business value of having pre-built, customizable templates rather than developing independently)

## Heads up

- warning: "Please note that the View mode cannot export .pbix files and edit the report." (about version BC27)
