# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Copilot and agent data movement across geographies

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/74703/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-07-14 · 1167 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 (BC28) introduces controls for managing Copilot and agent data movement across Azure geographies. Starting July 1, 2026, environments may process AI requests outside their primary data residency region, with consent managed via the Copilot & agent capabilities page.

## Key points

- Copilot relies on Azure OpenAI Service, which exists only in certain regions, so prompts and results may be processed in another Azure geography starting July 1, 2026 for version 28.0 or later.
- Consent is controlled by the Allow data movement toggle on the Copilot & agent capabilities page; it is on by default where consent is needed, and turning it off makes Copilot unavailable.
- US-hosted environments need no consent; UK, Australia and India keep Copilot processing local but agent features are processed in another geography, and admins can keep Copilot while turning off agents.
- The post separates three concepts: the BC country/region version (localization), the Azure region for data residency (where business data is stored), and the Azure OpenAI geography (where AI processing happens).
- Microsoft may keep prompts and outputs for up to 24 hours for abuse monitoring and does not use them to train foundation models.
