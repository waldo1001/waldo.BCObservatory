# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Use self-billed invoices

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71666/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-04-06 · 392 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 (BC28) introduces self-billed invoices, allowing buyers to generate invoices on behalf of suppliers under mutual agreement. This feature streamlines purchasing processes through automated layouts, dedicated number series, vendor configuration, and a specialized report layout that formats self-billed invoices in sales invoice style.

## Key points

- Buyers can now create invoices on behalf of vendors when a self-billing agreement is enabled via a new Boolean field on the Vendor Card
- Purchase invoices for self-billing vendors are automatically marked as self-billed with restricted editable fields like Vendor Invoice No.
- Vendors can be assigned dedicated number series for self-billed invoices to ensure correct document sequencing separate from posted purchase invoice numbers
- A new report layout formats self-billed invoices in sales invoice style with buyer company information, vendor details, and VAT information
- Danish localizations can automatically create Digital Vouchers during self-billing posting for compliance, and vendors with self-billing agreements cannot receive inbound e-documents for accounts payable

## Quotes

- "Self‑billing reduces operational friction by allowing buyers to generate invoices on behalf of suppliers under a mutual agreement." (Explains the core benefit of self-billing in reducing buyer-supplier coordination burden and improving invoice reliability.)
- "All Purchase Invoices created for that vendor, whether from Purchase Orders or directly, are automatically marked as self‑billed." (Shows that the system automates invoice classification once self-billing is configured, reducing manual steps.)
