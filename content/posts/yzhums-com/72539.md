# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to display/add “Sent To” on the Sent Emails (8883, List) page?

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/72539/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-04-28 · 543 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The Sent Emails page (8883) shows the sender but not the recipients. The post adds a Sent To column with a page extension that reads the internal Message Id from table Sent Email via RecordRef and FieldRef, then calls Get and GetRecipients on the public Email Message codeunit to build a semicolon-separated list of To recipients.

## Key points

- Sent Emails uses a temporary source table, and table 8889 Sent Email has no Sent To field; all its fields except Id are internal.
- The standard Email Viewer gets recipients through Email Message Impl., which is internal and cannot be used from an extension.
- The public codeunit Email Message offers Get and GetRecipients, which can stand in for the internal implementation.
- RecordRef and FieldRef on the Sent Email table get around the internal access to read the Message Id in OnAfterGetRecord.
