# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to quickly remove Non-Alphanumeric Characters in AL

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/75649/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-09-02 · 223 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> This post explains how to remove non-alphanumeric characters from strings in Dynamics 365 Business Central using the built-in StringConversionManagement codeunit. It provides a practical example of applying character filtering during data validation on the Customer Card form.

## Key points

- Use codeunit 47 StringConversionManagement and its RemoveNonAlphaNumericCharacters method to filter strings
- The method leaves only letters A-Z and numbers 0-9, removing special characters, symbols, spaces, and emoji
- Can be applied in page extensions using OnBeforeValidate triggers to clean data during user input
- Offers a cleaner built-in solution compared to custom methods for handling non-alphanumeric character removal
