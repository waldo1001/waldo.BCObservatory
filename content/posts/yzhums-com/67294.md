# The public preview for Dynamics 365 Business Central 2025 release wave 2 (BC27) is available

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/67294/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-09-03 · 251 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Dynamics 365 Business Central 2025 release wave 2 (BC27) public preview is available for testing and feature review. Administrators can create preview sandboxes via the Business Central admin center, with the understanding that preview environments are temporary and cannot be promoted to production.

## Key points

- BC27 preview environment is now available for creation in the Business Central admin center
- Preview sandboxes are for testing only and cannot be updated to production versions
- The environment runs on W1 27.0 with Platform 27.0.38856.0 and Application 27.0.38460.38809
- BC25 introduced more flexible major update management, extending the update window from 30-60 days to 5 months with optional minor updates

## Quotes

- "Business Central 2024 wave 2 (BC25): Manage environment updates more flexibly - Major update: 30(60) days -> 5-month" (Shows the significant change in update management flexibility introduced in BC25, giving organizations more control over when to apply major updates.)
