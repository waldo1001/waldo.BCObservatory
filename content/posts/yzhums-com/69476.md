# Dynamics 365 Business Central: All Copilot and agent capabilities

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/69476/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-12-22 · 7257 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central offers multiple AI-powered Copilot features for productivity, including marketing text suggestions, number series generation, item substitution recommendations, sales line suggestions, analysis assist, autofill capabilities, bank reconciliation assistance, chat interface, record summaries, e-document mapping, and advanced search, plus agent capabilities for sales order automation.

## Key points

- Copilot provides AI writing assistance for product marketing text with customizable tone, format, and emphasis options
- Natural language prompts help generate and modify number series for documents and accounts automatically
- AI suggests substitute items by analyzing product data across multiple tables, with confidence scores for validation
- Sales line suggestions accept natural language input, files (CSV), or references to existing documents to populate order lines
- Analysis assist uses prompts to create data analysis views with filtering, sorting, and field grouping without manual configuration
