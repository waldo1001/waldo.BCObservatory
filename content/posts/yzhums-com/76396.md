# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to turn off feedback on AI-generated content

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/76396/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-09-30 · 259 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Administrators can disable Copilot feedback controls in Business Central by configuring settings in the Power Platform admin center. This setting affects all Copilot features across the tenant and takes effect after users sign out and back in.

## Key points

- Copilot feedback is enabled by default and users can rate AI-generated content with Like/Dislike controls
- Administrators can toggle off basic Copilot feedback to remove Like/Dislike controls
- Administrators can toggle off additional feedback to prevent sharing of prompts, responses, and log files
- Feedback configuration is managed at the tenant level in Power Platform admin center

## Quotes

- "Administrators can turn off the ability for users to provide Copilot feedback." (Explains the core capability for administrators to control feedback features across the tenant)
- "This feedback is anonymous and helps improve the service." (Clarifies the purpose of Copilot feedback and its benefit to Microsoft's service improvements)
- "Changes take effect when users sign out and sign back in." (Important timing information for administrators implementing feedback configuration changes)
