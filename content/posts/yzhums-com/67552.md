# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to quickly get User Experience/Experience Tier (Essential or Premium) in AL

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/67552/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-10-16 · 284 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Retrieving the user experience tier (Essential or Premium) in Business Central AL code using the Application Area Management Facade codeunit. This helps determine which features and functionality are available to users based on the company's configured experience level.

## Key points

- Business Central supports two experience tiers: Essential (common business functionality) and Premium (all functionality including Manufacturing and Service Management)
- The experience tier is configured via the Experience setting on the Company Information page
- Codeunit 9179 'Application Area Mgmt. Facade' provides the GetExperienceTierCurrentCompany method to programmatically retrieve the current tier in AL
- Developers can execute different code logic based on the experience tier setting for conditional feature availability
