# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to quickly determine if the installed AppSource (Global) app is up-to-date?

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/69185/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-11-27 · 428 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> AppSource extensions in Business Central can be checked for updates through the App Management page in the Business Central admin center, where the Latest Available Version field indicates whether updates are available. The post explains extension scopes and update mechanisms including automatic update cadence introduced in BC23.

## Key points

- Comparing the installed version in Extension Management with the AppSource listing works for some partner apps, but Microsoft apps show only major.minor on AppSource
- The App Management page in the Business Central admin center shows a Latest Available Version field; if it is empty, the app is up to date
- Available versions depend on the environment's current major version
- Since BC23, the Apps Update Cadence setting updates AppSource apps automatically with every minor and major update or only major updates; per-tenant extensions are not affected

## Quotes

- "If this field is empty, then the current AppSource app is the latest version." (Provides the simplest practical method to determine if an AppSource app needs updating by checking the Latest Available Version field in admin center)
