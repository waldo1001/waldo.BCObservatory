# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to quickly determine if the installed AppSource (Global) app is up-to-date?

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/69185/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-11-27 · 428 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> AppSource extensions in Business Central can be checked for updates through the App Management page in the Business Central admin center, where the Latest Available Version field indicates whether updates are available. The post explains extension scopes and update mechanisms including automatic update cadence introduced in BC23.

## Key points

- Extension Management in Business Central shows installed version numbers for AppSource apps, but Microsoft extensions may only display major.minor versions
- The App Management page in Business Central admin center directly shows the Latest Available Version field, making it the easiest way to check for updates
- AppSource extensions have Global scope, while PTE (partner-provided) and Dev extensions have different scopes and update behaviors
- Apps Update Cadence feature in BC23 allows automatic updates of AppSource apps with every minor update or only major updates, scheduled during environment update windows

## Quotes

- "If this field is empty, then the current AppSource app is the latest version." (Provides the simplest practical method to determine if an AppSource app needs updating by checking the Latest Available Version field in admin center)
