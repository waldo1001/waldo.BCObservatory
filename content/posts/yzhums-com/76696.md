# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29): Design document reports with the updated Word add-in (New design components)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/76696/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-10-15 · 1127 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 2 introduces new design components in the Word add-in for document reporting, allowing developers to create report layouts faster by inserting pre-built elements like addresses, field groups, amounts, signature lines, and notes with single clicks instead of manually building complex layouts.

## Key points

- The updated Word add-in for BC29 adds design components for common document parts: addresses, field groups, amounts, signature lines and notes.
- Each component is inserted with a single click, which the author says RDLC layouts cannot do, so layout creation is much faster.
- Install the latest Business Central add-in (version 3.0.0.0 rather than 2.0.4.0) from the Word Add-ins menu to get the Business Central ribbon tab.
- Amounts components arrive with example figures to be replaced with data pane fields; signature lines are layout only, with no digital signing or encryption.
- Notes components print free text of up to 10 lines on the report, unlike internal Business Central notes.
