# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29.1): Cross-environment Master Data Management

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/77263/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-11-11 · 917 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 wave 2 introduces cross-environment master data management, allowing organizations to synchronize critical business data like customers, vendors, and products across multiple environments using OAuth authentication within the same tenant. This feature maintains a single source of truth while reducing manual data entry and improving data consistency across development, test, and production environments.

## Key points

- Synchronize master data across development, test, and production environments to maintain consistency and reduce duplicate maintenance
- Requires OAuth authentication with Microsoft Entra app registration restricted to same-tenant connections
- Receiving companies can review synchronization logs and define which tables and fields to synchronize with optional filtering
- Changes in source company are immediately pulled by the job queue, supporting faster environment setup and smoother application lifecycle management

## Quotes

- "Keep critical business data consistent across environments with cross-environment master data management." (Summarizes the core business value proposition of preventing data inconsistencies across multiple BC instances)
- "synchronization only connects to environments in the same Microsoft Entra tenant." (Clarifies a critical security and architectural constraint that admins must understand when setting up the feature)
- "Changes in the main company are immediately pulled to the receiving companies by the job queue." (Explains the real-time nature of data synchronization, ensuring data remains current across environments)
