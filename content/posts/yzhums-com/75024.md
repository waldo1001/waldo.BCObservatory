## Key points

- Sales representatives are available Monday-Friday from 6 AM to 3 PM PT at 800-642-7676
- A global phone numbers list provides country-specific support contacts
- Billing support is offered in English during business hours (9 AM-5 PM weekdays) in most countries, with local language support varying by region
- Technical support is available 24/7 in English, with some local language options
- The same phone numbers apply to other Dynamics products including Dynamics 365, AX, CRM, GP, NAV, and SL
