> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces enhanced deferral and trial balance reports with faster Excel layouts, new subtotals for easier reconciliation, and analysis views for improved financial data filtering and period-end task efficiency.

## Key points

- Built-in deferral report Excel layouts now include subtotals to make balance reviews easier during reconciliation and audit processes
- Multiple standard finance Excel reports run significantly faster, helping finance teams analyze data and complete period-end tasks
- New analysis views on GL Registers and Fixed Assets Ledgers list pages enable improved filtering and slicing of ledger and asset activity
- Several sales Excel reports also received subtotal enhancements including Salesperson Commission and Customer/Item Sales
