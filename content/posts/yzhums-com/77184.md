# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29.1): Data mirroring to Microsoft Fabric (No APIs or OData Needed)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/77184/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-11-12 · 1734 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 wave 2 introduces data mirroring to Microsoft Fabric, allowing users to synchronize selected companies and tables to OneLake without needing APIs or OData. The feature enables direct analytics, reporting, and data integration with Fabric IQ, Power BI, and Data Factory.

## Key points

- Data mirroring replicates Business Central data to an open mirrored database in Microsoft Fabric's OneLake in Delta Parquet format
- Users can select specific companies and tables to synchronize, with continuous exports of ongoing changes
- Authentication requires either a custom app registration or the Microsoft Business Central Fabric Export application with proper service principal setup
- Workspace access and permissions must be configured through Microsoft Entra ID and Fabric workspace management
- Synchronization status and progress are monitored through the Export Summary and Fabric Synchronization Overview pages
