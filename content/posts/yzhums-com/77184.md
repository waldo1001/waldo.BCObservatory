# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29.1): Data mirroring to Microsoft Fabric (No APIs or OData Needed)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/77184/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-11-12 · 1794 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 2 introduces data mirroring to Microsoft Fabric, enabling users to select companies and tables for replication into OneLake without requiring APIs or OData. Business Central continuously syncs initial snapshots and ongoing changes in Delta Parquet format for use with Fabric analytics tools, Power BI, and Data Factory.

## Key points

- Data mirroring replicates selected Business Central companies and tables to Microsoft Fabric OneLake in Delta Parquet format
- Configuration requires setup of service principals, app registration, Fabric workspace permissions, and selecting tables to synchronize
- Initial export takes approximately two hours; subsequent continuous exports run at user-configurable intervals
- Built-in logging and monitoring provide visibility into synchronization health and export status

## Quotes

- "Data mirroring connects Business Central online to an open mirrored database in Microsoft Fabric. You select the companies and tables to synchronize." (Explains the core capability of selective data replication without requiring APIs.)
