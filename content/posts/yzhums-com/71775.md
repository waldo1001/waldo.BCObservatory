# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Modernizing analytical reports for inventory

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71775/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-27 · 339 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 release wave 1 modernizes inventory reports by replacing RDL layouts with Excel and Word formats, adding a new Item Age Composition report, and deprecating older redundant reports to simplify reporting and analysis.

## Key points

- Inventory reports now use Excel and Word layouts instead of outdated RDL formats for easier analysis and editing
- A new Item Age Composition by Quantity and Value report provides deeper insights into aging inventory
- Six inventory reports have been modernized including Inventory Top 10 List, Item Availability, and Inventory Purchase Orders
- Three older reports are being deprecated including Item Age Composition-Value and Items with Negative Inventory to reduce redundancy
