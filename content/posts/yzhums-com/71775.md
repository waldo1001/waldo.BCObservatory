# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Modernizing analytical reports for inventory

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71775/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-27 · 339 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 release wave 1 modernizes inventory reports by replacing RDL layouts with Excel and Word formats, adding a new Item Age Composition report, and deprecating older redundant reports to simplify reporting and analysis.

## Key points

- In BC28, modernizing a report means its RDL layout is replaced with Excel and Word layouts and its Microsoft Learn documentation is updated
- Modernized inventory reports include Inventory Top 10 List, Item Availability, Inventory Purchase Orders, Item/Vendor Catalog, Inventory Availability Plan, Inventory - Transaction Detail and Item Expiration - Quantity
- A new Item Age Composition by Quantity and Value report gives deeper insight into aging stock
- Item Age Composition - Value, Item Age Composition - Quantity and Items with Negative Inventory are deprecated, so check customer usage and customizations before upgrading
