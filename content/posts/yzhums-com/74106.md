# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to create a new item from a picture/image (Out-of-the-Box)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/74106/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-06-09 · 559 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central allows users to create new items directly from pictures using the out-of-the-box 'New from picture' action on the Items page. The system automatically analyzes the uploaded image, suggests an appropriate item category and template, and populates item attributes, then creates the item with the picture attached.

## Key points

- Click the down arrow next to New on the Items page to access the 'New from picture' action
- The system automatically analyzes the uploaded picture to suggest the most suitable category and template
- Item attributes are populated automatically based on the image analysis and selected template
- The item picture and auto-generated description are added automatically to the new item card
