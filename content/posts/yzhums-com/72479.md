# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Manage tasks from all agents in dedicated task pane

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/72479/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-04-23 · 333 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces a dedicated task pane that consolidates all tasks generated by AI agents across finance, purchasing, sales, and operations, allowing users to review, navigate to affected records, complete, or dismiss tasks from a single structured location.

## Key points

- A new dedicated task pane displays all agent-generated tasks in one place instead of scattered notifications across different pages
- Users can review task details, navigate to affected records, complete tasks, dismiss them, and navigate between previous and next tasks
- The feature benefits roles relying on AI workflows such as accountants, sales people, and managers who engage with multiple agents daily
- Tasks include suggestions, validations, follow-ups, access to draft documents, and other agent-initiated actions
- The task pane can be collapsed and reopened as needed, with an additional Agent Tasks page available for task management
