# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Filter receipt and shipment lines to quickly find documents you want to invoice (New filters on the Get Receipt Lines, Get Shipment Lines, and Get Service Shipment Lines pages)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71648/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-23 · 541 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 release wave 1 introduces new filters on Get Receipt Lines, Get Shipment Lines, and Get Service Shipment Lines pages to help users quickly locate and filter document lines by order number, vendor order details, or customer references when combining multiple receipts or shipments into invoices.

## Key points

- New filters allow filtering by Order No., Your Reference, External Document No., Vendor Order No., Vendor Shipment No., and Item Reference No. depending on document type
- Filtering on recognizable business fields reduces time searching for correct document lines and lowers risk of bringing in wrong lines
- Functionality applies to purchase receipts, sales shipments, and service shipments when creating combined invoices
- Users can select multiple receipt or shipment lines across different documents from the same vendor/customer to include in a single invoice
