# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): Cancel running environment update

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/69075/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-12-19 · 437 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2025 wave 2 introduces the ability to cancel running environment updates from the admin center, allowing administrators to halt in-progress updates and automatically restore the environment to its pre-update state without data loss.

## Key points

- Admins can cancel ongoing environment updates from the Business Central admin center by clicking the blue 'Updating' button and selecting Cancel update
- When canceled, the system automatically restores the environment to its pre-update state without requiring manual intervention or risking data loss
- Updates cannot be canceled during the Enforced Update Period for the source version
- After cancellation, the next update date is automatically scheduled and communicated via notification email

## Quotes

- "When you cancel an update, Business Central automatically starts a recovery operation to restore the environment to its pre-update state." (This shows the key safety feature that automatic recovery eliminates manual intervention risk and prevents data loss)
- "Updates can't be canceled on environments that are in the Enforced Update Period for the source version of the environment update." (This clarifies an important limitation administrators need to know when planning update cancellations)

## Heads up

- warning: "Updates can't be canceled on environments that are in the Enforced Update Period for the source version." (about version BC27)
