# Cumulative Update Summary for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (February, 2026)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/70491/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-02-07 · 800 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central February 2026 cumulative updates are available for versions BC27, BC26, BC25, and BC24, with specific platform and application build numbers for cloud and on-premises deployments. New customers automatically receive BC27.4, while existing customers get email notifications for available updates; BC25 introduces more flexible environment update management with extended major update windows and optional minor updates.

## Key points

- BC27.4 CU04 released for cloud (SaaS) with platform 27.0.45543.0 and application 27.4.45366.45675
- On-premises BC27.4 update available with platform 27.0.45396.0 and application 27.4.45366.45458
- BC25 (2024 wave 2) changes major update window from 30-60 days to 5 months and makes minor updates optional
- Multiple versions supported with clear lifecycle dates: BC27 (Oct 2025-Apr 2027), BC26 (Apr 2025-Oct 2026), BC25 (Oct 2024-Apr 2026), BC24 end-of-life
- Resources available including release plan, partner deck, YouTube videos, Yammer feedback group, and Ask Us Anything Office Hours

- "Major update: 30(60) days -> 5-month and Optional minor updates" (Key change in BC25 that gives existing customers significantly more flexibility in managing when they apply updates to their environments)
