# New Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central pricing effective October 2025 (First price increase)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/63897/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-05-06 · 374 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Microsoft announced the first Dynamics 365 Business Central price increase in over five years, effective October 1, 2025. The pricing affects new subscriptions and renewals after that date, with corresponding increases to storage entitlements per license tier.

## Key points

- This is the first Business Central price increase in more than five years, and it takes effect October 1, 2025. Current prices still apply to purchases made before that date.
- Storage included with each license goes up: Essentials from 2GB to 3GB per user, Premium from 3GB to 5GB per user, and Device from 1GB to 1.5GB.
- New subscriptions get the new prices, and existing subscriptions get them at their first renewal on or after October 1, 2025.
- The author expects Team Member and additional capacity prices to stay the same, because Microsoft did not mention changing them.
- Partners should contact current and prospective customers early so the change goes smoothly.

## Quotes

- "Microsoft are announcing the first price increase for Dynamics 365 Business Central in more than five years." (Establishes the significance and rarity of this pricing action, occurring for the first time in over five years)
- "To coincide with the price increase, Microsoft are increasing the storage entitlement included with each per user license." (Shows that the price increase is paired with increased value through higher storage allocations across all license tiers)
- "The updated prices apply to new subscriptions and to existing subscriptions upon their first renewal on or after October 1, 2025." (Clarifies the effective date for both new and existing customers, helping with transition planning)
