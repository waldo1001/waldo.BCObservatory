# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Match purchase invoices to multiple order and receipt lines (new Get Order Lines action)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71425/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-13 · 812 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> BC28 adds a Get Order Lines action on purchase invoices. It matches invoice lines to many purchase order lines and posted receipt lines, which is more flexible than the existing one-to-one Get Receipt Lines action. It works for received and non-received order lines. An optional Receipt on Invoice toggle on the order creates receipts when the invoice is posted. A Matched Order Lines page is used to review and adjust the matches.

## Key points

- The new Get Order Lines action lets you match one invoice line to multiple purchase order lines, not just one-to-one matching
- You can match invoice lines to orders whether or not receipts have been posted, providing flexibility for non-received goods
- The Receipt on Invoice toggle automatically creates receipts when the invoice is posted, streamlining the workflow
- The feature has limitations: no prepayments, item charges, projects, subcontracting, blanket orders, or intercompany transactions
- Matched Invoice Lines field on purchase orders tracks quantities matched via invoice lines

## Quotes

- "You can match each invoice line to any number of purchase order lines, regardless of whether receipts exist." (This shows the core flexibility gain: matching is decoupled from receipt status, enabling more workflows than the previous line-to-line approach)
- "When you turn on the new Receipt on Invoice toggle on the order, posting the linked invoice automatically creates the required receipts." (Describes the automation benefit that can reduce manual receipt entry steps when invoicing non-received orders)
