## Key points

- BC28 (version 28.0) is the major update for 2026 release wave 1, available starting April 2026
- Customers can schedule and reschedule their update date flexibly through the Business Central Admin Center
- The update window is 5 months long, with a mandatory deadline of September 1, 2026 for all environments
- Obsolete code is marked 12 months before removal to give developers time to migrate
- Notification emails inform customers of their scheduled update dates and allow modification through the Admin Center
