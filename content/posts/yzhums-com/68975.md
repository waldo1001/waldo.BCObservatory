# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27.1): Demo analytics with new enhanced demo data

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68975/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-11-14 · 384 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27.1) includes a new module in the Contoso Demo data tool that provides enhanced sample data for sales and purchasing documents such as quotes, orders, invoices, and returns. This enriched dataset enables partners and teams to demonstrate end-to-end business processes, train users, and validate configurations in realistic scenarios.

## Key points

- New Contoso Demo data module adds realistic sample data for sales and purchasing documents including quotes, orders, invoices, and returns
- Enhanced dataset enables demonstration of end-to-end business processes and user training in safe, risk-free environments
- Significantly increases volume of demo documents (e.g., 296 posted sales invoices vs. 214 in previous versions, 29 purchase orders vs. 15)
- Supports showcasing Power BI apps, Excel reports, and data analysis capabilities for sales and purchases

## Quotes

- "This enriched dataset enables you to showcase end-to-end business processes, test scenarios, and train users in a safe, risk-free environment." (Clarifies the primary business value of the new demo data for demonstrations and training purposes)
