# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to add custom pages, reports, and queries to Role Explorer (Customizing Role Explorer)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/66714/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-08-19 · 593 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Role Explorer displays content from role centers and can be extended by adding custom pages, reports, and queries to role centers. Enabling 'Show in Role Explorer' on a profile makes its role center content visible in the 'Explore more roles' section.

## Key points

- Role Explorer shows content from the current user's role center in the Home section and from other role centers when using 'Explore more roles'
- Custom pages, reports, and queries become searchable in Role Explorer when added to a role center and the role is enabled
- The 'Show in Role Explorer' setting on profile pages controls which role centers appear in the 'Explore more roles' section
- Bookmarks provide the simplest way to add custom content to Role Explorer without requiring customization
