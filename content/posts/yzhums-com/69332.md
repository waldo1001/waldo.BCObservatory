# Can Dynamics 365 Business Central Online (SaaS) be used in Mainland China? (Is BC accessible from Mainland China?)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/69332/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-12-17 · 479 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central Online (SaaS) is not officially available for purchase in Mainland China, but the post explores whether users in mainland China can access BC environments hosted in other regions through network connectivity testing. The post provides guidance on latency requirements and regional accessibility options.

## Key points

- Business Central was available in 242 countries and regions as of November 2025, but mainland China is not one of them, so it cannot be bought there
- The author tested from mainland China. AzureSpeed ranked Southeast Asia lowest in latency, then Japan West, then Japan East
- Microsoft says Business Central is designed for latency of about 250 to 300 ms or less from browser to Azure datacenter
- Tests through the Troubleshooting Connectivity page showed environments from several countries reachable, but the author notes results may vary and access is not guaranteed in the future
- Simplified Chinese platform support came in BC17.2. Translations come from partner packs, not from Microsoft

## Quotes

- "Business Central is designed for networks that have a latency of 250 - 300 milliseconds (ms) or less." (Establishes the performance baseline for users in mainland China to assess whether their network connectivity will support effective BC usage)
- "Microsoft has added Business Central platform support for Chinese (Simplified), Bulgarian, Greek, Romanian and Turkish languages in BC17.2" (Shows language localization efforts, though translation packs still require partner sourcing rather than first-party support)
