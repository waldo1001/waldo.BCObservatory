# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Understanding the State/Region/Province/County field on the Customer Card

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/72984/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-19 · 430 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> The State/Region/Province/County field on the Customer Card in Business Central dynamically changes its caption and visibility based on the Country/Region master data settings, specifically the 'County Name' and 'Address Format' configuration. Understanding these settings helps explain why the field appears differently across customers and environments.

## Key points

- The field caption changes dynamically to County, State, Region, or Province depending on country/region configuration
- Visibility and caption are controlled by two settings in Countries/Regions master: County Name and Address Format
- Address Format determines whether the field displays at all, while County Name sets the localized caption
- You can use Custom Address Format to adjust field display order and toggle visibility for specific countries

## Quotes

- "The field below 'City' dynamically changes its caption, it may display as 'County,' 'State,' 'Region,' or 'Province.'" (Explains the core behavior that causes confusion about why the field appears differently in different environments)
- "It is determined by the 'County Name' and 'Address Format' settings in the 'Countries/Regions' master." (Identifies the root cause configuration that controls field behavior, allowing users to troubleshoot visibility issues)
- "The 'Address Format' determines whether the field itself should be displayed on the page." (Clarifies the specific setting that controls whether the field appears at all versus just its caption)
