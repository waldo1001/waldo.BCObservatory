# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Copy Sales and Purchase Line Descriptions (Type G/L Account) to G/L Entries

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/64874/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-07-03 · 319 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central can copy G/L Account line descriptions from sales and purchase documents to their resulting general ledger entries when posting. Enable the 'Copy Line Description to G/L Entry' checkbox in Sales & Receivables Setup or Purchases & Payables Setup to include line descriptions in G/L entries for auditing and reconciliation purposes.

## Key points

- G/L Account line descriptions from sales and purchase documents are not copied to G/L entries by default
- Enable the 'Copy Line Description to G/L Entry' setting in Sales & Receivables Setup or Purchases & Payables Setup
- This feature helps with reconciling and auditing expenses by showing source document descriptions in general ledger
- The same functionality is available for service documents via Service Management Setup

## Quotes

- "When we post G/L Account in Sales Document or Purchase Document, line descriptions will not be copied to G/L Entries." (Explains the default behavior that users need to understand before enabling the feature)
- "When reconciling or auditing data, such as expenses, it is useful to see the source document line description in the general ledger entries." (Clarifies the business value and use case for this functionality)
