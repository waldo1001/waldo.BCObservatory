# Business Central 2025 wave 1 (BC26.3): Automate payables processes with the Payables Agent

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/65721/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-07-08 · 504 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> The Payables Agent in Business Central 2025 wave 1 automates accounts payable tasks by extracting vendor invoices from email and SharePoint, creating purchase invoice drafts for review, and using purchase history to auto-fill document details. This feature reduces manual data entry and errors while freeing AP professionals for higher-value work.

## Key points

- Payables Agent sources vendor invoice attachments (PDF) from Microsoft 365 email and SharePoint, then imports them into E-Documents
- The agent creates purchase invoice document drafts using past purchase history and company accounting policies for auto-population
- Setup involves activating the agent, configuring email accounts, and choosing which invoice fields should be auto-filled
- Feature is available in BC26.3 but currently limited to United States and United Kingdom regions
