# Business Central 2025 wave 1 (BC26.3): Automate payables processes with the Payables Agent

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/65721/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-07-08 · 504 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The Payables Agent in Business Central 2025 wave 1 (tested in 26.3) automates accounts payable work. It picks up vendor invoice PDFs sent to a Microsoft 365 mailbox, imports them into E-Documents, and creates draft purchase invoices for review, using past purchase history to fill in details. The post walks through activating the agent, setting up the email account, and choosing which extra fields to auto-fill.

## Key points

- In the first version, the agent reads PDF invoice attachments from a Microsoft 365 email account, imports them into E-Documents, then moves the files to an archive folder.
- The agent matches senders to registered vendors and creates draft purchase documents for a person to review.
- Past invoice data is used to suggest values, and you can configure extra fields to auto-fill; that page opens only after clicking Update.
- Setup covers activating the agent, configuring the email account, and an optional sample invoice guide.
- Regions are not fully confirmed: the author suspects US and UK only, as with the Sales Order Agent, and found it missing in the Japan (W1) region.
