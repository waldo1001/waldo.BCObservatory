# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): Automatically create multiple fixed asset cards

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68230/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-10-07 · 490 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27) introduces automatic creation of multiple fixed asset cards when posting purchase invoices. Instead of manually creating asset cards or using bulk import methods, users can now specify a quantity on purchase lines and the system automatically generates individual fixed asset cards for each unit upon posting.

## Key points

- Automatically generates multiple fixed asset cards from a single purchase invoice line based on quantity specified
- Eliminates manual card creation for bulk purchases, reducing effort and errors in asset management
- New field 'No. of Fixed Asset Cards' allows users to control how many asset cards are created from a purchase line
- All fixed asset attributes except the asset number are copied from the asset template selected in the purchase line

## Quotes

- "Automatically creating multiple fixed asset cards when you post a purchase invoice streamlines asset management processes." (Explains the core benefit of the feature for reducing manual work in asset management)
- "Microsoft has added a new field, No. of Fixed Asset Cards. It is hidden by default, but can be added via the Personalization feature." (Identifies the specific control mechanism for users to manage how many asset cards are created)
- "Yes, fixed asset cards will be automatically created when posting." (Confirms the automation happens at posting time, not during entry)
