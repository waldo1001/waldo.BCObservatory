# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to change Due Date of posted documents

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/63315/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-06-18 · 548 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Explains two standard ways to change the due date after posting: the Update Document action on posted sales invoices, and Edit list on Customer Ledger Entries. Either way, the invoice and the ledger entry stay in sync. For purchases, the due date can only be changed in Vendor Ledger Entries. By default the due date comes from the document date and the payment terms formula, not the posting date.

## Key points

- Due date is calculated from document date and payment terms code, not posting date, and can be edited after posting
- Posted sales invoices can be modified via the Update Document action which updates both the invoice and related customer ledger entries
- Customer and vendor ledger entries support direct due date editing through the edit list feature
- For purchases, only vendor ledger entries allow due date modification; the posted purchase invoice update page does not include this field

## Quotes

- "Due date in Business Central is the date when something is due to be paid." (Defines the fundamental concept users need to understand before modifying due dates in the system)
