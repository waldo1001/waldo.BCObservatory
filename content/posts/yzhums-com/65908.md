# The release plan for Dynamics 365 Business Central 2025 release wave 2 (BC27) is available now!!!

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/65908/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-07-16 · 1223 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The 2025 release wave 2 plan for Dynamics 365 Business Central (BC27) is now available, covering new functionalities rolling out from October 2025 to March 2026. The plan introduces Copilot agents, sustainability features, e-documents, supply chain improvements, and development enhancements across multiple systems.

## Key points

- The wave covers October 2025 to March 2026, with general availability starting October 1, 2025 and early access on August 4, 2025
- Copilot and agent items include an enhanced Sales Order Agent, enhanced chat, autofill, summaries and an MCP server for AI agents
- Country updates add more regions and let UK localization install as an extension
- Supply chain adds subcontracting in production and quality evaluation of incoming goods; e-documents add shipment and transfer shipment types
- Many sustainability items cover Scope 3 value chain, ESG reporting and energy tracking; financial items include multiple fixed asset cards and excise tax

## Quotes

- "The 2025 release wave 2 plan covers all new functionalities that Microsoft plans to release to market from October 2025 to March 2026." (Establishes the timeline and scope of the entire release wave that impacts all Business Central users)
- "Scale your business without the traditional friction that growth brings. With powerful generative AI, you can eliminate tedious tasks." (Highlights the core value proposition of Copilot and AI agents for automating manual work and enabling business scaling)
- "Business Central provides rich supply chain management functionality for sales, purchase, invoice, inventory, warehouse management, project management, service order management, and manufacturing." (Summarizes the comprehensive scope of supply chain capabilities being expanded in this release wave)
