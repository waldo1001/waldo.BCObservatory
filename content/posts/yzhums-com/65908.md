> The 2025 release wave 2 plan for Dynamics 365 Business Central (BC27) is now available, covering new functionalities rolling out from October 2025 to March 2026. The plan introduces Copilot agents, sustainability features, e-documents, supply chain improvements, and development enhancements across multiple systems.

## Key points

- Release wave 2 spans October 2025 through March 2026 with general availability starting October 1, 2025
- Copilot and autonomous agents enable sales order automation, enhanced chat, field autofill, and sustainability insights
- New localization options available including UK as an extension and expanded country coverage
- Supply chain features include subcontracting, quality evaluation, e-documents for shipments, and sustainability Scope 3 tracking
- Development focus on AL extensions for Copilot and new fixed asset capabilities in financial management
