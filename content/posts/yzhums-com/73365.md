# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Why can’t a Super User create a new Company

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/73365/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-06-05 · 512 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The post explains why users with the SUPER permission set cannot create a new company in Business Central. When a specific company is designated in the SUPER permission set assignment, the user's rights become restricted to only that company, preventing them from creating new companies across the system.

## Key points

- Only users with the SUPER permission set can create a new company in Business Central
- When a specific company is designated during permission set assignment, access is limited to that company only
- If no company is specified when assigning a permission set, it applies to all companies
- Users cannot remove a company designation from their own permissions; only administrators with SUPER or SECURITY permission sets can modify this
- This company restriction principle applies to all permission sets, not just SUPER

## Quotes

- "Only users with the SUPER permission set can create a new company." (Clarifies the fundamental requirement for company creation in Business Central)
- "When you have multiple companies in Business Central, managing permissions across companies requires extra consideration." (Explains why company designation matters in multi-company environments)
- "If you don't specify the company when assigning a permission set, the permission set then applies to all companies." (Shows the key difference between specifying and not specifying a company during assignment)
