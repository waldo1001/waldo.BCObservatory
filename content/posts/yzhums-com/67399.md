# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): Find pages and reports with advanced Tell Me search (AI-powered feature)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/67399/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-09-04 · 487 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Advanced Tell Me search in Business Central 2025 wave 2 uses AI and semantic meaning to help users find pages and reports without remembering exact names, reducing search time and improving productivity. The feature is in public preview, available only for online, and must be manually enabled in Feature Management.

## Key points

- AI-powered semantic search goes beyond exact text matching to find relevant pages and reports
- New Advanced tab appears in Tell Me after enabling the feature in Feature Management
- Feature is currently available only for Business Central online
- Users must sign out and back in for changes to take effect
- Some localization issues reported with language packs affecting search functionality

## Quotes

- "This AI-powered feature helps you quickly locate any page or report, even when you don't remember the exact name." (Demonstrates the core value of the feature in reducing user frustration and search time)
- "This feature adds an advanced search option in Tell Me that finds results based on semantic meaning, not just exact text matches." (Explains the key technical difference between standard and advanced search capabilities)
- "This feature is available only for Business Central online." (Important limitation for users to understand deployment scope)
