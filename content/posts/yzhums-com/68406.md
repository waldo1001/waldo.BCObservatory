# Major update of Dynamics 365 Business Central 2025 release wave 2 (BC27) is available for existing customers

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68406/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-10-10 · 430 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The BC27 (2025 release wave 2) major update is now available to existing customers, with dates that vary by country and region. Admins can move the scheduled date within the update period through the Business Central Admin Center, and the last day to apply it is February 28, 2026. The author's test update from 26.5 to 27.0 shows up in the environment operation log and ends with a confirmation email.

## Key points

- BC27 major update is generally available with scheduled rollout dates varying by region
- Customers can reschedule update dates within the update period using the Business Central Admin Center
- Last day to apply the update is February 28, 2026; the example notice schedules SandboxNA260 for November 5, 2025
- Environment operation logs track update progress and completion with notification emails confirming successful upgrades
