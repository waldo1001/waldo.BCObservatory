# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): Get early access preview versions as a partner – Early Access Preview (Next Major) environment in Cloud/SaaS

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68438/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-10-14 · 406 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Partners with the Dynamics 365 Business Central Partner Sandbox license can access Early Access Preview (EAP) environments for BC27 before general availability, allowing early testing of new features with an additional sandbox environment quota. This helps partners prepare for the next major version without affecting existing sandbox resources.

## Key points

- Partner Sandbox license holders get automatic access to Early Access Preview environments for the next major Business Central version
- EAP environments provide an extra sandbox quota separate from existing partner sandbox environments
- Environments update automatically with weekly builds during development for continuous testing and feedback
- EAP is currently available for DE, GB, and US localizations only
- Partners need valid MPN ID and at least five employees to qualify for Partner Sandbox license

## Quotes

- "Tenants with the partner sandbox license automatically get an extra environment quota for a sandbox environment on the early access preview version." (Shows that EAP doesn't consume existing sandbox quotas, making it easier for partners to test without sacrificing development resources)
- "Early previews are available for the DE, GB, and US localizations." (Clarifies the geographic and localization scope of the EAP offering)
- "Because the Cloud (SaaS) version does not support the Next Major version, partners have had to use Docker." (Explains why EAP solves a previous pain point for partners developing AppSource apps)

## Heads up

- warning: "EAP environments may be deleted without notice" (about version BC27)
- preview-disclaimer: "Please note that this is only available in Partner Sandbox license." (about version BC27)
