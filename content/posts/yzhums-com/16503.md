# Dynamics 365 Business Central Cloud (SaaS): Find out when your license expires (expiration date)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/16503/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-08-21 · 411 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Checking Business Central license expiration dates in the Microsoft 365 admin center. Users can find subscription renewal or expiration dates on the Billing > Your products page, with indicators for recurring billing or fixed expiration dates.

## Key points

- Navigate to Microsoft 365 admin center Billing section to view all subscriptions
- Circle arrow icon indicates recurring billing; a date means the subscription expires on that date
- Trial environments do not display expiration dates
- Expired products show a Disabled status with an expiration message

## Quotes

- "Business Central is a full cloud solution now, included as an app in Microsoft 365." (Establishes that Business Central is now a cloud-only solution requiring Microsoft 365 integration and Entra ID authentication.)
- "In the Microsoft 365 admin center, go to the Billing -> Your products page." (Provides the exact navigation path to find license expiration information.)
