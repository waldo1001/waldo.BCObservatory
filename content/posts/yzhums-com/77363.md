# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29.1): Use batch totals for payment journals

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/77363/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-11-13 · 322 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 2 adds a Batch Total (LCY) field to the Payment Journal page, displaying the cumulative total for payment batches to improve review and approval processes before posting.

## Key points

- New Batch Total (LCY) field displays sum of Amount (LCY) for visible payment journal lines
- Batch total updates when line amounts change or batch name is selected, showing 0 for empty batches
- Total accounts for line signs so positive and negative amounts offset each other
- Improves payment processing control and streamlines review and approval workflows before posting or export
- Not yet added to Cash Receipt Journals or General Journals as of this update

## Quotes

- "Before you post or export a payment journal, review Batch Total (LCY) at the bottom of the page." (Describes the new workflow step for verifying payment batches before committing transactions)
