# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Black Friday 1K AI Power-Up campaign (Free copilot credits)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/69256/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-11-30 · 381 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Microsoft's Black Friday 1K AI Power-Up campaign offers participating Business Central customers 1,000 monthly Copilot Credits for three months (January-April 2026) to try agent features. Agent capabilities like the Sales Order Agent and Payables Agent consume credits per action, and the campaign requires version 27.1 or later; participation is limited and selection-based.

## Key points

- Participating customers receive 1,000 Copilot Credits monthly for three months (Jan-Apr 2026)
- Agent features consume credits: Sales Order Agent actions cost 5 credits, Payables Agent invoice processing costs 50 credits per invoice
- Requires Business Central version 27.1 or later
- Campaign participation is limited and subject to technical readiness and capacity constraints
- Without campaign credits, agent features are not free and require consumption-based billing

## Quotes

- "the Agent features in Business Central is not free. Microsoft launched this campaign to allow more end customers to experience these new features" (Clarifies that agent capabilities normally require paid credits, making the campaign significant for trial access)

## Context

- Features: Sales Order Agent, Payables Agent, Email processing, Sales quote generation, Sales order creation, Invoice processing
- Versions: 27.1
