# Dynamics 365 Business Central Blog Series: Adding Barcodes

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/65570/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-06-30 · 506 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> This blog series covers barcode functionality in Dynamics 365 Business Central, including one-dimensional and two-dimensional barcode generation in reports, barcode scanning through AL code, printing barcodes from production orders, and integrating external barcode services with Swiss QR Code and QR code capabilities.

## Key points

- Business Central supports both 1D and 2D barcodes (QR, Data Matrix, PDF417, Aztec, Maxi Code, Swiss QR) directly in reports without customization
- Barcode scanning can be invoked programmatically from AL code to add Scan Barcode actions with continuous scanning capability
- External APIs and services can generate specialized barcodes like colored QR codes, Telepen, and Swiss QR Codes
- Barcode printing from production orders is available as a built-in feature without requiring customization
- QR codes can embed page URLs, allowing users to link directly to Business Central records like customer cards

## Context

- Features: One-dimensional barcodes in reports, Two-dimensional barcodes (Aztec, Data Matrix, Maxi Code, PDF417, QR-Code), Barcode scanning from AL, Swiss QR Code generation, External barcode services integration, Print barcodes from production orders, QR code page URL embedding
- Versions: BC18, BC19.1, BC23, BC26
