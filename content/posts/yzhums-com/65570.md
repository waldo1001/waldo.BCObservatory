# Dynamics 365 Business Central Blog Series: Adding Barcodes

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/65570/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-06-30 · 506 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> This blog series covers barcode functionality in Dynamics 365 Business Central, including one-dimensional and two-dimensional barcode generation in reports, barcode scanning through AL code, printing barcodes from production orders, and integrating external barcode services with Swiss QR Code and QR code capabilities.

## Key points

- The series covers 1D barcodes in reports (BC18) and 2D barcode fonts such as Aztec, Data Matrix, Maxi Code, PDF417 and QR (BC19.1)
- External services or APIs can produce barcodes such as Telepen, Swiss QR and colored QR codes, and Swiss QR can also be generated without external services
- Barcode scanning can be started from AL to add a Scan Barcode action with continuous scanning
- Print and scan barcodes (BC23) and printing barcodes from production orders (BC26) work without customization
- A QR code can hold a Business Central page URL, for example a customer card link

## Context

- Features: One-dimensional barcodes in reports, Two-dimensional barcodes (Aztec, Data Matrix, Maxi Code, PDF417, QR-Code), Barcode scanning from AL, Swiss QR Code generation, External barcode services integration, Print barcodes from production orders, QR code page URL embedding
- Versions: BC18, BC19.1, BC23, BC26
