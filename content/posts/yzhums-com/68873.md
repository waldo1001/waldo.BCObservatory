# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27.1): Connect AI agents to Business Central through MCP server (Dynamics 365 Business Central MCP)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68873/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-11-10 · 729 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27.1) introduces the Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, enabling AI agents to connect to Business Central through a standardized API. This allows seamless integration with intelligent agents from platforms like Azure OpenAI and Copilot Studio, automating tasks and enabling conversational interaction with ERP data.

## Key points

- MCP is an open standard that lets AI agents discover and invoke operations on Business Central entities like customers, items, and sales orders in a plug-and-play way
- Business Central now has a new page to manage MCP APIs, supporting standard APIs and custom APIs with configurable permissions and dynamic tool mode
- Microsoft Copilot Studio includes a built-in Business Central MCP connector that allows agents to query customer lists and other data through standardized connections
- The feature enables automation of repetitive tasks, streamlines workflows, and provides real-time data synchronization between AI agents and Business Central

## Quotes

- "MCP standardizes how applications provide context to language models. This standardization helps applications integrate seamlessly with different data sources and tools." (Clarifies why MCP matters for achieving true AI-driven automation across business systems)
