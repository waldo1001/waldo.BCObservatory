# Cumulative Update Summary for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central (December, 2025)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/69394/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-12-15 · 786 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Roundup of the December 2025 cumulative updates for Business Central, listing build numbers for BC27 CU02 (27.2, SaaS and on-premises), BC26 CU08, BC25 CU14 and BC24 CU18. It also links release plan resources and reprints Modern and Fixed Lifecycle Policy support dates for versions back to BC13.

## Key points

- BC27 CU02 is version 27.2: SaaS uses platform 27.0.42930.0 and on-premises uses platform 27.0.42799.0, both with application 27.2.42879
- New customers start on BC27.2 automatically, while existing customers are notified by email when the update is available to them
- Other December releases are 26.8, 25.14 and 24.18, with BC24 now marked end of life
- Version 27.x is supported from Oct 1, 2025 to Apr 6, 2027 under the Modern Lifecycle Policy; BC14 under the Fixed Policy reached its extended end date on Oct 14, 2025
- A side note recalls the earlier BC25 change extending the major update window from 30 (60) days to five months and making minor updates optional

## Quotes

- "New customers will automatically sign up for Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27.2)." (Shows the default enrollment strategy for new organizations and the push toward latest versions)
- "Manage environment updates more flexibly - Major update: 30(60) days -> 5-month and Optional minor updates" (Indicates significant policy change giving customers more control over update timing and reduces forced update pressure)

## Context

- Features: Flexible environment update management, Optional minor updates, Extended update cycles
- Versions: 27.2, 27.x, 26.8, 25.14, 24.18, 2025 release wave 2, 2025 release wave 1, 2024 release wave 2
