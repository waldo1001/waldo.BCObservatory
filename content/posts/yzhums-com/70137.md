# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Adding comments to Factbox/Report (Displaying Comments in a FactBox/Report)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/70137/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-01-22 · 523 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Adding comments to FactBox and reports in Business Central lets users attach contextual information to sales orders and other documents. The post shows how to display sales comments in a ListPart page extension and in reports using report extensions with dataitem linking.

## Key points

- Comments in Business Central capture extra information on cards and documents, with a limit of 80 characters per comment field.
- Comments on documents automatically transfer to related posted documents, and transfer rules can be configured in Sales & Receivables settings.
- To display comments in a FactBox, create a ListPart page for Sales Comment Line and add it to the Sales Order page extension using SubPageLink.
- Report extensions can add sales comment lines directly as dataitems linked to the header by Document Type and No., enabling comment display in printed reports.

## Quotes

- "Comments on ongoing documents are transferred to the related posted document." (This shows how Business Central automatically maintains comment information throughout the document lifecycle, reducing manual re-entry work.)
- "The Comment field has a length limit of 80 characters, Comment (6, Text[80])." (Understanding this constraint is critical for developers designing comment capture interfaces and validating user input.)
