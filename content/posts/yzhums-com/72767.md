- Updates 27.7 (2025 wave 2) and 26.13 (2025 wave 1) also released

- On-premises Modern Policy end dates: v28 Oct 2027, v27 Apr 2027, v26 Oct 2026
- Versions 15 to 25 and 13 are End of Life; version 14 (Fixed Policy) is past mainstream support, with extended support ending October 2025
