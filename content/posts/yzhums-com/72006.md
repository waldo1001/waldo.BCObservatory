# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Experience improved usability in manufacturing

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/72006/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-04-08 · 749 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> BC28 (2026 wave 1) improves manufacturing usability by enhancing serial-tracked item handling in output journals, ensuring capacity and overhead costs post to location-specific WIP accounts, and adding customizable notifications for non-certified BOMs and routings.

## Key points

- Explode Routing now respects item tracking, splitting serial-tracked items into separate journal lines with quantity 1 per serial number
- Capacity and overhead postings now use location codes from production orders, eliminating WIP account imbalances
- New My Notifications setting lets users control warnings for non-certified production BOMs and routings
- Location-specific inventory posting setup no longer required for overhead postings on blank locations

## Quotes

- "For serial-tracked items, Business Central splits the last routing operation into multiple lines with quantity 1 per serial number." (This ensures accurate production output recording for tracked items without manual workarounds.)
- "Business Central uses the production location when posting capacity and overhead." (This prevents WIP account imbalances when material and costs are posted to different locations.)
- "Now, Microsoft has added a new My Notifications setting so you can control whether to display warnings for non-certified production BOMs and routings." (Users can reduce notification noise while maintaining oversight of non-certified structures.)
