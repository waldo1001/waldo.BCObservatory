# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28.1): Enable Investment Boost for fixed assets (Bonus Depreciation Method)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/73564/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-27 · 613 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces Investment Boost for fixed assets, allowing eligible assets to deduct a configured percentage in the acquisition year while depreciating the remaining balance through standard methods. This feature reduces first-year tax burden and streamlines compliance with front-loaded investment incentive schemes like New Zealand's rules.

## Key points

- Investment Boost lets companies deduct a set percentage (e.g., 20-60%) of an asset's cost in the purchase year, then depreciate the remainder normally
- Administrators configure the bonus depreciation percentage and effective date in Fixed Asset Setup
- Individual assets can be marked with 'Use Bonus Depreciation' flag in the depreciation book to enable the incentive
- System maintains a clear audit trail by posting the boost deduction and adjusted depreciation as separate entries
- Validation prevents double-application and ensures the asset's availability date meets the effective-date rule
