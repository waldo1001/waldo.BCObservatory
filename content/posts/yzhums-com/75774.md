# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29): Show recently searched in Tell Me

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/75774/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-09-04 · 264 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 release wave 2 adds recently searched items to the Tell Me search feature (Alt+Q), allowing users to quickly access their three most recent searches without entering search terms. This speeds up finding pages, reports, and documentation.

## Key points

- Tell Me now displays the three most recent searches in a Recents section when no search terms are entered
- The feature works in both regular Tell Me and Advanced Tell Me search
- Recent searches persist across browser sessions but are cleared in incognito or private browsing modes after logout
- Users can still search for pages, reports, data, and Business Central documentation as before

## Quotes

- "Find pages and reports faster by accessing recent searches in Tell Me (Alt+Q)." (Explains the core benefit of the feature for improved user productivity)
- "When you don't enter any search terms, the Recents section displays your three most recent searches." (Clarifies exactly how and when the feature appears to users)
