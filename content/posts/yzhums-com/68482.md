# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to hide the Sales Order Agent and Payables Agent icons in the upper right corner

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68482/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-10-22 · 329 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> The Sales Order Agent and Payables Agent icons appear by default in Business Central's upper right corner. You can hide them by deactivating individual capabilities on the Copilot & agent capabilities page, then signing out and back in.

## Key points

- Sales Order Agent automates sales order capturing in Business Central
- Payables Agent processes vendor invoices end-to-end to reduce accounting bottlenecks
- Both agent icons display on the role center and embedded pages by default
- Deactivate agents through the Copilot & agent capabilities page
- Sign out and back in to make the icon visibility changes take effect
