# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Regarding the cost of the Copilot/Agent features (Manage consumption-based billing for agent capabilities)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68484/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-10-21 · 1029 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Copilot features in Business Central are included with the license at no extra cost, but new agent capabilities like Sales Order Agent and Payables Agent use consumption-based billing measured in Copilot Credits. Organizations can pay per credit consumed or purchase prepaid capacity through Microsoft Copilot Studio.

## Key points

- Copilot is included with the Business Central license at no extra cost, though fair-use policies or pricing may come later
- Sales Order Agent and Payables Agent are billed by consumption in Copilot Credits; pay-as-you-go costs $0.01 per credit
- A typical Sales Order Agent flow uses 14 credits, and the example of 100 requests per month comes to 1,650 credits (about $16.50)
- Payables Agent uses 50 credits per invoice plus 5 per invoice line
- Usage can be checked in the Power Platform admin center; Business Central licenses are not documented as including credits

## Quotes

- "Copilot in Business Central is included with your Business Central license at no extra cost." (Clarifies that core Copilot functionality is free, helping organizations understand baseline costs)
- "Selected agent capabilities in Business Central use consumption-based billing, charging per use." (Establishes that new agent features have variable costs based on actual usage, not fixed licensing)
