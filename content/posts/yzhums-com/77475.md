[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/77475/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-11-17 · 274 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central can automatically populate the Company Information page by pulling organization details from Microsoft 365, saving time during initial company setup. This feature uses the Assisted Setup wizard to retrieve billing account information already maintained in Microsoft 365.

## Key points

- Company details can be auto-filled from Microsoft 365 billing account information through Assisted Setup
- Users must verify the automatically populated data before proceeding
- Additional company information like logo and bank details can be manually added after the initial setup
- Business Central companies typically reside in the same Microsoft 365 tenant
