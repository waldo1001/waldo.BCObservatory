# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Enter Company Details quickly using information from Microsoft 365

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/77475/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-11-17 · 274 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central can automatically populate the Company Information page by pulling organization details from Microsoft 365, saving time during initial company setup. This feature uses the Assisted Setup wizard to retrieve billing account information already maintained in Microsoft 365.

## Key points

- Start from Settings, Assisted setup, Enter company details. You must allow a request to an external service before the Company Setup wizard opens.
- The wizard fills in basic company data taken from the billing account details in the Microsoft 365 admin center, and the user should check it before continuing.
- Later steps of the same wizard let you add a company logo, contact details and bank information.
- The Company Information data is used on documents such as invoice headers, and Business Central usually sits in the same tenant as Microsoft 365.
