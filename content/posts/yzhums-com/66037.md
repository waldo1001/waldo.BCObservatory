# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Can we edit data directly in FactBox?

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/66037/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-07-29 · 915 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> FactBoxes cannot be directly edited as they are display-only areas, but the post demonstrates three workaround approaches: importing external files, opening dedicated edit pages through actions or triggers, or modifying data through field triggers like OnAssistEdit.

## Key points

- FactBoxes are read-only display areas on the right side of pages that show related information
- Direct editing of FactBox content is not possible through customization, but workarounds exist
- Data can be indirectly modified by importing files, launching edit pages via actions, or using field triggers with OnAssistEdit
- The OnAssistEdit trigger allows opening a full page editor when clicking a field in a FactBox
- Field triggers can perform calculations or modifications without opening a separate page
