# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Calculate excise taxes

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/73513/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-26 · 659 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces automated excise tax calculation for products and services across different regions. The feature provides a flexible framework supporting various tax bases like weight, volume, and alcohol content, helping businesses comply with tax regulations and reduce calculation errors.

## Key points

- Excise taxes automate complex calculations for specific goods and services based on regional regulations
- Configuration requires setting up excise tax types with tax basis options including weight, volume, quantity, sugar content, and alcohol volume
- Excise entry types must be configured to specify which item ledger entry types contribute to taxable quantities
- Items and fixed assets are linked to excise tax types through the Item Card with unit of measure and quantity specifications
- Excise Journal automatically generates tax entries based on item ledger entries and calculates amounts using configured duty rates

## Quotes

- "The excise taxes feature helps businesses by automating the complex process of calculating excise tax amounts." (It shows the core business value of reducing manual work and errors in tax calculation)

- "Excise Tax Journal entries will be automatically generated based on Item Ledger Entry quantities for the specified date range." (It describes the key automation mechanism that processes tax entries without manual calculation)
