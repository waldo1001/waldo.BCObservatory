# Dynamics 365 Business Central: “Hide Company Bank Account” setup in General Ledger Setup (Update Company Bank Account on posted documents)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/64609/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-06-05 · 340 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2025 wave 1 introduces a Hide Company Bank Account setting in General Ledger Setup that controls whether the Company Bank Account Code field appears when users update posted sales documents. This setting helps manage which editable fields are available on the update pages for posted documents.

## Key points

- New setup field added in BC26 controls visibility of Company Bank Account on posted document updates
- When enabled, hides the Company Bank Account Code field from the Posted Sales Inv. - Update page
- Allows selective field editing on posted documents when information needs correction after posting
- Only applies to Posted Sales Invoice updates; not used for service invoices or purchase documents

- "Specifies whether or not the Company Bank Account can be updated on posted documents." (This is the exact definition of the new setting, clarifying its purpose in controlling field access on posted document updates.)
