# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29): Financial report changes are now always logged

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/76076/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-09-15 · 403 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Financial report changes in Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29) are now automatically logged in the change log without requiring manual setup, making it easier to audit modifications to financial row, column, and report definitions.

## Key points

- Changes to financial row, column, and report definitions are automatically recorded in the change log
- No manual activation of the change log is required for financial reports
- Users can track who modified what in financial reports and when, helping identify accidental changes
- The change log entries are chronologically ordered and show all direct modifications to tracked data
