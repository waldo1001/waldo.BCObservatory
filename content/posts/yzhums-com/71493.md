# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Enhanced demo data for sales, purchasing, and fixed assets analytics

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71493/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-17 · 439 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 (BC28) enhances demo data for sales, purchasing, and fixed assets modules to improve reporting and analytics demonstrations. The enhancements include more salespeople, open orders, backorders, deferral codes, discounts, purchasing dimensions, and fixed asset dimensions.

## Key points

- Sales demo data now includes distributed salespeople, expanded open orders and backorders, spread-out due dates, deferral codes, and line discounts
- Purchasing enhancements add a new purchasing department dimension and purchaser/return reason information to both purchase documents and analytics
- Fixed assets demo data now includes dimension setup and posted entries for better demonstration of fixed asset analytics

## Quotes

- "In Business Central 2024 wave 2 (BC25.3), the method of creating demo/sample data using Configuration packages was deprecated." (Important context that the old Configuration package method for demo data creation is no longer supported.)
