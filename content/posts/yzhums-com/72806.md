[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/72806/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-12 · 214 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 adds approval workflows for item journals and requisition worksheets, but these templates are missing from upgraded environments. Users must reset Microsoft templates via Tell Me to access the new workflow features.

## Key points

- Item journals and requisition worksheets now support approval workflows in BC28
- New workflow templates appear in fresh installations but are absent in upgraded environments
- Templates can be restored by selecting Tell Me, searching Workflow Templates, and choosing Reset Microsoft Templates
- This manual reset is currently the only way to access the new functionality in upgraded systems
