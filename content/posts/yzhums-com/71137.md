[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71137/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-05 · 389 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 release wave 1 introduces the ability to send posted sales shipments and return receipts by email, complementing the existing Print option. The update also adds a standard Document Attachment Factbox to these pages for storing and managing printed document versions.

## Key points

- Posted sales shipments and return receipts can now be sent directly by email from Business Central, aligning with the experience of posted invoices and credit memos
- The Send option includes multiple choices: Send by Email, standard sending options, or Attach as PDF
- Document Attachment Factbox has been added to Posted Sales Shipment, Posted Sales Shipments, Posted Return Receipt, and Posted Return Receipts pages for managing document versions

## Quotes

- "The Attachment part allows you to store and manage printed document versions for future reference, and is now also available on the following pages" (Clarifies that the Document Attachment Factbox extends document management capabilities to additional posted document pages)
