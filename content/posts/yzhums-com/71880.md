> Dynamics 365 Business Central 2026 wave 1 (BC28) is now generally available with version 28.0, featuring production and sandbox environment management improvements in the Admin Center. New customers automatically receive BC28, while existing customers will be notified when updates are available.

## Key points

- BC28 (2026 wave 1) is generally available with platform 28.0.48222.0 and application 28.0.46665.48257
- New customers automatically sign up for BC28; existing customers receive email notifications for updates
- Production and sandbox environment management capabilities added to Admin Center
- BC25 (2024 wave 2) update cycle changed to 5-month major updates and optional minor updates
- On-premises version 28.0 downloads delayed but coming shortly
