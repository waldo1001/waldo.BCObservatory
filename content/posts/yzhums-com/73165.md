# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28.1): Expense Management module (Manage employee expenses)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/73165/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-22 · 5454 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces a new native Expense Management module that enables employees to create, submit, and track expense reports with support for receipts, itemization, mileage, and per diem calculations. Administrators configure expense categories, payment methods, rules, locations, and approvers to manage employee reimbursements directly within the system without external integrations.

## Key points

- Expense Management is a new native BC28.1 module for capturing, approving, posting and reimbursing employee expenses without external tools
- Public preview is English and US only at first, with Australia, New Zealand and the UK following in July 2026
- Expense Agent Setup acts as the global configuration, covering rules, number series, approval workflow and mileage and per diem allowance settings
- Admins define payment methods, categories and subcategories, expense locations, rules, expense users linked to employees, and approvers
- Expenses are released, grouped into expense reports, optionally approved, then posted; the author tested with the Expense Agent disabled

## Quotes

- "With native expense reports, you can capture, approve, and reimburse employee expenses directly within Business Central." (Demonstrates the core value proposition of eliminating external solutions for expense management and enabling seamless integration with finance operations)
