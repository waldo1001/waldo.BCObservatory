# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Include approvals for requisition worksheets and item journals in your workflows (New standard workflows)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71039/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-06 · 469 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28) extends approval workflows to item journals and requisition/planning worksheets, allowing batch-level approval reviews before posting or converting documents. This prevents unauthorized modifications during pending approvals and works with Power Automate.

## Key points

- Item journals, physical inventory, output, and consumption journals now support approval workflows similar to general journal batches
- Requisition and planning worksheets can be sent for batch-level approval before converting lines to purchase documents or executing planning
- Active approval requests lock records against editing, deletion, or posting until approval is completed or canceled
- Line-level approvals are not yet supported; approvals only work at the batch level for worksheets

## Quotes

- "When an approval request is created for a worksheet batch, you can't insert, modify, or delete requisition or planning lines." (Demonstrates how approvals prevent data changes during pending decisions, protecting planning integrity)
- "Microsoft Power Automate support is available through updated workflow events and responses." (Enables integration with cloud-based automation tools beyond Business Central's standard workflows)
