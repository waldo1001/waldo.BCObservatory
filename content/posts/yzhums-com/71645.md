# Dynamics 365 Business Central: How to copy (synchronize) the Incoming Document to all Gen. Journal Lines

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71645/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-07 · 461 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> When assigning incoming documents to general journal lines in Business Central, the incoming document attachment only applies to one line at a time. This post explains the standard limitation and demonstrates a page extension customization that adds a synchronization action to copy an incoming document entry to all other journal lines in the same batch.

## Key points

- Incoming documents in BC can be attached to general journal lines but are limited to a single line by default.
- Standard BC does not allow the same incoming document to be assigned to multiple journal lines simultaneously.
- A custom page extension on the General Journal page can copy the incoming document entry number to other lines via a new Synchronize action.
- The customization uses ModifyAll to bulk-update the Incoming Document Entry No. field across multiple journal lines in one batch.
- Incoming documents attached to journal lines persist and display after the journals are posted.

## Quotes

- "Of course, these Incoming Documents will also be displayed after the Genral Journals are posted." (Confirms that attached incoming documents persist through to posted documents, providing an audit trail.)
