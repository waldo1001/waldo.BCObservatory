# Business Central 2025 wave 1 (BC26.2): Use enhanced analytics for manufacturing (updated Power BI app)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/65052/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-06-11 · 1554 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2025 wave 1 (BC26.2) includes significantly improved Power BI analytics for manufacturing, with new and updated reports for work centers, machine centers, production orders, and scrap tracking. This helps manufacturers track KPIs, analyze costs and variances, visualize production schedules, and optimize resource allocation.

## Key points

- New manufacturing Power BI reports cover work center statistics, machine center statistics, production order lists, routing Gantt charts, WIP valuation, and production overview dashboards
- Existing reports received visual layout updates including new matrices, charts, and drill-down capabilities for detailed analysis by work center, production order, and source number
- Reports highlight key metrics like actual costs, variances, scrap percentages, capacity utilization, and efficiency to support manufacturing planning and control
- Power BI provides flexible data visualization alternative to Business Central reports, allowing customization and cross-company dashboards accessible from Power BI website or embedded in Business Central
