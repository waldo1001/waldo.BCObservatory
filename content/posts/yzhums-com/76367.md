# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29): Reduce complexity of report datasets (Company information dataitem in Word layouts)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/76367/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-09-29 · 251 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Word report layouts in Business Central 2026 release wave 2 now include a shared Company Information dataitem across all report datasets, reducing developer effort and standardizing how company information is accessed in Word layouts.

## Key points

- Company Information dataitem is automatically included in all Word report layouts without requiring developers to define it
- The CompanyMetadata dataitem is accessed through BCReportInformation and contains Company Information details
- This feature eliminates tedious manual work for AL developers creating report datasets
- Continues functionality from BC26 that allows accessing report and request metadata in Word layouts
