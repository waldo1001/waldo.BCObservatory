# Business Central 2026 release wave 2 (BC29): Reduce complexity of report datasets (Company information dataitem in Word layouts)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/76367/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-09-29 · 251 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Word report layouts in Business Central 2026 release wave 2 now include a shared Company Information dataitem across all report datasets, reducing developer effort and standardizing how company information is accessed in Word layouts.

## Key points

- Word layouts in BC29 automatically get a Company Information dataitem shared by all report datasets, with no need for the developer to define it
- The dataitem shows up as CompanyMetadata under BCReportInformation and holds the Company Information details
- It is there for custom reports and for standard reports, even when the layout is completely empty
- It reduces report developer effort and standardizes company information use across layouts
- Builds on BC26, which first exposed report and request metadata in Word layouts

## Quotes

- "Word layouts now include a company information dataitem that is shared across all report datasets." (This is the core feature - it standardizes company information access and reduces complexity for report developers)
