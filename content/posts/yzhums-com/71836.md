# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Create contract service orders with One Service Item Line/Order setting

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71836/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-31 · 438 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The One Service Item Line/Order setting on the Service Management Setup page now applies when creating contract service orders in bulk, enforcing that each service order contains only one service item line. This ensures consistency with service management policies by allowing batch processing of service contracts without manual intervention.

## Key points

- Service Management Setup has a One Service Item Line/Order setting that limits each service order to one service item line
- The Create Contract Service Orders batch job now follows this setting
- With the setting on, a contract with several service items produces one service order per item
- With the setting off, a single service order holds all of the contract's service items
- The author found the option already available in BC27.5

## Quotes

- "The One Service Item Line/Order field enforces a business rule that restricts service orders to contain only one service item line per order." (This explains the core functionality and how the setting controls service order structure)
- "You can run the batch process without manual intervention or workarounds, which helps ensure consistency with your service management policies." (Highlights the business value of automated enforcement in bulk operations)
- "If the One Service Item Line/Order is enabled, the batch process creates one service order per service item in the contract." (Clarifies the exact behavior when the setting is enabled)
