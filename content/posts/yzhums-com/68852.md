# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Is it possible to change the Variant Code for an item that has already been posted?

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/68852/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-07-08 · 399 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Changing a variant code for an already-posted item in Business Central requires workarounds since item reclassification journals don't support variant changes. Two feasible solutions are using Item Journals for negative and positive adjustments, or using Assembly Orders to transfer quantities between variants.

## Key points

- Item variants help manage similar products with different attributes like color without creating separate items
- Item Reclassification Journals cannot change variant codes because variants are treated as distinct items in the system
- Use Item Journals with negative adjustments on the original variant and positive adjustments on the target variant
- Assembly Orders can also transfer inventory between variants by specifying the source variant in lines and target variant in posting details

## Quotes

- "In Business Central, item variants are a great way to keep your list of products under control in Business Central." (Explains the primary benefit of using variants to organize similar items with different attributes)
- "Theoretically, different variants are treated as distinct items in the system." (Clarifies why standard reclassification journals cannot change variant codes directly)
