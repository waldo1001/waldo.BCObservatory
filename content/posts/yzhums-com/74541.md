# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Upload, install, and manage PTEs in the admin center (Remove ability to upload and install PTEs from Extension Management page)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/74541/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-08-20 · 890 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> From Business Central 2026 wave 1, admins can upload, install, schedule and uninstall per-tenant extensions in the Admin Center or through Admin Center API 2.29. Uploading PTEs from the Extension Management page is deprecated and is expected to end in 2027 wave 1 (version 30.0). That page will still list installed extensions. On-premises admins should use the Apps Management PowerShell module.

## Key points

- Administrators can now upload and manage PTEs directly from the Business Central Admin Center or via Admin Center API version 2.29
- PTE management through the Extension Management page is being deprecated and will be removed in 2027 wave 1
- Scheduled installations from the Admin Center and Extension Management page are not synchronized and only visible in the experience where they were created
- Partners and customers should migrate deployment workflows and automation to the Admin Center or its API before the April 2027 deadline

## Quotes

- "Administrators can upload new PTEs, schedule their installation or update, and manage these operations directly through the Admin Center." (This explains the core new capability that replaces the previous Extension Management page workflow)
- "Scheduled PTE installations and updates are only visible in the experience where they were created, either the Admin Center or Extension Management." (This highlights a critical limitation during the transition period that administrators need to understand)
