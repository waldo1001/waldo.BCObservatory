[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/74858/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-11-19 · 632 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Worksheet pages in Business Central can be extended to dynamically hide and show headers and footers, allowing users to view more journal lines. The post demonstrates using Boolean variables and page actions to toggle header and footer visibility, similar to how the Account Schedule page works.

## Key points

- Worksheet pages display fewer lines than List pages because headers and footers consume space
- Boolean variables control the visibility state of header and footer groups dynamically
- Page actions with Visible properties allow users to toggle between Hide/Show states
- The Account Schedule page serves as a standard reference implementation for this pattern
