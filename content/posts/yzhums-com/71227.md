It includes version numbers, support timelines, and links to detailed release notes.

## Key points

- BC28 (2026 release wave 1) is available as public preview for testing and will not support production migration
- BC27.5 is current production version for 2025 wave 2, with BC26.11 available for 2025 wave 1 customers
- Support dates follow Modern Lifecycle Policy for on-premises with version 27.x supported until April 2027
- Preview environments may be updated or deleted without notice and cannot be upgraded to production

## Quotes

- "Sandboxes on a preview version are intended only for testing and review of features coming in the next release." (Clarifies that preview environments are restricted to testing and cannot become production environments)
- "The environment cannot be updated to a production version. It must remain in preview until it is deleted." (Critical limitation for organizations planning preview adoption, preventing migration paths to production)
