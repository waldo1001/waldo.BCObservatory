# Dynamics 365 Business Central: Open the same list in a new tab when switching companies

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/72442/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-07-16 · 464 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> The company switcher feature in Business Central allows users to quickly switch between companies and open the same list page in a new tab while keeping the original company context. This is useful when working across multiple companies and environments to compare data.

## Key points

- The company switcher pane is accessible from the upper-right corner near the search icon or via Ctrl+O keyboard shortcut
- Three switching options are available: Switch (same tab), Open in new tab (keeps original open), and Open in new tab and go to same page (list pages only)
- The 'Open in new tab and go to same page' option preserves your current page context when viewing the equivalent list in another company
- Company switcher provides an overview of all companies across all environments you have access to
