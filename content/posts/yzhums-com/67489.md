# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): Add more fields and columns by personalizing pages

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/67489/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-09-10 · 745 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2025 wave 2 adds more fields and columns available for personalization on various pages, including Statistics Group, Allow Multiple Posting Groups, Price Calculation Method, and alternate address fields on sales, purchase, and service documents.

## Key points

- Users can now personalize more pages by selecting from expanded field lists to display information relevant to their workflows
- New fields include Statistics Group on card pages, Allow Multiple Posting Groups and Price Calculation Method on Customer Template, and alternate address fields on sales and purchase documents
- Alternate address fields appear only when documents are configured to use alternate or custom addresses instead of defaults
- This enhancement continues Microsoft's ongoing effort to add personalization options based on community feedback across major updates
