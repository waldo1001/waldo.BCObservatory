# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): Update sandbox environments to preview versions

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/67592/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-09-10 · 567 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Sandbox environments can now be updated to the public preview of the next major version, so customers can test with their own data and extensions. This is a 2025 wave 1 feature that arrives in September with the BC27 preview. The environment must be on the previous major version, only sandboxes are supported, and preview environments are deleted automatically when the preview period ends.

## Key points

- Sandbox environments can be upgraded to preview versions starting one month before major releases
- Preview testing uses actual customer data and installed extensions for more accurate results
- Preview environments are automatically deleted when the preview period ends
- Only sandbox environments support preview versions; production environments cannot upgrade to previews
- Prerequisites require the environment to be on the previous major version (e.g., v26.x to upgrade to v27.0 Preview)

## Quotes

- "You can update your sandbox environments to a preview version. You can test updates with your own data and installed extensions." (Explains the core capability and its value for realistic testing before production)
