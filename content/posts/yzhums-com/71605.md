# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Post purchase invoices for drop shipments independently of related sales invoices

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/71605/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-03-19 · 664 words · tier community · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

> Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28) allows you to post purchase invoices for drop shipments independently of related sales invoices. Previously, you could not invoice a drop shipment purchase order until the associated sales order had been invoiced. This change simplifies the workflow for drop shipment scenarios.

## Key points

- Drop shipment purchase invoices can now be posted before the related sales order invoices are posted
- You can post the invoice directly from a purchase order or by using the Get receipt lines action
- This change removes certain posting logic checks that previously blocked purchase invoice posting for drop shipments
- Customizations relying on removed events (OnCheckAssocOrderLinesOnBeforeCheckOrderLine, OnBeforeCheckAssociatedSalesOrderLine, OnBeforeVerifyInvoicedQty, OnVerifyInvoicedQtyOnAfterGetSalesShipmentHeader) must be reviewed and adjusted

## Quotes

- "You can post invoices for drop shipment purchase orders, even if you haven't invoiced the related sales order." (This is the core new capability that improves workflow flexibility for drop shipment scenarios)
