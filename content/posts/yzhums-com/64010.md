# Business Central 2025 wave 2 (BC27): New Business Central Themes for Visual Studio Code (Light or Dark)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/64010/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2025-05-12 · 242 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2025 wave 2 introduces two new Visual Studio Code themes (Business Central Light and Business Central Dark) featuring the teal brand color. These themes are included in the AL extension package and can be installed from the pre-release version.

## Key points

- The AL changelog 16.0 adds two VS Code themes, Business Central Light and Business Central Dark, both built around teal
- The themes ship in the AL extension .vsix package
- Install the v16.0 pre-release of the AL extension (pre-release installs have been possible since BC22), then use the new Set Color Theme button
- Themes can also be switched with the standard VS Code command, and colors can be customized further

- "We are excited to introduce two new themes: Business Central Light and Business Central Dark." (Announces the core feature being released with the new themes)
- "These themes prominently feature our favorite color; teal, and are included in the .vsix package." (Explains how themes are delivered and their visual design focus)
