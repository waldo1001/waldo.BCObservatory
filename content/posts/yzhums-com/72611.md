# Business Central 2026 release wave 1 (BC28): Copy contacts from BC to Microsoft 365 (Outlook & Teams)

[Read the post](https://yzhums.com/72611/) · Dynamics 365 Lab - Yun Zhu (Yun Zhu, MVP) · 2026-05-18 · 697 words · tier community · reviewed (checked by Opus)

> Business Central 2026 wave 1 introduces improved contact synchronization that lets users copy contacts from BC to Microsoft 365 Outlook and Teams with a new Synchronize with Outlook action, including enhanced security for Outlook add-ins and deprecation of Exchange Synch setup pages.

## Key points

- New Synchronize with Outlook action on the Contacts list enables copying contacts to Outlook and Teams with filtering and direction control options
- Enhanced security requires extra one-time setup when installing Outlook add-ins; administrators can still use centralized deployment
- Force full sync option available to overcome synchronization issues by synchronizing contacts regardless of last modification date
- Users must upgrade to BC version 28.0 or later by October 1, 2026, as Exchange Web Services discontinuation will break these features in earlier versions

## Quotes

- "You can easily author mails or schedule meetings with business contacts from Outlook and Teams." (Demonstrates the workflow benefit of contact synchronization for users working in email and collaboration tools)

## Heads up

- warning: "we're deprecating the Exchange Synch setup pages that are no longer needed for connecting Business Central with Outlook and Exchange." (about version BC28)

## Context

- Features: Outlook contact synchronization, Outlook add-in, Teams integration, Centralized deployment, Force full sync
- Versions: 28.0, 2026 wave 1, BC28
