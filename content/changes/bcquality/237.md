# #237 knowledge(data-modeling): keep business state independent of Job Queue lifetime

> A new knowledge article explains how to preserve business state independently of Job Queue lifetime by storing it on business-owned records rather than relying on Job Queue Entry existence as a status indicator. The article includes good and bad code examples and adds discovery logic to the data-modeling review skill.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCQuality/pull/237) · merged into `main` on 2026-10-20 by RaimondStokkel · 5 files (+190 -4), 4 AL · feature · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Deleting a Job Queue Entry can lose business state when the entry is the only evidence of an unresolved condition
- State that must survive cleanup belongs on the business owner or a domain record with the right lifetime
- Job Queue Entry should run the work and reference the owner record
- Data-modeling review gains discovery cues for existence reads and cleanup paths
- A finding needs an identified business consumer and a deletion path that changes its result; a FlowField or table extension name alone is not enough
