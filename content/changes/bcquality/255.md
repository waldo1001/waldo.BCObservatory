# #255 Correct CurrReport.Skip guidance and retire false fixture expectations

> Corrects BCQuality reporting guidance that wrongly assumed statements after CurrReport.Skip() keep running in the same OnAfterGetRecord trigger. The Skip article was replaced under a new name, its false sample pair and fixture registration were retired, and the reporting review skill no longer flags that cue.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCQuality/pull/255) · merged into `main` on 2026-11-04 by JesperSchulz · 8 files (+55 -91), 6 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Corrected the false claim that code after CurrReport.Skip() keeps running in the same trigger
- Replaced the Skip article with one that separates same-trigger exit from later trigger dispatch, next-record processing, child traversal and dataset omission
- Retired the false bad/good sample pair and its evaluation fixture registration
- The reporting review skill drops the same-trigger-after-Skip cue but keeps the later-trigger concern
- The Break article wording was refined; its samples are unchanged
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
