# #146 knowledge: three false-positive guards from BCApps PR 10277, 10278 and 10346

> Quality rules were refined to eliminate false positives about missing cleanups in insert-only transfers, mandatory affixes in non-AppSource apps, and permission context lowering in tests. Three knowledge articles were added or updated with better preconditions to keep genuine defects reportable.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCQuality/pull/146) · merged into `main` on 2026-09-02 by gggdttt · 3 files (+34 -6), 3 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- New article clarifies that insert-only transfers are safe when the caller's OnValidate trigger cleans up the target range first
- AppSource affix requirement now correctly scoped to apps with AppSourceCop enabled and configured with mandatory affix
- Permission test guidance refined to recognize effective permission context through role composition, not just direct permission set assignment
- All three false positives were verified against actual BCApps code before writing the rules
- Genuine defects remain reportable: missing cleanup with different filters or reachable paths without cleanup
