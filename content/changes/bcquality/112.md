# #112 Add precision guards for systematic agent false-positive patterns

> Quality rules now include precision guards to prevent the agent from emitting documented false-positive patterns, such as missing tooltips on bound page fields, misunderstanding of Boolean page trigger defaults, and incorrectly flagging unfiltered drilldowns.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCQuality/pull/112) · merged into `main` on 2026-07-17 by gggdttt · 6 files (+140 -0), 6 AL · refactor · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Added precision guards to suppress four recurring false-positive findings that BC reviewers confirmed: missing tooltips on bound fields (inherit from table), Boolean page triggers defaulting to true, effective filters outside the diff, and CalcFields in different lifecycle triggers
- Breaking-change and upgrade reviews now require that affected symbols actually shipped in a released version, preventing false positives for new or unreleased-cycle apps
- Guards are narrowly scoped and documented with developer confirmation from evaluation feedback
- Direction is strictly conservative without suppressing genuine knowledge-backed findings
- Two BC developers confirmed the page-trigger default-return semantics
