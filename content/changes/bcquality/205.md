# #205 Preserve business filters when recommending Record.Get

> The performance guidance for replacing FindFirst with Record.Get now clarifies that additional business filters must be preserved, either by keeping the filtered FindFirst or by enforcing equivalent conditions after Get.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCQuality/pull/205) · merged into `main` on 2026-10-02 by demiliani · 4 files (+44 -5), 3 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Prevents false-positive recommendations when lookups combine full primary-key filters with other business conditions
- Documents that Record.Get ignores normal record filters, only respecting security filters
- Recommends two valid alternatives: retain filtered FindFirst or explicitly check business conditions after Get
- Extends examples to show both the problematic pattern and correct solutions
- Ensures performance recommendations do not introduce behavior-changing corrections
