> Added six new performance guidance articles with code examples and refined existing guidance on indexing, buffered inserts, aggregate processing, and FlowField caching based on the OptimAL Performance Initiative.

## What changed

- Six new knowledge articles covering workload-based indexes, overlapping indexes, buffered inserts, aggregate-before-write processing, scoped result caching, and repeated validation avoidance
- Each new article includes paired good and bad AL code examples demonstrating best practices
- Refinements to existing performance guidance on partial loads, temporary buffers, bulk updates, existence checks, primary-key caching, and SIFT-specific SetCurrentKey behavior
- Performance-review skill extended to evaluate against the new rules
- All examples are generalized from OptimAL learnings without partner or customer-specific information
