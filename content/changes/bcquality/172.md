# #172 Support standalone runners and complete app-folder reviews

> AL review skills now support standalone runners and folder-based app reviews by decoupling scheduling concerns and enabling parallel execution of independent review domains while maintaining deterministic result ordering.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCQuality/pull/172) · merged into `main` on 2026-09-09 by JesperSchulz · 25 files (+229 -51), 21 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Scheduling becomes an orchestrator responsibility, allowing isolated review leaves to run serially or concurrently
- Folder-path is defined as a complete review scope across standalone adapter and all AL review leaves
- Severity counts now calculate from deduplicated findings instead of summing leaf results
- Documentation reorganized with standalone-runner guide, walk-up app review flow, and conceptual architecture moved to docs/
- Sub-results ordering remains deterministic regardless of completion order
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
