# #131 Correct table-level data classification guidance

> Privacy guidance now states that a valid table-level DataClassification is inherited by fields unless they override it. The fixture and related PII article were updated to separate inherited defaults from genuinely unclassified fields, which should stop false-positive review comments asking for redundant field-level declarations.

## What changed

- Fields inherit a valid table-level DataClassification unless they override it
- Companion .good.al fixture and PII article updated to distinguish inherited defaults from unclassified fields
- Old guidance caused 19 false-positive privacy comments on a BCApps PR
- Findings are kept for fields whose inherited classification is missing or wrong
