# #13917 [Main][ALL-E] Posting a Sales Credit Memo with a different Unit of Measure does not convert the quantity before updating the originating Sales Order (Blanket Sales Order is updated correctly)

> Fixed a bug where posting a sales credit memo with a different unit of measure failed to convert the quantity before updating the originating sales order, causing incorrect deductions.

## What changed

- Credit memo quantities are now converted to the sales order line's unit of measure before updating shipped and invoiced quantities
- Previously, 8 PCS was incorrectly deducted as 8 SET instead of 2 SET when units differed
- Regression tests added for different, fractional, and matching units of measure scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
