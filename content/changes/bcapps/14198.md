# #14198 [SLS] Update accuracy prompts for GPT-5.6 Ceres migration

> Sales Lines Suggestions prompts and accuracy evaluations are updated for GPT-5.6 Ceres migration, with six task and tool prompts now packaged as app resources and semantic judgment replacing keyword matching for product meaning detection.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/14198) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-18 by qasimikram · 22 files (+743 -179), 3 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Six normal prompts packaged as app resources via NavApp.GetResourceAsText instead of Key Vault secrets
- Safety and meta prompts remain in Key Vault for central management
- Semantic evaluation with GPT-5.6 Ceres judge replaces keyword/feature matching for product meaning
- Whole-message fallback policy: mixed unsupported and supported requests route only to magic_function
- Quantities preserved including zero and fractions, datasets updated for routing, extraction and confidence checks
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
