# #9656 [main] - Incorrect Prepayment Invoice Amount for Sales Orders with Invoice Discount, Prices Including VAT and negative non-inventory line.

> Fixes incorrect prepayment invoice amounts for sales orders with invoice discounts, prices including VAT, and negative non-inventory lines by computing expected prepayment amounts line-by-line instead of from the order header.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9656) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-11 by v-ankitgoyal · 12 files (+745 -23), 9 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Prepayment calculation now uses each line's Amount and Prepayment % instead of header-level amounts
- Excludes negative and non-prepayment lines from prepayment base calculation
- Handles mixed prepayment percentages correctly across order lines
- Rounding correction only applies when there is genuine rounding surplus
- Fixes regression where posted prepayment invoices were understated
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
