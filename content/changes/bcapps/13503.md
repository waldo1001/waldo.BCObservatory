# #13503 [Main]-Prepayment amounts are incorrectly recalculated in statistics after partial posting when Prepmt. Amt. to Deduct Excl. VAT is manually adjusted

> Fixed the Sales Line prepayment recalculation so Prepmt. Line Amount no longer grows past the invoiced prepayment after a partial posting where Prepmt Amt to Deduct was raised manually. Statistics now show stable values, and the remaining quantity can be posted.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13503) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-18 by DhavalMore88 (community contribution) · 14 files (+322 -0), 12 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Added local procedure CapPrepmtLineAmountAfterOverDeduction, called in UpdatePrepmtAmounts after the Prepmt. Line Amount recalculation
- When an amount was deducted and the recalculated value exceeds both Prepmt. Amt. Inv. and the line's total prepayment, it is capped at the larger of the two
- Fixes the false 'prepayment invoices not posted' error when posting the remaining quantity
- Recalculation was triggered on closing Statistics via ReleaseSalesDocument.CalcAndUpdateVATOnLines and UpdateVATOnLines
- Applied in W1 and 11 localization layers, with regression tests in W1 and RU
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
