# #8874 [Field Service Integration] Rounding error on Service Line quantity write-back for Item-type service lines with non-divisible durations

> Service line quantities synced from Field Service are now properly rounded before validation to prevent precision errors on non-divisible durations.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/8874) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-09 by tomasevicst · 2 files (+109 -5), 1 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Wraps duration-to-hours division with UOMMgt.RoundQty before passing to Quantity validation
- Handles both defined and undefined item UoM rounding precision cases
- Covers five quantity-conversion sites in estimated and actual paths
- Falls back to 0.00001 precision when item precision is not configured
- Prevents validation errors on fractional hour quantities like 0.333... from 20-minute durations
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
