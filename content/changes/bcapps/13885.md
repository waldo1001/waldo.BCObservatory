# #13885 [Main]- Final sales invoice uses item Gen. Prod. Posting Group on prepayment deduction line

> Posting a final sales invoice no longer applies the item's Gen. Prod. Posting Group to the prepayment deduction line. The fix is in Sales-Post (codeunit 80) across all localized layers, with new prepayment tests.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13885) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-27 by DeepsShukla · 14 files (+296 -48), 12 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Prepayment deduction lines on the final sales invoice no longer take the item's Gen. Prod. Posting Group
- Small change in Sales-Post codeunit 80
- Applied in APAC, BE, CH, ES, FI, FR, GB, IT, NA, NO, RU and W1 layers
- Regression tests added to ERM Prepayment in W1 and RU
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
