# #12806 [main] Closing the BC browser tab during quote-to-order reservation leaves a partial sales order and the source quote

> Converting a sales quote to an order now holds off reservation until all quote lines have been copied. Closing the browser tab mid-conversion can no longer commit a partial sales order and leave the source quote behind.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12806) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-16 by Shikhverma · 5 files (+322 -5), 4 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Reservation during quote-to-order conversion is deferred until the conversion completes
- Prevents a committed partial order when the session is interrupted
- Change applied to codeunit 86 in the W1, CH, IT and RU layers
- Tests added in ERM Sales Quotes
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
