# #9608 Bug 642638: [master] IRS 1099 reporting fields inconsistently stamped on Vendor Ledger Entries for non-1099 vendors - General Journal vs. Purchase Invoice

> IRS 1099 reporting fields on vendor ledger entries are now consistently left empty for vendors without a 1099 Form Box No., whether posted through a General Journal or Purchase Invoice.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9608) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-07-24 by AleksanderGladkov · 2 files (+84 -0), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- IRS 1099 fields were being inconsistently stamped on vendor ledger entries for non-1099 vendors depending on the posting method
- The fix ensures IRS data is only copied to vendor ledger entries when the vendor actually has a 1099 Form Box No.
- Tests were added to verify that vendors without a Form Box No. get empty IRS fields from General Journal posting
- This prevents non-1099 vendors from having incorrect and non-editable IRS reporting fields on their ledger entries
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
