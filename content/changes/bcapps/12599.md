# #12599 [Master] - Slice 629913: [Expense Agent][VENDOR] Enforced background itemization required for approvals

> Expense Agent now requires itemization when an enabled Expense Policy with policy text applies to the expense category, regardless of the Expense Detail Required setting. Policy-enforced itemizations can skip subcategories, and the non-refundable amount becomes editable for such itemizations.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12599) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-21 by v-rohangarg20 · 13 files (+957 -80), 11 AL · feature · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Policy-enforced itemization is required when an enabled policy with text applies to an expense category, independent of Expense Detail Required
- Policy-enforced itemizations allow blank subcategories, while regular itemizations require them
- Policy-enforced itemizations are preserved through auto-population and rule validation
- Non-refundable amounts can be edited for policy-enforced itemizations but remain read-only for ordinary itemizations
- Comprehensive test coverage added for all Expense Detail Required values and policy scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
