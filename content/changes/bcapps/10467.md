# #10467 [Shopify] Fix more-expensive exchange refund: no balancing G/L line, no false processed-order warning

> Fixed Shopify exchange refund handling when the replacement item is more expensive than the returned item, eliminating the false processed-order warning and removing the spurious balancing G/L line that forced the credit memo total to zero.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10467) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-24 by onbuyuka · 5 files (+224 -35), 2 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Exchange line IDs are now fetched up front and excluded from the processed-order conflict detection to prevent false warnings on re-import
- The redundancy hash for processed orders now excludes exchange lines for consistent re-import behavior
- Credit memo no longer creates a spurious Refund Account G/L line when the refund contains exchange lines
- The credit memo total now correctly reflects the customer's remaining payment obligation instead of being forced to zero
- Two new unit tests validate the fix for exchanges where the replacement item costs more than the returned item
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
