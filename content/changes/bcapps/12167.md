## What changed

- Centralized existing completion telemetry emission for QuickBooks Online migrations
- Added new feature uptake telemetry event (0000JMQ) alongside existing completion event (0000JMR)
- One-time upgrade backfill detects and reports migrations completed before telemetry was introduced
- Existing Product = QuickBooks Online dimension preserved for backward compatibility
- Guarded by upgrade tag to ensure telemetry emits once per company
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
