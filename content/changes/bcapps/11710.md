# #11710 [29.x]-Not enough inventory available at vendor location for this order

> Subcontracting receipt posting no longer duplicates component consumption by suppressing automatic flushing for India Localization output lines that already have consumption posted via Delivery Challan.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11710) · merged into `releases/29.x` (BC29) on 2026-09-22 by v-ajaztabrez · 2 files (+13 -16), 2 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Component consumption was being posted twice during subcontracting receipt in India Localization
- The standard manufacturing flow and India Localization processes now coordinate to avoid duplicate flushing
- Subcontracting Order No is tracked on output journal lines to identify when to suppress automatic flushing
- Item tracking, capacity posting, and normal production flushing remain unaffected
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
