# #10083 [main]- Negative Shipped and Invoiced Quantities appear on Sales Order Lines after using the Copy Document from a Sales Credit Memo.

> Copying a sales credit memo from a posted invoice that contains duplicate items now correctly matches each credit memo line to a distinct invoice line, preventing negative shipped and invoiced quantities on sales order lines.

- Added an overload to GetSalesInvoiceLine that tracks already-consumed invoice lines to avoid duplicate matching
- Introduced FindUnusedSalesInvoiceLine helper method to skip previously matched invoice lines
- UpdateSalesOrderLineIfExist maintains a temporary buffer of resolved invoice lines to prevent reuse across multiple credit memo lines
- Fixes issue where all credit memo lines matched the first invoice line when copying from invoices with duplicate items
