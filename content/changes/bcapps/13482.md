# #13482 [Master][all-e]Purchase Invoice Statistics Non-Deductible VAT Amount does not match VAT Entries and G/L Entries when Non-Deductible VAT is enabled.

> Fixed a discrepancy where purchase invoice statistics showed different non-deductible VAT amounts than VAT entries and G/L entries by preserving document-allocated VAT amounts for manual entries and restricting recalculation to automatic journal VAT entries.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13482) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-09 by sanjmaurya · 3 files (+148 -1), 1 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Purchase invoice statistics now correctly matches VAT entries and G/L entries when non-deductible VAT is enabled
- Non-deductible VAT allocations from document posting are preserved for manual VAT entries
- Recalculation of non-deductible VAT is restricted to automatic journal entries only
- Resolves rounding differences caused by line splitting and dimension changes
- Regression tests added for purchase invoices, credit memos, and various VAT scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
