# #12328 [Master Data Management] Fix usage telemetry and make it emit max once per day

> Master Data Management usage telemetry is restored with a frequency limit to emit at most once per day, distinguishing cross-environment and same-environment synchronizations.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12328) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-08 by dcenic · 3 files (+59 -3), 3 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Master Data Management usage telemetry reintroduced after it was erroneously removed
- Telemetry now emits at most once per day instead of on every synchronization
- Cross-environment synchronization gets a separate tag from same-environment synchronization where supported
- Previously registered tag 0000JIR is brought back for its original purpose
- Changes touch the Master Data Management codeunit, its subscribers and the MDM Connection Details page
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
