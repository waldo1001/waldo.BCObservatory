# #13220 Fix average cost adjustment overflow on same-period transfers

> Same-period transfers no longer cause average cost adjustments to overflow when item quantities are zero or negative. The fix skips transfer recalculation when cumulative item ledger quantities are nonpositive, preserving transfer costs and normal forwarding.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13220) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-28 by alexei-dobriansky · 4 files (+287 -25), 3 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Prevents cost amplification from repeated transfer repricings when cumulative item quantity is zero or negative
- Skips transfer average-cost recalculation in Item-wide average costing when pre-exclusion quantity is nonpositive
- Reuses existing quantity summation through local parameters to avoid additional queries
- Includes regression tests for negative-balance, zero-balance, and positive-balance transfer scenarios
- Applied to W1, APAC, and RU layers
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
