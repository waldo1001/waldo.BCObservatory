# #9688 [main] Hidden breaking change and instability on sales/purchase line insert

> Fixed unreliable header existence checks for sales and purchase line insertion by renaming the control variable, adding an explicit verification procedure, and providing a dedicated method to suppress the check. These changes resolve false-positive errors while preventing invalid inserts.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9688) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-04 by JakovljevicDusan · 28 files (+1279 -287), 26 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Renamed HasSalesHeader and HasPurchaseHeader variables to SuppressSalesHeaderExistsVerification and SuppressPurchaseHeaderExistsVerification to clarify intent
- Added SetSuppressSalesHeaderExistsVerification() and SetSuppressPurchaseHeaderExistsVerification() procedures as alternatives to SetSalesHeader() and SetPurchaseHeader()
- Implemented explicit header existence checks in VerifySalesHeaderExists() and VerifyPurchaseHeaderExists() instead of relying on GetSalesHeader() and GetPurchaseHeader()
- Skip verification for temporary sales and purchase lines
- Retained original parameter names in OnBeforeGetSalesHeader() and OnBeforeGetPurchaseHeader() events to avoid breaking changes
- Breaking: existing extensions can stop compiling or working.
