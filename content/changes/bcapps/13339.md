# #13339 [29.x]Changing Negative Amount Format to Parentheses modifies displayed decimal precision despite unchanged rounding settings in the Financial Reports

> Financial Reports now preserve the configured decimal precision when displaying negative amounts in parentheses format instead of forcing a fixed two-decimal pattern.

## What changed

- Negative amount formatting no longer overrides the column's configured rounding settings
- The fix formats amounts according to precision first, then applies parentheses for negative values
- Decimal precision remains consistent whether showing negative amounts as minus signs or parentheses
