# #12894 [PayablesAgent] Add support for credit memos, recognised from the PDF and applied to the original invoice

> The Payables Agent now recognizes vendor credit notes in PDFs (EN 16931 type 381) and creates purchase credit memos instead of invoices. A credit memo is applied to the invoice printed on the document only when exactly one open posted invoice of the pay-to vendor matches; otherwise it stays unapplied with a warning.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12894) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-22 by Explorer986 · 9 files (+161 -25), 8 AL · feature · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Extraction returns the EN 16931 document type code and billing reference; code maps 381 to a credit memo, anything else to an invoice
- Creates purchase credit memos with the same draft and task experience as invoices
- Applies only to the stated invoice when exactly one open posted invoice of the pay-to vendor matches in an applicable currency; otherwise leaves it unapplied with a warning
- Draft gets an editable Applies-to Vendor Inv. No. field and a read-only outcome field
- Amount signs are normalised and the match is re-checked at finalization
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
