# #9711 Fix E-Document purchase draft header Sub Total overwrite; notify on mismatch

> E-Document purchase draft no longer silently overwrites the header Sub Total when lines are edited. Users are now notified when the header total diverges from the line totals beyond a rounding tolerance, with options to dismiss or ignore future warnings.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9711) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-14 by ventselartur · 8 files (+1587 -63), 6 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Purchase draft header Sub Total is preserved from the incoming document instead of being recalculated
- Added tolerance-based mismatch detection (rounding precision × line count) between header and line totals
- Notifications appear on the purchase draft subform with Dismiss and Don't-show-again actions
- Mismatch dismissal is persisted on the E-Document Purchase Header and re-arms when amounts are edited
- Telemetry logging captures detected mismatches with totals, tolerance, and difference values
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
