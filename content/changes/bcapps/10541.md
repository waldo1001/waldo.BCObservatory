# #10541 [Master] - Slice 641375: [Sustainability] Collect Amount from G/L Entries: track collected entries to prevent double counting

> Sustainability Journal's collect amount function now tracks which G/L entries have been collected to prevent double-counting emissions when running collection over overlapping or adjacent periods.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10541) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-16 by ViditGupta1277 · 15 files (+1267 -7), 13 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- New relation table links collected G/L entries to their corresponding sustainability entries
- Collect function excludes previously collected entries based on the relation table
- Indexed collected marker maintains fast exclusion filtering on large ledgers
- Reversing sustainability entries removes their links, making entries collectable again
- Follows the existing G/L to Item Ledger Relation model
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
