# #9954 Restoring undefined action. (Purchase Orders page can't be open in latest `main`)

> An action body was accidentally removed from the E-Document app, breaking purchase order pages. The action and its references have been restored to fix the compilation error.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9954) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-06 by mynjj · 5 files (+40 -2), 5 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- An action that was previously removed had no body or RunObject, causing it to not be emitted
- The missing action broke actionref references in purchase order pages
- Action bodies have been restored across multiple E-Document pages and extensions
- Analyzer that should have caught this issue was disabled during the gate check
- Pages can now be opened successfully again
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
