# #14061 [MAIN] Fix TDS calculation on multi-line purchase invoices (Bug 659187)

## What changed

- TDS calculation was incorrectly excluding earlier untaxed lines when the cumulative invoice total hit the annual threshold
- The fix preserves line-level TDS bases and ensures all applicable amounts are included in the calculation
- Use-case configuration updated from version 35 to 36 to implement the corrected logic
- Prevents scenarios where only later lines are taxed while earlier amounts are overlooked
- Resolution applies specifically to Indian localization TDS workflows
