## What changed

- Privacy notice check is no longer bypassed for evaluation companies
- Evaluation companies now see the same privacy notice requirement as standard companies
- Changes affect the Copilot Capability Implementation and Copilot Settings
- Users in evaluation companies must now explicitly agree to privacy terms
