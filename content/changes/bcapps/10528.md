# #10528 Track receipt on invoice per purchase order line

> Receipt on invoice tracking can now be configured at the purchase order line level instead of just header level, with vendor-level defaults that cascade to new purchase lines.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10528) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-31 by mynjj · 81 files (+1051 -159), 79 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Adds receipt on invoice control per purchase order line via new field in Purchase Line
- Introduces vendor-level receipt on invoice default setting in Vendor table
- Purchase lines inherit receipt on invoice from header or vendor configuration
- Column is hidden by default under personalization in Purchase Order page
- Includes upgrade logic to migrate existing header-level settings
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
