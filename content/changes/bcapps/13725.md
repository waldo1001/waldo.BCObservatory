# #13725 Reduce repeated ILE reads and JIT loads in average cost adjustment

> Codeunit 5895 Inventory Adjustment reduces repeated database reads and JIT loads during average cost calculation by caching Item Ledger Entry data, loading required fields upfront, and removing ineffective LoadFields calls.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13725) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-06 by alexei-dobriansky · 3 files (+99 -33), 3 AL · performance · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Caches the immutable Positive field from Item Ledger Entry in a dictionary to eliminate repeated lookups
- Loads all required chain fields upfront in ExcludeAvgCostOnValuationDate and IsAvgCostException to prevent JIT loads
- Removes ineffective LoadFields calls in AvgValueEntriesToAdjustExist and CollectOpenValueEntries
- Adds missing fields to load lists: Applied Entry to Adjust and Valuation Date
- Improves performance for high-volume items where processing time grew quadratically with value entries
