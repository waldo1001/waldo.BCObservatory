# #14413 [29.x][ALL-E] Posting a Sales Credit Memo with a different Unit of Measure does not convert the quantity before updating the originating Sales Order (Blanket Sales Order is updated correctly)

> Posting a sales credit memo with a different unit of measure now correctly converts the quantity before updating the originating sales order, preventing incorrect deductions from shipped and invoiced quantities.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/14413) · merged into `releases/29.x` (BC29) on 2026-11-26 by sanjmaurya · 4 files (+135 -7), 3 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Credit memo quantities are converted to the sales order line's unit of measure before updating order quantities
- Fixes incorrect quantity deductions when units differ (e.g., 8 PCS no longer incorrectly treated as 8 SET)
- Solution applies the sales order line's conversion factor to the credited base quantity
- Regression tests added for different, fractional, and matching unit scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
