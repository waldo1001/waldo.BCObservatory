# #11115 [29.x ]Trial Balance (Excel) report shows incorrect figures and formatting differences compared to Trial Balance (Obsolete) report in Business Central v28.2

> Trial Balance Excel reports now correctly apply global dimension filters (Department) when generating data, fixing incorrect figures that were appearing when dimension filters were combined with date filters.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11115) · merged into `releases/29.x` (BC29) on 2026-09-08 by Shikhverma · 5 files (+296 -8), 4 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Global Dimension 1 and 2 filters are now read from the G/L Account request record in the Trial Balance codeunit
- Dimension filters are applied with SetFilter on the query dimension columns for both the ending-date and opening-balance passes
- The standard, budget and consolidated Excel trial balance reports now offer the dimension filters as request filter fields, like the obsolete Trial Balance report
- By-period and previous-year reports use a separate path and are not covered
- Tests added for the dimension-filtered figures
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
