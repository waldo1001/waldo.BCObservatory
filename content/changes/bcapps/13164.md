# #13164 Inventory and Sustainability Power BI reports missing from Role Explorer despite being searchable

- Sustainability Manager role center Power BI Reports group moved from addlast(Reporting) to addfirst(Sections) to appear in Role Explorer
- Four Inventory Valuation reports added to the Warehouse Manager role center Power BI Reports group under Sections
- Reports were previously searchable but invisible in Role Explorer because they were only in the Reporting action area
- Change aligns Sustainability with other Power BI role center extensions that use Sections
