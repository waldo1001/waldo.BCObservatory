# #11359 [Master] - Bug 648886: [Sustainability][Value Chain] Transfer Order uses average emissions instead of Specific carbon tracking (lot/serial)

> Sustainability posting now correctly tracks specific emissions for lots and serial numbers across transfer orders, preventing the use of average emissions on later sales.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11359) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-15 by v-rohangarg20 · 2 files (+123 -2), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Transfer order emissions tracking now respects specific lot and serial numbers instead of aggregating them
- The transfer shipment application traversal is restricted to entries matching the same lot and serial number
- Fixes cases where multiple tracked items with different emissions were incorrectly averaged during transfer receipts
- Preserves specific tracking emissions through the entire supply chain from transfer to sale
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
