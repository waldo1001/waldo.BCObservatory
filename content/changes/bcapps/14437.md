- Corrected exchange rate inversion causing wrong CurrencyAmount calculations in SAF-T 1.30 and 1.40 files
- Updated account mapping logic to include all chart of accounts in export regardless of period activity
- Aligns with Norwegian Tax Administration requirements for complete account visibility in SAF-T files
- Enhanced test coverage with additional validation for currency calculations and account handling
- Changes affect both SAF-T 1.3 and 1.4 file generation
