# #12855 [E-Documents] Add support for credit memos, recognised from the PDF and applied to the original invoice

> E-Documents now recognize vendor credit notes in PDFs and create purchase credit memos instead of invoices. A credit memo is applied to the invoice printed on the document only when exactly one open posted invoice of the pay-to vendor matches in an applicable currency; otherwise it stays unapplied with a warning.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12855) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-21 by Explorer986 · 20 files (+1797 -31), 12 AL · feature · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Extraction returns the EN 16931 document type code and billing reference; code maps 381 to a credit memo and anything else to an invoice
- Credit memos are applied only to the stated invoice when exactly one open posted invoice of the pay-to vendor matches in an applicable currency
- Otherwise the credit memo is left unapplied with a warning, and finalizing re-checks the match
- Draft gets an editable Applies-to Vendor Inv. No. field and a read-only outcome field
- Amount signs are normalized from the lines first, then the header totals
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
