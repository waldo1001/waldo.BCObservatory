# #11560 [E-Documents Core] [Peppol] - Enabling EDI capabilities with E-Documents. Send path (Self-Billed Purchase Invoice/Credit Memo → E-Document)

> Enables exporting self-billed purchase invoices and credit memos as Peppol BIS 3.0 e-documents, with automatic type assignment based on vendor self-billing agreements and new manual creation actions on posted purchase pages.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11560) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-07 by jzaksauskas (community contribution) · 13 files (+1544 -11), 11 AL · feature · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Adds two new E-Document Type values for self-billed purchase documents to distinguish them from ordinary purchase invoices
- Implements automatic type assignment when posting purchase documents if vendor has active self-billing agreement
- Creates new self-billed Peppol export codeunit with swapped party roles using vendor as supplier and company as customer
- Adds manual actions on Posted Purchase Invoice and Credit Memo pages for creating self-billed e-documents
- Includes export validation for participant presence and VAT registration with graceful error logging
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
