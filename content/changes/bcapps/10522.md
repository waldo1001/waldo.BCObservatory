# #10522 [main] Bug 647499 Bin Replenishment with Pick by FEFO Creates Incorrect Inventory Movements

> Fixed bin replenishment with FEFO-based picking to correctly calculate quantities and clear processed worksheet lines in basic warehouse locations.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10522) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-27 by MSNehaNawal · 2 files (+162 -3), 1 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- FEFO inventory movements now exclude the destination bin when calculating available quantity to pick, ensuring correct lot selection and full quantity fulfillment
- Handled warehouse activity buffers for FEFO movements are now recorded with blank From Bin to match worksheet lines and enable proper deletion of processed lines
- Prevents incorrect inventory movements and unprocessed worksheet lines that occurred when the earliest FEFO lot was already in the replenishment bin
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
