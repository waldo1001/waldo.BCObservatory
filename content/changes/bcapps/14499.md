# #14499 Job Queue: resume interrupted finalization on task retry

> Job Queue Dispatcher now resumes interrupted finalization when a task retries after a lock timeout, preventing recurring jobs from being permanently stuck in the Finished state without scheduling their next recurrence.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/14499) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-27 by darjoo · 3 files (+219 -1), 2 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Detects when a task retry occurs after finalization failure by checking for In Process or Success log entries matching the System Task ID
- Resumes bookkeeping to finalize the log entry and call FinalizeRun to schedule the next recurrence or delete non-recurring entries
- Only acts on entries that are Finished, have Manual Recurrence off, and have a matching log entry, leaving other Finished entries untouched
- Emits new telemetry signal 0000WW6 to track when job queue entry finalization is resumed
- Includes new tests covering recurring entry rescheduling, non-recurring entry deletion, and entries left untouched when no log entry exists
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
