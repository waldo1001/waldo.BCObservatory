- Falls back to serial quantity instead of relying on 'Qty. to ship' field when it is zero
- Fixes issue where blank 'Default Quantity to Ship' setting caused subscription creation to fail
- Adds subscription handling for sales orders closed by drop shipment purchase order receipts
- Fake invoicing now correctly applied to sales orders released through drop shipment path
- Tests added to verify both scenarios
