# #11899 [Shopify] Tax matching fixes for country, Canadian HST, and zero-rate lines

> The Shopify Tax Matching Agent now correctly handles North American orders by restricting to US and Canadian orders, scoping Canadian HST/TVH jurisdictions to provinces, matching zero-rate tax lines, and improving the matching algorithm with updated keyword rules and prompt consistency.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11899) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-01 by onbuyuka · 19 files (+982 -89), 9 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Tax matching restricted to US and Canadian orders only
- Canadian HST/TVH jurisdictions scoped by province (e.g., ONHST, NSHST) to prevent rate conflicts across provinces
- Zero-rate tax lines now matched like other lines instead of remaining unmatched
- Matching prompt updated with consistent keyword rules, French jurisdiction names, and improved geographic scoping
- Tax Match Review experience updated to show 0% rates as 0.00 and preserve full Shopify tax titles when creating jurisdictions
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
