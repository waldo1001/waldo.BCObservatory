# #11734 [VIES Integration] Per-environment daily request rate-limit

> A per-tenant daily rate-limit of 2000 VIES lookups prevents a single tenant from flooding the shared outbound IP used by Business Central online, which could deny-list the IP for all co-located tenants.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11734) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-24 by dcenic · 4 files (+306 -0), 3 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- New table 243 'VAT Reg. No. Lookup Quota' and codeunit 247 'VAT Lookup Quota Mgt.' enforce a daily limit in codeunit 248 before SOAP calls to VIES
- The counter resets daily at UTC midnight and applies to all callers including extensions and background jobs
- Rate-limit of 2000 lookups per day is about 10x typical usage but 10x below the volume that triggers VIES IP deny-listing
- Applies only to Business Central online (SaaS); on-premises tenants are unaffected
- Tenants exceeding the limit receive an error, audit entry, and telemetry for the remainder of the UTC day
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
