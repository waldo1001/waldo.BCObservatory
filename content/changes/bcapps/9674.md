# #9674 Fix missing table permissions on Stale Check action in Check Management Subscriber

> Added missing table permissions to the Check Management Subscriber codeunit to allow the Stale Check action to properly modify bank account and customer/vendor ledger entries without permission errors.

## What changed

- Stale Check action was failing due to missing Modify permissions on ledger tables
- Added Permissions property to Check Management Subscriber codeunit
- Grants read-modify permissions on Bank Account Ledger Entry, Check Ledger Entry, Customer Ledger Entry, and Vendor Ledger Entry
- Resolves license permission errors during stale check operations
