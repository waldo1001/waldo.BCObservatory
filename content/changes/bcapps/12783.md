# #12783  [29.x] Subcontractor Price ignores Run Time when creating purchase cost for time-based subcontracting operations

> Subcontractor price calculation for time-based operations now correctly multiplies the unit cost by the operation's run time factor when creating purchase requisitions and orders.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12783) · merged into `releases/29.x` (BC29) on 2026-10-23 by sanjmaurya · 3 files (+550 -16), 2 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Subcontractor price for Time-based operations ignored the routing line's operation time factor
- New shared calculation multiplies the subcontractor price by the operation time factor and rounds to the unit-amount precision
- Applied to requisition worksheet lines, carried-out purchase lines, automatic repricing and directly created purchase lines
- Automated test added covering those pricing scenarios
- Fixes an incorrect Direct Unit Cost on subcontracting purchase and requisition lines
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
