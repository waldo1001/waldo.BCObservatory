# #13642 Preserve work descriptions on posted sales prepayment documents

> Work descriptions are now loaded and preserved on posted sales prepayment invoices and credit memos, matching the behavior of standard sales posting when invoice discount calculation is enabled.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13642) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-05 by JesperSchulz · 13 files (+486 -0), 9 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Work Description BLOB is loaded immediately before TransferFields in prepayment header creation to prevent data loss after discount redistribution
- Fixes issue where prepayment posting reloads the sales header after recalculating discounts, requiring the BLOB to be reloaded
- Four regression tests added covering prepayment invoices and credit memos with automatic invoice discount calculation enabled and disabled
- Changes propagated through Miapp localization mechanism to nine posting codeunits and three test codeunits across multiple regions
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
