# #11507 [Master] Fix Unix timestamp timezone conversion

> Unix timestamp conversion in TypeHelper now delegates to System.DateTime's Unix Timestamp codeunit to avoid incorrectly shifting UTC instants by the user's timezone offset, especially during daylight saving time.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11507) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-23 by haoranpb · 2 files (+52 -16), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- TypeHelper.EvaluateUnixTimestamp previously added timezone offset when converting Unix timestamps, causing one-hour shifts during summer time
- Conversion now uses System.DateTime's Unix Timestamp codeunit which correctly uses DateTimeOffset.FromUnixTimeSeconds
- Tests verify that the same UTC instant is returned regardless of user timezone (UTC or Central Europe Standard Time)
- Fixes issue where Central European summer time shifted the represented UTC instant by one hour
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
