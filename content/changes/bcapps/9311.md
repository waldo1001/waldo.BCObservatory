# #9311 Withholding Tax for Employee

> Withholding tax support for employee transactions has been added, allowing users to calculate and track employee-related withholding taxes similar to existing purchase-based functionality with new employee setup tables, calculation logic, and ledger entries.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9311) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-07-29 by ViditGupta1277 (community contribution) · 46 files (+2178 -6), 44 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- New employee withholding tax feature with setup tables including groups, group lines, and threshold accumulators
- Employee withholding calculation codeunit for transaction processing and subscriber patterns
- Extended employee card and employee ledger entry to support withholding tax tracking
- Withholding tax posting setup extended to support both purchase and expense transactions
- Permission sets updated to include new employee-related withholding tax objects
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
