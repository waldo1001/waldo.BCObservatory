# #13495 Migrate SOA auxiliary calls to GPT-5.6 and refine date-filter handling

> Sales Order Agent upgrades auxiliary model calls from GPT-4.1 to GPT-5.6 Ceres and refines item-search date-filter handling to better use system-generated availability dates when no customer delivery date applies.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13495) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-02 by qasimikram · 3 files (+7 -33), 2 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Migrate message and attachment relevance checks, email-template validation, and signature insertion to use GPT-5.6 Ceres instead of GPT-4.1
- Simplify item-search date-filter logic to accept page default availability period when no delivery date is requested or dates are in the past
- Prevent system-generated availability dates from appearing in customer-requested delivery dates
- Clear date filters once and accept resulting defaults instead of repeatedly rechecking
- Model results may differ with GPT-5.6 Ceres requiring evaluation and validation of various date scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
