# #13495 Migrate SOA auxiliary calls to GPT-5.6 and refine date-filter handling

> Sales Order Agent now uses GPT-5.6 Ceres instead of GPT-4.1 for its auxiliary model calls. Its instructions now accept the page's default availability period when no applicable delivery date was requested, and keep those system-generated dates out of the quote's Requested Delivery Date.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13495) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-02 by qasimikram · 3 files (+7 -33), 2 AL · feature · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Message and attachment relevance checks, email-template validation and signature insertion now call GetGPT56CeresLatest() instead of GetGPT41Latest()
- The agent instructions prompt now accepts the page's default availability period when no delivery date is requested or all requested dates are in the past
- A previous item's requested-date filter is cleared once, and the resulting default is accepted without rechecking
- System-generated availability dates stay out of the quote's Requested Delivery Date; explicit, non-past customer dates are still used as filters
- Results may differ with the new model and need evaluation; the environment must support the GPT-5.6 Ceres deployment
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
