# #10277 Slice 554749: Inventory put-away/pick support for subcontracting purchase lines and WIP item transfers

> Inventory put-away and pick functionality is now supported for subcontracting purchase lines and WIP item transfers, with proper handling of quantities, item tracking, bin propagation, and partial posting scenarios.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10277) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-20 by stevengrossGOB (community contribution) · 39 files (+7698 -163), 19 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Enables warehouse put-away/pick operations for subcontracting purchase orders and WIP transfers
- Preserves correct base quantities and production-order item tracking for last-operation lines
- Non-last-operation and WIP movements bypass physical inventory posting and synchronize WIP ledger entries
- Supports lot tracking, partial posting, direct transfers, blank-bin locations, and posted activity metadata
- Adds extension points via new base warehouse events
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
