# #13805 [29.x][all-e]Purchase Invoice Statistics Non-Deductible VAT Amount does not match VAT Entries and G/L Entries when Non-Deductible VAT is enabled. - CopyInitial commit

> Fixed a mismatch between the Non-Deductible VAT Amount in Purchase Invoice Statistics and the VAT and G/L entries created on posting. Purchase posting now keeps the document's allocated non-deductible VAT amounts for its VAT entries, and recalculation is limited to automatic journal VAT entries.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13805) · merged into `releases/29.x` (BC29) on 2026-11-10 by sanjmaurya · 3 files (+148 -1), 1 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Manual VAT entries created by purchase posting now keep the document's allocated non-deductible VAT, including rounding remainders
- Recalculation is limited to automatic journal VAT entries, so existing journal behavior is kept
- Splitting posting lines by dimensions or descriptions no longer changes the non-deductible VAT total
- Credit memos already skipped this recalculation
- Regression tests cover invoices, prices including VAT, credit memos and automatic journal calculations
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
