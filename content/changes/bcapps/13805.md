# #13805 [29.x][all-e]Purchase Invoice Statistics Non-Deductible VAT Amount does not match VAT Entries and G/L Entries when Non-Deductible VAT is enabled. - CopyInitial commit

> Fixed a calculation mismatch where purchase invoice statistics showed incorrect non-deductible VAT amounts compared to actual VAT and G/L entries when non-deductible VAT is enabled, by preserving the document's allocated amounts for manual entries.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13805) · merged into `releases/29.x` (BC29) on 2026-11-10 by sanjmaurya · 3 files (+148 -1), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Non-deductible VAT allocation for manual journal entries now preserves the document's rounding distribution instead of recalculating independently
- Automatic journal VAT entries retain their existing calculation behavior
- Fixes discrepancies that occurred when invoice lines were split by dimensions or descriptions
- Added regression tests for invoices, credit memos, and prices including VAT
- The issue affected purchase posting accuracy by up to 0.41 per invoice in some cases
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
