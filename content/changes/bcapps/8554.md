# #8554 [Shopify] Fix incorrect G/L line on credit memo for refund with exchange item

> Shopify connector now correctly handles credit memos for refunds involving exchange items by tracking exchange line items separately and emitting offsetting credit memo lines instead of incorrect G/L refund account entries.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/8554) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-07-21 by onbuyuka · 17 files (+416 -1), 9 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Tracks exchange items from Shopify via new paginated GraphQL queries on both order and refund imports
- Marks exchange-item order lines and skips them during sales invoice creation to prevent double-counting
- Generates synthetic negative-quantity refund lines during refund import to offset the exchange value in credit memos
- Resolves location from the fulfillment data when creating the credit memo line for exchange items
- Fixes incorrect G/L account posting that previously doubled the customer-balance impact of exchange values
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
