# #13344 [29.x]- Bug 647007 Copy Document on a Purchase Credit Memo 'Quantity Received', and 'Quantity Invoiced' reverted back on original Purchase Order

> Posting a purchase credit memo made by copying a posted invoice no longer reverts the received and invoiced quantities on the original purchase order. GetPurchaseInvoiceLine now skips the Order No. fallback for inventory item lines with no linked item ledger entry, while the corrective credit memo flow still reverts as designed.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13344) · merged into `releases/29.x` (BC29) on 2026-11-10 by MSNehaNawal · 18 files (+554 -16), 11 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- New IsCorrectiveCreditMemo helper adds an early exit in Purch. Cr. Memo Line GetPurchaseInvoiceLine
- For inventory item lines with no linked item ledger entry, the Order No. / Order Line No. fallback is skipped
- Corrective credit memos that apply item ledger entries still revert the order as before
- Table change made in the W1, NA, BE, ES, FI, GB, IT, NO, APAC, RU and SE layers
- Tests added in W1, NA, BE, CZ and IT; background posting tests adjusted
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
