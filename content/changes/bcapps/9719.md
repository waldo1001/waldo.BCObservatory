# #9719 [main] bug 643971 - Implement checks for blocked customers and vendors in advance letters

> Advance letter headers now validate that customers and vendors are not blocked or privacy-blocked, preventing creation and posting of advance letters for blocked parties.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9719) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-07-28 by DavidHolusa · 6 files (+271 -0), 4 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Added validation checks when setting vendor/customer on advance letter headers
- Blocks posting of advance letter payments if the vendor or customer is blocked or privacy-blocked
- Covers both purchase and sales advance letters with matching block types (Payment for vendors; Ship/Invoice for customers)
- Includes 14 tests covering all blocking scenarios for both purchase and sales advance letters
- Existing advance letters with blocked parties will fail on subsequent posting operations
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
