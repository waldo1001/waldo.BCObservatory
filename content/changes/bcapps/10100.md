# #10100 [Bug 644091] Separate B2B and B2C HSN summaries in GSTR-1 report

> The GSTR-1 report now handles B2B and B2C HSN summaries separately through conditional filtering based on the selected Nature of Supply, with fallback to combined output when no specific category is chosen.

## What changed

- HSN queries modified to support conditional filtering by Nature of Supply for B2B/B2C separation
- Report Excel generation logic updated to independently summarize B2B and B2C HSN data
- Blank Nature of Supply selection maintains backward compatibility by showing combined HSN output
- Fixes duplicate rows and incorrect totals that occurred when both B2B and B2C data existed for the same HSN
- Addresses GST portal requirements for separate B2B and B2C HSN reporting in GSTR-1
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
