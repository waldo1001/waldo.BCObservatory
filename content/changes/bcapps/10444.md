# #10444 [Bug][Subscription Billing] "Cancellation Possible Until" is rounded to the end of the month when the Notice Period is expressed in days

> Fixed an issue where subscription line cancellation dates were incorrectly rounded to month-end when notice periods were expressed in days or weeks, potentially allowing cancellations outside agreed notice windows. Added logic to only apply month-end alignment for month-based formulas.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10444) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-23 by miljance (community contribution) · 4 files (+284 -7), 2 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Subscription cancellation dates no longer snap to month-end for day or week-based notice periods
- Added DateFormulaManagement.IsMonthBasedDateFormula() to distinguish month-based from day/week-based formulas
- Applied fix to three calculation methods: CalculateCancellationPossibleUntil, CalculateTermUntilFromCancellationPossibleUntil, and CalculateTermUntilUsingExtensionTerm
- Composite formulas like <1Y+6M> are correctly identified as month-based, while formulas with day or week components are not
- Added 10 test methods covering day, week, month, quarter, and composite formulas
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
