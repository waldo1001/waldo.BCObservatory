# #11023 BE's PEPPOL "escompte" compensation

> Belgian PEPPOL invoices now apply discount compensation through additional allowance charges to reconcile tax calculations with displayed totals, aligning with local practices and legal requirements.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11023) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-10 by mynjj · 13 files (+663 -32), 11 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Added three new codeunits to handle escompte compensation, monetary info, and payment info for Belgian PEPPOL
- Implemented dual AllowanceCharge approach where discount affects tax base but additional charges restore totals to match Belgian standards
- Extended PEPPOL tax info provider interface with new methods for compensation handling
- Updated tax info codeunit with escompte logic and added test application with comprehensive test cases
- Validated against online PEPPOL validators and aligned with customer examples from Scrada
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
