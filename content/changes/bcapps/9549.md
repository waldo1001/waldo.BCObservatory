# #9549 636017 Move Purchases report action tooltips to report objects

> Tooltips for 18 purchase-related reports are now defined on the report objects themselves rather than on the page actions that invoke them, eliminating duplication across W1 and all country layers.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9549) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-04 by V-CWurtzen · 95 files (+27 -361), 95 AL · refactor · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Moved tooltip definitions from page actions to 18 report objects covering purchase statistics, vendor statements, and deletion reports
- Removed duplicate tooltip text from page actions in W1 and 55 page forks across 18 country layers
- Added report-level tooltips to 8 forked report objects to maintain consistency
- Metadata-only change with no runtime behavior impact
- Follows established cleanup pattern already applied to warehouse, manufacturing, service, sales and inventory modules
