# #9586 [master] Overdue Balance (LCY) filters do not behave as expected if you open the Customer Card from a document or journal.

> The Customer Card now applies a default date filter in the OnAfterGetCurrRecord function to ensure the Overdue Balance (LCY) calculation displays the correct overdue amount and drill-down filters work properly when opened from documents or journals.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9586) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-07-20 by neeleshsinghal · 12 files (+116 -0), 11 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Date filter now defaults to 0D..WorkDate() in OnAfterGetCurrRecord, not just OnOpenPage
- Overdue Balance (LCY) now shows the correct overdue amount instead of total balance when opening the card from documents or journals
- Drill-down filters on the overdue balance now correctly include the due-date filter regardless of how the card is opened
- Aligns the Customer Card behavior with the Vendor Card logic
- Fix applied across all localization layers (APAC, BE, ES, FR, GB, IT, NA, NL, NO, RU, W1)
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
