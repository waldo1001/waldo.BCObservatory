# #12996 [Master] - Bug 651041: [Sustainability][Value Chain] Item Reclassification Journal forces a non-zero Total CO2e and adds it to the item's footprint under Average carbon tracking

> Item Reclassification Journals are now exempt from the sustainability check that requires a non-zero CO2e value, so a stock move can post with zero Total CO2e and no longer adds emissions to the item's footprint. Other item journals keep the existing validation.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12996) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-29 by v-rohangarg20 · 2 files (+52 -11), 1 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Reclassification journals are exempted from the non-zero CO2e validation that applies to other item journals
- The reclassification source indicator is passed to sustainability posting validation to enable differential handling
- Other item journals maintain existing non-zero CO2e checks for non-renewable-energy subcategories
- Transfer Sustainability Value Entries correctly record zero CO2e amounts for reclassification moves
- Test coverage added for posting reclassification with zero Total CO2e
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
