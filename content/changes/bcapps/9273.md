# #9273 Extend Contoso Mfg shop calendar range to cover next-year due dates

> Contoso Coffee Manufacturing demo data calendar now covers the next calendar year, preventing backward scheduling from silently clamping production order dates to the current year end.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9273) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-20 by attilatoury · 1 file (+2 -2), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Expanded Calc. Machine Center Calendar and Calculate Work Center Calendar to generate entries for CurrentYear-1 through CurrentYear+1 instead of ending at CurrentYear
- Fixes silent failure where released production orders with next-year due dates would schedule backwards to 12/31/CurrentYear due to missing calendar entries
- Changed base year in AdjustDate calls from 1903 to 1904 to extend the calendar window by one year
- Demo data only change with minimal risk, adds one extra year of calendar entries per work/machine center
- Validation includes regenerating Contoso data and testing backward scheduling for next-year due dates
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
