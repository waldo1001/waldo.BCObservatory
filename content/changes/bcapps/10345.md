# #10345 [Main][ALL-E]Issues with Service Contract Invoicing and Retrospective Billing--when the second unposted Service Invoice is deleted the values on the Service Contract are no longer reset--The "Invoiced to Date" field does not resetInitial commit

> Fixed an issue where the Invoiced to Date field on service contracts failed to reset when a second unposted service invoice was deleted, by improving the logic to count only non-reversed ledger entries.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10345) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-03 by sanjmaurya · 3 files (+135 -11), 2 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Service contract invoicing no longer gets stuck when deleting multiple unposted invoices
- The logic now ignores reversed ledger entries that remain after invoice deletion
- Invoiced to Date correctly resets to zero on invoice deletion
- Only original sale entries without corresponding reversals are counted as invoiced
- Fixes blocking of new service invoices after deleting unposted invoices
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
