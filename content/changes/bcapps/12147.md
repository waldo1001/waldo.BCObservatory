## What changed

- Implements corporate card transaction import and processing with data exchange providers
- Adds merchant categorization and deduplication logic for transaction matching
- Creates settlement and statement management capabilities with approval workflows
- Extends bank account records and general journal lines with corporate card tracking fields
- Provides dashboard and UI pages for monitoring cards, exceptions, and settlement statuses
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
