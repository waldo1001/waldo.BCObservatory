# #11669 Fix reserved-lot re-picking after warehouse reclassification

> A sales line can now re-pick its reserved lot after the original pick was registered, the unposted shipment was deleted and the stock was moved back to a pick bin by warehouse reclassification. CalcLineReservedQtyOnInvt now applies the existing shipment-bin guard to registered quantity, which avoids the Nothing to handle error.

## What changed

- Fixed issue where reserved lots could not be re-picked after warehouse reclassification and shipment deletion
- Modified Warehouse Availability Mgt.CalcLineReservedQtyOnInvt to apply shipment-bin guard to registered quantities
- Guard remains item-wide to prevent duplicate global credit calculations
- Added seven comprehensive tests covering re-pick/lot-validation/registration flows and protective controls
- No breaking changes to public interfaces or existing helper functions
