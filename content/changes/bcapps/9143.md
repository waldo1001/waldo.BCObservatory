# #9143 Move French sales specifics to the new Sales FR first-party app

> French sales-specific functionality including SIREN No. fields and localized sales reports have been extracted into a new first-party Sales FR app, decoupling these features from the FR BaseApp layer.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9143) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-20 by ventselartur · 66 files (+27101 -18), 39 AL · refactor · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- New Sales FR first-party app created to host SIREN No. fields and French standard sales reports (Invoice, Credit Memo, Draft Invoice)
- Moved functionality includes page and table extensions, install/upgrade codeunits, and feature key management
- SIREN No. fields and GetSIRENNoWithCaption method obsoleted in FR BaseApp with CLEAN28/CLEANSCHEMA28 gates
- FR BaseApp code cleaned to remove inlined French sales specifics now provided by the app
- New app registered in build configuration files for independent versioning and deployment
