# #13063 [Bug][Subscription Billing] Rebilling usage data increases the subscription quantity once per subscription line

> Fixed rebilling of usage data for subscription lines so that subscription quantity increases only once instead of once per subscription line when a subscription has both customer and vendor lines with usage-based pricing.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13063) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-04 by miljance (community contribution) · 2 files (+51 -3), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Rebilled usage quantity was incorrectly applied multiple times per subscription due to repeated reads of the subscription header quantity
- Captures subscription quantity once per run in a dictionary before processing any lines, eliminating double-counting across customer and vendor lines
- Only affects rebilling scenarios with multiple usage-quantity subscription lines; non-rebilling usage data processing is unchanged
- Existing subscriptions and posted documents are not affected by the behavioral change
- Added test verifying correct single quantity increase when rebilling a subscription with both customer and vendor lines
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
