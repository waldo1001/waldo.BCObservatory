# #10382 [Expense Agent] Fix administrative recall history access

> Expense Report recall authorization is now restricted to the submitter or users with Unlimited Expense Approval, and non-submitter recalls are recorded as administrative activity outside the approval history.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10382) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-20 by t-prda · 3 files (+214 -5), 2 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Only the expense report submitter or users with Unlimited Expense Approval permission can recall pending expense reports
- Non-submitter recalls are recorded as BC User activity with Administrator role
- Administrative activity is kept separate from submitter and approver history participation
- Covers genuine submitter, mapped and unmapped administrator, and unauthorized recall scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
