# #10624 [MAIN]-Bug 647352 Issue with lot no assignment after posting a transfer order

> Transfer shipments for tracked items now correctly consume the matching lot or serial number instead of incorrectly reducing other lot quantities, by rejecting mismatched reservations and applying proper tracking filters.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10624) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-01 by v-praghav · 9 files (+752 -2), 7 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Fixes incorrect lot number reduction in transfer orders caused by reservation-based application not validating lot/serial tracking
- Adds validation to reject mismatched reservations that don't correspond to the correct tracking dimension
- Applies transfer tracking filters to ensure outbound entries consume the correct lot or serial number
- Changes made across multiple localization layers (APAC, CH, ES, IT, RU, W1) to ensure consistent behavior
- Includes test updates to verify the fix works correctly
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
