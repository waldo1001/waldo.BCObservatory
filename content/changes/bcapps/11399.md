# #11399 [master][Subscription Billing] 1M 'Align to End of Month' line with end date produces a 1-day prorated stub

> Fixed a bug in Subscription Billing where monthly billing with Align to End of Month created an unwanted one-day prorated line when the subscription ended. The period end date calculation now properly includes the subscription line end date.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11399) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-18 by neeleshsinghal · 2 files (+48 -1), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Monthly billing cycles with Align to End of Month no longer create spurious one-day prorated billing lines at the subscription end date
- CalculateNextToDate now caps the calculated period end date at the subscription line end date when applicable
- End-date handling improved to include the subscription line end date in the current billing period
- Fixes vendor contracts that were incorrectly generating extra billing lines
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
