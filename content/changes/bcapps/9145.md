# #9145 [E-Document] Show PDF preview factbox on purchase invoice pages

> A PDF preview factbox is added to purchase invoice pages to display the original invoice PDF linked to an inbound E-Document, appearing only when relevant and placed above the Incoming Documents factbox.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9145) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-07-09 by Explorer986 · 6 files (+203 -0), 6 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- PDF preview factbox added to Purchase Invoice, Purchase Invoices, Posted Purchase Invoice, and Posted Purchase Invoices pages
- Preview is read-only and only visible when the invoice is linked to an E-Document with a PDF source file
- Uses SubPageLink-driven approach with two wrapper CardParts to ensure reliable refresh on list and card pages
- Connectivity and rendering logic centralized in E-Document Helper codeunit without duplicating code
- No table schema changes or new stored data; purely additive UI enhancement
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
