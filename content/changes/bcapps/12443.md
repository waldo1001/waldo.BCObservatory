## What changed

- All Spanish SII (Sistema de Información Inmediata) objects across finance, purchasing, and sales modules are renumbered
- Changes include table extensions, page extensions, and codeunits related to VAT, payment methods, journal lines, and transaction history
- Affects role center activities, accounting cues, and posting setup for Spanish localization
- Numbering updates applied consistently across banking, invoicing, and document processing areas
- Changes are scoped to Spanish localization layer without affecting core base application logic
- Breaking: existing extensions can stop compiling or working.
