# #10342 Add GLN information to XRechnung and ZUGFeRD exports

> GLN identifiers with schemeID 0088 are now included in German XRechnung and ZUGFeRD electronic document exports, respecting customer GLN settings and address hierarchy rules.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10342) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-20 by djukicmilica (community contribution) · 4 files (+344 -23), 2 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- GLNs emitted with schemeID='0088' in XRechnung supplier, customer, and delivery identifiers
- Customer 'Use GLN in Electronic Document' setting is respected for XRechnung customer GLNs
- Delivery GLNs resolved from ship-to address first, then fallback to sell-to customer if opted-in
- ZUGFeRD documents now include buyer and ship-to GLNs with schemeID='0088'
- Test coverage added for supplier, customer, and address precedence scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
