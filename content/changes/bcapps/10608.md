# #10608 [master]-Bug 647093-[Subscription Billing] Incorrect Invoice Amounts when using explicit values in Subscription Line End Date

> Subscription billing now calculates correct invoice amounts and billing periods when using explicit end dates with daily or weekly billing rhythms and month-end alignment, by skipping month-end alignment for fixed-length day and week formulas.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10608) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-27 by MSNehaNawal · 2 files (+169 -7), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Fixed incorrect billing period boundaries and amounts for non-usage-based subscription lines with daily/weekly rhythms and month-end alignment
- Root cause was CalculateNextToDate() incorrectly applying month-end alignment to fixed-length day/week formulas
- Solution skips end-of-month alignment for day and week formulas while preserving it for month/quarter/year rhythms
- Correction applied at the source in CalculateNextToDate() eliminates billing boundary and proration errors
- Added test coverage for period calculation scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
