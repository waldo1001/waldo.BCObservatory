# #14243 [Main]- Lot-for-Lot accumulation splits supply into multiple requisition lines when Dampener Period and Safety Stock are enabled

> Fixed Lot-for-Lot accumulation in inventory planning to consolidate multiple requisition lines into one when both Dampener Period and Safety Stock are enabled.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/14243) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-27 by DeepsShukla · 2 files (+67 -0), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Lot-for-Lot orders are no longer split unnecessarily when planned within a dampener period
- Planning now allows new orders to grow within the lot accumulation period
- Requires both Dampener Period and Safety Stock settings to be active
- Multiple order lines are consolidated into a single requisition line
- Improves supply planning efficiency and reduces fragmentation
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
