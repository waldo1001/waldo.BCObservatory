# #12474 [Master]-Non-Deductible Reverse-Charge VAT Correction Is Posted to Purchase VAT Account Instead of Payment Discount Account.

> When posting non-deductible reverse-charge VAT corrections with payment discounts, the system now correctly retrieves the GL account from General Posting Setup instead of posting to the purchase VAT account.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12474) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-26 by neeleshsinghal · 12 files (+161 -18), 11 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Fixed GL account selection for non-deductible VAT corrections when payment discounts are involved
- GL account is now sourced from General Posting Setup configuration
- Prevents incorrect posting to purchase VAT account
- Affects multiple localizations including W1, BE, CH, ES, FI, FR, IT, NA, NO, RU, and APAC
- Includes updated test case for non-deductible VAT payment discount scenario
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
