# #10408 Shopify Copilot Tax Matching Agent: matching engine and data model (stack 2/3)

> Shopify Copilot Tax Matching Agent adds a headless matching engine that uses AI to match Shopify tax lines to Business Central tax jurisdictions, creates tax areas, and includes comprehensive unit tests.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10408) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-28 by onbuyuka · 49 files (+20256 -3), 16 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (machine-generated)

## What changed

- Implements tax matching engine with GPT-4.1 capability registration and deterministic provenance tracking
- Builds or reuses Tax Areas and seeds Tax Details for matched jurisdictions
- Includes configurable review-mode gate to hold Sales Document creation until human verification
- Adds comprehensive test app with AITest suites, unit tests, and accuracy datasets for matching logic
- Adds table extensions and permission sets to support tax matching workflow in Shopify connector
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
