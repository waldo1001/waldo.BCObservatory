# #14283 [Master]-Sales or purchase order with 100% prepayment: Prepmt. Line Amount drifts below Prepmt. Amt. Inv. after partial invoices, so the order can't be released or posted

> Prepayment line amount drift is fixed for sales and purchase orders with 100% prepayment by correcting rounding calculations after partial invoicing, allowing orders to be released and posted successfully.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/14283) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-20 by neeleshsinghal · 30 files (+616 -116), 26 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Prepmt. Line Amount no longer drifts below Prepmt. Amt. Inv. after partial invoices on orders with 100% prepayment
- Such sales and purchase orders can be released and posted again
- Sales Line and Purchase Line tables changed in W1 and many country layers
- Full prepayment rounding tests added for sales and purchases in the W1 and NA layers
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
