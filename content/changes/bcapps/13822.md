# #13822 Fix XRechnung export truncating item name to 40 characters

> XRechnung export in the German E-Document app no longer cuts the line item name (BT-153) to 40 characters. The full line Description, up to its 100-character field length, is now exported for sales and service invoices and credit memos.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13822) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-11 by pri-kise (community contribution) · 2 files (+68 -2), 1 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Removed 40-character limit on item names in XRechnung export for sales invoices and credit memos
- Full description field (up to 100 characters) is now exported without truncation
- Complies with XRechnung 3.0.2 specification and UBL 2.1 schema which have no length restriction on BT-153
- Added tests verifying that maximum-length item descriptions are properly exported
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
