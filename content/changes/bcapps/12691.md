# #12691 Support customer registration number as e-document party identifier for DE

> German customers can now use their registration number as the buyer identifier on outgoing e-invoices when no VAT ID or GLN is available.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12691) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-29 by Explorer986 · 9 files (+206 -11), 6 AL · feature · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Adds a toggle on the customer record to enable registration number use in e-documents
- Registration number is written as buyer legal registration in PEPPOL BIS, XRechnung and ZUGFeRD formats
- Extends existing functionality already available for company and vendor e-documents
- Only applies when the flag is enabled and VAT ID/GLN are unavailable
- Includes new test cases for all three e-document formats
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
