# #13991 [Main][All-E] Warehouse Pick cannot be updated with newly received quantity after Purchase Receipt has been posted

> Inventory pick creation now allows additional quantities from newly posted purchase receipts to be added to existing picks by deducting already-outstanding quantities.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13991) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-18 by sanjmaurya · 2 files (+357 -3), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Removes restriction that skipped sales lines already having warehouse activity
- Deducts quantities already outstanding on the pick to avoid duplication
- Adjusts item-tracking quantities to prevent duplication while preserving existing lines
- Users no longer need to delete and recreate picks after posting purchase receipts
- Includes new regression test
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
