# #12584 Bug 649862: [Subcontracting] Restore Get Receipt Lines for subcontracting receipts

> Restores the ability to copy uninvoiced subcontracting receipts to separate purchase invoices, allowing cumulative vendor invoicing while correctly assigning vendor costs to the original Capacity Ledger Entry and production order.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12584) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-20 by ChethanT · 5 files (+1239 -117), 1 AL · feature · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Uninvoiced subcontracting receipts can now be included in Get Receipt Lines workflow
- Posted receipt lines can be copied to separate purchase invoices for cumulative vendor invoicing
- Posting correctly assigns vendor cost to original Capacity Ledger Entry without creating duplicate output
- Supports untracked and item-tracked receipts from non-final routing operations
- Item-tracked receipts from final operations and warehouse receipts remain explicitly blocked pending further validation
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
