# #11434 [main] Incorrect Additional Currency Base and Additional Currency Amount calculation in VAT/GST Entries when Line Discount is applied on Sales Invoice in the Australian version.

> Fixed incorrect additional currency base and amount calculations in VAT entries for sales invoices with line discounts in the Australian version.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11434) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-21 by Shikhverma · 3 files (+203 -6), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- VAT entries now derive additional currency amounts from their own record's local currency amounts instead of inheriting a single line-level value
- Additional currency fields recalculated per posting record using proper exchange rate conversion with ACY rounding precision
- Discount VAT entries now receive correct sign and amount instead of duplicating the sales VAT entry values
- Amount including VAT (ACY) computed by addition to maintain exact consistency between base and VAT amounts
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
