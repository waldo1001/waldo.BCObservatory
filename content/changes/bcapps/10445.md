# #10445 [Bug][Subscription Billing] Contract Renewal Quote total ignores the Billing Base Period and shows the base-period amount instead of the renewal-term amount

> Contract Renewal Quote totals now show the renewal-term amount instead of the Billing Base Period amount. The contract-renewal calculation in Sales Subscription Line now converts from base period to billing rhythm before applying the renewal-term ratio, and period counts are reset per line.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10445) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-09 by miljance (community contribution) · 2 files (+157 -16), 1 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Renewal quote totals reflect the renewal term, e.g. 1,200 instead of 100 for 1M base, 12M rhythm and 12M term
- The contract-renewal branch lacked the base-period-to-rhythm conversion and applied only the renewal-term ratio
- Both branches now share one period conversion
- Period counts became per-line locals reset to 1, stopping values leaking between lines
- New tests cover differing periods, empty base period and complex rhythm
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
