- Prevents overwriting calculated worksheet costs with raw routing rates when no matching price exists
- Uses standard subcontracting worksheet cost formula as fallback instead of routing rate
- Skips financial repricing for non-open purchase orders to avoid validation errors
- Keys planned-date repricing on the final Order Date to catch all rescheduling scenarios
- Adds comprehensive regression tests covering pricing edge cases and date-edit scenarios
