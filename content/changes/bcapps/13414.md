# #13414 [Main]Payment Discount with Prices Incl. VAT provides unexpected results in the Spanish version

> In the Spanish version, when Prices Including VAT is on, the VAT base recalculation now subtracts the payment discount amount. Payment discounts are now reflected in sales and purchase totals and statistics. VAT-exclusive calculations are unchanged.

## What changed

- Fixes payment discount calculations when Prices Including VAT setting is enabled
- Corrects VAT base recalculation by subtracting payment discount amount
- Issue only affected Spanish localization with specific VAT settings (zero base discount and EC amount)
- Adds test coverage for payment discount scenarios with VAT-inclusive pricing
- Does not affect VAT-exclusive calculations
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
