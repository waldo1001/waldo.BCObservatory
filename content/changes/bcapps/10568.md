# #10568 Send sample invoices from the demo guide directly

> The Payables Agent demo guide now sends sample invoices immediately when selecting the email option, rather than deferring the send until setup is saved, ensuring invoices are sent regardless of the order of operations.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/10568) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-08-27 by Explorer986 · 2 files (+27 -9), 2 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Sample invoices are sent synchronously when the email option is chosen in the demo guide
- Send flags are cleared after invoices are sent to prevent duplicate sends when saving setup
- The final success screen only displays after the email has actually been transmitted
- The change removes the dependency on the order in which users perform setup steps
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
