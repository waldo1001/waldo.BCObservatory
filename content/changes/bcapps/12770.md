# #12770 [Main]-Project Ledger Entry does not get the modified amount from Purchase Order Statistics if the Purchase line is of type G/L Account in the Spanish version.

> Fixed a bug where project ledger entries did not reflect modified invoice discounts on G/L Account lines in purchase orders, even though G/L entries were correct.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12770) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-09 by DhavalMore88 (community contribution) · 16 files (+2454 -51), 14 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Project ledger entries now use the correct unit cost when partial invoices are posted with modified invoice discounts on G/L Account lines
- Two new procedures calculate and apply the per-unit invoice discount difference to project posting
- The fix accounts for indirect costs, prices including VAT, and currency conversions
- Item lines were not affected by the bug and remain unchanged
- The fix applies across all localization layers (W1, NA, BE, CH, ES, FI, GB, IT, RU, APAC)
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
