# #13203 [main] Bug 656535 Additional Reporting Currency Difference After Closing Income Statement - related to 625803

> Close Income Statement now passes the closed additional reporting currency (ACY) amount through a new manually bound codeunit, not through Source Currency Amount. Before this, posting recalculated ACY from LCY at the closing-date rate, which left an ACY difference after closing.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/13203) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-28 by MSNehaNawal · 11 files (+693 -43), 5 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Before the fix, the ACY amount was put in Source Currency Amount and ignored because the source currency code no longer matched the ACY
- A new manually bound codeunit subscribes to OnGLCalcAddCurrencyPostingNone and supplies the ACY amount
- Source Currency Amount now holds only real source currency values, and the 625803 behaviour stays the same
- The retained-earnings balance line also passes its ACY total through the handler
- New tests were added in the W1, BE, CZ, DE, ES and IT layers
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
