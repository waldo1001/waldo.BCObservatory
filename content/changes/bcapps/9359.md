# #9359 Sales Order Agent: ignore attachments larger than 25 MB during email retrieval

> Sales Order Agent now ignores email attachments larger than 25 MB during retrieval and replaces them with placeholder notes to prevent email processing failures when oversized files are encountered.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9359) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-14 by qasimikram · 4 files (+80 -22), 4 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Attachment sizes are checked before content retrieval using EmailMessage.Attachments_GetLength()
- Oversized attachments are replaced with small placeholder text files ending in .ignored.txt
- Only non-ignored attachments are copied to sales quotes during attachment transfer
- New enum value ExceedsFileSize added to SOA Email Attachment Status to track ignore reason
- Telemetry is emitted for ignored attachments with original file details
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
