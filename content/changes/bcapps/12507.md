# #12507 [DK Nemhandel][Currency] Remove unsafe fixed response-size caps on scaling/customer-configured feeds

> Removed unsafe fixed byte-size caps on integration responses that scale with customer data, relying instead on platform limits and structural validation for the Nemhandel and currency exchange rate services.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/12507) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-08 by dcenic · 4 files (+14 -125), 2 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Removed 64 KB cap on Nemhandel API that falsely rejected legitimate large companies with many registered receivers
- Removed 10 MB cap on currency exchange rate downloads where legitimate responses grow annually and vary by provider format
- Kept caps on genuinely bounded responses: VIES checkVat (64 KB) and MTD IP lookup (4 KB)
- Relies on platform HttpClient 150 MiB absolute limit for memory/DoS protection and schema validation for integrity
- Nemhandel responses measured from 0.5 KB (Mærsk) to 97 MB (Region Midtjylland); ECB full-history feed 7.8 MB and growing
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
