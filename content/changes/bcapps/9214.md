# #9214 WorkCenter/MachineCenter: add Capacity Shown In to Calendar and Load pages

> Work Center and Machine Center Calendar and Load pages now include a Capacity Shown In field to display capacity figures in different units of measure (seconds, minutes, hours, or days), matching the functionality already available on Work Center Group pages.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9214) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-06 by Busslar (community contribution) · 10 files (+445 -20), 8 AL · feature · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- Added Capacity Shown In field to the Work Center and Machine Center Calendar and Load pages
- Field initializes from Manufacturing Setup Show Capacity In and lets users switch capacity units
- Calendar matrix Load() gains a capacity unit parameter and converts Capacity (Effective) with TimeFactor
- Load lines SetLines() gains a unit parameter; Capacity, Allocated Qty. and Availability After Orders are converted, Load % unchanged
- Implementation mirrors the existing Work Center Group pages
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
