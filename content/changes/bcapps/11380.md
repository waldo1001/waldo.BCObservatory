# #11380 Honor explicit 0% discounts in Sales and Purchase price lists

> Fixed price calculation to honor explicit 0% discounts in Sales and Purchase price lists by distinguishing discount declarations from incidental zero amounts, allowing specific zero-discount lines to override generic positive discounts.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11380) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-08 by attilatoury · 3 files (+603 -4), 1 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- A line declares a discount when amount type is `Discount` or discount percentage is positive
- Explicit 0% can win through existing specificity rules; 0% in Price & Discount remains incidental
- Candidate acceptance happens before OnAfterIsBetterLine event, preserving subscriber vetoes
- At equal specificity, highest eligible discount still wins; explicit zero is not unconditional veto
- Covers Sales and Purchase price lists with variant and currency specificity; affects Shopify product price calculator
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
