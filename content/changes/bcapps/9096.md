# #9096 Align Bill-to Name 2 editability with the other bill-to address fields on sales documents

> Bill-to Name 2 field on sales documents is now editable under the same conditions as other bill-to address fields when custom address is selected or customer differs from sell-to customer.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/9096) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-08 by Franco111000 (community contribution) · 42 files (+142 -78), 37 AL · fix · tier official · **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## What changed

- Bill-to Name 2 editability aligned with other bill-to address fields on four sales pages
- Field now editable when Custom Address is selected or bill-to customer differs from sell-to customer
- Previously editable only when Another Customer option was selected
- Identical two-property change applied across all localization layers (APAC, BE, CH, ES, FR, GB, IT, NA, NL, NO, RU)
- Extended test coverage verifies editability alongside sibling address fields
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
