# #10361 [main] Line Discount Amount Rounding Discrepancy When Transferring Project Planning Lines to Sales Invoice

> Line Discount Amount is now correctly preserved when transferring project planning lines to a sales invoice, eliminating rounding discrepancies caused by recalculation from the rounded percentage.

## What changed

- Fixed rounding discrepancy where line discount amount was recalculated from rounded percentage instead of preserving the original value
- After percentage validation, the exact line discount amount from the planning line is now transferred to the sales line
- Handles partial invoicing and credit memos through quantity ratio calculation
- Converts discount amount to invoice currency when needed
- Added test case validating that discount amount remains accurate through the transfer process
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
