# #11339 Prevent order matching for invoice lines created by Get Receipt Lines

> Matched order line functionality now rejects invoice lines created through Get Receipt Lines, preventing inconsistent matching information when working with similar purchase orders.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11339) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-09-14 by VolodySyn · 11 files (+147 -0), 10 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- CheckLineCanBeMatched validation blocks matching attempts on lines with a populated Receipt No. field
- Guard enforced at both per-line entry points (page drill-down, action) and header-opened paths (codeunit functions)
- Error message immediately informs users that matched order lines aren't supported for Get Receipt Lines-created lines
- Test coverage includes both per-line and header-path rejection scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
