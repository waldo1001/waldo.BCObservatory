# #11256 [main] Add setup option to restore Sales and Purchase Order quantities when posting returns

> Added optional settings to control whether credit memo and invoice-correction operations restore quantities on originating sales and purchase orders, with both settings enabled by default to maintain current behavior.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11256) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-11-06 by JakovljevicDusan · 99 files (+4508 -752), 67 AL · feature · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- New setup fields added to Sales & Receivables Setup and Purchases & Payables Setup tables to control quantity restoration on corrective operations
- When enabled, existing quantity-restoration behavior is preserved; when disabled, quantities remain unchanged on originating orders
- Restoration-specific confirmations and warnings display only when the setting is enabled
- Prepayment deduction reversals proceed regardless of settings to maintain order consistency
- All existing and new companies default to enabled settings, and comprehensive test coverage includes both enabled and disabled scenarios
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
