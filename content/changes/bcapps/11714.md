# #11714 [Main]-Adjust Cost creates an additional unexpected variance amount due to rounding precision during cost adjustment.

> Cost adjustment for assembly items now uses unit-amount rounding precision instead of general ledger setup precision, eliminating unexpected variance amounts from rounding differences.

[Pull request](https://github.com/microsoft/BCApps/pull/11714) · merged into `main` (BC30) on 2026-10-14 by DeepsShukla · 5 files (+101 -5), 4 AL · fix · tier official · reviewed (checked by Opus)

## What changed

- During cost adjustment, cost shares are now rounded with unit-amount precision (0.00001) instead of the G/L Setup amount rounding precision
- Removes an unexpected extra variance that rounding created during cost adjustment of assembly items
- The Inventory Adjustment codeunit is changed in the W1, APAC and RU layers, and the Asm. Inventory Adjmt. Entry table extension in W1
- Adds a regression test to SCM Costing Bugs II
- Behaviour changes for users or extensions.
