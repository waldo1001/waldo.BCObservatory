- Get-ALTool probes nested bin/win32 or bin/linux paths first, then falls back to flat bin folder
- Get-CustomAnalyzers downloads to bin/Analyzers when present, otherwise to flat bin
- DownloadAlDoc resolves aldoc in nested or flat bin locations
- Enables workspace compilation with marketplace-packaged VSIX files that use flat layout
- Mirrors BcContainerHelper's existing dual-layout support for consistency
