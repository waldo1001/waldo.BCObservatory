## Code changes

Most BCApps behaviour changes were fixes. #11998 checks that a General Posting Setup combination exists before journal invoices post, #12868 fixes a rounding variance in Adjust Cost, and #12149 makes issuing several reminders or finance charge memos with Print produce one PDF. #12552 stops repeated production order status warnings when validating No. on a sales order. In subscription billing, #12967 filters billing totals by partner so same-numbered customer and vendor contracts no longer mix, and #12767 rolls back billing lines when vendor invoice creation fails. Other fixes cover GB reverse charge sales posting (#12813), the Danish VAT period parser (#12943), Get Receipt Lines for subcontracting (#12584) and Shopify tax matches after a rate conflict (#12561). New behaviour includes credit memo recognition from PDFs in E-Documents (#12855), enforced itemization in Expense Agent (#12599) with an entitlement fix (#12763), a Czech Country/Region of Origin field (#12846), a French XML audit export on the Audit File Export framework (#9104) and Master Data Management cleanup on environment copy (#13005). #12186 merges consecutive ModifyAll calls, so where RunTrigger is passed OnModify runs once per row. #13075 adds TDS events for India and #12443 renumbers Spanish SII objects. AL-Go #2413 limits automatic Copilot review triggers to opted-in setups. The rest is BCQuality knowledge and skill work, including #246 and #155.
