## Code changes

The behaviour changes this week were mostly fixes in BCApps. VAT posting group handling that regressed since Business Central 28.4 is corrected in #13085 and #12982, and GB reverse charge sales posting is fixed in #12813. Posting now checks that a General Posting Setup combination exists (#11998), and Adjust Cost no longer creates extra rounding variance (#12868). Subscription billing got two fixes: #12967 for credit memos when customer and vendor contracts share a number, and #12767 for billing lines left behind after a failed vendor invoice. Smaller fixes cover transfer line units of measure with Direct Transfer (#12541), reminder printing (#12149), the Swiss DTA payment report (#12754) and the Danish VAT period parser (#12943). New behaviour includes credit memo recognition from PDFs in the Payables Agent (#12894) and E-Documents (#12855). The Expense Agent gained alternate approvers (#11955) and enforced itemization (#12599). French accelerated depreciation moved into W1 (#12160). Refactors include the Sales FR app (#9143) and ES SII renumbering (#12443). #13075 adds TDS events for India. In AL-Go, #2402 adds a built-in Copilot review. BCQuality received many new knowledge articles, among them #230 and #228.
