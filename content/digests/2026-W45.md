Posting fixes cover return orders linked to posted sales invoices (#13417, #13547), partial purchase invoicing with non-base units (#13396), ACY across posting groups (#13488), reverse production entry (#12520) and production order refresh (#12161). Other fixes touch Fixed Asset List refresh (#13340, #13483), reminders (#13336), financial report negative formats (#13339) and Prices Overview (#9181), and #9527 opens E-Document Emailing to other extensions. BCQuality adds an AddLoadFields article (#253) and keeps comments in evaluation samples (#227).
