# Power BI sustainability app

> The Power BI sustainability app section covers the reports, semantic model, and KPI measures for Business Central sustainability data. It answers questions about emissions (CO2, CH4, N2O, CO2e), carbon credits, baselines and targets, water and waste, and social ESG metrics.

## Overview

The Power BI Sustainability app turns Business Central sustainability data into ready-made reports for sustainability managers, executives, HR and facility staff. The reports cover emissions, progress toward net zero, comparison with baselines and targets, water and waste, and employee-related ESG metrics.

Start with the "Sustainability Power BI App Reports" page, which lists the available reports. Then open the page for the report you need. Emissions reports include Sustainability Overview, Emissions by Category and Scope, CO2e Analysis, CO2e Decomposition Tree, CO2e Key Influences and Journey to Net Zero Carbon. Realized Emissions vs Baseline and vs Target compare actuals with scorecard values. Water and Waste Analysis and Social Analysis cover facilities and employees.

For the data behind the reports, use the semantic model page (fact and dimension tables in a star schema) and the KPIs and measures page (formulas and data sources).

## Key points

- Sustainability Power BI App Reports is the entry page listing all reports in the app.
- Emissions reports: Sustainability Overview, Emissions by Category and Scope (CO2, CH4, N2O, CO2e KPIs), and CO2e Analysis (month-on-month emissions, carbon credits purchased, net emissions).
- CO2e Decomposition Tree and CO2e Key Influences help find which dimensions and factors drive emission changes.
- Journey to Net Zero Carbon tracks emissions by global dimensions and scopes, including renewable energy and purchased carbon credits.
- Realized Emissions vs Baseline and vs Target compare actual emissions with values from sustainability scorecards.
- Water and Waste Analysis compares usage by facility against configured water capacity and targets, with year-over-year and day-over-day metrics.
- Social Analysis reports employee metrics: absence hours, gender distribution, qualifications and union memberships.
- The semantic model uses fact tables (sustainability ledger entries, employee data, goals) and dimension tables (accounts, employees, emission fees, responsibility centers).
