# Power BI finance app

> The Power BI finance app section covers the embedded Finance Power BI app in Business Central: its setup, semantic model, KPI reference, and individual reports. It answers questions about receivables and payables aging, profitability, liquidity, liabilities, budget comparison, and ledger entry reconciliation.

## Overview

The Finance Power BI app embeds Power BI reports in Business Central so users can analyze finance data without leaving the client. Reports are aimed at different audiences: executives get KPI dashboards, managers get detailed analysis, and finance staff get operational and ledger-level reports.

Start with the Finance Power BI app page for an overview, then Set up the Finance Power BI app, which covers G/L account categories, mapping them to the semantic model categories, and date filters. The semantic model page explains the star schema, and the KPIs and measures page documents how each indicator is calculated and where its data comes from.

The remaining pages each describe one report. They fall into groups: financial position and performance (Financial Overview, Profitability, EBITDA and EBIT, Balance Sheet by Month, Income Statement by Month, Budget Comparison), liquidity and obligations (Liquidity Ratios, Liabilities Analysis), receivables and payables (Aged Receivables, Aged Payables, Average Collection Period, Late Payments), and detail reports (General Ledger Entries, Detailed Customer and Vendor Ledger Entries).

## Key points

- The Finance app embeds Power BI reports in Business Central, with dashboards for executives, detailed reports for managers, and operational reports for finance staff.
- Setup involves configuring G/L account categories and subcategories, mapping them to Power BI semantic model categories, and configuring date filters for performance.
- The semantic model is a star schema: fact tables for customer ledger, G/L, vendor ledger, and budget entries, and dimension tables for accounts, customers, vendors, and budgets.
- The KPIs and measures reference lists Activity, Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Liquidity, Profitability, and Customer Ledger measures with calculations and data sources.
- Aged Receivables and Aged Payables (Back Dating) reports use customizable aging periods based on posting date, due date, or document date, with payment terms grouping.
- Balance Sheet by Month and Income Statement by Month show hierarchical month-to-month views with drill-through to ledger entries.
- Budget Comparison reports Net Change, Budget Amount, Variance to Budget, and Variance to Budget % with G/L budget and shortcut dimension filtering.
- Liquidity (Current, Quick, Cash ratios) and Liabilities (Debt Ratio, Debt-to-Equity) reports assess short-term health and leverage.
