# Obsolete reports (will be removed)

> Obsolete manufacturing analytics reports in Business Central that are marked for removal. It covers cost reports (Detailed Calculation, cost shares, BOM compare, Prod. Order - Calculation) and capacity reports (machine and work center lists and load reports), and answers what each shows and what replaced it.

## Overview

This section lists older manufacturing reports that Microsoft Learn flags as obsolete and due for removal. The pages fall into two groups: cost reports and capacity reports.

The cost group includes Detailed Calculation (99000756), Item BOM Compare List (99000758), Prod. Order - Calculation (99000767), Rolled-up Cost Shares and Single-Level Cost Shares. They break down material, capacity, subcontract and overhead costs for items, BOMs and production orders. The capacity group includes the Machine Center List and Work Center List reports, plus the Load and Load/Bar reports for machine centers and work centers. These show setup data, or the workload and overloads in a period.

Start with the page for the report you are using and check whether it names a replacement. Several do: the load and load/bar reports point to Work/Machine Center Load, Item BOM Compare List to Compare Production Cost Shares, and Prod. Order - Calculation to Production Order Statistics. Plan to move to those reports.

## Key points

- Detailed Calculation (99000756) shows single-level cost for items: BOM details, routing operations, work center costs, setup and run times, and scrap.
- Item BOM Compare List (99000758) compares two production BOMs by component, cost, quantity and cost difference; replaced by Compare Production Cost Shares.
- Prod. Order - Calculation (99000767) lists expected operation and component costs; replaced by Production Order Statistics.
- Rolled-up Cost Shares shows how material, capacity and overhead costs roll up through BOM levels.
- Single-Level Cost Shares shows unit cost split into material, capacity, subcontract and overhead.
- Machine Center Load, Machine Center Load/Bar, Work Center Load and Work Center Load/Bar are obsolete and replaced by Work/Machine Center Load.
- The Load/Bar reports show overloaded centers with capacity, production order needs and efficiency bars, with a configurable overload threshold.
- Machine Center List and Work Center List show setup data and can be filtered by number, name, capacity and efficiency; Work Center List also filters by group code and unit cost.
