## Overview

This section collects the Australia-specific banking and payment pages in Business Central. Each page covers one task: comparing bank cash flow, creating check installments, paying vendors by EFT, printing bank account reconciliation reports, and printing deposit slip reports.

The pages are independent and have no subtopics. Start with the page that matches your task. For vendor payments, read the EFT page. For reconciling bank accounts, read the reconciliation reports page and the bank cash flow comparison page. The summaries are short, so check each Learn page for the detailed steps.

## Key points

- Compare bank cash flow [AU]: used to analyze and reconcile cash positions.
- Create check installments [AU]: manages payment schedules by check.
- Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) [AU]: pays vendors through Australia's EFT payment system.
- Print Bank Account Reconciliation Reports: supports reconciling bank accounts in the Australian version.
- Print deposit slip reports [AU]: processes deposit transactions according to local requirements.

## Learn pages

- [Compare bank cash flow [AU]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-compare-bank-cash-flow): Use the Bank Detail Cashflow Compare report to compare the cash flow within a specific bank for a specified period in the Australian version.
- [Create check installments [AU]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-create-check-installments): Learn how to create check installments for postdated checks, define the number of installments, interest percent, and the period for the checks in the Australian version
- [Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) [AU]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/electronic-funds-transfer-eft-): Pay vendors by using the electronic funds transfer (EFT) system in Australia.
- [Print Bank Account Reconciliation Reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-print-bank-account-reconciliation-reports): In the Australian version, the Bank Account Reconciliation report lists open bank ledger entries as either unpresented checks or unrecorded deposits.
- [Print deposit slip reports [AU]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-print-deposit-slip-reports): In the Australian version, use the Deposit Slip report to view cash and check details in a format required by the bank.
