# Tax

> Learn section Business functionality > Local functionality > Australia > Tax: 10 Microsoft Learn pages. Index of what Learn documents here, linked to Learn.

Path: [Business functionality](../../../business-functionality.md) > [Local functionality](../../local-functionality.md) > [Australia](../australia.md) > Tax · tier official · system localization · no narrative yet

## Learn pages

- [Adjust Settlement Exchange Rates for VAT Entries (Australia)](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-adjust-settlement-exchange-rates-for-vat-entries): Use a batch job to settle VAT entries based on the government exchange rate in the Australian version of Business Central.
- [Australian Tax overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/tax): This article is an overview of articles that cover how to set up and use the tax functionality in Australia.
- [Calculate and Post Withholding Tax Settlements (AU)](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-calculate-and-post-withholding-tax-settlements): Learn how to calculate and post the withholding tax (WHT) in the Australian version of Business Central.
- [Calculate goods and services tax on prepayments (AU)](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-calculate-goods-and-services-tax-on-prepayments): Learn how to calculate Goods and Services Tax (GST) on partial payments or prepayments, using the total invoice amount rather than the partial payment amount.
- [Print goods and service tax settlement reports (AU)](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-print-goods-and-service-tax-settlement-reports): Learn how to print a periodic goods and services tax (GST) settlement in the Australian version of Business Central.
- [Set up goods and services tax posting [AU]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-set-up-goods-and-service-tax-posting): Learn how to apply goods and services tax (GST) to goods and services for the Australian Taxation Office (ATO).
- [Set up revenue types for withholding tax [AU]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-set-up-revenue-types-for-withholding-tax): Revenue types serve to categorize withholding tax (WHT) entries and are utilized for WHT certificates in the Australian version.
- [Set Up Vendors Without ABN for Calculating WHT](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-set-up-vendors-without-abn-for-calculating-the-withholding-tax): In Business Central, Withholding Tax (WHT) is calculated for local vendors without an Australian Business Number (ABN), as required by tax law.
- [Set Up Withholding Tax [AU]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/how-to-set-up-withholding-tax): Learn how a company sets up withholding tax (WHT) for vendor payments on the General Ledger Setup page.
- [Withholding Tax in the Australian version](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Australia/withholding-tax): In the Australian version, Withholding Tax (WHT) is tax withheld by a company when making a payment to a vendor.
