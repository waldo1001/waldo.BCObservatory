# Germany

> Germany local functionality in Business Central: VAT reporting and ELSTER submissions, EU Sales List, Intrastat, digital audit exports, German e-invoicing, delivery reminders, and the Vendor Payments List report. It answers setup, submission, export and compliance questions for German companies.

## Overview

This section collects the features Business Central adds for German companies. The landing page lists what is available: VAT reporting and declarations, Intrastat reports, delivery reminders, digital audit data exports, German electronic invoicing (xRechnung, ZUGFeRD, Peppol) and vendor payment reports. It also describes planned regulatory requirements for 2026 release wave 2 and 2027 release wave 1.

The subtopics split the work by area. VAT covers VAT reports, ELSTER advance notifications, VAT-VIES declarations, Intrastat setup and corrections. Core finance covers GoBD/GDPdU audit exports, e-invoicing and Intrastat. Purchasing covers delivery reminders to vendors. General covers BilMoG exchange rate adjustment, EU Sales List submission via ELMA5, registration numbers on reports and the G/L Setup Information report.

Start with the Germany local functionality page for an overview and the planned requirements. Then go to the subtopic that matches your task. The Vendor Payments List report has its own page here. It is also available in Austria and Switzerland.

## Key points

- The landing page lists VAT reporting, Intrastat, delivery reminders, digital audit exports, e-invoicing and vendor payment reports, and mentions planned requirements for 2026 release wave 2 and 2027 release wave 1.
- VAT: set up and create VAT reports, send sales VAT advance notifications to ELSTER, file VAT-VIES declarations for the EU Sales List, and correct submitted reports.
- Core finance: digital audit exports (GoBD/GDPdU) and electronic invoicing in XRechnung, Peppol BIS 3 and ZUGFeRD formats.
- Intrastat reporting for German authorities is covered in both the VAT and Core finance subtopics.
- Purchasing: delivery reminders for overdue deliveries, including reminder terms, levels, text and vendor codes, and generating, reviewing and issuing reminders.
- General: currency exchange rate adjustment under BilMoG, EU Sales List submission to BZSt via ELMA5, registration numbers on reports, and the G/L Setup Information report.
- The Vendor Payments List report can be sorted by vendor or chronologically. It has standard, foreign currency amounts and posting information layouts, and shows payment status. It is available in Austria, Germany and Switzerland.
