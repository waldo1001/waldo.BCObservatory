# VAT

> Spanish VAT functionality in Business Central: Reports 340, 347 and 349, Equivalence Charges, telematic VAT statement templates and exports in text and XML, cash accounting setup, and SII setup. It answers how to set up, create and submit these declarations to Spanish tax authorities.

Path: [Business functionality](../../../business-functionality.md) > [Local functionality](../../local-functionality.md) > [Spain](../spain.md) > VAT · tier official · system finance · narrative reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

This section covers the VAT reporting tools of the Spanish version. The "VAT Reports in the Spanish Version" page gives the entry point, listing Equivalence Charge tax, telematic VAT, VAT statements and VIES declarations for EU sales. Equivalence Charges (EC) are tracked in an EC % field kept separate from the VAT % field.

Reports 340, 347 and 349 each have a concept page and a how-to page for creating the report. Report 340 lists invoices and taxes issued or received in a period. Report 347 is the annual declaration of sales and purchases, including cash payments. Report 349 is the periodic declaration of intra-community trade with EU countries.

For electronic filing, you first create text or XML templates (Transference Format pages), then export the VAT statement in the matching format. Separate pages cover cash accounting setup for Report 340 (unrealized VAT and VAT posting groups) and SII setup for VAT reporting. Start with the overview page, then go to the report or setup you need.

## Key points

- Report 340 documents sales and purchase invoices with operation codes and cash payments, monthly or quarterly; creation uses fiscal year, minimum payment amount and G/L account selection.
- Report 347 is an annual declaration based on VAT Date; the how-to covers identifying unapplied cash payments, creating the declaration and generating 347 declaration labels.
- Report 349 reports EU intra-community trade using VAT entries filtered by EU country codes and operation codes E, M and H; it can be submitted telematically or on CD-ROM, with credit memo warnings to correct.
- Equivalence Charges use the EC % field, separate from VAT %, with a combined VAT + EC % field for display.
- Telematic VAT templates are defined in text format (Transference Format page: positions, lengths, types, data sources) or XML format (XML Transference Format page: field mappings, indentation levels, value types).
- VAT statements are exported through the Generate txt file or Generate XML file actions, with filters for open or closed VAT entries, period, date and additional reporting currency.
- Cash accounting for Report 340 requires enabling Unrealized VAT in General Ledger Setup and setting up VAT Posting Setup with G/L accounts for sales and purchases.
- SII setup covers enabling the module, batch submission with automatic thresholds, marking documents as accepted, VAT exemption codes and customer NIF registration.
