# Tax

> US tax in Business Central: sales tax setup (tax groups, jurisdictions, areas, details), use and purchase tax, unrealized sales tax, and IRS 1099 and 1096 form setup, tracking, reporting and submission. It answers setup and compliance questions for US companies.

Path: [Business functionality](../../../business-functionality.md) > [Local functionality](../../local-functionality.md) > [United States](../united-states.md) > Tax · tier official · system localization · narrative reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

This section covers tax features specific to the US version of Business Central. Two areas are covered: sales and use tax, and IRS information reporting.

For sales tax, start with Basic Tax Setup in the US Version (tax groups, default tax accounts, jurisdictions, areas). Then see Sales tax in the US Version for tax details, rates and effective dates. Separate pages cover Use Tax and Purchase Tax, and unrealized sales tax with sales payment discounts.

For IRS reporting, start with the 1099 overview, then Set Up IRS 1099 Forms Reporting (assisted setup guide or manual setup). Next, Track and Review Vendor Data for IRS 1099 Forms, then IRS 1099 Forms Reporting and Submission. Form 1096 has its own setup and reporting page. A legacy workflow page applies to versions before 27.0. A regulatory page lists 1099 box format changes, available from version 24.0 and mandatory from 27.0.

## Key points

- Basic tax setup covers the non-taxable tax group code, default sales and purchase tax accounts, reverse charge for purchases, tax jurisdictions and tax areas.
- Sales tax details are set per jurisdiction and tax group, with tax type, effective date, and rates below and above the maximum.
- IRS 1099 setup is done through the IRS Forms Guide or manually: reporting periods, forms, form boxes, vendor defaults, print settings, and copying setup across companies.
- Vendor tracking uses 1099 liable marking, form numbers and form box numbers on purchase documents, with vendor form box adjustments and corrections.
- 1099 submission supports IRIS integration, magnetic media or manual methods: create form documents, release them, submit transmissions, correct errors, handle rejected transmissions, and email vendor copies.
- The overview lists 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC, 1099-INT and 1099-DIV, with IRIS electronic filing and substitute copy printing.
- Form 1096 transmits paper 1099 forms and needs the extension activated, number series, company EIN and IRS contact information.
- The legacy 1099 workflow (Update Form Boxes, IRS 1099 Code and Amount fields) applies to versions before 27.0. The Update Form Boxes action supports regulatory box changes, available from 24.0 and mandatory from 27.0.
