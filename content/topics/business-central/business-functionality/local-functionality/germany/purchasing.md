## Learn pages

- [Assign delivery reminder codes to vendors [DE]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Germany/how-to-assign-delivery-reminder-codes-to-vendors): Enable delivery reminders for overdue purchases by assigning delivery reminder terms to vendors in the German version.
- [Delivery Reminders [DE]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Germany/delivery-reminders): Delivery reminders are used to track overdue vendor shipments, and to remind vendors about overdue deliveries in the German version.
- [How to Create Delivery Reminders Manually [DE]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Germany/how-to-create-delivery-reminders-manually): This article provides examples of how to create delivery reminders manually in the German version.
- [How to Generate Delivery Reminders [DE]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Germany/how-to-generate-delivery-reminders): In the German version of Business Central, you can create delivery reminders when a purchase isn't delivered as expected.
- [Issue delivery reminders in the German version](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Germany/how-to-issue-delivery-reminders): Learn how to issue and print delivery reminders to vendors in the German version of Business Central.
- [Print test reports for delivery reminders [DE]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Germany/how-to-print-test-reports-for-delivery-reminders): After creating delivery reminders and making changes in the German version, you can print the test reports or issue the delivery reminders.
- [Set Up Delivery Reminder Terms, Levels, and Text [DE]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Germany/how-to-set-up-delivery-reminder-terms-levels-and-text): To create delivery reminders in the German version, specific setup steps are required.
- [Set Up Delivery Reminders [DE]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/Germany/how-to-set-up-delivery-reminders): Use purchase delivery reminders in the German version of Business Central to notify vendors about overdue deliveries.
