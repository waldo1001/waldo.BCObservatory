# Spain

> Spain local functionality in Business Central: VAT declarations and SII, banking and electronic payments, electronic invoices (VERI*FACTU, Cartera), core finance books and reports, and general compliance items. It answers setup and how-to questions for Spanish tax and legal requirements.

## Overview

The Spain section collects the features that adapt Business Central to Spanish tax, banking and accounting rules. It has no pages of its own and is split into five subtopics: VAT, Banking & payments, Electronic invoices, Core finance and General.

VAT is the largest group (14 pages) and covers Reports 340, 347 and 349, Equivalence Charges, telematic VAT statements, cash accounting and SII setup. Banking & payments deals with CCC bank codes, vendor payment files and the 340 cash payments declaration. Electronic invoices covers VERI*FACTU, SII invoice types and Cartera. Core finance covers official books and ASC exports, and General covers corrective invoices, due date limits, NACE codes and operation codes.

Start with General for basic company and invoice setup, then move to the subtopic that matches your task: VAT or Electronic invoices for tax reporting, Banking & payments for payment files, Core finance for statutory books.

## Key points

- VAT: Reports 340, 347 and 349, Equivalence Charges, telematic VAT statement templates with text and XML export, cash accounting and SII setup.
- Banking: CCC bank codes, bank account setup, and vendor payment exports in AEB N34, N34.1, E-PAY and SEPA formats, including voiding exports.
- Banking also covers payment days, non-payment periods and the 340 cash payments declaration.
- Electronic invoices: VERI*FACTU setup in embedded mode or through the external B2BRouter integration.
- Electronic invoices also covers SII invoice types and Cartera modules for receivables and payables bill/payment documents.
- Core finance: chart of accounts handling, transaction numbering, official account books, invoice books, income statement closing, G/L account discount handling and ASC export of financial reports.
- General: corrective invoices, legal due date limits and payment terms, NACE codes on company information, and operation codes for VAT reporting.
