## Learn pages

- [Apply General Ledger Entries [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-apply-general-ledger-entries): Learn how to apply general ledger entries to justify ledger balances on asset and liability accounts.
- [Close Years [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-close-years): Learn how to close fiscal years and manage accounting periods in the French version of Business Central.
- [Fiscal Periods and Fiscal Years (FR)](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/fiscal-periods-and-fiscal-years): A fiscal year usually consists of 12 monthly periods. In the French version of Business Central, you can have two open fiscal years simultaneously.
- [General Ledger in France](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/general-ledger): Special general ledger functionality exists for the French version of Business Central.
- [How to Close Income Statement Accounts [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-close-income-statement-accounts): Learn how to close the income statement accounts in the French version of Business Central
- [How to Fiscally Close Accounting Periods](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-fiscally-close-accounting-periods): Learn how to fiscally close the accounting period to make sure that no more general ledger entries can be posted.
- [How to Fiscally Close Years](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-fiscally-close-years): Learn the process of fiscally closing periods in a fiscal year to prevent further general ledger postings.
- [How to Post the Year-End Closing Entry [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-post-the-year-end-closing-entry): Learn how to post the year-end closing entries after you use the Close Income Statement batch job.
- [How to Specify Posting Periods [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-specify-posting-periods): Learn how to specify posting periods to limit the period in which posting is allowed.
- [How to Unapply General Ledger Entries [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-unapply-general-ledger-entries): Learn the steps to easily unapply general ledger entries using the Chart of Accounts feature in Business Central.
- [How to view ledger reconciliations](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-view-ledger-reconciliations): Learn how to view general ledger reconciliation with the customer ledger and vendor ledger in the French version of Business Central.
- [Open a New Fiscal Year [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-open-a-new-fiscal-year-duplicate): Learn the steps to open a fiscal year and define its accounting periods in the French version of Business Central.
- [Print General Ledger Reports [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-print-general-ledger-reports): Learn how to print general ledger reports that comply with financial accounting standards. These reports include details about ledger, customer, vendor, and bank entries.
- [Reopen Accounting Periods [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/how-to-reopen-accounting-periods): Learn how to reopen a fiscally closed accounting period to post general ledger entries in the French version of Business Central.
- [Year End Processes Overview [FR]](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/LocalFunctionality/France/year-end-processes-overview): Overview of the fiscal year-end closing processes in the French version of Business Central, including compliance with French regulations.
