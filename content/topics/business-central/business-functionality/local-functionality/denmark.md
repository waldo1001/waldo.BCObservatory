# Denmark

> Denmark local functionality in Business Central covers Danish compliance: auditing and SAF-T export, VAT reporting, banking and payments with FIK, OIOUBL electronic invoicing, and payroll data definitions. It answers questions about setting up and using these Danish-specific features.

Path: [Business functionality](../../business-functionality.md) > [Local functionality](../local-functionality.md) > Denmark · tier official · system localization · narrative reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

This section describes what Business Central adds for Denmark. The landing page frames it around compliance with the Danish bookkeeping act, including 5-year data retention and mandatory digital vouchers, and points to the main feature areas.

Four subtopics cover the areas in detail: Auditing (Regnskab Basis CSV export, SAF-T export and import, Danish standard chart of accounts), VAT (VAT Reconciliation Report, VAT registration number format for Intrastat, VAT-VIES and EC Sales List), Banking & payments (Payments and Reconciliations (DK) extension and FIK transaction text values), and Electronic invoicing (OIOUBL setup, customers, profiles, XML export). A separate page covers the Payroll Data Definitions (DK) extension.

Start with the Denmark local functionality page for the overview, then go to the subtopic that matches your task. For audit exports, check the standard chart of accounts and GL account mapping first.

## Key points

- The landing page lists compliance with the bookkeeping act, 5-year data retention and mandatory digital vouchers, and is tagged 2026 release wave 2.
- Auditing covers Regnskab Basis CSV export and SAF-T export and import, and needs the Danish standard chart of accounts for GL account mapping.
- VAT covers the VAT Reconciliation Report, VAT registration number formatting for Intrastat, and VAT-VIES reporting with the EC Sales List.
- Banking uses the Payments and Reconciliations (DK) extension with supported bank file formats, plus FIK transaction text values in the payment reconciliation journal.
- Electronic invoicing uses the OIOUBL format for the Danish public sector, with extension setup, customers and profiles, and XML document creation and export.
- Payroll Data Definitions (DK) imports payroll transactions from Danish payroll service providers using data exchange definitions.
- The payroll extension maps transaction types to general ledger accounts and uses a payroll trans. import format.
