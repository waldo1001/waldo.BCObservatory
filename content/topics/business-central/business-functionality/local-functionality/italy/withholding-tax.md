# Withholding tax

> Withholding tax in the Italian version of Business Central: creating withholding tax codes and assigning them to vendors, setting tax rates with starting dates, calculating tax on purchase invoices, generating withholding tax entries automatically when vendor payments are processed, and printing withholding tax reports for amounts payable to the Italian government. Use it for Italian setup and reporting questions.

## Overview

This section covers withholding tax for Italian operations. It has two pages and no subtopics: one for setup and one for reporting.

Start with the setup page. It explains how to create withholding tax codes, assign them to vendors, and configure tax rates with starting dates. It then shows how the tax is calculated on purchase invoices and how withholding tax entries are generated automatically when you process vendor payments. Residency and contribution details are also tracked.

After the setup is in place and payments are processed, use the reporting page. It describes how to print withholding tax reports to display and manage the withholding taxes payable to the Italian government. The report uses a Reference Month and Reference Year and has options for detailed information (Print Details) and final processing (Final Printing).

## Key points

- Withholding tax codes are created first and then assigned to vendors.
- Tax rates can be configured with starting dates.
- Withholding tax is calculated on purchase invoices.
- Processing vendor payments automatically generates withholding tax entries.
- Residency and contribution details are tracked as part of the setup.
- The report is printed for a Reference Month and Reference Year.
- The Print Details option gives detailed information in the report.
- Final Printing is the option for final processing of the report.
