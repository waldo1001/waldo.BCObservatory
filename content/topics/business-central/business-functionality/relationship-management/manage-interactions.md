# Manage interactions

> Interaction management in Business Central relationship management: recording communications with contacts and segments, using interaction templates, handling attachments, and setting up automatic recording for documents, emails, and phone calls. It answers how-to and setup questions about tracking communication history.

Path: [Business functionality](../../business-functionality.md) > [Relationship management](../relationship-management.md) > Manage interactions · tier official · system crm · narrative reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

This section explains how to track communications with business contacts. An interaction can be an email, letter, telephone call, or meeting. Interactions are based on interaction templates, and the log entries record details such as communication mode, initiator, costs, and status.

Interactions can be created manually for a single contact or for a segment. You can log them, create follow-ups, or postpone them and resume later. They can also be recorded automatically when sales, purchase, and service documents are created, posted, or printed. Emails and phone calls can be tracked too.

Start with "Manage Interactions with Your Contacts" for the overview of templates and logs. Then use "Create and Manage Interactions with Contacts" and "Create Interactions for Contacts and Segments" for day-to-day work. "Set Up Automatic Recording of Interactions" covers the Interaction Template Setup page. "Manage Attachments for Contact Interactions" covers files on log entries and templates.

## Key points

- Interactions track emails, letters, telephone calls, and meetings with contacts.
- Interaction templates define the interaction; use the Create Interaction action with an Interaction Template Code.
- For segments, you can use Log Segment, Create Follow-up Segment, or postpone and resume the interaction later.
- Interactions can be recorded automatically when sales, purchase, and service documents are created, posted, or printed.
- Automatic recording is configured on the Interaction Template Setup page and covers sales documents, purchase documents, service orders, email, and phone calls.
- Log entries show communication mode, initiator, costs, and status.
- Attachments on log entries and templates can be opened, imported, exported, removed, and copied.
- Attachments support Word template integration.
