# Microsoft Teams

> Microsoft Teams integration with Business Central: installing the Business Central app, searching contacts, sharing records and page links, adding Business Central tabs, and administering licensing, permissions, deployment and privacy. It answers setup, usage and access questions for Teams users and administrators.

## Overview

This section describes how Business Central works inside Microsoft Teams through the Business Central app. Users can look up customers, vendors and prospects, share records as interactive cards or page links in conversations, and add Business Central tabs to channels and chats.

The pages split into user tasks and administrator tasks. User pages cover installing the app (from the Teams marketplace or from within Business Central), searching contacts, sharing records, adding tabs, and changing environment or company in Settings. Administrator pages cover licensing, permissions, centralized deployment, messaging policies, card privacy, and letting Microsoft 365 license holders without a Business Central license see shared records.

Start with the overview page, "Integrate Business Central with Microsoft Teams", then use the install page. Administrators should read "Manage Microsoft Teams Integration with Business Central" and the page on access without a Business Central license.

## Key points

- The Business Central app for Teams can be installed from the Teams marketplace or from within Business Central. It needs an online user account and administrator-granted access to Business Central data.
- Contacts (customers, vendors, prospects) can be searched from the Teams command box or the message compose area. Results show contact details, interaction history and outstanding payments, and contact cards can be shared.
- Records can be shared as interactive cards or as page links in Teams conversations. Card details can be viewed in Teams, and data can be edited there.
- A Business Central tab can be added to Teams channels and chats to show list and card pages, with environment and company switching and view management.
- Microsoft 365 license holders without a Business Central license get read-only access in the tab. Business Central licenses give full functionality based on assigned permissions.
- Administrators can configure environments so users without a Business Central license can view shared records in Teams chat and channels, using table access control.
- Administration covers centralized deployment of the app, record data visibility, and messaging policies for card sharing.
- The Settings page in the Teams app lets users switch environment and company, view their account, and sign out and in.
