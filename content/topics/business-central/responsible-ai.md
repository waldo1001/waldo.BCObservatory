# Responsible AI

> Responsible AI documentation for Business Central Copilot features and agents. It answers questions about what each AI feature does, its limitations, safety controls, data handling, evaluation, and how to review output or give feedback.

## Overview

This section collects Responsible AI FAQs and application cards, one per AI feature in Business Central. Each page explains what the feature does, how it was evaluated, its limitations, and how users should review and give feedback on results. There are no subtopics, so all content sits on these pages.

The pages fall into a few groups. Copilot assists cover bank reconciliation, number series, sales lines, marketing text, analysis assist, summarize, Autofill and Advanced Tell Me. Agents cover Sales Order Agent, Payables Agent and Expense Agent. Application cards cover Copilot in Business Central and Shopify Tax Matching. Two pages describe features that are retired or deprecated: item substitutions, and e-document matching with purchase orders (replaced by the Payables Agent).

Start with the index page, "Responsible AI FAQs for Dynamics 365 Business Central", to find the feature you need. Read the Copilot application card for the general picture of permissions and grounded answers. Then open the page for the specific feature.

## Key points

- The Copilot application card describes how Copilot answers natural-language questions about Business Central data within user permissions, with references to the source information.
- Several features are marked preview: bank reconciliation assist, Suggest Number Series, sales line suggestions, analysis assist, Autofill (version 26.4), Expense Agent and summarize.
- Agents covered: Sales Order Agent (email requests to quotes and orders), Payables Agent (vendor invoices and credit notes as draft purchase documents, no posting), and Expense Agent (receipts to draft expense reports for human review).
- Shopify Tax Matching maps free-text Shopify tax descriptions to Business Central tax jurisdictions for US-based organizations, with confidence indicators, rate-conflict detection, and review and approval.
- Marketing Text Suggestions generates product descriptions from item attributes and categories, with tone, emphasis and format settings.
- Advanced Tell Me adds semantic search with synonyms and acronyms through an Advanced tab in the Tell Me pane.
- The Copilot suggest item substitutions feature is retired, so use manual substitution setup. E-documents Matching Assistance is deprecated in favor of the Payables Agent.
- Autofill FAQ covers Bing web search, clipboard suggestions, content filtering, data residency and security.
