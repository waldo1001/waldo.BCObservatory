# Business process walkthroughs

> Business process walkthroughs are step-by-step end-to-end scenarios in the CRONUS demonstration company. They answer questions about how to practice tasks such as sales campaigns, prepayments, approval workflows, project WIP, warehouse picking, cash flow forecasts and the Shopify Connector.

Path: [Get started](../../get-started.md) > [Learn](../learn.md) > Business process walkthroughs · tier official · system none · narrative reviewed (checked by Opus)

## Overview

The walkthroughs let you practice common Business Central tasks from start to finish in the CRONUS demonstration company. Each page covers one scenario and has no subtopics, so the pages stand alone and can be read in any order.

The scenarios fall into a few areas. Sales and marketing: conducting a sales campaign and setting up and invoicing sales prepayments. Purchasing and finance: a purchase approval workflow and cash flow forecasts built from financial reports. Projects: managing projects and calculating work in process. Operations and integration: basic warehouse picking and shipping, and the Shopify Connector.

Start with the overview page to see the full list, then pick the walkthrough that matches the task you want to learn or test.

## Key points

- All walkthroughs use the CRONUS demonstration company for hands-on practice.
- Sales campaign walkthrough: campaign setup, contact segmentation, data mining, interaction logging, letters and email, recording responses.
- Sales prepayments walkthrough: prepayment percentages, prepayment invoices, posting, final invoice, and job queue automation.
- Purchase approval workflow walkthrough: approval users, amount limits, notification methods, workflow templates, and internal notes.
- Project walkthroughs: project setup, fixed pricing, copying, payment by installments, and WIP calculation, posting to G/L and reversal.
- Cash flow forecast walkthrough: row and column definitions, cash flow accounts, forecast formulas, and printing.
- Warehouse walkthrough: inventory picks for method B with bin setup and warehouse release, without separate shipment posting.
- Shopify Connector walkthrough: product and inventory sync, customer import, order sync, prices, discounts, and B2B flows.
