# Troubleshooting and FAQs

## Overview

This section collects short question-and-answer pages for Business Central. It has no subtopics. It holds four standalone pages, each focused on one area where users and admins often need quick answers.

Three pages are FAQs. The Teams FAQ covers sign-in, tabs, contact search, cards for Business Central links, the details window, and guest collaboration. The Tell Me FAQ explains how users find actions, pages, reports and records, and how permissions and extensions affect results. The Copilot FAQ covers licensing, data access, capabilities, and troubleshooting for the Copilot experience that replaces Chat with Copilot starting in version 29.

The fourth page lists minimum requirements for Business Central online, including browsers, mobile operating systems, Outlook, Teams, and the Excel add-in. Start there when planning a rollout or diagnosing a client problem. Use the FAQ pages when a question concerns a specific feature.

## Key points

- Teams FAQ covers app installation and sign-in, Business Central tabs in Teams, contact search, card expansion for links, the details window, and the Share to Teams action.
- Tell Me searches for actions, pages, and reports, and can also search company data through the Advanced tab.
- Tell Me results are filtered by user permissions, work on mobile, and can include custom extensions.
- The Copilot FAQ describes the new Copilot experience that replaces Chat with Copilot starting in version 29.
- Copilot has read-only access to Business Central data, uses Work IQ for Microsoft 365 data, and has chat capability control, region and language support, and admin access controls.
- Minimum requirements list supported browsers: Edge, Chrome, Firefox, and Safari.
- Mobile support covers the latest three major versions of Android and iOS.
- Requirements also cover Outlook, Teams desktop and web, the Excel add-in, Administration Center browsers, and on-premises considerations.
