# Get the most out of Microsoft 365 apps

> Microsoft 365 integration in Business Central: working with Business Central data in Excel, Word, OneDrive, Outlook and Teams. It answers questions on editing lists in Excel, mail merge with Word templates, sharing files and records, and syncing contacts to Outlook.

## Overview

This section covers how Business Central works together with Microsoft 365 apps so users can stay in familiar tools. Each page deals with one app: Excel, Word, OneDrive, Outlook and Teams. The pages are independent, so you can read only the one that matches your task.

Start with the Excel page if you need to export or edit list data, or with the Word templates page for bulk communication with contacts, customers and vendors. The OneDrive and Teams pages cover sharing files and records with colleagues. The Outlook page covers saving Business Central contacts to Microsoft 365 so they show up in Outlook and Teams.

## Key points

- Excel: use Open in Excel or Edit in Excel on lists. Edit in Excel uses the Add-in pane to publish changes back, and Copilot in Excel can help with data cleaning, preparation and analysis.
- Word templates: use entities as data sources, add merge fields, and apply the template to generate documents or send emails. Related and unrelated entities and field prefixes are supported.
- OneDrive: open Business Central files in OneDrive to edit them in online Excel, Word or PowerPoint. Share with specific people or via a link (Send link, Copy link).
- OneDrive sharing includes file version history and access control.
- Teams: share a Business Central record as an interactive card in a conversation, or share or copy a page link. Card details can be viewed and data edited in Teams.
- Outlook: the Synchronize with Outlook action saves contacts to Microsoft 365 for use in Outlook and Teams. You can filter contacts and choose the contacts folder.
- Contact sync supports full and delta synchronization, with optional two-way synchronization. The page is tied to 2026 release wave 1 (v28).
