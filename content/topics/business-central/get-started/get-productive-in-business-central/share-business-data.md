# Share business data

> Sharing business data from Business Central: copying links, sharing records to Microsoft Teams, opening and sharing files in OneDrive, and viewing or editing lists in Excel. It answers how-to questions about each sharing method and when to use it.

Path: [Get started](../../get-started.md) > [Get productive in Business Central](../get-productive-in-business-central.md) > Share business data · tier official · system none · narrative **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## Overview

This section describes the ways users can get Business Central data in front of other people or into other Microsoft 365 apps. It starts with an overview page that lists the main methods: copy link, share to Teams, share through OneDrive, open in Excel, and copy rows or tables, including filtered views.

Three pages go deeper. One covers sharing records and page links in Teams as interactive cards. One covers opening Business Central files in OneDrive and sharing them with specific people or by link. One covers exporting and editing lists in Excel with the Open in Excel and Edit in Excel actions, including Copilot in Excel.

Start with the overview page to pick a method, then go to the page for that method.

## Key points

- Copy Link and Share to Teams let users send a page or record to colleagues without leaving their workflow.
- In Teams, a shared Business Central card is interactive: users can view card details and edit data in Teams.
- Open in OneDrive copies a file there so it can be edited in online Excel, Word, or PowerPoint.
- OneDrive sharing offers the Share action, Send link, and Copy link, with access control and file version history.
- Open in Excel exports a list, while Edit in Excel lets users change data and publish it back through the Excel Add-in pane.
- Copilot in Excel can help with data cleaning, preparation, analysis, and multi-step workflows.
- Rows or tables can be copied and shared, and filtered views can be shared.
