## Learn pages

- [Authoring guide for Business Central](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/help/writing-guide): Tips and tricks for authoring in MarkDown files when you extend or customize the Help for Dynamics 365 Business Central.
- [Configure context-sensitive help](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/help/context-sensitive-help): Learn about how to add context-sensitive help to your Business Central solution, both as an app publisher, an administrator, and as a developer.
- [Configure the Help experience](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/deployment/configure-help): Learn how to give your customers access to the right Help content for Business Central online and on-premises.
- [Contribute to the Help](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/help/contributor-guide): Tips and tricks for working with the GitHub repos and Markdown files for Dynamics 365 Business Central.
- [Migrate legacy help to the Business Central format](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/upgrade/migrate-help): Reuse your existing Help content from Dynamics NAV or other products, and make it available to your Business Central solution.
- [User assistance model](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/user-assistance): Learn about the user assistance model for Dynamics 365 Business Central and how your solution can comply with it.
