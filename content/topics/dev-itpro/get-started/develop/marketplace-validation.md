# Marketplace validation

> Marketplace validation covers what Business Central apps must meet before and during Microsoft marketplace submission. It answers questions on the technical validation checklist, marketing validation rules, and landing page and video guidance, plus a technical validation FAQ.

Path: [Get started](../../get-started.md) > [Develop](../develop.md) > Marketplace validation · tier official · system none · narrative **unreviewed** (model text not yet checked)

## Overview

This section helps partners get a Business Central app through marketplace validation. It has two sides: technical validation of the extension, and marketing validation of the offer listing. A third group of pages gives practical advice on selling the app once it is listed.

Start with the Technical validation checklist. It lists the mandatory requirements for extensions before submission and explains how validation is done, including self-validation with BcContainerHelper and AppSourceCop analysis. The Marketing validation FAQ covers the listing side: what to state about editions, countries, languages, branding, and help pages. The Marketplace technical validation FAQ subtopic (10 pages) answers detailed questions on submission, app identity, code-signing, affixes, and more.

The landing page and video pages are guidance for marketing. They are not validation requirements. Use them after the listing basics are settled.

## Key points

- The Technical validation checklist lists mandatory requirements before submission, including manifest validation, affix registration, digital code signing, permission sets, and extension publishing.
- Self-validation is possible with BcContainerHelper and AppSourceCop analysis.
- The Marketing validation FAQ requires statements on supported editions, countries/regions, and languages.
- Marketing validation also covers help and support pages, product naming conventions, and marketplace badge usage, and it describes the manual validation process.
- The technical validation FAQ subtopic has 10 pages. They cover app identity, code-signing, affixes and ID ranges, Application Insights, previews, offer types, dependencies, licensing, and support channels.
- The sales landing page guide covers layout, headline copy, pain-based messaging, product benefits, customer testimonials, and video demonstrations.
- The video guide describes why, how, and what videos and customer testimony. It also covers video structure and persona targeting.
