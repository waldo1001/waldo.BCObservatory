# Split your expenses

> Expense Agent: Split your expenses. Announced, GA 2027-04 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Split a single expense across multiple allocations so costs are accurately assigned to the appropriate expense user, project, department, customer, or expense category. Expense Agent helps you define the split and ensures the allocations add up to the original expense amount. Each allocation is recorded separately for reporting and accounting while remaining linked to the original expense. Benefits • Accurately distribute expenses across multiple users, projects, departments, customers, or expense categories.
