# Data mirroring to Microsoft Fabric

> Data mirroring to Microsoft Fabric. Announced, GA 2027-04 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Connect Business Central to Microsoft Fabric to make business data available for analytics, reporting, and data integration. Choose the Business Central companies and tables to synchronise, and make the data available in OneLake for use with Fabric IQ, Power BI, Data Factory, and other analytics experiences. Get started quickly with ready-made sample Power BI apps, while built-in logging provides visibility into synchronisation health and helps troubleshoot issues.

[Microsoft 365 roadmap 574126](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=574126) · no area · announced · roadmap status "In development", phase General Availability

## What Microsoft says

Connect Business Central to Microsoft Fabric to make business data available for analytics, reporting, and data integration. Choose the Business Central companies and tables to synchronise, and make the data available in OneLake for use with Fabric IQ, Power BI, Data Factory, and other analytics experiences. Get started quickly with ready-made sample Power BI apps, while built-in logging provides visibility into synchronisation health and helps troubleshoot issues.
