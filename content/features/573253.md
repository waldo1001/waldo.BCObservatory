# More countries and languages

> Expense Agent: More countries and languages. In preview, GA 2026-10 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Expense Agent supports more languages and regions, enabling an experience for users in all countries/regions where Business Central is available, and adding the following languages: Czech, Dutch, Finnish, Icelandic, Italian, Norwegian, and Swedish.
