# Set expense spend limits in Expense Management

> Expense Agent: Set expense spend limits in Expense Management. Announced, GA 2027-04 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Take control of employee spending earlier in the expense process with configurable spend limits. Define thresholds that reflect your organization’s expense policies and apply them by time period for individual employees, teams, or the whole company. Expenses that exceed predefined limits can be prevented or flagged, with immediate feedback during expense creation. Benefits • Proactively manage expenses before reimbursement and accounting processes begin.

## What Microsoft says

Take control of employee spending earlier in the expense process with configurable spend limits. Define thresholds that reflect your organization’s expense policies and apply them by time period for individual employees, teams, or the whole company. Expenses that exceed predefined limits can be prevented or flagged, with immediate feedback during expense creation. Benefits • Proactively manage expenses before reimbursement and accounting processes begin. • Reduce approval rework by giving employees immediate feedback when expenses exceed defined thresholds. • Support budget adherence across employees, teams, and the organization with configurable spending limits.
