# Adding invited guests from calendar as participants to your expense

> Expense Agent: Adding invited guests from calendar as participants to your expense. Announced, GA 2027-04 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Spend less time manually adding attendees to meal-related business expenses. Expense Agent can match the expense to the relevant Outlook calendar event and add invited guests as expense participants, helping keep participant information aligned with the meeting invitation. Benefits • Automatically identifies relevant Outlook calendar meetings when expense participants are required. • Retrieves invited attendees from the matching calendar invitation.

## What Microsoft says

Spend less time manually adding attendees to meal-related business expenses.
