# specify when receipts must contain a VAT number

> Expense Agent: specify when receipts must contain a VAT number. Announced, GA 2027-05 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Help ensure expense receipts include required company VAT information by applying your organization’s receipt validation rules. Expense Agent can check submitted receipts for a valid company VAT number (Tax ID) when your configured rules require one, and flag receipts for review or prevent them from moving forward based on your policies. Benefits • Configure when a company VAT number is required on submitted expense receipts.

[Microsoft 365 roadmap 573611](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=573611) · Expense Agent · announced · roadmap status "In development", phase General Availability

## What Microsoft says

Help ensure expense receipts include required company VAT information by applying your organization’s receipt validation rules. Expense Agent can check submitted receipts for a valid company VAT number (Tax ID) when your configured rules require one, and flag receipts for review or prevent them from moving forward based on your policies. Benefits • Configure when a company VAT number is required on submitted expense receipts. • Automatically validate required VAT information during expense processing. • Identify receipts that do not meet configured tax identification requirements and handle them according to your policies.
