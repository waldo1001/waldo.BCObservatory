# Add EUDR certification information

> Sustainability Management: Add EUDR certification information. Announced, GA 2026-11 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Record EUDR certification information for items under the EU Deforestation Regulation. Different lots of the same item can carry different certificates, origins and validity, it is stored on Lot No. Information, not on the item. An item is opted in with a single EUDR Relevant flag on the Item Card, which enforces lot tracking with lot information. Certificate data can be printed on a new EUDR Sales Invoice.

[Microsoft 365 roadmap 573251](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=573251) · Sustainability Management · announced · roadmap status "In development", phase General Availability

## What Microsoft says

Record EUDR certification information for items under the EU Deforestation Regulation. Different lots of the same item can carry different certificates, origins and validity, it is stored on Lot No. Information, not on the item. An item is opted in with a single EUDR Relevant flag on the Item Card, which enforces lot tracking with lot information. Certificate data can be printed on a new EUDR Sales Invoice. Certificate fields: EUDR Certificate No.; Certification Scheme; Valid From; Valid To; Country of Production; DDS Reference Number; DDS Verification No.
