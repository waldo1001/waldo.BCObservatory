# Add an interim approver when you submit an expense report

> Expense Agent: Add an interim approver when you submit an expense report. Announced, GA 2026-11 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). This feature introduces support for a simple interim approval scenario for expense reports. When someone submits an expense report, organizations can designate an interim approver to review and approve expenses before the final approver can review and approve. Interim approver must review expense reports to confirm whether they're complete and compliant before final approver.
