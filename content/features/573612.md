# Self-billing enablement

> E-Documents: Self-billing enablement. In preview, GA 2027-01 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Streamline self-billing by automatically creating, managing, and exchanging electronic documents for self-billed invoices as part of the invoice lifecycle. This reduces manual document handling while helping ensure self-billed transactions include the appropriate electronic documents in standardized formats used by local regulations and trading partners. Benefits • Automate electronic document generation and handling for self-billed invoices.

[Microsoft 365 roadmap 573612](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=573612) · E-Documents · in preview · roadmap status "In development", phase General Availability

## What Microsoft says

Streamline self-billing by automatically creating, managing, and exchanging electronic documents for self-billed invoices as part of the invoice lifecycle. This reduces manual document handling while helping ensure self-billed transactions include the appropriate electronic documents in standardized formats used by local regulations and trading partners. Benefits • Automate electronic document generation and handling for self-billed invoices. • Integrate electronic documents directly into the self-billing invoice lifecycle. • Support consistent vendor communications and the use of standardized electronic document formats.
