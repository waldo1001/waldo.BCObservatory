# Track AL compiler diagnostics in automated builds

> Development: Track AL compiler diagnostics in automated builds. Generally available, GA 2026-10 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). "Have ALTool workspace builds write structured, project-specific compiler error logs that automated systems can retain, analyze, and use for code-quality tracking. Business value: Development teams can retain structured compiler and analyzer results from workspace builds, making it easier to track code-quality regressions and integrate AL diagnostics with automated alerting and reporting systems."
