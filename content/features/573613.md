# Cross-environment Master Data Management

> Finance: Cross-environment Master Data Management. In preview, GA 2027-01 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Keep critical business data consistent across environments with cross-environment master data management. Centrally manage and govern master data—including customers, vendors, products, and reference data—and keep it synchronized wherever it is used, reducing duplicate maintenance and simplifying administration. Benefits • Maintain a single source of truth for master data across development, test, and production environments.

## What Microsoft says

Keep critical business data consistent across environments with cross-environment master data management. Centrally manage and govern master data—including customers, vendors, products, and reference data—and keep it synchronized wherever it is used, reducing duplicate maintenance and simplifying administration. Benefits • Maintain a single source of truth for master data across development, test, and production environments. • Reduce manual effort and inconsistent records by synchronizing master data across environments. • Improve data accuracy and reliability with standardized data definitions and governance policies, while supporting faster environment setup and smoother application lifecycle management.
