# Fill forms faster with Autofill - Auto paste

> Copilot and agents: Fill forms faster with Autofill - Auto paste. Announced, GA 2027-04 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Save time entering information into Business Central with Auto paste. Building on Autofill, Auto paste uses AI to identify relevant information from text or HTML copied to your clipboard and map it to the appropriate fields on a Business Central page. Benefits Reduce manual data entry when bringing information from external sources into Business Central. Populate multiple relevant fields from copied content instead of copying and pasting individual values.

## What Microsoft says

Save time entering information into Business Central with Auto paste. Building on Autofill, Auto paste uses AI to identify relevant information from text or HTML copied to your clipboard and map it to the appropriate fields on a Business Central page. Benefits Reduce manual data entry when bringing information from external sources into Business Central. Populate multiple relevant fields from copied content instead of copying and pasting individual values. Extend AI-assisted data entry to more everyday Business Central workflows.
