# Configure and use bonded locations for excise duty calculations

> Finance: Configure and use bonded locations for excise duty calculations. In preview, GA 2027-01 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Reduce manual work and support accurate excise duty handling by applying bonded-location rules to inventory. Designate warehouses or storage locations as bonded so excise duties can be deferred while goods remain in bonded storage and assessed when goods move to non-bonded locations or enter taxable circulation, according to configured tax rules. Benefits • Configure specific warehouses or storage locations for bonded inventory and deferred excise duty treatment.

[Microsoft 365 roadmap 573615](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=573615) · Finance · in preview · roadmap status "In development", phase General Availability

## What Microsoft says

Reduce manual work and support accurate excise duty handling by applying bonded-location rules to inventory. Designate warehouses or storage locations as bonded so excise duties can be deferred while goods remain in bonded storage and assessed when goods move to non-bonded locations or enter taxable circulation, according to configured tax rules. Benefits • Configure specific warehouses or storage locations for bonded inventory and deferred excise duty treatment. • Account for bonded location status when calculating excise duties on inventory transactions. • Automatically assess duties when goods leave bonded storage or enter taxable circulation.
