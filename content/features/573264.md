# Use batch totals for payment journals

> Finance: Use batch totals for payment journals. Announced, GA 2026-11 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). The Batch Total field on the Payment Journal page enhances payment processing by enabling you to work with and review a cumulative total for a payment journal batch. By surfacing batch-level totals directly in payment journals, organizations gain better control over outgoing payments and can streamline review and approval processes.
