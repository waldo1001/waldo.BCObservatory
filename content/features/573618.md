# Automate employee payments with ACH bank integration

> USA: Automate employee payments with ACH bank integration. Announced, GA 2027-02 (Microsoft 365 roadmap). Organizations in the United States can simplify employee expense reimbursements by paying approved claims directly to employees’ bank accounts through ACH. When used with the Expense Agent, approved reimbursements can be processed automatically, reducing manual payment handling and helping employees get reimbursed faster and more accurately.
