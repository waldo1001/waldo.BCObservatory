## Procedures

- `InsertCheck(var CheckLedgEntry: Record "Check Ledger Entry"; RecordIdToPrint: RecordID)`: Creates a new check ledger entry with sequential numbering and duplicate validation. Validates check number uniqueness within the bank account before insertion.
- `VoidCheck(var GenJnlLine: Record "Gen. Journal Line")`: Voids a check by creating reversing entries and updating check ledger entry status. Handles currency conversion and validates payment document types.
- `FinancialVoidCheck(var CheckLedgEntry: Record "Check Ledger Entry")`: Performs financial voiding of a posted check by creating offsetting journal entries. Unapplies vendor/customer invoices and reverses all financial impacts while maintaining audit trail.
- `GenJournalLineGetSystemIdFromRecordId(GenJournalLineRecordId: RecordId): Guid`
- `ProcessElectronicPayment(var GenJournalLine: Record "Gen. Journal Line"; WhichProcess: Option ,Void,Transmit)`: Processes electronic payment transmission or voiding based on specified operation type. Manages electronic payment status updates and data exchange integration.
