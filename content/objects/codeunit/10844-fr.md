## Procedures

- `MigratePaymentData()` (internal): Copies all French payment data of the current company from the base application tables and fields into the tables and fields of the Payment Management FR app.
- `RemapPaymentStepObjectIDs()` (internal): Points the payment steps at the report and XMLport objects of the Payment Management FR app. The migrated payment steps still refer to the object IDs of the base application objects, which were re-created in the app under new IDs. Object IDs that are not mapped, and the value 0, are left untouched. ...
- `CountRecordsToMigrate(var TempDocumentEntry: Record "Document Entry" temporary)` (internal): Counts the records that the data update will migrate, so that they can be reviewed before the update is started. The counted tables are the tables that migrates.
