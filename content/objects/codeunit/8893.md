## Procedures

- `GetDefaultEmailAccount(var EmailAccount: Record "Email Account"): Boolean`
- `GetEmailAccount(Scenario: Enum "Email Scenario"; var EmailAccount: Record "Email Account"): Boolean`: Gets the email account used by the given email scenario. If the no account is defined for the provided scenario, the default account (if defined) will be returned.

- `UnassignScenario(Scenario: Enum "Email Scenario")`: Unassign an email scenario. The scenario will then use the default email account.
