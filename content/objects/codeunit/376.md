## Procedures

- `ToggleReconNo(var CheckLedgEntry: Record "Check Ledger Entry"; var BankAccReconLine: Record "Bank Acc. Reconciliation Line"; ChangeAmount: Boolean)`: Toggles reconciliation number assignment for a check ledger entry. Links or unlinks the check entry from the bank reconciliation line.
- `SetReconNo(var CheckLedgEntry: Record "Check Ledger Entry"; var BankAccReconLine: Record "Bank Acc. Reconciliation Line")`: Assigns a reconciliation number to a check ledger entry. Creates the connection between the check and bank reconciliation line.
- `RemoveReconNo(var CheckLedgEntry: Record "Check Ledger Entry"; var BankAccReconLine: Record "Bank Acc. Reconciliation Line"; Test: Boolean)`: Removes reconciliation number from a check ledger entry. Disconnects the check entry from the bank reconciliation line.
- `RemoveApplication(var CheckLedgerEntry: Record "Check Ledger Entry")`: Removes application from a check ledger entry. Clears reconciliation references and updates related bank reconciliation line.
