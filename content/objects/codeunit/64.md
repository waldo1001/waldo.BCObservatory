## Procedures

- `CreateInvLines(var SalesShptLine2: Record "Sales Shipment Line")`: Creates sales invoice lines from the specified sales shipment lines.
- `InsertInvoiceLineFromShipmentLine(var SalesShptLine2: Record "Sales Shipment Line"; TransferLine: Boolean; var PrepmtAmtToDeductRounding: Decimal)`: Inserts a single sales invoice line from a shipment line.
- `SetSalesHeader(var SalesHeader2: Record "Sales Header")`: Sets the sales header for creating invoice lines from shipments.
- `UpdateItemChargeLines()`: Updates item charge line assignments for invoiced shipment lines.
- `GetItemChargeAssgnt(var SalesShptLine: Record "Sales Shipment Line"; QtyToInvoice: Decimal)`: Retrieves item charge assignments from a sales order for a shipment line.
- `CalcInvoiceDiscount(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line")`: Calculates the invoice discount for a sales line based on sales setup.
- `CalcUpdatePrepmtAmtToDeductRounding(SalesShptLine: Record "Sales Shipment Line"; SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"; var RoundingAmount: Decimal)`: Calculates and updates the prepayment amount to deduct rounding for a shipment line.
- `AdjustPrepmtAmtToDeductRounding(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"; RoundingAmount: Decimal)`: Adjusts the prepayment amount to deduct by applying any rounding differences.
- `CheckSalesShptLineVATBusPostingGroup(SalesShptLine: Record "Sales Shipment Line"; SalesHeader: Record "Sales Header")`: Validates that the VAT business posting group on the shipment line matches the sales header.
- `GetSalesOrderInvoices(var TempSalesInvoiceHeader: Record "Sales Invoice Header" temporary; OrderNo: Code[20])`: Retrieves a list of posted sales invoices related to a specific sales order.
- `CopyDocumentAttachments(var SalesShipmentLine: Record "Sales Shipment Line"; var SalesLine2: Record "Sales Line")`: Copies document attachments from the sales order line to the invoice line.
