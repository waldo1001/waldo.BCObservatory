## Procedures

- `PostPrepmtInvoiceYN(var SalesHeader2: Record "Sales Header"; Print: Boolean)`: Posts a prepayment invoice for the sales order after prompting the user for confirmation.
- `PostPrepmtCrMemoYN(var SalesHeader2: Record "Sales Header"; Print: Boolean)`: Posts a prepayment credit memo for the sales order after prompting the user for confirmation.
- `Preview(var SalesHeader: Record "Sales Header"; DocumentType: Option)`: Previews the posting of a prepayment document without actually posting it.
- `GetReport(var SalesHeader: Record "Sales Header"; DocumentType: Option Invoice,"Credit Memo")`: Prints the posted prepayment invoice or credit memo.
- `SetDocumentType(NewPrepmtDocumentType: Option)`: Sets the prepayment document type to be used for posting.
