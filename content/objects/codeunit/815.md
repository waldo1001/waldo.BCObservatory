## Procedures

- `Check(TableID: Integer)`: Validates that the correct table is being used for invoice posting.
- `SetPreviewMode(NewPreviewMode: Boolean)`: Sets whether the invoice posting is running in preview mode.
- `SetSuppressCommit(NewSuppressCommit: Boolean)`: Sets whether database commits should be suppressed during invoice posting.
- `SetHideProgressWindow(NewHideProgressWindow: Boolean)`: Sets whether the progress window should be hidden during invoice posting.
- `SetParameters(NewInvoicePostingParameters: Record "Invoice Posting Parameters")`: Sets the invoice posting parameters to be used during posting.
- `SetTotalLines(TotalDocumentLine: Variant; TotalDocumentLineLCY: Variant)`: Sets the total lines used for posting the customer ledger entry.
- `ClearBuffers()`: Clears all temporary buffers used for deferral and invoice posting.
- `PrepareLine(DocumentHeaderVar: Variant; DocumentLineVar: Variant; DocumentLineACYVar: Variant)`: Prepares a sales line for invoice posting by calculating amounts and creating invoice posting buffer entries.
- `SetSalesTax(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"; var InvoicePostingBuffer: Record "Invoice Posting Buffer")` (internal)
- `PrepareInvoicePostingBuffer(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"; var InvoicePostingBuffer: Record "Invoice Posting Buffer")`: Initializes the invoice posting buffer with values from the sales line.
- `PostLines(DocumentHeaderVar: Variant; var GenJnlPostLine: Codeunit "Gen. Jnl.-Post Line"; var Window: Dialog; var TotalAmount: Decimal)`: Posts all invoice posting buffer lines to the general ledger.
- `PrepareJobLine(SalesHeaderVar: Variant; SalesLineVar: Variant; SalesLineACYVar: Variant)`: Prepares and posts job-related entries for the sales line.
- `CheckCreditLine(SalesHeaderVar: Variant; SalesLineVar: Variant)`: Validates the sales line for credit memo posting. Currently not implemented.
- `PostLedgerEntry(SalesHeaderVar: Variant; var GenJnlPostLine: Codeunit "Gen. Jnl.-Post Line")`: Posts the customer ledger entry for the sales invoice or credit memo.
- `PostBalancingEntry(SalesHeaderVariant: Variant; var GenJnlPostLine: Codeunit "Gen. Jnl.-Post Line")`: Posts the balancing entry to apply payment to the customer ledger entry when a balancing account is specified.
- `CalcDeferralAmounts(SalesHeaderVar: Variant; SalesLineVar: Variant; OriginalDeferralAmount: Decimal)`: Calculates the deferral amounts for the sales line based on the quantity being invoiced.
- `CreatePostedDeferralSchedule(SalesLineVar: Variant; NewDocumentType: Integer; NewDocumentNo: Code[20]; NewLineNo: Integer; PostingDate: Date)`: Creates the posted deferral schedule from the sales line deferral information.
