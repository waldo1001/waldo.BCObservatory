## Procedures

- `GetTimezoneOffset(SourceDateTime: DateTime; TimeZoneId: Text): Duration`: Retrieves the offset from the requested time zone at the time of the requested datetime. This takes into account any daylight saving time conditions that may apply.
- `GetTimezoneOffset(SourceDateTime: DateTime): Duration`: Retrieves the offset from the user time zone at the time of the requested datetime. This takes into account any daylight saving time conditions that may apply.
- `GetTimezoneOffset(SourceDateTime: DateTime; SourceTimeZoneId: Text; DestinationTimeZoneId: Text): Duration`: Retrieves the offset of the destination time zone from the source time zone for the indicated datetime. This takes into account any daylight saving time conditions that may apply.
- `TimeZoneSupportsDaylightSavingTime(TimeZoneId: Text): Boolean`: Checks whether the indicated time zone supports daylight saving time.
- `IsDaylightSavingTime(DateTimeToCheck: DateTime; TimeZoneId: Text): Boolean`: Checks whether the requested datetime falls within the indicated time zone's daylight saving time period.
