## Procedures

- `ResetSubscribedSKU(): Boolean`: Sets the enumerator for the subscribed SKUs to its initial position, which is before the first subscribed SKU in the collection.
- `NextSubscribedSKU(): Boolean`: Advances the enumerator to the next subscribed SKU in the collection. If only known service plans should be included, it advances to the next SKU known in Business Central.
- `SubscribedSKUCapabilityStatus(): Text`: Gets the capability status of the subscribed SKU that the enumerator is currently pointing to in the collection.
- `SubscribedSKUConsumedUnits(): Integer`: Gets the number of licenses assigned to the subscribed SKU that the enumerator is currently pointing to in the collection.
- `SubscribedSKUObjectId(): Text`: Gets the object ID of the subscribed SKU that the enumerator is currently pointing to in the collection.
- `SubscribedSKUPrepaidUnitsInEnabledState(): Integer`: Gets the number of prepaid licenses that are enabled for the subscribed SKU that the enumerator is currently pointing to in the collection.
- `SubscribedSKUPrepaidUnitsInSuspendedState(): Integer`: Gets the number of prepaid licenses that are suspended for the subscribed SKU that the enumerator is currently pointing to in the collection.
- `SubscribedSKUPrepaidUnitsInWarningState(): Integer`: Gets the number of prepaid licenses that are in warning status for the subscribed SKU that the enumerator is currently pointing to in the collection.
- `SubscribedSKUId(): Text`: Gets the unique identifier (GUID) for the subscribed SKU that the enumerator is currently pointing to in the collection.
- `SubscribedSKUPartNumber(): Text`: Gets the part number of the subscribed SKU that the enumerator is currently pointing to in the collection. For example, "AAD_PREMIUM" OR "RMSBASIC."
- `ResetServicePlans()`: Sets the enumerator for service plans to its initial position, which is before the first service plan in the collection.
- `NextServicePlan(): Boolean`: Advances the enumerator to the next service plan in the collection.
- `ServicePlanCapabilityStatus(): Text`: Gets the service plan capability status.
- `ServicePlanId(): Text`: Gets the service plan ID.
- `ServicePlanName(): Text`: Gets the service plan name.
- `IncludeUnknownPlans(): Boolean`: Checks whether to include unknown plans when moving to the next subscribed SKU in the subscribed SKUs collection.
- `SetIncludeUnknownPlans(NewIncludeUnknownPlans: Boolean)`: Sets whether to include unknown plans when moving to the next subscribed SKU in subscribed SKUs collection.
