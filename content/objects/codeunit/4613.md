## Events published

- `OnBeforeAddFrom(var FromName: Text; var FromAddress: Text; EmailMessageId: Guid)` (integration): Allows switching of From name and address when sending the email. The email used to connect to the server is still the same and the from address needs to have permission to Send As.

## Procedures

- `AddFrom(Name: Text; Address: Text)`: Add the name and email address the message is being sent from.
- `AddFrom(Name: Text; Address: Text; EmailMessageId: Guid)`: Add the name and email address the message is being sent from.
- `SetToRecipients(Recipients: List of [Text])`: Adds the recipients that this email is being sent to.
- `SetCCRecipients(Recipients: List of [Text])`: Adds the cc recipients that this email is being sent to.
- `SetBCCRecipients(Recipients: List of [Text])`: Adds the bcc recipients that this email is being sent to.
- `SetSubject(Subject: Text)`: Adds the subject of this email.
- `SetBody(Body: Text)`: The body of the email. The body is expected to be HTML formatted.
- `SetBody(Body: Text; HTMLFormatted: Boolean)`: Sets the body of the email.
- `AddAttachment(AttachmentInStream: InStream; AttachmentName: Text): Boolean`: Adds an attachment to the email.
- `GetMessage(var EmailMimeMessage: DotNet MimeMessage)` (internal)
