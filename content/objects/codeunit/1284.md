## Procedures

- `GenerateSecretPassword(): SecretText`: Generates a password that consists of a number of characters defined by the method, and meets the conditions.
- `GenerateSecretPassword(Length: Integer): SecretText`: Generates a password that consists of a user-defined number of characters, and meets the conditions.
- `IsPasswordStrong(Password: SecretText): Boolean`: Check whether the password meets the following conditions: - Contains at least the number characters defined by event, but it cannot be less than eight. - Contains uppercase and lowercase characters, digits, and special characters. - Does not contain sequences of characters. For example, aaa or 123.
- `GetPasswordMinLength(): Integer`: Gets the minimum length of the password. It is defined by event, but it cannot be less than eight.
