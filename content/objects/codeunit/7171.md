## Procedures

- `CalcAvailability(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Decimal`: Calculates the available quantity to promise for the item on the sales line.
- `CalcAvailabilityDate(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Date`: Calculates the availability date based on the shipment date or work date.
- `CalcAvailableInventory(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Decimal`: Calculates the available inventory for the item on the sales line.
- `CalcScheduledReceipt(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Decimal`: Calculates the scheduled receipt quantity for the item on the sales line.
- `CalcGrossRequirements(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Decimal`: Calculates the gross requirement for the item on the sales line.
- `CalcReservedRequirements(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Decimal`: Calculates the reserved receipt for the item on the sales line.
- `CalcReservedDemand(SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Decimal`: Calculates the reserved requirement for the item on the sales line.
- `GetQtyReservedFromStockState(SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Enum "Reservation From Stock"`: Gets the reservation from stock state for the sales line.
- `CalcNoOfSubstitutions(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Integer`: Calculates the number of item substitutes available for the sales line item.
- `CalcNoOfSalesPrices(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Integer`: Calculates the number of sales prices available for the sales line.
- `CalcNoOfSalesLineDisc(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Integer`: Calculates the number of sales line discounts available for the sales line.
- `LookupItem(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line")`: Opens the Item Card page for the item on the sales line.
- `ResetItemNo()`: Resets the cached item number in the Available to Promise codeunit.
- `GetItem(var SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Boolean`: Gets the item record for the sales line.
