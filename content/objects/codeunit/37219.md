## Procedures

- `ValidateDocument(RecordVariant: Variant)`: Validates a service credit memo header against PEPPOL 3.0 requirements. Checks mandatory fields, formats, and business rules at the document level.
- `ValidateDocumentLines(RecordVariant: Variant)`: Validates all service credit memo lines associated with a service header against PEPPOL 3.0 requirements. Iterates through all lines and performs line-level validation checks.
- `ValidateDocumentLine(RecordVariant: Variant)`: Validates a single service credit memo line against PEPPOL 3.0 requirements. Performs validation checks on individual line fields and business rules.
- `ValidatePostedDocument(RecordVariant: Variant)`: Validates a posted service document against PEPPOL 3.0 requirements. Validates the posted document for compliance before export or transmission.
- `ValidateLineTypeAndDescription(RecordVariant: Variant): Boolean`: Validates the service line
