## Procedures

- `CancelPostedExpenseReport(var PostedExpenseReportHeader: Record "Posted Expense Report Header")`: Prompts the user for confirmation and cancels the posted expense report. Reverses the G/L transactions and the Expense Ledger Entry, releases the related Expense records, and marks the header status as Canceled.
