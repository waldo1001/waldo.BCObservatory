## Procedures

- `CancelPostedInvoice(var SalesInvoiceHeader: Record "Sales Invoice Header"): Boolean`: Cancels the posted sales invoice by creating and posting a corrective credit memo.
- `CreateCreditMemoCopyDocument(var SalesInvoiceHeader: Record "Sales Invoice Header"; var SalesHeader: Record "Sales Header"): Boolean`: Creates a credit memo document as a copy of the posted sales invoice without posting it.
- `CreateCorrectiveCreditMemo(var InvoiceNotification: Notification)`: Creates a corrective credit memo from a notification action triggered on a posted sales invoice.
- `ShowAppliedEntries(var InvoiceNotification: Notification)`: Shows the applied customer ledger entries for the posted sales invoice referenced in the notification.
- `SkipCorrectiveCreditMemo(var InvoiceNotification: Notification)`: Dismisses the corrective credit memo notification without taking any action.
- `CancelPostedInvoiceCreateNewInvoice(var SalesInvoiceHeader: Record "Sales Invoice Header"; var SalesHeader: Record "Sales Header")`: Cancels the posted sales invoice and creates a new sales invoice as a copy for correction.
- `TestCorrectInvoiceIsAllowed(var SalesInvoiceHeader: Record "Sales Invoice Header"; Cancelling: Boolean)`: Tests whether the posted sales invoice can be corrected or canceled by checking payment status, customer blocks, and other conditions.
- `FindItemLedgEntries(var ItemLedgEntry: Record "Item Ledger Entry"; InvNo: Code[20])`: Finds all item ledger entries associated with the specified posted sales invoice.
- `ErrorHelperHeader(HeaderErrorType: Enum "Correct Sales Inv. Error Type"; SalesInvoiceHeader: Record "Sales Invoice Header")`: Raises an error message based on the header error type encountered during invoice correction validation.
- `UpdateSalesOrderLineIfExist(SalesCreditMemoNo: Code[20])` (internal): Updates the related sales order lines when a credit memo exists for the specified credit memo number.
