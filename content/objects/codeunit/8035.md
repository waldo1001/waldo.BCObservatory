## Procedures

- `StartActivity(ActivityText: Text; NewTotalCount: Integer)`: Opens the progress dialog (only when GuiAllowed) and starts the elapsed-time clock.
- `UpdateProgress(ProcessedCount: Integer; DetailText: Text)`: Updates the dialog. Redraws are throttled (at most once per second, plus whenever the detail text changes or the last item is reached) to avoid excessive flicker.
- `Finish()`: Closes the progress dialog if it is open.
