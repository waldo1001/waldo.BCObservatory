- `Submit(var SpendRequest: Record "Spend Request"; SubmitterExpenseUserNo: Code[20]; SubmissionComment: Text)` (internal): Submits a travel request on behalf of an expense user with an optional submitter comment, for example a justification when resubmitting a rejected travel request.
- `PrepareManualRelease(var SpendRequest: Record "Spend Request")` (internal): Starts a new approval for an open travel request released from the Business Central client, like a submission without a comment.
