## Procedures

- `FindOrCreateExpenseVendor(var Expense: Record Expense)`: Tries to match the expense to an existing Business Central vendor or creates an Expense Vendor record for accountant review. Updates Expense."Expense Vendor No.".
- `RequestApproval(var ExpenseVendor: Record "Expense Vendor")`: Submits the expense vendor for accountant approval (Unmatched/Rejected → Pending Approval).
- `Approve(var ExpenseVendor: Record "Expense Vendor")`: Approves the expense vendor. Creates a new BC Vendor if not yet matched, or confirms the existing match.
- `Reject(var ExpenseVendor: Record "Expense Vendor"; RejectReason: Text[250])`: Rejects the expense vendor with a mandatory reason.
