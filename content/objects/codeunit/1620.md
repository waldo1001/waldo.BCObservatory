## Procedures

- `CheckSalesDocument(SalesHeader: Record "Sales Header")`: Validates a sales document header against PEPPOL requirements including company information, customer data, and required fields.
- `CheckSalesDocumentLines(SalesHeader: Record "Sales Header")`: Validates all lines of a sales document against PEPPOL requirements.
- `CheckSalesDocumentLine(SalesLine: Record "Sales Line")`: Validates a single sales document line against PEPPOL requirements including unit of measure codes, descriptions, and VAT settings.
- `CheckSalesInvoice(SalesInvoiceHeader: Record "Sales Invoice Header")`: Validates a posted sales invoice and all its lines against PEPPOL requirements.
- `CheckSalesCreditMemo(SalesCrMemoHeader: Record "Sales Cr.Memo Header")`: Validates a posted sales credit memo and all its lines against PEPPOL requirements.
- `CheckSalesLineTypeAndDescription(SalesLine: Record "Sales Line"): Boolean`: Checks if a sales line has a type set but is missing a description.
