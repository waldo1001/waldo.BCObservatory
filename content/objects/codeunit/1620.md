- `CheckSalesDocumentLine(SalesLine: Record "Sales Line")`: Validates a single sales document line against PEPPOL requirements including unit of measure codes, descriptions, and VAT settings.

- `CheckSalesCreditMemo(SalesCrMemoHeader: Record "Sales Cr.Memo Header")`: Validates a posted sales credit memo and all its lines against PEPPOL requirements.
