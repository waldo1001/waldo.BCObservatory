## Procedures

- `ValidateDocument(RecordVariant: Variant)`: Validates a sales document header against PEPPOL 3.0 requirements. Validates mandatory fields, formats, and business rules at the document level.
- `ValidateDocumentLines(RecordVariant: Variant)`: Validates all sales document lines associated with a sales header against PEPPOL 3.0 requirements. Iterates through all lines and performs line-level validation Validates.
- `ValidateDocumentLine(RecordVariant: Variant)`: Validates a single sales document line against PEPPOL 3.0 requirements. Performs validation Validates on individual line fields and business rules.
- `ValidatePostedDocument(RecordVariant: Variant)`: Validates a posted sales document against PEPPOL 3.0 requirements. Validates the posted document for compliance before export or transmission.
- `ValidateLineTypeAndDescription(RecordVariant: Variant): Boolean`: Validates the sales line
- `CheckCurrencyCode(CurrencyCode: Code[10])`: Validates that the currency code is a valid 3-character ISO currency code.
- `CheckCountryRegionCode(CountryRegionCode: Code[10])`: Validates that the country/region code has a valid 2-character ISO code.
- `CheckShipToAddress(SalesHeader: Record "Sales Header")`: Validates the ship-to address fields on a sales header.
- `CheckTaxCategory(SalesLine: Record "Sales Line")`: Validates the tax category for a sales line.
- `EnsureZeroRate(VatPercent: Decimal; TaxCategoryCode: Code[10])`: Ensures the VAT percentage is zero for tax categories that require it.
- `EnsurePositiveRate(VatPercent: Decimal; TaxCategoryCode: Code[10])`: Ensures the VAT percentage is positive for tax categories that require it.
- `EnsureSingleOutsideScopeVATBreakdown(SalesLine: Record "Sales Line")`: Ensures only a single outside scope VAT breakdown exists for PEPPOL compliance.
