- `CalculateWithSalesHeader(var TempSalesHeader: Record "Sales Header"; var TempSalesLine: Record "Sales Line")`: Calculates invoice discount using the provided sales header without updating it.
- `CalculateInvoiceDiscountOnLine(var SalesLineToUpdate: Record "Sales Line")`: Calculates and applies invoice discount to a single sales line.
- `CalculateIncDiscForHeader(var TempSalesHeader: Record "Sales Header")`: Calculates invoice discount for a sales header if automatic calculation is enabled.
