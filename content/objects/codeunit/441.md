- `AssertPrepmtAmountNotMoreThanDocAmount(DocumentTotalInclVAT: Decimal; PrepmtTotalInclVAT: Decimal; CurrencyCode: Code[10]; InvoiceRoundingSetup: Boolean)`: Validates that prepayment amount does not exceed the total document amount including VAT.
