## Procedures

- `GetLastEntryNo(): Integer`: Gets the entry number of the last detailed customer ledger entry in the table.
- `UpdateDebitCredit(Correction: Boolean)`: Updates the debit and credit amounts based on the sign of the amount and correction flag.
- `SetZeroTransNo(TransactionNo: Integer)`: Sets the transaction number to zero and assigns an application number for all entries with the specified transaction number.
- `GetUnrealizedGainLossAmount(EntryNo: Integer): Decimal`: Calculates the sum of unrealized gain and loss amounts for a customer ledger entry.
