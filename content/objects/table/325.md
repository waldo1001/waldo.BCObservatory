- `GetSalesAccount(Unrealized: Boolean): Code[20]`: Returns the appropriate sales VAT G/L account based on whether unrealized VAT is being processed. Retrieves either realized or unrealized sales VAT account with validation for required account setup.
- `GetPurchAccount(Unrealized: Boolean): Code[20]`: Returns the appropriate purchase VAT G/L account based on whether unrealized VAT is being processed. Retrieves either realized or unrealized purchase VAT account with validation for required account setup.
- `GetRevChargeAccount(Unrealized: Boolean): Code[20]`: Returns the appropriate reverse charge VAT G/L account based on whether unrealized VAT is being processed. Retrieves either realized or unrealized reverse charge VAT account for EU and domestic reverse charge transactions.
