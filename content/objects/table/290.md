## Procedures

- `CheckVATDifference(NewCurrencyCode: Code[10]; NewAllowVATDifference: Boolean)`: Validates VAT difference against maximum allowed VAT difference limits.
- `InsertLine(): Boolean`: Inserts or updates VAT amount line with calculated amounts, combining with existing line if found.
- `GetLine(Number: Integer)`: Retrieves VAT amount line by sequential number from the recordset.
- `VATAmountText(): Text[30]`: Generates descriptive text for VAT amount display based on VAT percentage.
- `GetTotalLineAmount(SubtractVAT: Boolean; CurrencyCode: Code[10]): Decimal`: Calculates total line amount across all VAT amount lines with optional VAT subtraction.
- `GetTotalVATAmount(): Decimal`: Calculates total VAT amount across all VAT amount lines in the recordset.
- `GetTotalInvDiscAmount(): Decimal`: Calculates total invoice discount amount across all VAT amount lines.
- `GetTotalInvDiscBaseAmount(SubtractVAT: Boolean; CurrencyCode: Code[10]): Decimal`: Calculates total invoice discount base amount with optional VAT subtraction.
- `GetTotalVATBase(): Decimal`: Calculates total VAT base amount across all VAT amount lines.
- `GetTotalAmountInclVAT(): Decimal`: Calculates total amount including VAT across all VAT amount lines.
- `GetTotalVATDiscount(CurrencyCode: Code[10]; NewPricesIncludingVAT: Boolean): Decimal`: Calculates total VAT discount amount based on rounding differences.
- `GetAnyLineModified(): Boolean`: Checks whether any VAT amount line has been manually modified.
- `SetInvoiceDiscountAmount(NewInvoiceDiscount: Decimal; NewCurrencyCode: Code[10]; NewPricesIncludingVAT: Boolean; NewVATBaseDiscPct: Decimal)`: Distributes invoice discount amount proportionally across VAT amount lines.
- `SetInvoiceDiscountPercent(NewInvoiceDiscountPct: Decimal; NewCurrencyCode: Code[10]; NewPricesIncludingVAT: Boolean; CalcInvDiscPerVATID: Boolean; NewVATBaseDiscPct: Decimal)`: Applies invoice discount percentage to VAT amount lines with proportional distribution.
- `CalcLineAmount(): Decimal`: Calculates line amount after subtracting invoice discount from original line amount.
- `CalcVATFields(NewCurrencyCode: Code[10]; NewPricesIncludingVAT: Boolean; NewVATBaseDiscPct: Decimal)`: Recalculates VAT amounts and VAT base based on current line amount and VAT parameters.
- `GetBaseLCY(PostingDate: Date; CurrencyCode: Code[10]; CurrencyFactor: Decimal): Decimal`: Converts VAT base amount from foreign currency to local currency using exchange rate.
- `GetAmountLCY(PostingDate: Date; CurrencyCode: Code[10]; CurrencyFactor: Decimal): Decimal`: Calculates VAT amount in local currency by converting from foreign currency.
- `DeductVATAmountLine(var VATAmountLineDeduct: Record "VAT Amount Line")`: Deducts amounts from current VAT amount lines using corresponding lines from another recordset.
- `ApplyNonDeductibleVAT(NonDeductibleVAT: Decimal)` (internal)
- `UpdateLines(var TotalVATAmount: Decimal; Currency: Record Currency; CurrencyFactor: Decimal; PricesIncludingVAT: Boolean; VATBaseDiscountPercHeader: Decimal; TaxAreaCode: Code[20]; TaxLiable: Boolean; PostingDate: Date)`: Updates VAT amount lines with calculated VAT amounts and totals based on currency, pricing settings, and tax configuration. Performs comprehensive VAT calculations including sales tax processing and currency conversions.
- `CopyFromPurchInvLine(PurchInvLine: Record "Purch. Inv. Line")`: Copies VAT-related fields from posted purchase invoice line to create VAT amount line for analysis or reporting.
- `CopyFromPurchCrMemoLine(PurchCrMemoLine: Record "Purch. Cr. Memo Line")`: Copies VAT-related fields from posted purchase credit memo line to create VAT amount line for analysis or reporting.
- `CopyFromSalesInvLine(SalesInvoiceLine: Record "Sales Invoice Line")`: Copies VAT-related fields from posted sales invoice line to create VAT amount line for analysis or reporting.
- `CopyFromSalesCrMemoLine(SalesCrMemoLine: Record "Sales Cr.Memo Line")`: Copies VAT-related fields from posted sales credit memo line to create VAT amount line for analysis or reporting.
