## Procedures

- `PopulateVATBusGrp()`: Populates the table with standard VAT business posting groups for wizard selection. Creates default entries for Domestic, EU, and Export VAT business posting groups.
- `InsertBusPostingGrp(GrpCode: Code[20]; GrpDesc: Text[100]; IsDefault: Boolean)`: Inserts a VAT business posting group entry for wizard selection with specified parameters. Creates temporary record for user selection during VAT setup wizard process.
- `ValidateVATBusGrp(): Boolean`: Validates that at least one VAT business posting group is selected for creation. Checks user selections to ensure wizard can proceed with VAT business posting group setup.
- `CheckExistingCustomersAndVendorsWithVAT(VATBusPostingGroupCode: Code[20]): Boolean`: Checks whether customers or vendors already exist with the specified VAT business posting group. Validates data dependencies before allowing VAT business posting group modifications.
