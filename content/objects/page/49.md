For a procedure body or the calls of one procedure, ask bc-code-atlas (external, by Stefan Maron, MIT; default corpus w1-28), which the bc-observatory plugin connects: `bcatlas_resolve_node(object_type: "page", object_name: "Purchase Quote")`, then `bcatlas_get_procedure_body` or `bcatlas_get_neighbors` on the returned id.
