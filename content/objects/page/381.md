- `SetUserInteractions()`: Sets user interaction styling based on the application status of entries. Updates visual styling to indicate applied, mismatched, or unmatched entries.
- `ShowAll()`: Shows all available bank account ledger entries for reconciliation without filtering. Resets filters and displays the complete set of entries for the bank account.
- `ShowNonMatched()`: Filters the list to show only bank account ledger entries that have not been matched to statement lines. Useful for identifying outstanding transactions during reconciliation.
- `ShowReversed()`: Shows reversed bank account ledger entries in the reconciliation list. Includes previously hidden reversed entries for complete transaction visibility.
- `HideReversed()`: Hides reversed bank account ledger entries from the reconciliation list. Excludes reversed entries to focus on active transactions for reconciliation.
- `SetBankRecDateFilter(StatementDate: Date)`: Applies date-based filtering to show entries within the bank reconciliation date range. Filters entries to optimize reconciliation matching by focusing on relevant date periods.
