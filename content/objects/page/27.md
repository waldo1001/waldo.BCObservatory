- `bcatlas_resolve_node(object_type: "page", object_name: "Vendor List")`, then `bcatlas_get_neighbors` or `bcatlas_get_procedure_body` on the returned id.
- CLI: `node bc-code-atlas.js resolve-node page "Vendor List"`
