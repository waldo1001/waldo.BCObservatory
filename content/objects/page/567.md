## Procedures

- `GetDimSelBuf(var TheDimSelectionBuf: Record "Dimension Selection Buffer")`: Retrieves all dimension selections with change criteria from the temporary buffer. Transfers complete dimension configuration including filters and new values to calling process.
- `InsertDimSelBuf(NewSelected: Boolean; NewCode: Text[30]; NewDescription: Text[30]; NewNewDimValueCode: Code[20]; NewDimValueFilter: Text[250])`: Adds a dimension to the selection buffer with change criteria including new dimension value codes. Automatically resolves dimension descriptions and configures lookup tables for various entity types.
