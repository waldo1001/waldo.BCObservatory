Events mode: who subscribesRing 1 becomes the object's published events (diamonds, sized by subscriber count); picking one fans out its subscribers, coloured by their system. Events with no subscribers stay as a count, not as ink.
HoverThe edge goes active (#C6D6FF, 2px) and the tooltip names the relation kind and the field that carries it. Everything else holds still: no reflow, no dimming on hover.
Keyboard focusThe picture is a view on a list. Tab enters a ring, arrows walk it, Enter selects, E expands, Esc steps back along the trail. The focus ring (accent, 2px, offset 2px) shows on the node and on its list row together.
"+ 163 more": big rings stay listsThe picture never draws more than 7 per ring (the most connected). The rest open in the panel, grouped by system with counts, filterable by relation kind. This is what keeps 170 from becoming a hairball.
Per page: the one-hop diagramA 130px inline SVG in the side column: the object, its top neighbour per ring, the total. Rendered at build time, no script, a few hundred bytes. It links into this explorer already centred; the galaxy locator sits above it.
Empty and loadingEmpty says so in a sentence and offers the two next moves. Loading draws the centre and the empty rings at once (they need no data), then fills ring by ring; with reduced motion the nodes simply appear.
