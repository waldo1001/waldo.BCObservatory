Tilt 0: this is DA is not a separate page. At Tilt 0 the system is the flat galaxy with every kind of thing on one plane. Nothing new is loaded until the slider moves.
TiltingThe plane tips (fixed angle, no free orbit) and splits into four, each thing sliding to its own plane without changing x/y. Lines between planes fade in only at full tilt. Reduced motion: a cut.
Core sampleFocusing any star lights what sits straight above and below it and dims the rest to 0.3. The panel lists the same sample per plane. Focus a hub and the sample goes down into the objects its Learn pages name.
Country lens: what does BE change in Finance?The country plane becomes one country. Every W1 object it replaces gets a dashed line down to its BE copy; untouched objects dim. Planes the lens does not use fold away. The panel lists the replaced objects and exits to the country diff for the why.
Coverage lens: what is missingThe inverse view: documented objects dim, objects with no Learn page above them get an accent outline, and the count is in the panel as a list. The same lens on the hub plane marks hubs with no video above.
List per planeThe fallback and, for counting, the better view: one tab per plane, one row per thing, one column per other plane with the number of lines to it. Sort by a column to get the gaps. Tab, arrows, Enter focus a row and its core sample.
